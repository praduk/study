An **axiom** is one sentence. An **axiom schema** supplies one axiom for each formula of the language (subject to stated restrictions). Separation and Replacement are schemas, so ZF is not finitely listed by the familiar English names alone.

The course uses the standard nonempty-domain semantics of first-order logic. Equality is logical identity. Ordered pairs will be represented by Kuratowski pairs, but all later results are representation-independent once the ordered-pair property is proved.

ZF denotes Extensionality, Pairing, Union, Power Set, Infinity, Separation, Replacement, and Foundation. With the standard nonempty-domain semantics, empty-set existence is derived here directly from Separation; some equivalent axiom lists state it separately. ZFC is ZF plus Choice. Other equivalent axiom lists occur in the literature; changing the list is harmless only after the equivalences have been checked.
