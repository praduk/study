An $L$-sentence is an $L$-formula $φ$ with $FV(φ)=∅$. An $L$-theory is any set of $L$-sentences. A theory may be finite or infinite and may be inconsistent. Its vocabulary is the least sublanguage containing every nonlogical symbol that occurs in its sentences.

Some books reserve `theory` for a deductively closed set. This course does not. When closure matters, it is stated explicitly.
