For a term $t$, the expression $φ[t/x]$ denotes the result of replacing the free occurrences of $x$ in $φ$ by $t$. The substitution is admissible, or $t$ is free for $x$ in $φ$, when no variable occurring in $t$ becomes bound after replacement.

Recursively, substitution passes through connectives. At $Qyψ$, if $y=x$ then free occurrences of $x$ inside are already bound and no replacement is made. If $y≠x$ and $y$ does not occur in $t$, substitute inside. If $y≠x$ but $y$ occurs in $t$, first rename the bound variable $y$ to a fresh variable, then continue. This last clause is what prevents capture.
