Every structure satisfying $P(c)$ has some $P$-object, so its reduct satisfies $∃xP(x)$. Conversely, any model of $∃xP(x)$ can be expanded by interpreting the new constant $c$ as a witness, so the two sentences are equisatisfiable.

They are not equivalent as sentences of the expanded language. Take domain $\{0,1\}$, let $P$ hold only of $0$, and interpret $c$ as $1$. Then $∃xP(x)$ is true while $P(c)$ is false. Skolemization chooses an expansion; it does not assert that every interpretation of the fresh symbol supplies a witness.
