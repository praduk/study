There is no derivation schema of that form. Take the instance $A=p$ and $B=q$, where $p,q$ are distinct atoms, and choose a valuation with $v(p)=0$ and $v(q)=1$. Then $p\to q$ and $q$ are true while $p$ is false, so $p\to q,q\not\models p$. If a derivation schema $A\to B,B\vdash A$ existed, substituting $p,q$ for $A,B$ and applying natural-deduction soundness would imply this false semantic consequence. The invalid pattern is affirming the consequent.
