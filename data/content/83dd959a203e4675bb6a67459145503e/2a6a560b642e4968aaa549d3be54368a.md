If $𝔑≺𝔐$ and the existential holds in $𝔐$, elementarity makes it hold in $𝔑$, whose witness lies in $N$; elementarity again makes the instance true in $𝔐$.

Conversely, assume the witness condition. Prove by structural induction that $𝔑$ and $𝔐$ agree on every formula with parameters from $N$. Atomic agreement is the substructure condition; Boolean cases follow by induction. For an existential, truth in $𝔑$ supplies a witness in $N$ and hence truth in $𝔐$ by induction. Truth in $𝔐$ supplies, by the assumed condition, a witness $b∈N$ whose instance is true in $𝔐$ and therefore in $𝔑$ by induction. Universal formulas follow from existential formulas and negation. Thus all formulas agree, which is elementarity.
