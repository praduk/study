Equality introduction permits $t=t$ for any term $t$. Equality elimination, or substitution of identicals, permits inference of $φ[t/x]$ from $s=t$ and $φ[s/x]$, provided both displayed substitutions are free for $x$ in $φ$.

Symmetry follows by applying elimination to $s=t$ and the formula $x=s$, starting from $s=s$. Transitivity follows by substituting in $s=t$ using $t=u$. Congruence for functions and relations is obtained by repeated equality elimination.
