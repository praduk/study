Let the parents be $C\cup\{p\}$ and $D\cup\{\neg p\}$, and suppose a valuation satisfies both. If it falsified the resolvent $C\cup D$, it would falsify every literal in $C$ and every literal in $D$. To satisfy the first parent it would then have to make $p$ true. To satisfy the second it would have to make $\neg p$ true, which is impossible. Hence it satisfies the resolvent.

Inducting along a resolution derivation, every valuation satisfying all initial clauses satisfies every derived clause. No valuation satisfies the empty clause. Therefore deriving it proves that the initial clause set has no satisfying valuation.
