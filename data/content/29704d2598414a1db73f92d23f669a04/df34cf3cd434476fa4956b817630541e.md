Expand $L$ by constants $c_α$ for $α<κ$, and let $T'=T∪\{c_α≠c_β:α<β<κ\}$. Every finite subset of $T'$ mentions only finitely many new constants. Interpret them as distinct elements in an infinite model of $T$; hence every finite subset is satisfiable. Compactness gives a model $𝔐'⊨T'$, whose domain has at least $κ$ elements.

Apply downward Löwenheim–Skolem in the expanded language, with $A$ the set of interpretations of all $c_α$. This yields an elementary substructure $𝔑'≺𝔐'$ containing $A$ and having size at most $\max(κ,|L|,ℵ_0)=κ$. The distinct constants force size at least $κ$, so $|N'|=κ$. The reduct of $𝔑'$ to $L$ is a model of $T$ of cardinality $κ$.
