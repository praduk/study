Finite strings over the proof alphabet can be enumerated effectively. For each string, mechanically check whether it codes a correct finite natural-deduction derivation: formation rules, rule applications, discharged assumptions, substitutions, and eigenvariable side conditions are all decidable finite checks. Output the conclusion whenever the checked derivation has no undischarged assumptions. Soundness ensures that every output is valid. Completeness ensures that every valid sentence has some finite derivation and will eventually be encountered. The procedure need not halt when a proposed sentence is invalid, so this proves semidecidability rather than decidability.
