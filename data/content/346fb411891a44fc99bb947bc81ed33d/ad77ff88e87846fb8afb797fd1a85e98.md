By the Well-Ordering Theorem, choose ordinals $\alpha,\beta$ and bijections
$A\approx\alpha$, $B\approx\beta$. Let $\kappa=|A|$ and $\lambda=|B|$ be the least ordinals equinumerous with them. These are cardinals. Ordinal trichotomy gives exactly one of
$\kappa<\lambda$, $\kappa=\lambda$, or $\lambda<\kappa$.

If $\kappa\le\lambda$, inclusion $\kappa\hookrightarrow\lambda$, composed with the chosen bijections, gives an injection $A\to B$; symmetrically in the other case. Equality of cardinal representatives is exactly equinumerosity. Mutual strict inequalities are impossible by ordinal trichotomy and Cantor--Schröder--Bernstein, so the alternatives are exclusive.
