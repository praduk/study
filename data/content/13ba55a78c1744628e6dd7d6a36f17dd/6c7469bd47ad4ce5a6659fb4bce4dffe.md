An $L$-structure $𝔑$ is a substructure of $𝔐$ when $N⊆M$, it contains the interpretations of constants, is closed under interpreted functions, and each relation of $𝔑$ is the restriction of the corresponding relation of $𝔐$ to tuples from $N$.

Write $𝔑≺𝔐$ when, in addition, for every formula $φ(\bar x)$ and tuple $\bar a$ from $N$, $𝔑⊨φ(\bar a)$ iff $𝔐⊨φ(\bar a)$. Then $𝔑$ is an elementary substructure. Negation and quantifiers make an arbitrary substructure potentially non-elementary.
