Suppose $W\setminus X$ were nonempty. Since $W$ is well-ordered, it would have a least member $w$. Every $x<w$ must lie in $X$; otherwise $x$ would be a smaller member of $W\setminus X$. Thus $W_{<w}\subseteq X$. The induction hypothesis then gives $w\in X$, contradicting the choice of $w$. Hence $W\setminus X$ is empty and $X=W$.
