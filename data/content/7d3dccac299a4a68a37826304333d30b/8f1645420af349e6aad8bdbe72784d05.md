Suppose $U$ were a set containing every set. By Separation,

$$
R=\{x\in U:x\notin x\}
$$

would be a set. Since $U$ contains every set, $R\in U$. By the defining condition for $R$,

$$
R\in R\leftrightarrow R\notin R,
$$

which is impossible in classical logic. Therefore no such set $U$ exists. Notice that the proof uses only Separation once a proposed universal set is supplied; it does not invoke unrestricted comprehension.
