The membership relation well-orders $\theta$. If $\theta\setminus X$ were nonempty, let $\alpha$ be its least member. Every $\beta<\alpha$ would then belong to $X$, which says $\alpha\subseteq X$. The hypothesis would give $\alpha\in X$, a contradiction. Therefore $X=\theta$.

For the formula version, use Separation to form
$X=\{\alpha<\theta:P(\alpha)\}$ and apply the set version.
