Reflexivity follows from equality introduction and deductive closure. Symmetry and transitivity are derivable from equality elimination, so closure puts their conclusions in $Δ$. Thus $∼$ is an equivalence relation.

Suppose $s_i∼t_i$ for $1≤i≤n$. Repeated substitution of identicals in the formula $f(s_1,…,s_{i-1},x,s_{i+1},…,s_n)=f(s_1,…,s_n)$ derives $f(\bar s)=f(\bar t)$, so function application respects classes. Repeated substitution in $R(s_1,…,s_n)$ gives $R(\bar s)↔R(\bar t)$; maximal consistency and deductive closure therefore make one atomic formula a member of $Δ$ exactly when the other is.
