For a set $Γ$ of formulas, $Γ⊨φ$ means: for every $L$-structure $𝔐$ and assignment $g$, if $𝔐,g⊨γ$ for every $γ∈Γ$, then $𝔐,g⊨φ$. For sets of sentences, assignments may be omitted.

Formulas $φ$ and $ψ$ are logically equivalent when $⊨φ↔ψ$. A formula is valid exactly when its negation is unsatisfiable. Also $Γ⊨φ$ exactly when $Γ∪\{¬φ\}$ is unsatisfiable. The symbol $⊨$ records this model-theoretic relation; it does not mean that a derivation has been written.
