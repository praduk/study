Consider the positive integers ordered by divisibility: $a\preceq b$ iff $a$ divides $b$.

1. Prove that $\preceq$ is a partial order but not a linear order.
2. Identify a least element and show there is no greatest element.
3. Prove that every nonempty set of positive integers has a $\preceq$-minimal element, while the order is not a well-order.
