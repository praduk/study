For every set $A$, there is a set $P$ such that

$$
\forall x\,[x\in P\leftrightarrow x\subseteq A].
$$

By Extensionality $P$ is unique and is denoted $\mathcal{P}(A)$. The axiom collects **all subsets** of $A$ that exist in the ambient universe; it is stronger than merely saying that each individual subset is a set.
