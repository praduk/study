Universal elimination: from $∀xφ$ infer $φ[t/x]$, provided $t$ is free for $x$ in $φ$.

Universal introduction: from $φ$ infer $∀xφ$, provided $x$ is not free in any undischarged assumption on which that occurrence of $φ$ depends. Equivalently, reason with an arbitrary fresh parameter and generalize only when it carries no special assumption. The restriction blocks the invalid step from $P(x)$ as an assumption to $∀xP(x)$.
