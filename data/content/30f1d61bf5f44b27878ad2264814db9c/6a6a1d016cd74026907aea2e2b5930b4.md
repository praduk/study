Existence is by induction on rank. Assign the prescribed values to rank-zero formulas. Once $F$ has been assigned to all formulas of rank at most $k$, a formula of rank $k+1$ has, by unique readability, exactly one unary or binary outer constructor and children of rank at most $k$; the displayed equations assign it exactly one value. This defines $F$ on every finite rank.

For uniqueness, let $F$ and $G$ satisfy the equations. Structural induction shows $F(A)=G(A)$. The equality holds on atoms and constants by the base equations. If it holds for $A$, then $F(\neg A)=n(F(A))=n(G(A))=G(\neg A)$. If it holds for $A$ and $B$, then $F(A\circ B)=b_{\circ}(F(A),F(B))=b_{\circ}(G(A),G(B))=G(A\circ B)$. Hence $F=G$.
