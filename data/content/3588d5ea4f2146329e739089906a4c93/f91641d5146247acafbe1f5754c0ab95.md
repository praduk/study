Formulas $A$ and $B$ are **logically equivalent**, written $A\equiv B$, when

$$\llbracket A\rrbracket_v=\llbracket B\rrbracket_v$$

for every valuation $v$. Equivalently, $A\models B$ and $B\models A$; equivalently, $\models A\leftrightarrow B$. The symbol $\equiv$ is metalinguistic here: it asserts equality of truth functions rather than forming a larger object-language formula.
