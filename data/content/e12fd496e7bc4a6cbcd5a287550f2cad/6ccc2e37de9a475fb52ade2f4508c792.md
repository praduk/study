Let $T$ be consistent. By the maximal Henkin theory lemma, extend $T$ in an expanded language to a maximal consistent Henkin theory $Δ$. Build the quotient term model $𝔐_Δ$. For every $σ∈T$, also $σ∈Δ$, so the truth lemma gives $𝔐_Δ⊨σ$. Therefore $𝔐_Δ⊨T$. Reducing $𝔐_Δ$ to the original language leaves the interpretations of all original symbols unchanged, so every original sentence in $T$ remains true. Thus the reduct is a model of $T$.
