Let $S=\{A\in\mathsf{Form}:Q(A)\}$. Assumption 1 puts every atom and both constants in $S$. Assumption 2 says $S$ is closed under the unary formula constructor. Assumption 3 says it is closed under every binary formula constructor. By @math:prop:syntax:df:formula, $\mathsf{Form}$ is the least set containing the atoms and constants and closed under those constructors. Therefore $\mathsf{Form}\subseteq S$. Since $S\subseteq\mathsf{Form}$ by definition, $S=\mathsf{Form}$, so every formula has property $Q$.
