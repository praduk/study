For reflexivity, every valuation satisfying every member of $\Gamma$ satisfies $A$ because $A$ is one of those members.

For monotonicity, let a valuation satisfy $\Delta$. Since $\Gamma\subseteq\Delta$, it satisfies $\Gamma$, and $\Gamma\models A$ then makes it satisfy $A$.

For cut, let a valuation satisfy $\Gamma$. The first hypothesis makes it satisfy $B$, so it satisfies $\Gamma\cup\{B\}$. The second hypothesis then makes it satisfy $A$. In each case the chosen valuation was arbitrary, proving the asserted consequence.
