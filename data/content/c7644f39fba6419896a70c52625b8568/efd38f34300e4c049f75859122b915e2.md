For all formulas $A,B,C$, the following logical equivalences hold:

- $\neg\neg A\equiv A$.
- $\neg(A\land B)\equiv\neg A\lor\neg B$ and $\neg(A\lor B)\equiv\neg A\land\neg B$.
- $A\to B\equiv\neg A\lor B$.
- $A\leftrightarrow B\equiv(A\to B)\land(B\to A)$.
- $A\land B\equiv B\land A$ and $A\lor B\equiv B\lor A$.
- $(A\land B)\land C\equiv A\land(B\land C)$ and likewise for $\lor$.
- $A\land(B\lor C)\equiv(A\land B)\lor(A\land C)$, with the dual distributive law obtained by interchanging $\land$ and $\lor$.
- $A\land\true\equiv A$, $A\lor\false\equiv A$, $A\lor\true\equiv\true$, and $A\land\false\equiv\false$.
- $A\lor\neg A\equiv\true$ and $A\land\neg A\equiv\false$.
