The equality follows by structural induction on $A$. For an atom $p$, the left side is $\llbracket\sigma(p)\rrbracket_v$, which is $v_{\sigma}(p)$ by definition and hence the right side. Constants are unchanged by substitution and have fixed values. For negation, apply the induction hypothesis to the child and then the same unary truth function. For a binary formula, apply the induction hypotheses to both children and then the truth function of its connective.

If $A$ is a tautology, then for any $v$, the induced $v_{\sigma}$ is a valuation, so $\llbracket A\rrbracket_{v_{\sigma}}=1$. The equality just proved gives $\llbracket A\sigma\rrbracket_v=1$. Since $v$ was arbitrary, $A\sigma$ is a tautology.
