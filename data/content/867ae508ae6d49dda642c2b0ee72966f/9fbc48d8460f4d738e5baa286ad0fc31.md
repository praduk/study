The reverse implication follows by substitution. For the forward implication, observe

$$
\bigcap\langle a,b\rangle=\{a\},
\qquad
\bigcup\langle a,b\rangle=\{a,b\};
$$

these identities also hold when $a=b$. Equality of the two ordered pairs therefore gives $\{a\}=\{c\}$, hence $a=c$, and also $\{a,b\}=\{c,d\}=\{a,d\}$.

If $b=a$, the last equality forces $d=a$; otherwise $b\in\{a,d\}$ and $b\ne a$, so $b=d$. In either case $b=d$. Thus both coordinates agree.
