Let $a<b$. Suppose each $f_n$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$, the derivatives $f_n'$ converge uniformly on $(a,b)$ to $g$, and $f_n(x_0)$ converges for one $x_0\in[a,b]$. Then $f_n$ converges uniformly on $[a,b]$ to a function $f$, and $f'=g$ on $(a,b)$.

The bounded interval and the one-point convergence condition matter.

**Memory cue (added).** Uniform slopes plus one anchored value control the whole function.
