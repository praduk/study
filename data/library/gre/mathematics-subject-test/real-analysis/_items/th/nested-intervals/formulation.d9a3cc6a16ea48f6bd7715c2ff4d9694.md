For nested nonempty closed bounded intervals

$$
I_1\supseteq I_2\supseteq I_3\supseteq\cdots,
$$

the intersection $\bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty}I_n$ is nonempty. If their lengths tend to zero, the intersection contains exactly one point.

**Memory cue (added).** Closed, bounded, nested: something survives; shrinking lengths leave one point.
