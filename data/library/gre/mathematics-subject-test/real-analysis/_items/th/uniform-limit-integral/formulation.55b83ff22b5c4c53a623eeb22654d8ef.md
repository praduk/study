If each $f_n$ is Riemann integrable on the bounded interval $[a,b]$ and $f_n\to f$ uniformly there, then $f$ is Riemann integrable and

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_a^b f_n(x)\,dx=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Uniform error times interval length bounds integral error.
