Let $p(x)=a_nx^n+\cdots+a_0$ with $a_n\ne0$, $n\ge1$, and complex roots $r_1,\ldots,r_n$ counted with multiplicity. Then

$$
\sum_{i=1}^n r_i=-\frac{a_{n-1}}{a_n},
\qquad
\prod_{i=1}^n r_i=(-1)^n\frac{a_0}{a_n}.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** One root contributes one minus when you expand the product of $(x-r_i)$ factors.
