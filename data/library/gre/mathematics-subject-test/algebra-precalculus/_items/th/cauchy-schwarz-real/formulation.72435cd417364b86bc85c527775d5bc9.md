For real lists $a_1,\ldots,a_n$ and $b_1,\ldots,b_n$,

$$
\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_i b_i\right)^2
\le
\left(\sum_{i=1}^n a_i^2\right)
\left(\sum_{i=1}^n b_i^2\right).
$$

Equality holds when the two vectors are linearly dependent, including a zero vector.

**Memory cue (added).** Square of the dot product is at most product of squared lengths.
