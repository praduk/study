For nonnegative real $x_1,\ldots,x_n$ with $n\ge1$,

$$
\frac{x_1+\cdots+x_n}{n}\ge (x_1\cdots x_n)^{1/n}.
$$

Equality holds exactly when all $x_i$ are equal. In particular, $(x+y)/2\ge\sqrt{xy}$ for $x,y\ge0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Arithmetic is Above Geometric; equality means all alike.
