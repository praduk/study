For $a,b>0$ and real $x,y$,

$$
a^xa^y=a^{x+y},\qquad \frac{a^x}{a^y}=a^{x-y},
$$

$$
(a^x)^y=a^{xy},\qquad (ab)^x=a^xb^x.
$$

Positive bases avoid the domain and branch failures of arbitrary real or complex powers.

**Memory cue (added).** Multiply same bases: add exponents. Power of a power: multiply exponents.
