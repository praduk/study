For a positive odd integer $n$,

$$
x^n+y^n=(x+y)\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}(-1)^k x^{n-1-k}y^k.
$$

The last sign is positive because $n-1$ is even.

**Memory cue (added).** Odd sum: plus outside, alternating inside. Obtain it by replacing $y$ with $-y$ in the difference formula.
