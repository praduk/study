For an integer-coefficient polynomial $a_nx^n+\cdots+a_0$ with $a_n\ne0$, suppose $p/q$ is a root with integers $p,q$, $q>0$, and $\gcd(p,q)=1$. Then

$$
p\mid a_0,\qquad q\mid a_n.
$$

These are necessary conditions, not a guarantee that a candidate is a root.

**Memory cue (added).** Top divides the tail; bottom divides the head.
