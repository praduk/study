For real $a<b$:

- $a+c<b+c$ for every real $c$.
- If $c>0$, then $ac<bc$.
- If $c<0$, then $ac>bc$.
- If $0<a<b$, then $0<1/b<1/a$.

Do not multiply by an expression of unknown sign without splitting cases.

**Memory cue (added).** Negative multiplier flips; positive reciprocal reverses the order.
