For real coefficients and $a\ne0$, define $\Delta=b^2-4ac$.

- $\Delta>0$: two distinct real roots.
- $\Delta=0$: one real root of multiplicity two.
- $\Delta<0$: two nonreal complex conjugate roots.

**Memory cue (added).** Positive separates, zero repeats, negative leaves the real line.
