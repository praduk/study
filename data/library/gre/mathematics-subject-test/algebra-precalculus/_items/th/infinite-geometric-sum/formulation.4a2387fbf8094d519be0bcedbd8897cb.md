For a real or complex ratio $r$,

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}r^n=\frac1{1-r}
\quad\text{if and only if }|r|<1.
$$

For $|r|\ge1$, the terms fail to tend to zero.

For first term $a\in\R$ or $\C$ and $|r|<1$,

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}ar^n=\frac{a}{1-r}.
$$

If $a=0$, the scaled series is zero for every $r$.

**Memory cue (added).** Start at power zero: one over one minus the ratio. The ratio must shrink in magnitude.
