For $a,b,c\in\mathbb C$ with $a\ne0$, the roots of $ax^2+bx+c=0$ are

$$
x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}.
$$

Either square root of the discriminant yields the same pair of roots.

**Memory cue (added).** Negative middle, plus-or-minus the root; everything sits over twice the leading coefficient.
