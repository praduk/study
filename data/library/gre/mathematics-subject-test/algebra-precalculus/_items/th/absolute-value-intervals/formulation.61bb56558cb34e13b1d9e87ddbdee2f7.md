For real $x$,

$$
|x|<a\iff -a<x<a\quad(a>0),
$$

$$
|x|\le a\iff -a\le x\le a\quad(a\ge0).
$$

For a negative right-hand side, neither inequality has a solution.

**Memory cue (added).** Absolute value is distance from zero: less than a radius means inside the interval.
