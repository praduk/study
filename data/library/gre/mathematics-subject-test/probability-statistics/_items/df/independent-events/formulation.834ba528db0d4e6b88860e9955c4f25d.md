Events $A,B$ are independent if and only if $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$. Independence is different from being disjoint. If $P(B)>0$, independence is equivalent to $P(A\mid B)=P(A)$.

**Memory cue (added).** Independent means learning one does not change the probability of the other.
