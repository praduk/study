For $\lambda>0$, $X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda)$ has

$$
P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^k}{k!},\qquad k=0,1,2,\ldots.
$$

At $\lambda=0$, the distribution is concentrated at zero.

**Memory cue (added).** Poisson mass: exponential downweight, parameter power, factorial divisor.
