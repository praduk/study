For a nonnegative integer $n$ and $0\le p\le1$, $X\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(n,p)$ counts successes in $n$ independent Bernoulli trials with common success probability $p$:

$$
P(X=k)=\binom nkp^k(1-p)^{n-k},\qquad k=0,\ldots,n.
$$

At $p=0$ or $1$, use the corresponding certain count (equivalently, $0^0=1$ in this formula).

**Memory cue (added).** Choose the success positions, then multiply success and failure probabilities.
