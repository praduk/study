A probability measure $P$ assigns nonnegative numbers to events, has $P(S)=1$ for the sample space $S$, and is countably additive on pairwise disjoint events:

$$
P\left(\bigcup_i A_i\right)=\sum_iP(A_i).
$$

It follows that $0\le P(A)\le1$ for every event.
