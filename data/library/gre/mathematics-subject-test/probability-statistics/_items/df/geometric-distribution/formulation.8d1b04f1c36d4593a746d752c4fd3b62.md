For $0<p\le1$, if $X$ counts independent trials up to and including the first success with common success probability $p$, then

$$
P(X=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p,\qquad k=1,2,\ldots.
$$

For $p=1$, $X=1$ with probability one. This convention counts trials, not failures.

**Memory cue (added).** Fail $k-1$ times, then succeed once.
