If $A_1,\ldots,A_n$ form a partition of the sample space and each $P(A_i)>0$, then

$$
P(B)=\sum_{i=1}^nP(B\mid A_i)P(A_i).
$$

Zero-probability partition pieces can be omitted.

**Memory cue (added).** Split into disjoint cases; weight each conditional probability by its case probability.
