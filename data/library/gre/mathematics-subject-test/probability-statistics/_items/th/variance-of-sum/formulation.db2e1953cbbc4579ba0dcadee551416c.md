For real random variables $X,Y$ with finite second moments,

$$
\operatorname{Var}(X+Y)=\operatorname{Var}(X)+\operatorname{Var}(Y)+2\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y),
$$

where $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=E[(X-E[X])(Y-E[Y])]$. If $X,Y$ are independent, covariance is zero and the variances add.

**Memory cue (added).** Squaring a sum creates the cross term: twice the covariance.
