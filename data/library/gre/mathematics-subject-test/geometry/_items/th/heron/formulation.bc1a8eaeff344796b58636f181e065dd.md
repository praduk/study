For a Euclidean triangle with side lengths $a,b,c>0$ satisfying the strict triangle inequalities, let its semiperimeter be $s=(a+b+c)/2$. Its area is

$$
A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}.
$$

The nonnegative square root gives area.

**Memory cue (added).** Start with the semiperimeter; multiply by “semiperimeter minus each side”; then take the square root.
