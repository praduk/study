A real sequence $(a_n)$ converges to $L\in\R$ exactly when

$$
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\in\N\ \forall n\ge N:
\quad |a_n-L|<\varepsilon.
$$

The cutoff $N$ may depend on $\varepsilon$, but must work for every later index.

**Memory cue (added).** Every tolerance gets one eventual cutoff.
