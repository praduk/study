Suppose $a_n\ne0$ eventually and the extended limit exists:

$$
L=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left|\frac{a_{n+1}}{a_n}\right|.
$$

- $L<1$: $\sum a_n$ converges absolutely.
- $L>1$ (including $L=\infty$): the series diverges.
- $L=1$: the test is inconclusive.

**Memory cue (added).** Next over now; below one shrinks. Factorials often cancel.
