For eventually positive terms $a_n,b_n$, if

$$
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{a_n}{b_n}=c,
\qquad 0<c<\infty,
$$

then $\sum a_n$ and $\sum b_n$ either both converge or both diverge.

**Memory cue (added).** A finite positive ratio means the same tail size.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 11.

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** A positive series resembling a power benchmark suggests comparison or limit comparison.
