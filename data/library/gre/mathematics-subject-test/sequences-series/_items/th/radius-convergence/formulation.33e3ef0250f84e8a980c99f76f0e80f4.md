A power series $\sum c_n(x-a)^n$ has a radius $R\in[0,\infty]$:

- it converges absolutely for $|x-a|<R$;
- it diverges for $|x-a|>R$;
- real endpoints require separate testing.

With reciprocal conventions $1/0=\infty$ and $1/\infty=0$,

$$
R=\frac1{\limsup_{n\to\infty}|c_n|^{1/n}}.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Radius first, endpoints separately.
