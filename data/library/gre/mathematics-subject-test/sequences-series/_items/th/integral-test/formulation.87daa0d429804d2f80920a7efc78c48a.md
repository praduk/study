If $f$ is positive, continuous, and nonincreasing on $[N,\infty)$, with $a_n=f(n)$, then

$$
\sum_{n=N}^{\infty}a_n
\quad\text{and}\quad
\int_N^\infty f(x)\,dx
$$

converge or diverge together. They need not have equal values.

**Memory cue (added).** Positive, continuous, decreasing: compare the sum with area.
