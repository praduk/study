Suppose the extended limit exists:

$$
L=\lim_{n\to\infty}\sqrt[n]{|a_n|}.
$$

- $L<1$: $\sum a_n$ converges absolutely.
- $L>1$ (including $L=\infty$): the series diverges.
- $L=1$: the test is inconclusive.

**Memory cue (added).** An nth root unwraps an nth power.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, pp. 11, 12.

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** An entire term raised to the nth power often simplifies under an nth root.
