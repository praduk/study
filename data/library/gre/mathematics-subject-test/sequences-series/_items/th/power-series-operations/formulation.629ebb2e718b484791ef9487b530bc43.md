Inside $|x-a|<R$, where $R>0$ is the radius of convergence,

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(x-a)^n
=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n c_n(x-a)^{n-1},
$$

and

$$
\int_a^x\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(t-a)^n\,dt
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{c_n}{n+1}(x-a)^{n+1}.
$$

Both new series have the same radius; endpoint behavior can change.

**Memory cue (added).** Inside the radius, calculus works term by term.
