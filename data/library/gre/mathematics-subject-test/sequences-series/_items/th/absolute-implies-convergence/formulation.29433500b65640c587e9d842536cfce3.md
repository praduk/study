For real or complex terms,

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}|a_n|<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n\text{ converges}.
$$

The reverse implication need not hold.

**Memory cue (added).** If magnitudes fit, signed terms fit.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 12.

**Trap (sheet, p. 30).** Absolute convergence implies convergence; the converse fails.
