If $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ converges, then $a_n\to0$.

If $a_n$ fails to tend to zero, the series diverges. The converse is false: terms tending to zero do not guarantee a convergent sum.

**Memory cue (added).** Terms must vanish; that is an entrance test, not an admission ticket.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 11.

**Trap (sheet, p. 30).** $a_n\to0$ is necessary, not sufficient, for convergence of $\sum a_n$.
