For $f,g\in F[x]$ with $F$ a field and $g\ne0$, there are unique $q,r$ with $f=qg+r$ and either $r=0$ or $\deg r<\deg g$. Euclid computes a gcd, conventionally chosen monic; Bézout expresses it as $uf+vg$. Every ideal of $F[x]$ is principal. Dividing by $x-a$ leaves remainder $f(a)$.

**Recognition cue.** Reduce a polynomial pair just as for integer gcds, or factor if a common divisor is visible.

**Boundary check.** The field hypothesis allows division by a leading coefficient.
