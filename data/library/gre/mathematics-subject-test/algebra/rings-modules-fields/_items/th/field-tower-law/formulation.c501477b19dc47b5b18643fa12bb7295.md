For fields $F\subseteq K\subseteq L$ with finite extension degrees,

$$
[L:F]=[L:K][K:F].
$$

Here $[K:F]$ is the vector-space dimension of $K$ over $F$. Thus every intermediate degree divides the full finite degree.

**Recognition cue.** Use divisibility to rule out an intermediate field before finding generators.

**Boundary check.** Degrees multiply along an actual nested tower, not arbitrary pairs of extensions.
