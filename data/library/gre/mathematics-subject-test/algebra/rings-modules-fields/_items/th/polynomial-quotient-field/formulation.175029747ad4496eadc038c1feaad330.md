For irreducible $p\in F[x]$ of degree $d\ge1$, the quotient $F[x]/(p)$ is a field. Every class has a unique representative of degree below $d$, giving basis $1,\bar x,\ldots,\bar x^{d-1}$ over $F$. If $|F|=q<\infty$, it has $q^d$ elements. Reduce powers using $p(\bar x)=0$; inverses can be found by multiplication or polynomial Bézout.

**Recognition cue.** Replace the defining polynomial by a relation on powers immediately.

**Boundary check.** A reducible modulus gives a quotient ring with zero divisors, not a field.
