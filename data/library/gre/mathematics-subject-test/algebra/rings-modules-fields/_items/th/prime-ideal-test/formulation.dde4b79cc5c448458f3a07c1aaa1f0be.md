For a proper ideal $I$ of a commutative ring with identity,

$$
I\text{ is prime}\iff R/I\text{ is an integral domain}.
$$

Equivalently, $ab\in I$ implies $a\in I$ or $b\in I$. Properness excludes $I=R$.

**Recognition cue.** Test whether a quotient has zero divisors.

**Boundary check.** Prime need not mean maximal; $\Z$ is a domain but not a field.
