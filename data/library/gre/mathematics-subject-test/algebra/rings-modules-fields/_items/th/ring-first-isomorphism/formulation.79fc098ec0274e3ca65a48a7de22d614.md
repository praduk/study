For a ring homomorphism $\phi:R\to S$, its kernel is an ideal, its image is a subring, and

$$
R/\ker\phi\isoto\im\phi.
$$

With the unital convention used here, maps preserve identity. Evaluation homomorphisms often identify quotient rings directly.

**Recognition cue.** Find the image and kernel of a simple map instead of constructing cosets individually.

**Boundary check.** The quotient matches the image, which may be smaller than the codomain.
