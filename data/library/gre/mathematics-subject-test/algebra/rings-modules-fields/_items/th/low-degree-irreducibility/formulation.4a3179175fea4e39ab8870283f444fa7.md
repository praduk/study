A polynomial of degree two or three over a field is irreducible iff it has no root in that field. Any reducible polynomial of those degrees has a linear factor. In higher degree, a polynomial can factor without linear factors, so absence of roots is insufficient. Irreducibility always depends on the base field.

**Recognition cue.** Test all elements of a small field for a quadratic or cubic.

**Boundary check.** Do not promote the no-root criterion to degree four.
