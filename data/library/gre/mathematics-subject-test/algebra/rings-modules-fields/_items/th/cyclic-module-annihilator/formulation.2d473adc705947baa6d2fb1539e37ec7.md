For $m$ in a module over a commutative ring $R$,

$$
\operatorname{Ann}_R(m)=\{r\in R:rm=0\},\qquad Rm\isoto R/\operatorname{Ann}_R(m).
$$

The annihilator is an ideal. For $[a]\in\Z/n\Z$ with $n\ge1$, its cyclic submodule has size $n/\gcd(n,a)$ and its annihilator in $\Z$ is $(n/\gcd(n,a))\Z$.

**Recognition cue.** One gcd gives both the additive order and the annihilator generator.

**Boundary check.** The annihilator here is an ideal in the scalar ring $\Z$, not a set of classes modulo $n$.
