If $R$ is a principal ideal domain and $M$ is a finitely generated $R$-module, then

$$
M\isoto R^r\oplus R/(d_1)\oplus\cdots\oplus R/(d_k),
$$

where the $d_i$ may be chosen nonzero nonunits with $d_1\mid d_2\mid\cdots\mid d_k$. The free rank is $r$; the other summands are torsion. For finite abelian groups this specializes to the $R=\Z$ case.

**Recognition cue.** Separate free and torsion parts; use coprime cyclic factors to recognize a single cyclic group.

**Boundary check.** Finitely generated is not the same as finite, and arbitrary rings need not have this decomposition.
