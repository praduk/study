For a proper ideal $I$ of a commutative ring with identity,

$$
I\text{ is maximal}\iff R/I\text{ is a field}.
$$

Every maximal ideal is prime. In $F[x]$ for a field $F$, a nonzero proper principal ideal $(p)$ is maximal iff $p$ is irreducible.

**Recognition cue.** Identify the quotient using evaluation or irreducibility.

**Boundary check.** A nonzero constant polynomial generates the whole ring, not a maximal ideal.
