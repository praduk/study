If $\alpha$ is algebraic over a field $F$, its minimal polynomial $m_\alpha$ is the monic polynomial of least positive degree in $F[x]$ vanishing at $\alpha$. It is irreducible, and

$$
[F(\alpha):F]=\deg m_\alpha.
$$

A polynomial merely known to vanish at $\alpha$ gives an upper bound, not necessarily the exact degree.

**Recognition cue.** Prove irreducibility before using a proposed defining polynomial as the minimal polynomial.

**Boundary check.** Changing the base field can change the minimal polynomial.
