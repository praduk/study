For integer $n\ge2$, $[a]\in\Z/n\Z$ is a unit iff $\gcd(a,n)=1$. Bézout gives an inverse: if $ax+ny=1$, then $[x]=[a]^{-1}$. The unit count is $\varphi(n)$.

**Recognition cue.** Use a gcd before attempting to divide modulo $n$.

**Boundary check.** A nonzero residue need not be a unit.
