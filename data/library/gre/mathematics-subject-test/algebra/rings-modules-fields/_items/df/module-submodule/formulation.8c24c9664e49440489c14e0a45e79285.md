A unital $R$-module is an abelian group $(M,+)$ with scalar multiplication satisfying distributivity in both inputs, $(rs)m=r(sm)$, and $1m=m$. A submodule is an additive subgroup closed under all $R$-scalars. Modules over a field are vector spaces; modules over general rings may lack bases. Quotients by submodules are modules, and kernel/image isomorphism theorems apply.

**Recognition cue.** For $R=\Z$, modules are precisely abelian groups with integer multiplication.

**Boundary check.** Vector-space basis conclusions need a field; torsion prevents a free $\Z$-basis.
