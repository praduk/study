Here rings are commutative with identity; domains and fields require $1\ne0$. An integral domain has no nonzero zero divisors. A field is a nonzero ring in which every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse. Every field is a domain, and every finite domain is a field. An infinite domain need not be a field.

**Recognition cue.** To distinguish the structures, look for a nonzero product equal to zero or a noninvertible nonzero element.

**Boundary check.** A finite ring is not automatically a field.
