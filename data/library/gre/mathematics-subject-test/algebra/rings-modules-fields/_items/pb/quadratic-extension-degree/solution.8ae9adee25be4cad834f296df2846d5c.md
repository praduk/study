**Pattern cue.** Find a vanishing irreducible polynomial.

**Fast route.** $\sqrt5$ satisfies $x^2-5$. It is irrational: a reduced rational square equal to five would force numerator and denominator both divisible by five. Thus the quadratic has no rational root and is irreducible, so the degree is two.

**Trap check.** A polynomial that vanishes must still be shown minimal.
