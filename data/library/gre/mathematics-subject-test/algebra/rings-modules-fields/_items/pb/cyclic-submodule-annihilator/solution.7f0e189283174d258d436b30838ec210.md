**Fastest valid route.** The additive order of $[8]$ modulo $12$ is

$$\frac{12}{\gcd(12,8)}=3,$$

so $N=\{[0],[8],[4]\}$ has three elements. Also,

$$n[8]=[0]\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 12\mid8n
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad 3\mid n.$$

Thus $\operatorname{Ann}_{\mathbb Z}([8])=3\mathbb Z$. The answer is **(A)**.
