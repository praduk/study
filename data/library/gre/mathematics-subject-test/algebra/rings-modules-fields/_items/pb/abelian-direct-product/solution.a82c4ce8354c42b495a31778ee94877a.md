**Answer: (C).** $\Z/36\Z$

**Pattern cue.** Coprime cyclic orders combine into one cyclic group.

**Fast route.** The pair $([1],[1])$ has order $\lcm(4,9)=36$, equal to the group size. Hence the product is cyclic of order thirty-six. The other order-thirty-six choices have maximum element orders six and eighteen.

**Trap check.** Equal cardinality alone does not imply group isomorphism.
