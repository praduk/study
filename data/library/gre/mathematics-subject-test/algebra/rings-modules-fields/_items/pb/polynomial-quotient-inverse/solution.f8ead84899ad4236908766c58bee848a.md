**Fastest valid route.** In the quotient, $x^2=-1=2$ in $\mathbb F_3$. Test the closely related factor $x+2$:

$$(x+1)(x+2)=x^2+3x+2=x^2+2=2+2=1.$$

Therefore $\overline{x+2}$ is the inverse, so the answer is **(C)**.

For existence, $x^2+1$ has no root in $\mathbb F_3$ and is therefore irreducible; the quotient is indeed a field with $9$ elements.
