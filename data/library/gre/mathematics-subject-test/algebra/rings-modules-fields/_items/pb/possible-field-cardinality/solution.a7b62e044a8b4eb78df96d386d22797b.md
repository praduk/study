**Answer: (D).** $16$

**Pattern cue.** Finite fields have prime-power size.

**Fast route.** Only $16=2^4$ is a prime power. Existence here can be witnessed by $\F_2[x]/(x^4+x+1)$: it has no roots in $\F_2$ and is not divisible by the only monic irreducible quadratic $x^2+x+1$ (remainder one), so the quartic is irreducible.

**Trap check.** Prime-power size is necessary for fields, but a specific ring of that size may not be a field.
