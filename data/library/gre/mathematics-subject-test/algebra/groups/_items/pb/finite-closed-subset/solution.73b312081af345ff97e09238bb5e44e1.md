**Answer: (B).** $H$ is a subgroup.

**Pattern cue.** Finiteness forces inverses.

**Fast route.** For $h\in H$, positive powers eventually repeat; cancellation in $G$ gives a positive power equal to $e$, and then a power equal to $h^{-1}$ lies in $H$. Closure plus identity and inverses makes $H$ a subgroup.
