A group is a nonempty set $G$ with a binary operation $G\times G\to G$ that is associative, has a two-sided identity $e$, and gives each element a two-sided inverse. Commutativity is an additional property, not a group axiom. Identity and inverses depend on the operation.

**Recognition cue.** For an unfamiliar operation, solve for its identity and inverse before judging it.

**Boundary check.** The symbol zero need not be the identity.
