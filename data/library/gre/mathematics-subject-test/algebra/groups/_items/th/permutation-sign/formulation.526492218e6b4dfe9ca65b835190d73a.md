A cycle of length $k$ has sign $(-1)^{k-1}$. Signs multiply under composition. Thus a product of disjoint cycles has sign given by the product of their signs. The symmetric group has $|S_n|=n!$; for $n\ge2$, its even-permutation subgroup has $|A_n|=n!/2$.

**Recognition cue.** Count parity using cycle lengths, not by expanding every transposition.

**Boundary check.** An even-length cycle is an odd permutation.
