For a subgroup $H$ of finite $G$, the left cosets partition $G$ into sets of size $|H|$, so

$$
|G|=[G:H]|H|.
$$

Element orders and subgroup orders therefore divide $|G|$. A divisor condition alone need not guarantee an element or subgroup of that order.

**Recognition cue.** Eliminate impossible orders or divide to count cosets.

**Boundary check.** A statement that an order could occur needs an example, not only divisibility.
