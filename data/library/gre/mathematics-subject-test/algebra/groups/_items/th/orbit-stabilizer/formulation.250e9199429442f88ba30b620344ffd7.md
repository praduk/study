For an action of a finite group $G$ on a set $X$, define $Gx=\{gx:g\in G\}$ and $G_x=\{g:gx=x\}$. Then

$$
|Gx|=[G:G_x]=\frac{|G|}{|G_x|}.
$$

The stabilizer is a subgroup; different orbits partition $X$.

**Recognition cue.** An object with many symmetries has a smaller orbit.

**Boundary check.** The orbit need not be all of $X$.
