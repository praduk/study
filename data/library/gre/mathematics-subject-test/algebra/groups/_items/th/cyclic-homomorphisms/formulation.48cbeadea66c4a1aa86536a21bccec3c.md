A homomorphism from $\Z/m\Z$ into a group is determined by the image $h$ of $[1]$, and is well-defined iff $h^m=e$ (or $mh=0$ additively). For positive $m,n$, there are exactly $\gcd(m,n)$ homomorphisms $\Z/m\Z\to\Z/n\Z$.
