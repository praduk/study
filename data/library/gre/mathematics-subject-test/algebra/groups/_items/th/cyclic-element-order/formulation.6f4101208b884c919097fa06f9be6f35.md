If $g$ has finite order $n$, then

$$
|g^k|=\frac{n}{\gcd(n,k)}.
$$

In the additive group $\Z/n\Z$, the order of $[k]$ is the same ratio. In particular, $g^k$ generates $\genby{g}$ iff $\gcd(n,k)=1$.

**Recognition cue.** Turn a power-order question into one gcd.

**Boundary check.** Additive order of a residue is not its multiplicative order.
