For a finite group $G$ acting on a finite set $X$, the number of orbits is

$$
\frac1{|G|}\sum_{g\in G}|\operatorname{Fix}(g)|,
$$

where $\operatorname{Fix}(g)=\{x\in X:gx=x\}$. Count pairs $(g,x)$ fixed under the action to derive the formula.

**Recognition cue.** Count configurations unchanged by each symmetry, then average.

**Boundary check.** Do not divide the total configuration count by the group order unless the action is free.
