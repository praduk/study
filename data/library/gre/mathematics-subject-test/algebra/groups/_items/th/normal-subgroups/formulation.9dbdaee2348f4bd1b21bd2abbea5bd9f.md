A subgroup $N\le G$ is normal iff $gNg^{-1}=N$ for every $g\in G$, equivalently $gN=Ng$ for every $g$. The coset product defines a quotient group $G/N$ exactly when $N$ is normal. Kernels are normal; subgroups of abelian groups and subgroups of index two are normal.

**Recognition cue.** Check conjugation, abelianness, or index two before forming a quotient.

**Boundary check.** Being a subgroup alone does not make coset multiplication well-defined.
