A cyclic group of order $n$ has exactly one subgroup of order $d$ for every positive divisor $d\mid n$; it is generated by $g^{n/d}$. It has exactly $\varphi(d)$ elements of order $d$. Thus it has $\tau(n)$ subgroups and $\varphi(n)$ generators, where $\tau$ counts positive divisors and $\varphi$ counts coprime residue classes.

**Recognition cue.** Subgroup counts use divisors; generator or element-order counts use totients.

**Boundary check.** Uniqueness per divisor is a cyclic-group fact.
