Disjoint cycles commute. A permutation written as disjoint cycles has order equal to the least common multiple of the cycle lengths; omitted fixed points have length one. In a product of permutations, composition order must be stated; here products act rightmost first.

**Recognition cue.** Trace each point through a product before reading its order.

**Boundary check.** Overlapping cycles cannot be treated as disjoint.
