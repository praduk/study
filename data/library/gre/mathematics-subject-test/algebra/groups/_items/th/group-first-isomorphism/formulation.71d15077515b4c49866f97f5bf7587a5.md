For a group homomorphism $\phi:G\to H$,

$$
G/\ker\phi\isoto\im\phi.
$$

For finite $G$, $|G|=|\ker\phi|\,|\im\phi|$. The map is injective iff its kernel is trivial. Surjectivity is needed to replace image by the entire codomain.

**Recognition cue.** Compute whichever of kernel and image is easier, then divide.

**Boundary check.** The size of the codomain can exceed the size of the image.
