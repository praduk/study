A nonempty subset $H$ of a group $G$ is a subgroup iff $ab^{-1}\in H$ for all $a,b\in H$. A finite nonempty subset closed under multiplication is automatically a subgroup: positive powers of any element repeat, forcing the identity and an inverse into the set.

**Recognition cue.** Use one-step closure, or exploit finiteness when inverses are not explicitly given.

**Boundary check.** The finite shortcut fails for infinite subsets such as positive integers inside the additive group $\Z$.
