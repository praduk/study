For $n\ge2$ and $\gcd(a,n)=1$,

$$
a^{\varphi(n)}\equiv1\pmod n.
$$

The order of $a$ in the unit group divides $\varphi(n)$; it may be smaller. Euler's theorem is a finite-group statement about units.

**Recognition cue.** For a composite modulus, check coprimality before reducing the exponent.

**Boundary check.** It can give a false result for a nonunit.
