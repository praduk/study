For $n\ge2$, $a$ has a multiplicative inverse modulo $n$ iff $\gcd(a,n)=1$. If $ax+ny=1$, then the inverse is $x\pmod n$. It is unique as a residue class. Multiplication by a unit permutes the residue classes.

**Recognition cue.** Try a short product or obtain the inverse by Euclidean back-substitution.

**Boundary check.** A negative Bézout coefficient is a valid inverse representative.
