For integers $a,b$ not both zero, let $d=\gcd(a,b)$. Then $ax+by=c$ has integer solutions iff $d\mid c$. Given one solution $(x_0,y_0)$, all are

$$
x=x_0+(b/d)t,\qquad y=y_0-(a/d)t,\qquad t\in\Z.
$$

Additional positivity or range conditions restrict $t$.

**Recognition cue.** Test solvability before searching; then count allowed parameter values.

**Boundary check.** Positive and nonnegative requirements differ at endpoints.
