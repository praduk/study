For pairwise coprime positive moduli, specified residue classes determine one class modulo the product. More generally, $x\equiv a\pmod m$ and $x\equiv b\pmod n$ are compatible iff $\gcd(m,n)\mid(a-b)$; if compatible, the solution is unique modulo $\lcm(m,n)$. Several congruences are compatible iff their residues agree modulo every pairwise gcd.

**Recognition cue.** First test compatibility; then exploit a common residue or substitute $x=a+mt$.

**Boundary check.** For shared factors the period is the lcm, not the product.
