An equation manipulation is valid only on its stated domain. Squaring both sides gives a necessary consequence but can add solutions. Dividing by an expression may discard its zero cases. Canceling a rational factor leaves the original holes excluded. Always check candidates in the original equation.

**Recognition cue.** Before a square, division, or cancellation, record the condition that makes the step reversible.

**Boundary check.** An algebraically correct transformed equation can describe a larger or smaller solution set.
