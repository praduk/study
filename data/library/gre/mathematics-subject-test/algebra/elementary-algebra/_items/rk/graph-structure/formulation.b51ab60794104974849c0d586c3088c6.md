On a domain invariant under $x\mapsto-x$, $f$ is even iff $f(-x)=f(x)$ and odd iff $f(-x)=-f(x)$. An injective even function cannot have a domain containing both $a$ and $-a$ for $a\ne0$. Before solving, check domain, intercepts, asymptotes, periodicity, and monotonicity; these are cues, not universal shortcuts.

**Recognition cue.** Symmetry can eliminate inverse claims or predict paired solutions.

**Boundary check.** Parity requires a symmetric domain; cancellation can hide a hole rather than remove it.
