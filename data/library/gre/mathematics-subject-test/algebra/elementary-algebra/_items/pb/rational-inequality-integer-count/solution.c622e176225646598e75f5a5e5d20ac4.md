**Fastest valid route.** Put the inequality over one denominator instead of cross-multiplying by an expression of unknown sign:

$$\frac{x-1}{x+2}-\frac12=\frac{x-4}{2(x+2)}<0.$$

The critical points are the excluded value $x=-2$ and the zero $x=4$. The quotient is negative exactly on $(-2,4)$. Its integer points are

$$-1,0,1,2,3,$$

so there are $5$. The answer is **(C)**.
