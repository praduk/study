For nonnegative real $x_1,\ldots,x_n$,

$$
\frac{x_1+\cdots+x_n}{n}\ge (x_1\cdots x_n)^{1/n},
$$

with equality iff all $x_i$ are equal. In particular, for $x>0$ and $c>0$, $x+c/x\ge2\sqrt c$, with equality at $x=\sqrt c$.

**Recognition cue.** A fixed product suggests a lower bound on a sum; a fixed sum suggests an upper bound on a product.

**Boundary check.** A bound is an actual optimum only if the equality case is allowed.
