For real $x,c$ and $a\ge0$,

$$
|x-c|\le a\iff c-a\le x\le c+a.
$$

For $a>0$, $|x-c|\ge a$ describes the two outer rays. Sums of distances can be analyzed by splitting at their centers; $|x-a|+|x-b|\ge |a-b|$, with equality exactly between $a$ and $b$.

**Recognition cue.** Use interval geometry before introducing cases.

**Boundary check.** For strict inequalities, remove boundary points; for a negative upper bound there is no solution.
