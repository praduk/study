For integer $n\ge0$ and $r\ne1$,

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{n}r^k=\frac{1-r^{n+1}}{1-r}.
$$

At $r=1$, the sum is $n+1$. A shifted sum factors out its first power. This finite identity requires no assumption $|r|<1$.

**Recognition cue.** Constant ratio or a factored difference of powers suggests a finite geometric sum.

**Boundary check.** There are $n+1$ terms from exponent $0$ through $n$.
