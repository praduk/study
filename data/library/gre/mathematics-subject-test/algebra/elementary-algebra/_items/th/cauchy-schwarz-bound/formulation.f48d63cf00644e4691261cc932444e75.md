For real $a_i,b_i$,

$$
(\sum_i a_ib_i)^2\le(\sum_i a_i^2)(\sum_i b_i^2).
$$

Equality holds iff the two vectors are linearly dependent. Thus a linear expression is bounded on a sphere by the product of vector lengths.

**Recognition cue.** A linear quantity with a sum-of-squares constraint suggests a dot product.

**Boundary check.** Choose the equality direction with the requested sign for a maximum or minimum.
