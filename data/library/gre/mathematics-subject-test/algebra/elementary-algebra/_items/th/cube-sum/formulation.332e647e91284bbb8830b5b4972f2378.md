For integer $n\ge0$,

$$
\sum_{k=1}^n k^3=\left(\frac{n(n+1)}2\right)^2.
$$

This is the square of the sum of the first $n$ integers, not the sum of their squares.

**Recognition cue.** A cubic power sum may collapse to one squared triangular number.

**Boundary check.** The formulas for squares and cubes are different.
