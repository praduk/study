For $a>0$, $a\ne1$, and $x,y>0$,

$$
\log_a(xy)=\log_a x+\log_a y,\quad \log_a(x/y)=\log_a x-\log_a y,
$$

$$
\log_a(x^r)=r\log_a x,\quad \log_a x=\frac{\ln x}{\ln a}.
$$

Here $r$ is real and $x>0$. The logarithm is the inverse of the corresponding exponential.

**Recognition cue.** Combine logarithms after recording positivity of every original argument.

**Boundary check.** A positive product does not guarantee each original logarithm is defined.
