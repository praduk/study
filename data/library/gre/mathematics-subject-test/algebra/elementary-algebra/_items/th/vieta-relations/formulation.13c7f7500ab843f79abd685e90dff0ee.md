For $a_nx^n+\cdots+a_0$ with $a_n\ne0$ and complex roots $r_i$ counted with multiplicity,

$$
\sum_i r_i=-a_{n-1}/a_n,\qquad \prod_i r_i=(-1)^n a_0/a_n.
$$

For $ax^2+bx+c$, $r+s=-b/a$ and $rs=c/a$. For $x^3-s_1x^2+s_2x-s_3$, the pairwise product sum is $s_2$. Useful reductions are $r^2+s^2=(r+s)^2-2rs$ and, for nonzero roots, $1/r+1/s=(r+s)/(rs)$.

**Recognition cue.** A symmetric root expression can often be read from coefficients without solving.

**Boundary check.** Reciprocals require nonzero roots; retain alternating signs and multiplicities.
