For integer $n\ge0$,

$$
(x+y)^n=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk x^{n-k}y^k,\qquad \binom nk=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}=\binom n{n-k}.
$$

Match the desired exponent before evaluating the coefficient. For a product of two expansions, sum over the exponent pairs that reach the target.

**Recognition cue.** One term of a large power can be extracted without expanding the entire polynomial.

**Boundary check.** Include both the coefficient and the power of every scalar multiplier.
