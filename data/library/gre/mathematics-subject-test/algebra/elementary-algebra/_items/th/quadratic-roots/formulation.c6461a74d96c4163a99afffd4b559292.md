For $ax^2+bx+c=0$ with $a\ne0$,

$$
x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}.
$$

With real coefficients, $\Delta=b^2-4ac$ gives two distinct real roots if $\Delta>0$, one repeated real root if $\Delta=0$, and no real roots if $\Delta<0$.

**Recognition cue.** A parameter question about number of roots may need only the discriminant.

**Boundary check.** Handle $a=0$ separately; do not divide by a parameter before checking whether it vanishes.
