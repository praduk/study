For $n\ge1$, the sum of an arithmetic progression with first term $a$ and common difference $d$ is

$$
S_n=\frac n2\bigl(2a+(n-1)d\bigr).
$$

In particular, $\sum_{k=1}^n k=n(n+1)/2$ and $\sum_{k=1}^n(2k-1)=n^2$.

**Recognition cue.** Count terms first, then average the first and last.

**Boundary check.** The last term is $a+(n-1)d$, not $a+nd$.
