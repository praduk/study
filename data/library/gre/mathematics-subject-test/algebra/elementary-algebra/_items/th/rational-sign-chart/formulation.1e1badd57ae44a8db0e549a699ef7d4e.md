To solve $P(x)/Q(x)>0$ (or another comparison), first move terms to one side. Split the real line at every real zero of $P$ and $Q$, exclude all original denominator zeros, and test the sign in each interval. A factor changes sign across a root exactly when its multiplicity is odd.

**Recognition cue.** A quotient inequality with a variable denominator calls for a sign chart.

**Boundary check.** Canceling a common factor does not restore the original excluded point.
