For real or complex $a,b$,

$$
(a\pm b)^2=a^2\pm2ab+b^2,\quad a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b),
$$

$$
a^3-b^3=(a-b)(a^2+ab+b^2),\quad a^3+b^3=(a+b)(a^2-ab+b^2).
$$

For positive integer $n$, $x-y$ divides $x^n-y^n$; if $n$ is odd, $x+y$ divides $x^n+y^n$.

**Recognition cue.** Opposite signs or repeated powers suggest a product before expansion.

**Boundary check.** The middle sign changes between the sum-of-cubes and difference-of-cubes factors.
