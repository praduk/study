For $a,b>0$ and real $x,y$,

$$
a^xa^y=a^{x+y},\quad a^x/a^y=a^{x-y},\quad (a^x)^y=a^{xy},\quad (ab)^x=a^xb^x.
$$

For $a>0$, $a\ne1$, the map $x\mapsto a^x$ is one-to-one; it is increasing for $a>1$ and decreasing for $0<a<1$.

**Recognition cue.** Rewrite both sides using one positive base.

**Boundary check.** Unrestricted real exponent identities do not extend to negative bases; a base below one reverses order.
