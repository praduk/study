For real numbers (or Euclidean vectors),

$$
|x+y|\le |x|+|y|,\qquad \bigl||x|-|y|\bigr|\le |x-y|.
$$

The vector version replaces absolute values by norms. In $\R$, the minimum of $|x-a|+|x-b|$ is $|a-b|$.

**Recognition cue.** Known lengths often bound an unknown sum or difference immediately.

**Boundary check.** An upper bound alone need not be attained; check geometry or signs.
