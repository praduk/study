For integer $n\ge0$,

$$
\sum_{k=1}^n k^2=\frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}6.
$$

A shifted range is the difference of two initial sums. Expand $(k+c)^2$ to combine this formula with arithmetic sums.

**Recognition cue.** A quadratic summand suggests separating square, linear, and constant sums.

**Boundary check.** Use the lower endpoint minus one when subtracting initial sums.
