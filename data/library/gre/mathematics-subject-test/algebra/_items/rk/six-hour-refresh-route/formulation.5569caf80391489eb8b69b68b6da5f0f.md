## 4. Reversible equation steps and extraneous roots — 4 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:elementary-algebra:rk:equation-domain-checks

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:elementary-algebra:pb:radical-equation
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:elementary-algebra:pb:division-lost-root

**Speed cue:** Write a tiny restriction list before squaring, dividing, or canceling. Factoring to a zero product often keeps cases visible.

**Check:** Squaring can add roots; dividing can lose roots. Substitute candidates into the original equation.

Cumulative subject time: **20 min**.

## 5. Rank–nullity and injectivity — 6 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:th:rank-nullity

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:rank-nullity-polynomial-space
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:composition-rank-bound

**Speed cue:** Use domain dimension minus rank. Test whether prescribed outputs can be produced by a simple input instead of constructing a full kernel basis.

**Check:** $P_n$, the polynomials of degree at most $n$, has dimension $n+1$. A maximum-rank bound must be attainable before it gives the minimum nullity.

Cumulative subject time: **26 min**.

## 7. Eigenvalues, trace, determinant, and similarity — 4 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:th:eigenvalue-invariants

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:trace-determinant-from-eigenvalues
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:missing-eigenvalue

**Speed cue:** Trace is the sum and determinant the product of eigenvalues, counted with multiplicity. Read triangular or block-triangular structure first.

**Check:** These identities do not require diagonalizability. Row reduction does not preserve eigenvalues.

Cumulative subject time: **34 min**.

## 8. Diagonalizability and eigenspace dimensions — 6 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:th:diagonalizability

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:jordan-block-diagonalizability
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:repeated-diagonal-eigenvalue

**Speed cue:** Distinct eigenvalues in the working field suffice. With repeated roots, count eigenspace dimensions; if the sole eigenvalue is $\lambda$, diagonalizability forces $A=\lambda I$.

**Check:** Repeated eigenvalues do not automatically fail; the working field must contain all eigenvalues.

Cumulative subject time: **40 min**.

## 9. Orthogonal projection onto a line or subspace — 5 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:th:orthogonal-projections

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:orthogonal-projection-diagonal-line
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:linear-algebra:pb:distance-to-plane

**Speed cue:** Use a dot-product projection rather than solving all coordinates. For a plane, use its normal and the signed residual in its defining equation.

**Check:** A sum of separate projection terms needs an orthonormal basis, or denominators for orthogonal nonunit vectors.

## 11. Roots and irreducibility in degrees two and three — 4 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:rings-modules-fields:th:low-degree-irreducibility

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:rings-modules-fields:pb:irreducible-quadratic-over-f2
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:rings-modules-fields:pb:quartic-no-real-roots

**Speed cue:** For degree two or three over a field, test for roots. Over a tiny finite field, evaluating every residue is usually short.

**Check:** No roots does not imply irreducible in degree four or higher; the base field matters.

## 12. Modular inverses and Bézout — 6 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:th:modular-inverse

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:pb:inverse-seventeen-mod-forty-three
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:pb:inverse-product

**Speed cue:** First check $\gcd(a,n)=1$, where $a$ is the residue and $n$ the modulus. Try a short product or use Euclidean back-substitution; verify the final inverse by multiplication modulo n.

**Check:** A negative inverse representative is valid. If the gcd is greater than one, no multiplicative inverse exists.

Cumulative subject time: **60 min**.

## 13. Short cycles in modular powers — 4 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:th:short-power-cycle

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:pb:short-modular-power-cycle
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:pb:minus-one-power

**Speed cue:** Reduce the base first; check whether a small power is 1 or -1 modulo n. Any verified positive period returning to 1 can reduce the exponent.

**Check:** For a nonunit, repetition may start after exponent zero; do not blindly use Fermat or Euler.

Cumulative subject time: **64 min**.

## 14. Chinese remainders and compatibility — 6 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:th:chinese-remainders

Practice immediately after this concept:

- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:pb:chinese-remainder-minus-one
- @gre:mathematics-subject-test:algebra:number-theory:pb:crt-five-seven

**Speed cue:** Look for a common small residue such as -1 first. Otherwise express the first congruence as an integer-parameter family and substitute into the next.

**Check:** With shared factors, test compatibility modulo the gcd of the moduli; the solution period is their least common multiple, not generally their product.

Cumulative subject time: **70 min**.
