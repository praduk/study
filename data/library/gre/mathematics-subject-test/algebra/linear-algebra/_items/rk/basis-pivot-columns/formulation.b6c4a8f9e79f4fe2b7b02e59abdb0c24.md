A basis is an ordered or unordered collection of vectors that is both linearly independent and spanning. All bases of a finite-dimensional vector space have the same cardinality. After row reduction, pivot columns of the **original matrix** form a basis of its column space. Nonzero rows of echelon form form a basis of the row space.

**Recognition cue.** Locate pivots with reduction, then return to original columns.

**Boundary check.** Row operations generally change the column space itself.
