For compatible matrices, $(AB)^T=B^TA^T$. For invertible square matrices, $(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}$. Matrix multiplication generally does not commute. Solving $AXB=C$ with invertible $A,B$ gives $X=A^{-1}CB^{-1}$.

**Recognition cue.** Undo compositions in reverse order.

**Boundary check.** Left and right multiplication cannot be interchanged.
