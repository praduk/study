In a real inner-product space, for $u\ne0$,

$$
\operatorname{proj}_{\spanvs\{u\}}v=\frac{\iprod{v}{u}}{\iprod{u}{u}}u.
$$

For an orthonormal basis $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ of $W$,

$$
\operatorname{proj}_Wv=\sum_j\iprod{v}{u_j}u_j.
$$

The residual is orthogonal to $W$ and its norm is the distance to $W$.

**Recognition cue.** Choose the line's spanning vector or the plane's normal according to which is given.

**Boundary check.** The sum formula needs orthonormal vectors; a merely independent basis is insufficient.
