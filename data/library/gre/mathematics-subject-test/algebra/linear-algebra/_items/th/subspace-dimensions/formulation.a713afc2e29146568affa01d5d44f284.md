For finite-dimensional subspaces $U,W$,

$$
\dim(U+W)=\dim U+\dim W-\dim(U\cap W).
$$

In an $n$-dimensional ambient space, $\dim(U\cap W)\ge\dim U+\dim W-n$. The sum is direct iff the intersection is $\{0\}$.

**Recognition cue.** Subtract the overlap when combining subspaces.

**Boundary check.** Intersection dimension is not usually the smaller of the two dimensions.
