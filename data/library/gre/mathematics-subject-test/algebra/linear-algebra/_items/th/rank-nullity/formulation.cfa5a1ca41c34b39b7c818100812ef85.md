For a linear map $T:V\to W$ with finite-dimensional domain,

$$
\dim V=\dim\ker T+\dim\im T.
$$

$T$ is injective iff $\ker T=\{0\}$ and surjective iff $\im T=W$. For an $m\times n$ matrix, rank plus nullity equals $n$, the number of columns.

**Recognition cue.** Count independent output constraints instead of constructing a full nullspace basis.

**Boundary check.** Use domain dimension; polynomial space $P_n$ has dimension $n+1$.
