For $n\times n$ matrices, $\det(AB)=\det A\det B$ and $\det(cA)=c^n\det A$. Swapping two rows changes sign; scaling one row by $c$ multiplies the determinant by $c$; adding a multiple of another row leaves it unchanged. Triangular determinants are diagonal products.

**Recognition cue.** Use transformations of determinants before expanding matrices.

**Boundary check.** Scaling the whole matrix scales all $n$ rows.
