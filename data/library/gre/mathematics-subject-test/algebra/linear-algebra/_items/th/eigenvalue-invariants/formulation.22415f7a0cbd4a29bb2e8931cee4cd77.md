For an $n\times n$ matrix, use $\chi_A(t)=\det(tI-A)$. Its roots in a splitting field are eigenvalues counted with algebraic multiplicity. Then

$$
\tr A=\sum_i\lambda_i,\qquad\det A=\prod_i\lambda_i.
$$

Triangular or block-triangular matrices expose diagonal blocks in $\chi_A$. Similar matrices have the same characteristic polynomial, trace, and determinant.

**Recognition cue.** Read triangular structure or use sum/product constraints before determinant expansion.

**Boundary check.** Row-equivalent matrices need not have the same eigenvalues.
