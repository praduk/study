For ordered domain basis $\mathcal B=(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ and codomain basis $\mathcal C$, column $j$ of $[T]_{\mathcal C\leftarrow\mathcal B}$ is $[T(b_j)]_{\mathcal C}$. Hence $[T(v)]_{\mathcal C}=[T]_{\mathcal C\leftarrow\mathcal B}[v]_{\mathcal B}$.

**Recognition cue.** Evaluate the map only on basis vectors.

**Boundary check.** Coordinates of basis images go in columns, not rows.
