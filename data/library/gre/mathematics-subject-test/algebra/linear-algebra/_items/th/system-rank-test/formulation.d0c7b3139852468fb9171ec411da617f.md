For $Ax=b$ over $\R$ or $\C$, a solution exists iff $\rank A=\rank[A\mid b]$. A consistent system with $n$ unknowns has $n-\rank A$ free parameters. It has a unique solution iff the rank is $n$; otherwise it has infinitely many. Over a finite field, positive nullity gives finitely many solutions instead.

**Recognition cue.** Compare ranks before solving every variable.

**Boundary check.** Full column rank gives at most one solution; consistency is still required.
