For an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ over a field, the following are equivalent: $A$ is invertible; $\det A\ne0$; $\rank A=n$; $\ker A=\{0\}$; its columns form a basis; and $Ax=b$ has exactly one solution for every $b$. Equivalently, zero is not an eigenvalue.

**Recognition cue.** Use the condition already given; do not calculate an inverse just to establish its existence.

**Boundary check.** Rectangular matrices need separate injectivity and surjectivity tests.
