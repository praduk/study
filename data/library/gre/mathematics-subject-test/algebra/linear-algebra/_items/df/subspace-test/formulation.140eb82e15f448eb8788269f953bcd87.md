A nonempty subset $W$ of a vector space $V$ over a field $F$ is a subspace iff $au+bv\in W$ for all $u,v\in W$ and $a,b\in F$. Equivalently, check zero, addition, and scalar multiplication. Solution sets of homogeneous linear equations are subspaces.

**Recognition cue.** A condition equal to a nonzero constant often describes an affine set instead.

**Boundary check.** Containing zero alone is insufficient; a union of subspaces need not be a subspace.
