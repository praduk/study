**Answer: (C).** $R$ is diagonalizable over $\C$ but not $\R$.

**Pattern cue.** Field dependence of eigenvalues.

**Fast route.** $\chi_R(t)=t^2+1$ has distinct roots $i,-i$ over $\C$, giving diagonalizability there, but no real eigenvalues. Also $R^TR=I$ and $\det R=1$.

**Trap check.** Orthogonal does not mean all eigenvalues are real.
