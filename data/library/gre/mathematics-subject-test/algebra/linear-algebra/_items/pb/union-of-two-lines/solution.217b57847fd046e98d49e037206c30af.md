**Answer: (D).** $U\cup W$ is not a subspace.

**Pattern cue.** One counterexample to closure.

**Fast route.** $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ lie in the union but their sum $(1,1)$ does not. In contrast $U+W=\R^2$ and $U\cap W=\{0\}$.

**Trap check.** Union and vector-space sum are different operations.
