Is $A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1\\0&2\end{pmatrix}$ diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$?

**(A)** Yes; every triangular matrix is diagonalizable.

**(B)** Yes; its determinant is nonzero.

**(C)** No; it has two distinct eigenvalues.

**(D)** No; its trace is zero.

**(E)** No; its eigenspace for $2$ has dimension $1$.
