**Fastest valid route.** The map is onto: for any $(a,b)\in\mathbb R^2$, the polynomial

$$p(x)=a+b(x-1)$$

satisfies $T(p)=(a,b)$. Thus $\operatorname{rank}T=2$. Since $\dim P_3(\mathbb R)=4$, rank-nullity gives

$$\dim(\ker T)=4-2=2.$$

The answer is **(C)**. Equivalently, the kernel consists of polynomials divisible by $(x-1)^2$, a two-dimensional subspace inside $P_3(\mathbb R)$.
