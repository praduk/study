**Answer: (C).** $\{(x,y):x=2y\}$

**Pattern cue.** Homogeneous linear constraint versus nonlinear or affine conditions.

**Fast route.** $x-2y=0$ is the kernel of a linear functional, hence a subspace. The union of axes fails addition; the affine line and circle omit zero; the half-plane fails multiplication by $-1$.

**Trap check.** Test closure, not merely whether the set contains zero.
