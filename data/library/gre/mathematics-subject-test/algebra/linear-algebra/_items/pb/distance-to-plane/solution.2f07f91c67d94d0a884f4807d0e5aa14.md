**Answer: (C).** $2\sqrt3$

**Pattern cue.** Project onto the normal rather than find an in-plane basis.

**Fast route.** A normal is $(1,1,1)$, so the distance is $|1+2+3|/\sqrt3=2\sqrt3$.

**Trap check.** The distance uses the normal component, not the norm of the projection into the plane.
