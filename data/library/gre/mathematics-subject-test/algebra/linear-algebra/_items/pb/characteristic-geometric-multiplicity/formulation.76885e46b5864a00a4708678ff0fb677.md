For

$$A=\begin{pmatrix}
2&1&0\\
0&2&0\\
1&0&-1
\end{pmatrix},$$

use the convention $\chi_A(\lambda)=\det(\lambda I-A)$. Which statement is true?

(A) $\chi_A(\lambda)=(\lambda-2)(\lambda+1)^2$.

(B) The eigenvalue $2$ has geometric multiplicity $2$.

(C) $A$ is diagonalizable over $\mathbb R$.

(D) The eigenvalue $2$ has algebraic multiplicity $2$ and geometric multiplicity $1$.

(E) The eigenvalue $-1$ has algebraic multiplicity $2$.
