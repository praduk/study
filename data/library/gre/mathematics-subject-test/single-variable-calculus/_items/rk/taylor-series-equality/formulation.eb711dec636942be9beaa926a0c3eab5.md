The Taylor series at $a$ is

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(x-a)^n.
$$

It equals $f(x)$ at a given $x$ exactly when $R_n(x)=f(x)-P_n(x)\to0$. An analytic function equals its Taylor series near the center.

**Clarification to the sheet:** existence of derivatives of every order does not by itself justify the printed equality.

**Memory cue (added).** A convergent formula is not automatically your function: the remainder must vanish.
