For $a>0$ and $|x|\ge a$, use $x=a\sec\theta$ on a branch covering the required $x$-range. Then

$$
\sqrt{x^2-a^2}=a|\tan\theta|,\qquad
dx=a\sec\theta\tan\theta\,d\theta.
$$

For $x\ge a$, one may take $0\le\theta<\pi/2$, giving $|\tan\theta|=\tan\theta$.

**Memory cue (added).** Secant-squared minus one becomes tangent-squared; taking a square root introduces an absolute value.
