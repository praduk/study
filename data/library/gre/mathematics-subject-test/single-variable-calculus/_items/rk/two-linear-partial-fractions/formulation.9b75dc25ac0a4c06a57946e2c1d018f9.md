If $a\ne b$ and $\deg P<2$, then

$$
\frac{P(x)}{(x-a)(x-b)}
=\frac{A}{x-a}+\frac{B}{x-b},
\qquad x\ne a,b.
$$

Multiplying through gives $P(x)=A(x-b)+B(x-a)$. Substituting $x=a,b$ determines the constants.

**Clarification to the sheet:** divide out a polynomial part first if the numerator degree is at least two. Repeated factors require a different form.

**Memory cue (added).** One constant for each distinct simple pole; clear denominators before evaluating at a root.
