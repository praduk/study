On an interval, $f''>0$ implies concave up, and $f''<0$ implies concave down.

An inflection point is a point of continuity at which the graph changes concavity. Merely having $f''(c)=0$ does not establish an inflection point.

**Memory cue (added).** An inflection is a change of bend, not just a zero in a derivative.
