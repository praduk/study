For real $n\ne-1$ on $x>0$,

$$
\int x^n\,dx=\frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C.
$$

For integer powers this extends to intervals on which the expression is defined. The exponent $-1$ uses the logarithm rule instead.

**Memory cue (added).** Raise the power, then divide by the new power.
