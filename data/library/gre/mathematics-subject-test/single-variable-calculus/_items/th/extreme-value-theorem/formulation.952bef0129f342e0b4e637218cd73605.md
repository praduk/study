A continuous real function on a nonempty compact interval $[a,b]$ is bounded and attains both its maximum and minimum. There exist $u,v\in[a,b]$ such that

$$
f(u)\le f(x)\le f(v)\qquad(x\in[a,b]).
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Continuous plus closed-and-bounded interval: the extreme values are reached, not merely approached.

Source: StudySheet.pdf, p. 6.

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** Continuous on a closed bounded interval: check extrema, intermediate values, and uniform continuity.
