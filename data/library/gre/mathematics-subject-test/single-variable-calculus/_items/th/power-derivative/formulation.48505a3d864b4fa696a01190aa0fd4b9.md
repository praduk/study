For a real constant $n$ and $x>0$,

$$
\frac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}.
$$

For positive integer $n$, this is the polynomial derivative for all real $x$. The constant power $x^0=1$ has derivative zero. Negative integer powers require $x\ne0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Bring the power down, subtract one from it.
