For a differentiable real function on an interval:

- $f'>0$ throughout implies $f$ is strictly increasing.
- $f'<0$ throughout implies $f$ is strictly decreasing.
- $f'=0$ throughout implies $f$ is constant.

These are interval-wide conclusions, not conclusions from one derivative value.

**Memory cue (added).** Positive climbs, negative descends, identically zero stays level.
