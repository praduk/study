Suppose $g(x)\le f(x)\le h(x)$ for all $x$ sufficiently near $a$ in the relevant domain, except possibly $a$. If

$$
\lim_{x\to a}g(x)=\lim_{x\to a}h(x)=L,
$$

then $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L$.

**Memory cue (added).** Same destination for both walls forces the middle to follow.
