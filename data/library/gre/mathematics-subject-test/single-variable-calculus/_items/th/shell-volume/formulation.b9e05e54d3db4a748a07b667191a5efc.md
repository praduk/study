For nonoverlapping cylindrical shells of radius $\rho(x)\ge0$, height $h(x)\ge0$, and thickness $dx$,

$$
V=2\pi\int_a^b \rho(x)h(x)\,dx.
$$

Choose the slicing variable to match shells parallel to the axis of rotation.

**Memory cue (added).** Circumference times height times thickness.
