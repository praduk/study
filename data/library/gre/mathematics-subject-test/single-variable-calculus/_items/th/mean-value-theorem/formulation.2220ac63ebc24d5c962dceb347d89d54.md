Let $a<b$. If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$, then some $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies

$$
f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Some instantaneous slope equals the whole trip’s average slope.
