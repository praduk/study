If $g$ is differentiable at $x$ and $f$ is differentiable at $g(x)$, then

$$
(f\circ g)'(x)=f'(g(x))\,g'(x).
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Differentiate outside, keep inside; then multiply by the inside derivative.
