For $C^1$ functions $u,v$,

$$
\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du.
$$

For fixed endpoints,

$$
\int_a^b u(x)v'(x)\,dx
=[u(x)v(x)]_a^b-\int_a^b v(x)u'(x)\,dx.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Keep the product, subtract the swapped integral; it is the product rule rearranged.
