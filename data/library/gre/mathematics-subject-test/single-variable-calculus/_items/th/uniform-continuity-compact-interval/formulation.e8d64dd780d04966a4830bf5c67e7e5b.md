If $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous, then it is uniformly continuous: for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is a single $\delta>0$ such that, for all $x,y\in[a,b]$,

$$
|x-y|<\delta\implies |f(x)-f(y)|<\varepsilon.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** One $\delta$ works across the whole interval.
