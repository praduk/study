For a differentiable curve $x=x(t)$, $y=y(t)$, at points with $x'(t)\ne0$,

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y'(t)}{x'(t)}.
$$

A zero $x'$ requires separate analysis rather than division.

**Memory cue (added).** Both rates use the same parameter; divide vertical rate by horizontal rate.
