If $f:[a,b]\to\mathbb R$ is continuous and $N$ lies between $f(a)$ and $f(b)$, then some $c\in[a,b]$ satisfies

$$
f(c)=N.
$$

If $N$ is strictly between the endpoint values, one may take $c\in(a,b)$. Uniqueness is not guaranteed.

**Memory cue (added).** A continuous graph cannot jump over an intermediate height.
