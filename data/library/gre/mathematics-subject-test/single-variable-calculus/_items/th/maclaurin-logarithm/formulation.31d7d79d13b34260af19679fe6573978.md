For $-1<x\le1$,

$$
\ln(1+x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n+1}\frac{x^n}{n}
=x-\frac{x^2}{2}+\frac{x^3}{3}-\cdots.
$$

The denominator is $n$, not $n!$. At $x=-1$ the series diverges; at $x=1$ it converges to $\ln2$.

**Memory cue (added).** Alternating every power, divide by its exponent; keep the right endpoint, lose the left.
