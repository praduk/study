Suppose $f$ is continuous at $c$ and differentiable on each side nearby.

- $f'$ changes from positive to negative at $c$: strict local maximum.
- $f'$ changes from negative to positive: strict local minimum.
- The same strictly positive or strictly negative sign on both sides gives neither.

The sign behavior must hold on neighboring intervals, not merely at sampled points.

**Memory cue (added).** Climb then descend: peak. Descend then climb: valley.
