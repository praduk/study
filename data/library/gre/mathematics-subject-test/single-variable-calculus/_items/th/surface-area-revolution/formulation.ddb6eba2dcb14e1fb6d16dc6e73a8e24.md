For a $C^1$ graph $y=f(x)$ rotated about the $x$-axis,

$$
S=2\pi\int_a^b |f(x)|\sqrt{1+[f'(x)]^2}\,dx.
$$

The sheet’s formula without the absolute value assumes $f(x)\ge0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Circumference times slanted arc length: $2\pi$ times radius times $ds$.
