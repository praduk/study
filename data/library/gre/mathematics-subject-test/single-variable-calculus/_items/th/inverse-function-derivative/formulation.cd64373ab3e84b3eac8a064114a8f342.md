Suppose $f$ is $C^1$ near $x_0$ and $f'(x_0)\ne0$. Its local inverse near $y_0=f(x_0)$ satisfies

$$
(f^{-1})'(y_0)=\frac1{f'(x_0)}.
$$

The input to $f'$ is $x_0=f^{-1}(y_0)$, not $y_0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Undoing swaps rise and run; evaluate at the original input.
