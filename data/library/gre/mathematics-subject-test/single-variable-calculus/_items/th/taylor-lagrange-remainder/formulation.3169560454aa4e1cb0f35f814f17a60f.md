Suppose $f$ is $C^{n+1}$ on an interval containing $a$ and $x$. Then for some $c$ between $a$ and $x$,

$$
f(x)=P_n(x)+R_n(x),\qquad
R_n(x)=\frac{f^{(n+1)}(c)}{(n+1)!}(x-a)^{n+1}.
$$

For $x=a$, the remainder is zero.

**Memory cue (added).** The error looks like the next Taylor term, but its derivative is evaluated at an unknown intermediate point.
