If $f$ is differentiable on an interval and $|f'(t)|\le M$ throughout it, where $M\ge0$, then for any $x,y$ in the interval,

$$
|f(x)-f(y)|\le M|x-y|.
$$

The mean value theorem supplies the estimate.

**Memory cue (added).** Maximum slope times horizontal distance bounds vertical change.
