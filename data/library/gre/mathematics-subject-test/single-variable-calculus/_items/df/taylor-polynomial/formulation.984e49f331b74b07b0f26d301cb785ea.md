If $f$ has derivatives through order $n$ at $a$, its degree-at-most-$n$ Taylor polynomial at $a$ is

$$
P_n(x)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}\frac{f^{(k)}(a)}{k!}(x-a)^k.
$$

The case $a=0$ is called the Maclaurin polynomial. Define the remainder by $R_n(x)=f(x)-P_n(x)$.

**Memory cue (added).** Derivative order matches power; divide by that order’s factorial.
