For a real function defined in a neighborhood of $a$, continuity at $a$ means

$$
\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a).
$$

Thus the limit must exist and equal the actual value; at a domain endpoint use the corresponding one-sided limit.

**Memory cue (added).** Limit exists, value exists, and they agree.
