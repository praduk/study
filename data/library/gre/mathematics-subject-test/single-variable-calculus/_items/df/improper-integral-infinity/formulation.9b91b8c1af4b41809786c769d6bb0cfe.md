If $f$ is integrable on every finite $[a,b]$, define

$$
\int_a^\infty f(x)\,dx
=\lim_{b\to\infty}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx
$$

when the limit exists and is finite. Otherwise the improper integral diverges.

**Memory cue (added).** Infinity is a limit of finite endpoints, not a number to substitute.
