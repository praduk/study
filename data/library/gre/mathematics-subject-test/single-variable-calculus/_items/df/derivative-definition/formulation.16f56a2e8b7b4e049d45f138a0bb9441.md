The derivative of $f$ at an interior point $a$ is the finite limit

$$
f'(a)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}h,
$$

when it exists. It is the limiting slope of secants through the point.

**Memory cue (added).** Output change over input change, then shrink the input change to zero.
