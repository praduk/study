Let $X$ be compact, $Y$ be Hausdorff, and $f:X\to Y$ be a continuous bijection. Then $f^{-1}$ is continuous, so $f$ is a homeomorphism.

A continuous bijection between arbitrary topological spaces need not have a continuous inverse.

**Memory cue (added).** Compact source, Hausdorff target: the inverse comes free.
