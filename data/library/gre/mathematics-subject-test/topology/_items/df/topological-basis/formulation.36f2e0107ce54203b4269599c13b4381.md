A collection $\mathcal B$ of subsets of $X$ is a basis if it covers $X$ and whenever $x\in B_1\cap B_2$ with $B_1,B_2\in\mathcal B$, some $B_3\in\mathcal B$ satisfies $x\in B_3\subseteq B_1\cap B_2$. The generated open sets are unions of basis elements.
