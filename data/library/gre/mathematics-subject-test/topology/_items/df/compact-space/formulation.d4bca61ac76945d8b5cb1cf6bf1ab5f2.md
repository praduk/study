A space $X$ is compact if every family of open sets whose union contains $X$ has a finite subfamily whose union still contains $X$.

**Memory cue (added).** Every open cover can be reduced to finitely many pieces.

*Source: StudySheet.pdf, p. 24.*

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** Compactness connects finite subcovers, continuous images, and extrema of continuous real functions on nonempty domains.
