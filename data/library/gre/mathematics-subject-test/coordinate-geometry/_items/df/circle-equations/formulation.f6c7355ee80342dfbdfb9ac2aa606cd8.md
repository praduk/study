The circle of center $(h,k)$ and radius $r>0$ is the set

$$
(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2.
$$

For $x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0$, completing the square gives center $(-D/2,-E/2)$ and

$$
r^2=\frac{D^2+E^2}{4}-F.
$$

A positive $r^2$ gives a circle, zero a single point, and negative no real points.

**Memory cue (added).** Halve the linear coefficients and change their signs; complete both squares.
