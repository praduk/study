For two distinct points with $x_1\ne x_2$, the line’s slope is

$$
m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}.
$$

Its equation can be written $y-y_1=m(x-x_1)$ or $y=mx+b$, where $b$ is the $y$-intercept. A vertical line instead has equation $x=c$ and no finite slope.

**Memory cue (added).** Rise over run; point-slope subtracts the known point.
