Polar coordinates $(r,\theta)$ represent

$$
x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta.
$$

Thus $r^2=x^2+y^2$, and $\tan\theta=y/x$ when $x\ne0$. The tangent ratio alone does not identify the quadrant. Under the usual $r\ge0$ choice, $r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$.

**Memory cue (added).** Cosine gives the horizontal coordinate; sine gives the vertical. Check the quadrant before using an inverse tangent.
