For $a,b>0$,

$$
\frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1
$$

has foci $(\pm c,0)$ with $c^2=a^2+b^2$, and asymptotes

$$
y=\pm\frac ba x.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Hyperbola has a minus in its equation but a plus in its focal relation. Set the quadratic part to zero for asymptotes.
