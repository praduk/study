For $a\ne0$, $y=a(x-h)^2+k$ has vertex $(h,k)$ and vertical axis $x=h$.

For $p\ne0$, the points equidistant from focus $(0,p)$ and directrix $y=-p$ satisfy

$$
x^2=4py.
$$

The sign of $p$ determines the opening direction.

**Memory cue (added).** The unsquared coordinate points along the axis; the coefficient is four times the focal parameter.
