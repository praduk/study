If a nonnegative continuous radius $r(\theta)$ describes a region once as $\alpha\le\theta\le\beta$, its area is

$$
A=\frac12\int_\alpha^\beta r(\theta)^2\,d\theta.
$$

Repeated angular coverage can count area more than once.

**Memory cue (added).** A tiny sector has area one-half radius-squared times angle.
