For a $C^1$ polar curve $r=r(\theta)$, $\alpha\le\theta\le\beta$,

$$
L=\int_\alpha^\beta
\sqrt{r(\theta)^2+\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2}\,d\theta.
$$

This is the length traversed; retracing counts again.

**Memory cue (added).** Angular motion contributes $r$; radial motion contributes $r'$. Square both and add.
