Distinct nonvertical lines are parallel iff their slopes are equal. Two nonvertical lines are perpendicular iff

$$
m_1m_2=-1.
$$

Distinct vertical lines are parallel; a vertical line is perpendicular to a horizontal line.

**Memory cue (added).** Parallel: same slope. Perpendicular: negative reciprocal, with horizontal/vertical handled separately.
