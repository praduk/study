For $a\ge b>0$,

$$
\frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1
$$

is an ellipse with semiaxes $a,b$ and foci $(\pm c,0)$, where

$$
c^2=a^2-b^2.
$$

When $a=b$, it is a circle and the two foci coincide.

**Memory cue (added).** Ellipse has a plus in its equation but a minus in its focal relation.
