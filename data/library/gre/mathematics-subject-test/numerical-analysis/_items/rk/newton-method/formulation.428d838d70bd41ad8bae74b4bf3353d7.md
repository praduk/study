To approximate a root of $f$, use

$$
x_{n+1}=x_n-\frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)},
$$

provided $f'(x_n)\ne0$. This is the zero of the tangent line at $x_n$. A nearby simple root and sufficient smoothness support local fast convergence; the method is not globally guaranteed.

**Memory cue (added).** New equals old minus height over slope.
