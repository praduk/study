The Simpson approximation using endpoints and midpoint is

$$
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx\approx\frac{b-a}{6}\left[f(a)+4f\left(\frac{a+b}{2}\right)+f(b)\right].
$$

Composite Simpson’s rule requires an even number of equal subintervals.

**Memory cue (added).** Weights $1,4,1$; whole span divided by $6$. Pair the subintervals.
