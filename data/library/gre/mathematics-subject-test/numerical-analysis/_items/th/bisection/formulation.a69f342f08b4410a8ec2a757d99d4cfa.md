If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and $f(a)f(b)<0$, then a root lies in $(a,b)$. Keep the half-interval with a sign change at each step (or stop if the midpoint is a root). After $n$ completed bisections, the bracket length is $(b-a)/2^n$.

**Memory cue (added).** A sign-changing bracket survives; its width halves each time.
