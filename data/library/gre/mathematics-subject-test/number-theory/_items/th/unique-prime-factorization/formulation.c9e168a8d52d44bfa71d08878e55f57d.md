Every integer $n>1$ is a product of primes, uniquely up to rearranging the factors. Equivalently, $n=\prod_{i=1}^k p_i^{a_i}$ with distinct primes $p_i$ and positive integers $a_i$, uniquely once the primes are ordered.

**Memory cue (added).** Prime factors are the building blocks; only their order is free.
