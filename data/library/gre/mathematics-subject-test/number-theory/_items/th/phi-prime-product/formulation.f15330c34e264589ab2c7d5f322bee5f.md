If $n=\prod_{i=1}^k p_i^{a_i}$ with distinct primes $p_i$ and positive integers $a_i$, then

$$
\varphi(n)=n\prod_{i=1}^k\left(1-\frac1{p_i}\right).
$$

Use each distinct prime once in the product.

**Memory cue (added).** Start with $n$; for each distinct prime, remove its fraction $1/p$.
