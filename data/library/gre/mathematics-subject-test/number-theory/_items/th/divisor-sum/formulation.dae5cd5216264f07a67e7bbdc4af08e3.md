If $n=\prod_{i=1}^k p_i^{a_i}$, then the sum $\sigma(n)$ of its positive divisors is

$$
\sigma(n)=\prod_{i=1}^k(1+p_i+\cdots+p_i^{a_i})
=\prod_{i=1}^k\frac{p_i^{a_i+1}-1}{p_i-1}.
$$

For $n=1$, the empty product is $1$.

**Memory cue (added).** Count divisors by exponent choices; sum divisors by multiplying geometric sums.
