If $p$ is prime and $a$ is an integer with $p\nmid a$, then $a^{p-1}\equiv1\pmod p$. Equivalently, for every integer $a$, $a^p\equiv a\pmod p$. The first form requires $a$ to be a unit; the second includes $p\mid a$.

**Memory cue (added).** Prime modulus: exponent $p-1$ returns a unit to $1$; exponent $p$ returns any residue to itself.
