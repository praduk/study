For pairwise coprime positive integers $n_1,\ldots,n_k$, reduction modulo each factor gives a ring isomorphism

$$
\mathbb Z/(n_1\cdots n_k)\mathbb Z
\cong\prod_{i=1}^k\mathbb Z/n_i\mathbb Z.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** One residue modulo the product is the same information as one residue on every coprime factor.
