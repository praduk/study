If a real second-order constant-coefficient homogeneous ODE has characteristic roots $\alpha\pm i\beta$, with $\beta\ne0$, then

$$
y=e^{\alpha x}
\bigl(C_1\cos(\beta x)+C_2\sin(\beta x)\bigr).
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Real part grows or decays; imaginary part oscillates.
