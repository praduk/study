For a linear differential operator $L$, if $L[y_p]=g$, then all solutions of $L[y]=g$ are

$$
y=y_h+y_p,
\qquad L[y_h]=0.
$$

Subtracting one particular solution leaves a homogeneous solution.

**Memory cue (added).** All solutions = one particular + all homogeneous.
