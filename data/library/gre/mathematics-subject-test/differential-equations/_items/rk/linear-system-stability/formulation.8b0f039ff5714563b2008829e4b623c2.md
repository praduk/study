For a constant real linear system $x'=Ax$:

- all eigenvalues with negative real parts: asymptotically stable sink;
- all with positive real parts: unstable source;
- some positive and some negative real parts, with none zero: saddle behavior;
- in real dimension two, a pair $\pm i\beta$ with $\beta\ne0$: a center, stable but not asymptotically stable.

Zero real parts require separate analysis outside the stated two-dimensional center case.

**Memory cue (added).** Real parts govern growth; nonzero imaginary parts rotate.
