For

$$
y'=g(x)h(y),
$$

on an interval where $h(y)\ne0$, separate and integrate:

$$
\int\frac{dy}{h(y)}=\int g(x)\,dx+C.
$$

Before dividing, retain the constant solutions $y\equiv y_0$ for which $h(y_0)=0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Separate variables, but save the zero factors.
