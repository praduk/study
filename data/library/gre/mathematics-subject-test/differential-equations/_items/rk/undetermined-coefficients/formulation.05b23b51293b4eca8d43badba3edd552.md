For a linear ODE with constant coefficients, the method of undetermined coefficients uses a trial particular solution built from the forcing's polynomial, exponential, sine, or cosine form, including their products and sums.

If the trial overlaps a homogeneous solution, multiply it by a sufficient power of $x$ to remove that overlap.
