For $y'+p(x)y=q(x)$ with continuous $p,q$ on an interval, choose

$$
\mu(x)=e^{\int p(x)\,dx}.
$$

Then $(\mu y)'=\mu q$, so

$$
y=\frac1\mu\left(\int\mu q\,dx+C\right).
$$

Normalize the coefficient of $y'$ to one first.

**Memory cue (added).** Multiply to make one product derivative: mu-y.
