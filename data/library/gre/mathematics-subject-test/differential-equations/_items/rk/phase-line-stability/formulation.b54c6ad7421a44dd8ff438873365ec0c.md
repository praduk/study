For $x'=f(x)$ with $f\in C^1$ near an isolated equilibrium $x_*$, use the sign of $f(x)$:

- arrows toward $x_*$ from both sides: locally asymptotically stable;
- arrows away on both sides: unstable;
- arrows toward on one side and away on the other: semistable.

An arrow points right when $f(x)>0$ and left when $f(x)<0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Toward attracts; away repels; one of each is semistable.
