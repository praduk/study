A subset $I$ of a ring $R$ is a two-sided ideal exactly when:

- $I$ is an additive subgroup of $R$;
- $rI\subseteq I$ and $Ir\subseteq I$ for every $r\in R$.

The whole ring is an ideal; **proper** means $I\ne R$.

**Memory cue (added).** An additive subgroup that absorbs multiplication from both sides.
