If $\alpha$ is algebraic over a field $F$, its minimal polynomial $m_\alpha(x)\in F[x]$ is the unique monic irreducible polynomial satisfying

$$
m_\alpha(\alpha)=0.
$$

Equivalently, it is the monic polynomial of least positive degree vanishing at $\alpha$. A transcendental element has no minimal polynomial.

**Memory cue (added).** Monic, least degree, vanishes; therefore irreducible.
