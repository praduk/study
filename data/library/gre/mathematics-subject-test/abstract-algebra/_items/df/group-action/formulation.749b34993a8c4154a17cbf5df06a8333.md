An action of a group $G$ on a set $X$ is a homomorphism $G\to S_X$, where $S_X$ is the group of bijections of $X$.

Equivalently, an operation $(g,x)\mapsto gx$ satisfies $ex=x$ and $(gh)x=g(hx)$ for every $g,h\in G$ and $x\in X$.

**Memory cue (added).** Group multiplication agrees with doing the actions in succession.
