A subgroup $N\le G$ is normal, written $N\triangleleft G$, exactly when

$$
gNg^{-1}=N
\qquad\text{for every }g\in G.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Normal means unchanged by conjugation.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 19.

**Trap (sheet, p. 30).** A subgroup of a nonabelian group is not automatically normal. Check invariance under conjugation.
