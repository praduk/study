A group is a nonempty set $G$ with a binary operation $G\times G\to G$ such that:

- $(ab)c=a(bc)$ for all $a,b,c\in G$;
- an identity $e\in G$ satisfies $eg=ge=g$ for every $g\in G$;
- every $g\in G$ has an inverse $g^{-1}\in G$ with $gg^{-1}=g^{-1}g=e$.

The binary operation includes closure.
