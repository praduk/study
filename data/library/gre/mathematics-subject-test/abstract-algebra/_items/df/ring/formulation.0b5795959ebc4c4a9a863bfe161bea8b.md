Following the sheet, a ring $R$ has addition and multiplication such that:

- $(R,+)$ is an abelian group;
- multiplication is associative;
- multiplication distributes over addition on both sides.

A **commutative** ring also satisfies $ab=ba$. A ring **with unity** has a multiplicative identity $1$. The word “ring” here does not by itself require unity.

**Memory cue (added).** Additive group, associative multiplication, two distributive laws.
