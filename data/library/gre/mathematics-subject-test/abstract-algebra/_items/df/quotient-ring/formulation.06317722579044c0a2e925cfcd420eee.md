For a two-sided ideal $I$ of $R$, the quotient ring $R/I$ consists of cosets $r+I$, with

$$
(r+I)+(s+I)=(r+s)+I,
$$

$$
(r+I)(s+I)=rs+I.
$$

The ideal conditions make both operations well-defined.

**Memory cue (added).** Do the arithmetic on representatives, then take the coset.
