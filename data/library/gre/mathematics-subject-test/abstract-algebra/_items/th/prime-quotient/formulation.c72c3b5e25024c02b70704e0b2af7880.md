For an ideal $I$ in a commutative ring $R$ with unity,

$$
I\text{ is prime}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
R/I\text{ is an integral domain}.
$$

Prime ideals are proper, so the quotient is not the zero ring.

**Memory cue (added).** A prime ideal gives a domain quotient.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 20.

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** A quotient domain points to a prime ideal; a quotient field points to a maximal ideal.
