For a ring homomorphism $\phi:R\to S$ (preserving addition and multiplication), $\ker\phi=\{r:\phi(r)=0\}$ is a two-sided ideal and

$$
R/\ker\phi\cong\im\phi.
$$

The isomorphism is $r+\ker\phi\mapsto\phi(r)$.

**Memory cue (added).** Collapse the zero-output ideal; obtain the image.
