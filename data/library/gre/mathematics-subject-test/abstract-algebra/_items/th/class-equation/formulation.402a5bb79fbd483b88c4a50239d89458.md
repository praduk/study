For a finite group $G$, choose one representative $x_i$ from each noncentral conjugacy class. Then

$$
|G|=|Z(G)|+\sum_i[G:C_G(x_i)].
$$

Here $Z(G)=\{z\in G:zg=gz\text{ for every }g\in G\}$ is the center, and $C_G(x)=\{g\in G:gx=xg\}$ is the centralizer. Central elements have singleton conjugacy classes.

**Memory cue (added).** Center first; then one orbit size per noncentral class.
