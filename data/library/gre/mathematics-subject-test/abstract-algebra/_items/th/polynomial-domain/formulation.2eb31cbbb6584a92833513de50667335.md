If $F$ is a field, then $F[x]$ is a Euclidean domain using polynomial degree. Consequently it is a principal ideal domain (every ideal has one generator) and a unique factorization domain (factorization into irreducibles is unique up to units and order).

**Memory cue (added).** Degree division gives Euclidean, then principal, then unique factorization.
