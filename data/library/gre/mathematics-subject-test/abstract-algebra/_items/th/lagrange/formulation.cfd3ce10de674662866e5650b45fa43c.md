If $G$ is finite and $H\le G$, then

$$
|G|=[G:H]\,|H|.
$$

In particular, $|H|$ divides $|G|$, and the order of every $g\in G$ divides $|G|$.

**Memory cue (added).** Equal-size cosets tile the group.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 18.

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** For a finite group, look for subgroup orders, element orders, and coset indices.
