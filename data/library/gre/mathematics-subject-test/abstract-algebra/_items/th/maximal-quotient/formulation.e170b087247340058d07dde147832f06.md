For an ideal $I$ in a commutative ring $R$ with unity,

$$
I\text{ is maximal}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
R/I\text{ is a field}.
$$

Every maximal ideal is therefore prime.

**Memory cue (added).** Maximal makes a field; fields are domains.
