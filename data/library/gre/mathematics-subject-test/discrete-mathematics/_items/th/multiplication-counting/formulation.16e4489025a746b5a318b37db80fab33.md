If a construction has $r$ stages and stage $i$ has exactly $n_i$ choices after every permissible earlier choice, then there are $\prod_{i=1}^r n_i$ constructions. Fixed continuation counts are the condition; probabilistic independence is not needed.

**Memory cue (added).** A fixed number of choices at each stage multiplies.
