For a finite simple undirected graph with $n\ge1$ vertices, the following are equivalent:

- connected and acyclic;
- connected with $n-1$ edges;
- acyclic with $n-1$ edges;
- exactly one simple path between every pair of distinct vertices.

In particular, a tree has $n-1$ edges.

**Memory cue (added).** Connectivity and no cycles balance at exactly $n-1$ edges.
