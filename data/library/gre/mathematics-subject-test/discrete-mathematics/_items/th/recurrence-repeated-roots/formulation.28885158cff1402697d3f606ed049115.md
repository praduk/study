For an order-$k$ homogeneous linear constant-coefficient recurrence with nonzero coefficient of $a_{n-k}$, a nonzero characteristic root $r$ of multiplicity $m$ contributes

$$
(A_0+A_1n+\cdots+A_{m-1}n^{m-1})r^n
$$

to the general solution.

**Memory cue (added).** Multiplicity $m$ needs powers of $n$ from $0$ through $m-1$.
