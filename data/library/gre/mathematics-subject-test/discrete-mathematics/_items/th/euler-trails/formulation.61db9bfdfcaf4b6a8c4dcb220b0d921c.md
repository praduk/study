For a finite connected undirected graph:

- an **Euler circuit**, using every edge exactly once and returning to the start, exists iff every vertex has even degree;
- an **Euler trail**, using every edge exactly once and allowing distinct endpoints, exists iff there are exactly zero or two odd-degree vertices.

With two odd vertices, those must be the endpoints.

**Memory cue (added).** Every intermediate entrance needs an exit; only two endpoints may be odd.
