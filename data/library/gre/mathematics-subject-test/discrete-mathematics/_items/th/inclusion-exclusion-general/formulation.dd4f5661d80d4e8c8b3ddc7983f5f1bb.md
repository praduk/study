For finite sets $A_1,\ldots,A_n$,

$$
\left|\bigcup_{i=1}^n A_i\right|
=\sum_{\varnothing\ne J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}}
(-1)^{|J|+1}\left|\bigcap_{j\in J}A_j\right|.
$$

For three sets: add the three single sizes, subtract the three pairwise intersections, then add the triple intersection.

**Memory cue (added).** Odd-size intersections plus; even-size intersections minus.
