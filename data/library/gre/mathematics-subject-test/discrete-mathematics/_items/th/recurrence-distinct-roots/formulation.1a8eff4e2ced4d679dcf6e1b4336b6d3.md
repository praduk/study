For the recurrence $a_n=c_1a_{n-1}+\cdots+c_ka_{n-k}$ for $n\ge k$, with $c_k\ne0$ and $k$ distinct characteristic roots $r_1,\ldots,r_k$ over $\mathbb C$, every solution has the form

$$
a_n=A_1r_1^n+\cdots+A_kr_k^n.
$$

The initial values determine the constants.

**Memory cue (added).** One root supplies one exponential sequence.
