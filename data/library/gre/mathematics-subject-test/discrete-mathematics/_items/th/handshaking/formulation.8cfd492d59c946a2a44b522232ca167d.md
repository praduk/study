For a finite undirected graph,

$$
\sum_{v\in V}\deg(v)=2|E|.
$$

Consequently, the number of vertices of odd degree is even. If loops are allowed, each loop contributes two to degree.

**Memory cue (added).** Every edge shakes two hands.

*Source: StudySheet.pdf, p. 27.*

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** A degree total suggests counting each edge at both ends.
