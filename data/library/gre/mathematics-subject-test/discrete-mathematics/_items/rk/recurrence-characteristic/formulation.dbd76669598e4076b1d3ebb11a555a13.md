For the constant-coefficient recurrence

$$
a_n=c_1a_{n-1}+\cdots+c_ka_{n-k},
$$

trying $a_n=r^n$ leads to

$$
r^k-c_1r^{k-1}-\cdots-c_k=0.
$$

For a genuine order-$k$ recurrence, take $c_k\ne0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Replace each backward step by one lower power of $r$.
