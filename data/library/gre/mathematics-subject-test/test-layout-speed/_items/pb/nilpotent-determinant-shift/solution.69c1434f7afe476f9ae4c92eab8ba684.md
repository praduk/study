**Fast route.** Over $\C$, any eigenvalue $\lambda$ of $N$ satisfies $\lambda^3=0$, so all three eigenvalues, counted with algebraic multiplicity, are zero. The eigenvalues of $I+N$ are therefore all $1$, and their product is $1$. No diagonalizability assumption is needed.

**Trap check.** A nilpotent matrix need not be the zero matrix or diagonalizable.
