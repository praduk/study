If $n$ objects contain $n_i$ indistinguishable copies of type $i$, with $\sum_i n_i=n$, their distinct linear arrangements number $n!/(\prod_i n_i!)$. Temporarily label all copies and then divide by the independent permutations of identical copies. Restrictions such as adjacency require an additional counting step.
