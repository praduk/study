For integers $n\ge0$ and $k\ge1$, nonnegative integer solutions of $x_1+\cdots+x_k=n$ number $\binom{n+k-1}{k-1}$. Positive solutions number $\binom{n-1}{k-1}$ when $n\ge k$, and zero otherwise. For fixed lower bounds $x_i\ge b_i$, substitute $y_i=x_i-b_i$. Upper bounds need subtraction or generating functions.
