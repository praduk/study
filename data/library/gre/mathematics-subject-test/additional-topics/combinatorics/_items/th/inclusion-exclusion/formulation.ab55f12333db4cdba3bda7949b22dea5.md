For finite sets $A_1,\ldots,A_k$, the size of their union is the sum of single-set sizes, minus the sum of pairwise intersection sizes, plus triple intersections, continuing with alternating signs. An element appearing in $r\ge1$ sets contributes $\sum_{j=1}^r(-1)^{j+1}\binom rj=1$. “At least one” can also be counted as total minus none.
