A derangement of $n$ labeled objects is a permutation fixing none. Inclusion–exclusion gives $D_n=n!\sum_{j=0}^n(-1)^j/j!$. An alternative is $D_n=(n-1)(D_{n-1}+D_{n-2})$ for $n\ge2$, with $D_0=1,D_1=0$. “No fixed point” is different from “no two objects adjacent.”
