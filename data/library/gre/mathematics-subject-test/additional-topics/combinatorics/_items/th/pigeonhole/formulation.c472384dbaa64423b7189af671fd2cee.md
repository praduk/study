Putting $N$ objects into $k\ge1$ boxes forces at least one box to contain $\lceil N/k\rceil$ objects. To force at least $r$ in a box, $(r-1)k+1$ objects suffice and are necessary when every occupancy up to $r-1$ is possible. Choose boxes to reflect the obstruction, such as residue classes or complementary pairs.
