**Pattern cue.** Partition four-element subsets according to whether one distinguished element is present.

**Fast route.** Of the four-subsets of a nine-set, those omitting the distinguished element number $\binom84$; those including it number $\binom83$. Thus the sum is $\binom94=126$.

**Trap.** The lower indices differ by one while the upper indices agree.
