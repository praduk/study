**Answer.** $a_n=(n+1)2^n$.

**Fast route.** $(r-2)^2=0$, so $a_n=(A+Bn)2^n$. The initial conditions give $A=1$ and $2(1+B)=4$, hence $B=1$. Substitution gives both the recurrence and initial terms.
