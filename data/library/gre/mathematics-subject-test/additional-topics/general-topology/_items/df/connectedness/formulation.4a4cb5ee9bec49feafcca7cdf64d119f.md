A space is connected iff it has no separation into two disjoint nonempty open sets whose union is the space. Continuous images and products of connected spaces are connected. A subset of $\R$ is connected exactly when it is an interval (including singletons and the empty set under the usual convention). Path connectedness implies connectedness; the converse fails in general.
