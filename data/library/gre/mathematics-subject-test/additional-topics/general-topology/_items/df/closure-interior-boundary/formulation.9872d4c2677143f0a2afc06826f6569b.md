The closure $\closure A$ is the smallest closed set containing $A$; equivalently every neighborhood of a closure point meets $A$. The interior $\Int A$ is the largest open subset of $A$. The boundary is $\partial A=\closure A\setminus\Int A$. A set is dense in $X$ iff its closure is $X$. All four notions depend on the ambient topology.
