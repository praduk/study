A basis for the product topology on $\prod_{i\in I}X_i$ consists of products $\prod_iU_i$ where each $U_i$ is open and $U_i=X_i$ except at finitely many indices. In a finite product these are ordinary open rectangles. Allowing infinitely many proper coordinate restrictions defines the generally finer box topology.
