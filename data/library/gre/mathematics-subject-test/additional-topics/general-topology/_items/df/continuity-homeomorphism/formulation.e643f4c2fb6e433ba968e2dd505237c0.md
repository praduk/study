A map $f:X\to Y$ is continuous iff $f^{-1}(U)$ is open in $X$ for every open $U\subseteq Y$. A homeomorphism is a bijection for which both $f$ and $f^{-1}$ are continuous. Continuous maps need not send open sets to open sets. A continuous bijection alone need not be a homeomorphism.
