For a surjection $q:X\to Y$, the quotient topology makes $V\subseteq Y$ open exactly when $q^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$. A map $g:Y\to Z$ is continuous iff $g\circ q$ is continuous. Test the full preimage of a set, not just one representative of an identified point.
