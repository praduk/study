For $Y\subseteq X$, the subspace-open sets are $Y\cap U$ with $U$ open in $X$. A set can be open in $Y$ without being open in $X$. Neighborhoods, closure, connectedness, and compactness of a subset must be interpreted in the specified ambient space.
