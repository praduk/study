A continuous bijection $f:X\to Y$ from a compact space to a Hausdorff space is a homeomorphism. Indeed, a closed subset of $X$ is compact; its image is compact and therefore closed in $Y$. Thus $f$ is a closed bijection, which makes $f^{-1}$ continuous.
