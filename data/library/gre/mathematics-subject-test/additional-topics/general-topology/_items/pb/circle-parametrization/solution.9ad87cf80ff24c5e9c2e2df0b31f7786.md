**Pattern cue.** A missing endpoint can break continuity of the inverse.

**Fast route.** The points $e^{i(2\pi-1/n)}$ converge to $1$, but their inverse images $2\pi-1/n$ do not converge to $f^{-1}(1)=0$. The domain is not compact, so the compact-to-Hausdorff theorem does not apply.

**Trap.** Being bounded is not enough; the half-open interval is not compact.
