**Answer.** Dense subset $\Q^2$; basis of open rectangles with rational endpoints.

**Pattern cue.** Apply rational approximation separately in each coordinate.

**Fast route.** Every open ball contains a point with rational coordinates. Every point in an open set lies in a sufficiently small rectangle with rational endpoints contained in that open set. Both collections are countable by finite products of countable sets.

**Trap.** A dense set of points and a basis of open sets are different objects.
