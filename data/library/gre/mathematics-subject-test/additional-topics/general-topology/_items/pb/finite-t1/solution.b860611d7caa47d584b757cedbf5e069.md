**Pattern cue.** Use finite unions of closed singletons.

**Fast route.** For any $x$, the complement $X\setminus\{x\}$ is a finite union of closed singletons and is therefore closed. Thus every singleton is open, and every subset is a union of open singletons.

**Trap.** The finite assumption matters: an infinite cofinite space is $T_1$ but not discrete.
