**Answer.** Path connected but not convex.

**Pattern cue.** A detour can connect points when a straight segment fails.

**Fast route.** Connect each point radially to the unit circle, travel along an arc, then move radially to the other point; all radii stay positive. The segment from $(1,0)$ to $(-1,0)$ passes through the omitted origin, disproving convexity.

**Trap.** Failure of one straight segment does not disprove path connectedness.
