**Answer.** Yes.

**Pattern cue.** Use rational density and countability separately.

**Fast route.** For any $x$ in an open interval $U$, choose rational $a<x<b$ with $(a,b)\subseteq U$. Thus these intervals form a basis. Their indexing set is a subset of $\Q^2$, hence countable.

**Trap.** Basis elements need not have rational points as their only members.
