**Answer.** Compact, but neither $T_1$ nor Hausdorff.

**Pattern cue.** A finite space is compact; inspect its closed sets and neighborhoods.

**Fast route.** Every cover contains $X$, so a one-set subcover works. The singleton $\{a\}$ is not closed, and the only nonempty neighborhood of either point is $X$.

**Trap.** Compactness alone does not imply separation or closedness of subsets.
