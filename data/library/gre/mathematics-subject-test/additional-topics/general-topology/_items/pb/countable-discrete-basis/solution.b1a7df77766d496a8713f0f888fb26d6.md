**Answer.** Both properties hold exactly when $X$ is countable.

**Pattern cue.** Singletons force both the basis and dense-set sizes.

**Fast route.** Every basis must contain every singleton, since each singleton is open. Every dense subset must equal $X$, since a missing point has a disjoint singleton neighborhood. Conversely a countable set has a countable singleton basis and is dense in itself.

**Trap.** A large discrete space is not separable merely because every singleton is simple.
