**Answer.** It is a continuous bijection, but not a homeomorphism.

**Pattern cue.** Check the inverse-image definition in each direction.

**Fast route.** Every preimage of a usual open set is discrete-open, so the map is continuous. The inverse is not continuous: the discrete-open singleton $\{0\}$ has a nonopen preimage in the usual real line.

**Trap.** Bijective and continuous do not imply inverse continuity.
