In $\forall x\exists y\,P(x,y)$, the choice of $y$ may depend on $x$. In $\exists y\forall x\,P(x,y)$, one $y$ must work for every $x$. A universal statement over an empty domain is true; an existential statement over an empty domain is false. Always state the domains when comparing quantifier orders.
