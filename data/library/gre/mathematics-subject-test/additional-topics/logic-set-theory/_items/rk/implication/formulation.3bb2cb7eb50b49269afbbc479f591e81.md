An implication $P\Rightarrow Q$ is false exactly when $P$ is true and $Q$ false. It is equivalent to $\neg P\lor Q$ and to its contrapositive $\neg Q\Rightarrow\neg P$. It is not generally equivalent to its converse $Q\Rightarrow P$. In $P\Rightarrow Q$, $P$ is sufficient for $Q$, while $Q$ is necessary for $P$.
