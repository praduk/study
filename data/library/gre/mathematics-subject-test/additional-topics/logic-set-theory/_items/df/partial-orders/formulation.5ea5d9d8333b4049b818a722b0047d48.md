A partial order is reflexive, antisymmetric, and transitive. Antisymmetric means $x\le y$ and $y\le x$ imply $x=y$; it does not mean “not symmetric.” A total order also compares every pair. In a partial order, a maximal element has nothing strictly above it, whereas a maximum is at least every element; there can be several maximal elements but at most one maximum.
