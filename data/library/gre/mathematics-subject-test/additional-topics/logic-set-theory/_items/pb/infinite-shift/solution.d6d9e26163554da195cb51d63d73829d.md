**Answer.** Injective but not surjective.

**Pattern cue.** For infinite sets, equal domain and codomain do not make the two properties equivalent.

**Fast route.** Equal outputs give $m+1=n+1$, hence $m=n$. The codomain value one has no preimage.

**Trap.** Do not apply a finite-set cardinality argument to an infinite set.
