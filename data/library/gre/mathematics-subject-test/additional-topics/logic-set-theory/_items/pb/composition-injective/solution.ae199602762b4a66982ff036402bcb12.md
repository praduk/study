**Answer.** $f$ must be; $g$ need not be.

**Pattern cue.** Start with equal outputs of the map you want to test.

**Fast route.** If $f(a)=f(a')$, applying $g$ and using injectivity of the composition gives $a=a'$. For a counterexample to injectivity of $g$, take singleton $A$, two-point $B$, singleton $C$, and constant maps. The composition on a singleton is injective.

**Trap.** A composition tests $g$ only on the image of $f$.
