**Pattern cue.** Separate objects by finite length, then take a countable union.

**Fast route.** Polynomials of degree at most $n$ correspond to $\Q^{n+1}$, a countable set. Union over $n\ge0$, including the zero polynomial, remains countable.

**Trap.** Infinite rational sequences form a different, uncountable collection.
