If $g\circ f=\id_A$ for $f:A\to B$, then $f$ is injective. If $f\circ g=\id_B$, then $f$ is surjective. A two-sided inverse makes $f$ bijective and is unique. These implications need no choice assumption; existence of right inverses for arbitrary surjections does involve choice.
