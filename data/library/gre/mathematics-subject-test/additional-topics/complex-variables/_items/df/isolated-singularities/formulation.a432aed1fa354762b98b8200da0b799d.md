For a function holomorphic on $0<|z-a|<r$, its Laurent series determines the isolated singularity at $a$: no negative-power terms means removable; finitely many negative powers with lowest exponent $-m$ means a pole of order $m$; infinitely many negative-power terms means essential. A branch point does not fit this single-valued punctured-disk classification.
