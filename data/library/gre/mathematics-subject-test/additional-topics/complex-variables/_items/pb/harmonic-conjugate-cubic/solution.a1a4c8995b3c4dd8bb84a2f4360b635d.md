**Answer.** $v(x,y)=3x^2y-y^3+C$.

**Pattern cue.** Recognize the real part of a complex power or use both Cauchy–Riemann equations.

**Fast route.** Expanding $(x+iy)^3$ gives the stated real part and imaginary part $3x^2y-y^3$. Direct checks give $v_y=3x^2-3y^2=u_x$ and $v_x=6xy=-u_y$.

**Trap.** An arbitrary additive constant is permitted, but an arbitrary function is not.
