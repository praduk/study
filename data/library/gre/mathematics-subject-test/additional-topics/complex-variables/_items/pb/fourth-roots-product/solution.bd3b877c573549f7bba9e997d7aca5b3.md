**Answer.** $16$.

**Pattern cue.** Use the constant coefficient or add the polar arguments.

**Fast route.** The roots of $z^4+16$ have product $16$ by the degree-four Vieta relation. Polar form also gives modulus product $2^4=16$ and argument sum $4\pi$, hence $16$.

**Trap.** The right-hand side $-16$ is not the product of the roots.
