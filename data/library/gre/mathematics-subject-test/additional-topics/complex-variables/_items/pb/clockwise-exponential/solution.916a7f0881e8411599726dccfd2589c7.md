**Answer.** $-\pi i$.

**Pattern cue.** Denominator power gives derivative order; orientation supplies the sign.

**Fast route.** The counterclockwise value is $2\pi i(e^z)''|_{z=0}/2!=\pi i$. Clockwise orientation negates it.

**Trap.** A third power in the denominator calls for the second derivative.
