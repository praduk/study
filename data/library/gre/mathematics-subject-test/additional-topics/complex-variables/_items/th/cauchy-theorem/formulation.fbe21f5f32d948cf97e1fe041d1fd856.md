If $f$ is holomorphic on a simply connected domain, its integral around every closed piecewise smooth curve in that domain is zero. In particular, for a simple closed contour it suffices that $f$ be holomorphic on a neighborhood of the contour and its interior. Holomorphicity only along the contour is insufficient.
