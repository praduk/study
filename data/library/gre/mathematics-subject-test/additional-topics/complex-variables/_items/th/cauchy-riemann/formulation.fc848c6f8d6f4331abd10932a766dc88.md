If $f=u+iv$ has continuous first partial derivatives on an open set, then $f$ is holomorphic there exactly when $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$ throughout that set. At a point, complex differentiability implies these equations when the partial derivatives exist. Equations holding only at an isolated point do not prove holomorphicity on a neighborhood.
