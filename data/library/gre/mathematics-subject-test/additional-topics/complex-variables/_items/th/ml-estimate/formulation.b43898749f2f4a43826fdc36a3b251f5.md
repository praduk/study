If $C$ is a piecewise continuously differentiable contour of length $L$, $f$ is continuous on $C$, and $|f(z)|\le M$ there, then $|\int_C f(z)\,dz|\le ML$. Parametrize and apply the triangle inequality. This is an upper bound, not generally the exact integral; the modulus bound must hold along the entire contour.
