A nonconstant holomorphic function on a connected open set cannot attain a local maximum of its modulus at an interior point. In particular, if a function is continuous on a closed disk and holomorphic in its interior, its maximum modulus on that disk is attained on the boundary. This is a statement about $|f|$, not an ordering of complex values.
