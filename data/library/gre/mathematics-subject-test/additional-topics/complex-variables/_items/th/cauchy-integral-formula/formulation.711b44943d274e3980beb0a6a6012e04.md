Let $C$ be a counterclockwise simple closed piecewise smooth curve, $a$ inside it, and $f$ holomorphic on a neighborhood of $C$ and its interior. For every integer $n\ge0$, $\oint_C f(z)/(z-a)^{n+1}\,dz=2\pi i f^{(n)}(a)/n!$. The power of the denominator is one more than the derivative order; clockwise orientation reverses the sign.
