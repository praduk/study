For $z=re^{i\theta}\ne0$ and an integer $n\ge1$, its $n$ distinct $n$th roots are $r^{1/n}e^{i(\theta+2\pi k)/n}$, $0\le k<n$. Powers multiply the argument and raise the modulus; arguments are only defined modulo $2\pi$ unless a branch is chosen. The number zero has only one distinct $n$th root.
