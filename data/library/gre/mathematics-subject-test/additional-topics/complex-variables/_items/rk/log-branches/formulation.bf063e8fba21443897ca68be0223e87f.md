For $k\in\Z$, a logarithm of $z\ne0$ has values $\ln|z|+i(\arg z+2\pi k)$. On a simply connected open domain avoiding zero, one can choose a holomorphic logarithm with derivative $1/z$. There is no single-valued holomorphic logarithm on all of $\C\setminus\{0\}$: integrating its proposed derivative around the unit circle would give $2\pi i$, whereas a derivative of a single-valued primitive integrates to zero.
