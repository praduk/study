For $z=x+iy$, $\conj z=x-iy$ and $|z|^2=z\conj z=x^2+y^2$. If $z\ne0$, $1/z=\conj z/|z|^2$. Conjugation respects sums and products; $|zw|=|z||w|$. For a quotient, multiply numerator and denominator by the denominator's conjugate.
