For chords $AB$ and $CD$ intersecting at an interior point $P$, $PA\cdot PB=PC\cdot PD$. From an exterior point $P$, tangent length $PT$ and secant intersections $A,B$ satisfy $PT^2=PA\cdot PB$, with $PA$ the external segment and $PB$ the whole secant. Two secants have equal external-times-whole products. These are unsigned length formulas in the stated configurations.
