Positive lengths $a,b,c$ form a nondegenerate triangle iff each is less than the sum of the other two. The interior angles sum to $\pi$. In a right triangle the legs and hypotenuse satisfy $a^2+b^2=c^2$. Useful integer triples include $(3,4,5),(5,12,13),(7,24,25),(8,15,17)$; scale all three lengths together. The $45$–$45$–$90$ ratio is $1:1:\sqrt2$, and the $30$–$60$–$90$ ratio is $1:\sqrt3:2$.
