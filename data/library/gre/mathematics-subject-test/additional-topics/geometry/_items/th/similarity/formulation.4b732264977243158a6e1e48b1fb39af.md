Similar figures preserve angles and multiply all corresponding lengths by one factor $k>0$. Perimeters scale by $k$, areas by $k^2$, and volumes by $k^3$. Congruence is the special case $k=1$. Uniform scaling must be distinguished from a general affine transformation, which may scale different directions differently.
