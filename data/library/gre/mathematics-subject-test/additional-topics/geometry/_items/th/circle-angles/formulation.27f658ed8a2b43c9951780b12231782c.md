A radius to a point of tangency is perpendicular to the tangent line. An inscribed angle equals half the measure of its intercepted arc (the arc not containing the angle's vertex). Thus an angle subtending a diameter is right. Inscribed angles standing on the same chord from the same side agree; opposite-side angles can be supplementary.
