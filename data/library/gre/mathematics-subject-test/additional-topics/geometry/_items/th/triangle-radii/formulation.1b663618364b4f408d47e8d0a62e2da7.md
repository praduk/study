For a nondegenerate triangle with area $K$, semiperimeter $s$, sides $a,b,c$, inradius $r$, and circumradius $R$, $K=rs=abc/(4R)$. The first follows by splitting the triangle into three triangles of height $r$. In a right triangle the circumcenter is the hypotenuse midpoint, so $R=c/2$.
