For a Euclidean triangle, $K=bh/2=ab\sin C/2$, where $C$ is the angle between sides $a,b$. With semiperimeter $s=(a+b+c)/2$, Heron's formula is $K=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$. Choose the formula matching the supplied data. Degenerate side triples have zero area and do not satisfy the strict triangle inequalities.
