A circle of radius $r>0$ has circumference $2\pi r$ and disk area $\pi r^2$. For a sector of angle $\theta$ radians, arc length is $r\theta$ and sector area is $r^2\theta/2$. Convert degrees to radians first: $180^\circ=\pi$ radians. For a proper sector with $0<\theta<2\pi$, the perimeter includes two radii as well as its arc.
