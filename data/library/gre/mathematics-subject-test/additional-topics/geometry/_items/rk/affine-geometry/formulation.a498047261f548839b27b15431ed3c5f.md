An invertible affine map $x\mapsto Ax+b$ preserves lines, parallelism, and ratios along a line. It scales planar areas by $|\det A|$ and spatial volumes by $|\det A|$, but need not preserve lengths or angles. Rigid motions preserve lengths and angles; similarities also allow a uniform length factor.
