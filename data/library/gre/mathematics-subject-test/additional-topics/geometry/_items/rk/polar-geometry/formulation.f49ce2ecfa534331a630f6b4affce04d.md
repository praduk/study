Polar coordinates satisfy $x=r\cos\theta,y=r\sin\theta$. The same point can have multiple representations, such as $(r,\theta+2\pi)$ or $(-r,\theta+\pi)$. For a polar region swept once between angles $\alpha,\beta$ with radial extent $0\le r\le R(\theta)$, area is $\tfrac12\int_\alpha^\beta R(\theta)^2\,d\theta$. Avoid counting repeated sweeps twice.
