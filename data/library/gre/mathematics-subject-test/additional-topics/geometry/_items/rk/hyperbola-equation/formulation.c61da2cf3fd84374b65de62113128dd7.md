For $a,b>0$, $x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2=1$ has vertices $(\pm a,0)$, foci $(\pm c,0)$ with $c^2=a^2+b^2$, and asymptotes $y=\pm(b/a)x$. The absolute difference of distances to the two foci is $2a$. The positive squared term sets the transverse axis.
