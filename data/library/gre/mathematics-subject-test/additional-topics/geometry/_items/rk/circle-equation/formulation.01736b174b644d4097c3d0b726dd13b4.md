A circle centered at $(h,k)$ with radius $r>0$ has equation $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$. In $x^2+y^2+Dx+Ey+F=0$, complete squares to get center $(-D/2,-E/2)$ and radius squared $(D^2+E^2)/4-F$. A negative radius squared gives no real points; zero gives a single point.
