For nonzero Euclidean vectors $u,v$, $u\cdot v=\|u\|\|v\|\cos\theta$. Orthogonality is $u\cdot v=0$. Projection onto a nonzero vector $v$ is $(u\cdot v)v/(v\cdot v)$. The denominator is squared length; using an unnormalized vector directly in the directional cosine gives wrong scales.
