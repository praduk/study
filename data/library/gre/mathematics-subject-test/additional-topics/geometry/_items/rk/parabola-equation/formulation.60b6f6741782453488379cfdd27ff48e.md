The parabola $(x-h)^2=4p(y-k)$, $p\ne0$, consists of points equidistant from focus $(h,k+p)$ and directrix $y=k-p$. Its vertex is $(h,k)$; the sign of $p$ sets the opening direction. For a horizontal parabola interchange the coordinate roles.
