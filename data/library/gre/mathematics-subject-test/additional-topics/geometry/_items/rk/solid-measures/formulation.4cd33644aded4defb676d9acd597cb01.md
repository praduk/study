A prism or cylinder has volume $Bh$ for base area $B$ and perpendicular height $h$; a pyramid or cone has volume $Bh/3$. A sphere has volume $4\pi r^3/3$ and surface area $4\pi r^2$. A right circular cylinder has total surface area $2\pi rh+2\pi r^2$; a right circular cone has lateral area $\pi r\ell$ and total area $\pi r\ell+\pi r^2$, where $\ell=\sqrt{r^2+h^2}$ is slant height.
