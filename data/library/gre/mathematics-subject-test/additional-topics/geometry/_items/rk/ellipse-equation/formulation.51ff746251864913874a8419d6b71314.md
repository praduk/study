For $a\ge b>0$, $x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2=1$ is an ellipse with semiaxes $a,b$, foci $(\pm c,0)$ where $c^2=a^2-b^2$, and constant sum of distances to the foci $2a$. If $a=b$, it is a circle and the foci coincide. Translations shift the center and both foci.
