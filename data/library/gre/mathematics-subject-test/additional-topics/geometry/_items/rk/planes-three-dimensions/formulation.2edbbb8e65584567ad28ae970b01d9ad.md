A plane through $\mathbf p$ with nonzero normal $\mathbf n$ is $\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf x-\mathbf p)=0$. In coordinates, $Ax+By+Cz+D=0$ has normal $(A,B,C)\ne(0,0,0)$. The distance from $Q=(x_0,y_0,z_0)$ is

$$
\frac{|Ax_0+By_0+Cz_0+D|}{\sqrt{A^2+B^2+C^2}}.
$$

For three noncollinear points $P,Q,R$, a normal is $(Q-P)\times(R-P)$. To find the perpendicular foot of a point $\mathbf q$, subtract $[\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf q-\mathbf p)/\|\mathbf n\|^2]\mathbf n$. The distance denominator is normal length; the projection coefficient uses its squared length.
