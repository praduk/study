For two planar vectors $u,v$, triangle area is $|\det(u,v)|/2$. In $\R^3$, $\|u\times v\|$ is parallelogram area, and $|u\cdot(v\times w)|$ is parallelepiped volume. The determinant can be signed, but ordinary area and volume are nonnegative. A zero triple product means the three vectors are linearly dependent.
