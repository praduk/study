**Fast route.** $x^2+(y-2)^2=(y+2)^2$ simplifies to $x^2=8y$. Both original distances are nonnegative, so squaring introduces no extra branch here.

**Trap.** Distance to the horizontal directrix is $|y+2|$, not $y-2$.
