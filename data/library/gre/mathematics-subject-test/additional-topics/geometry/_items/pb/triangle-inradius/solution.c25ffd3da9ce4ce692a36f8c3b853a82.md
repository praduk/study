**Correct choice: (B).**

**Fastest valid route.** The semiperimeter is $s=21$. Heron's formula gives

$$
K=\sqrt{21(21-13)(21-14)(21-15)}
=\sqrt{21\cdot8\cdot7\cdot6}=84.
$$

Since $K=rs$, the inradius is $r=84/21=4$.

**Verification.** The product under the square root is $7056=84^2$. Also, decomposing the triangle into three smaller triangles with common height $r$ gives

$$
K=\frac12r(13+14+15)=21r,
$$

so $84=21r$ again yields $r=4$.
