**Answer.** $\pi/2$.

**Pattern cue.** Integrate one-half the squared radial bound.

**Fast route.** $K=\tfrac12\int_0^{\pi/2}4\cos^2\theta\,d\theta=2(\pi/4)=\pi/2$. Geometrically this is the upper half of a unit disk centered at $(1,0)$.

**Trap.** The polar area factor is one-half; the range sweeps only half the disk.
