**Answer.** Yes, at $(3,2,0)$.

**Pattern cue.** Convert point data to a direction vector, then solve coordinates in a convenient order.

**Fast route.** The direction $Q-P=(1,1,-1)$ gives $L=(1,0,2)+t(1,1,-1)$. Equality of $x$ coordinates forces $1+t=3$, so $t=2$. Equality of $y$ coordinates gives $2=-1+3s$, so $s=1$. The $z$ coordinates are both zero, verifying the intersection. Nonproportional directions make it the only intersection.

**Trap.** Solving only two of the three coordinate equations does not certify an intersection in space.
