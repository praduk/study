**Answer.** $6x+3y+2z=6$, equivalently $x+y/2+z/3=1$.

**Pattern cue.** Use intercepts directly, or form a normal from two edge vectors.

**Fast route.** The intercept form gives $x/1+y/2+z/3=1$. To verify without that shortcut, the differences $(-1,2,0)$ and $(-1,0,3)$ have cross product $(6,3,2)$; using the first point gives $6(x-1)+3y+2z=0$. Each supplied point satisfies the result, and the nonzero cross product proves noncollinearity.

**Trap.** Three collinear points would not determine a unique plane.
