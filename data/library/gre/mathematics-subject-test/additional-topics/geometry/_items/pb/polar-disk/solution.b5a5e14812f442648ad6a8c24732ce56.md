**Pattern cue.** Multiply by $r$ before converting to Cartesian coordinates.

**Fast route.** $r^2=2r\cos\theta$ gives $x^2+y^2=2x$; complete the square. The origin is included.

**Trap.** Dividing by $r$ in reverse may hide the origin.
