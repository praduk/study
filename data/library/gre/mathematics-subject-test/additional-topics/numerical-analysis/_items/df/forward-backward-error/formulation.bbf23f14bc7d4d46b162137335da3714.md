Forward error measures the difference between a computed answer and the exact answer for the stated input. Backward error measures an input change that makes the computed answer exact. For $Ax=b$ with fixed $A$, the residual $r=A\widehat x-b$ is the perturbation to the right-hand side making $A\widehat x=b+r$ exact. Small residual or backward error need not imply small forward error.
