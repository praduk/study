**Answer.** $x_1=2$; $x_{n+1}-1=(x_n-1)/2$ away from the root.

**Pattern cue.** A multiple root changes Newton’s error reduction.

**Fast route.** $x_{n+1}=x_n-(x_n-1)^2/[2(x_n-1)]=(x_n+1)/2$. The error halves rather than squares.

**Trap.** The usual quadratic-convergence statement assumes a simple root.
