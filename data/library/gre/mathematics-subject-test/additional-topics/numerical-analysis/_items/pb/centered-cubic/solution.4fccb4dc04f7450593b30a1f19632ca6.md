**Answer.** $3x^2+h^2$; error $h^2$.

**Pattern cue.** Expand symmetrically to cancel even powers in the numerator.

**Fast route.** $(x+h)^3-(x-h)^3=6x^2h+2h^3$. Divide by $2h$ and subtract $3x^2$.

**Trap.** Centered differencing is exact for quadratics, not for all smooth functions.
