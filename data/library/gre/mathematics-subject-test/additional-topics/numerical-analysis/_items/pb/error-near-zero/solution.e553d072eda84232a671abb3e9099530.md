**Answer.** The first has larger absolute error; the second has larger relative error.

**Pattern cue.** Compute both measures before comparing.

**Fast route.** The absolute errors are $1$ and $0.001$. Relative errors are $1/100=0.01$ and $0.001/0.001=1$.

**Trap.** An absolute-error comparison can reverse after normalization.
