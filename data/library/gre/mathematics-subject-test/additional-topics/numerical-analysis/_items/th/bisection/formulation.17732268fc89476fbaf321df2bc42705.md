If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and $f(a)f(b)<0$, a root lies inside. Retaining the sign-changing half at each step gives bracket length $(b-a)/2^n$ after $n$ bisections. The midpoint of that final bracket has error at most $(b-a)/2^{n+1}$ relative to a root in it. Endpoint zeros can be handled immediately.
