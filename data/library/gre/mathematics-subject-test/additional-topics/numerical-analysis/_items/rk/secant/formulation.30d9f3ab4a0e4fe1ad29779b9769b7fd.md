The secant update is $x_{n+1}=x_n-f(x_n)(x_n-x_{n-1})/[f(x_n)-f(x_{n-1})]$, provided the denominator is nonzero. It replaces the Newton derivative by a divided difference. For $f\in C^2$ near a simple root and sufficiently close distinct starts for which the iterates are defined, convergence is locally superlinear; there is no general bracket-preservation guarantee.
