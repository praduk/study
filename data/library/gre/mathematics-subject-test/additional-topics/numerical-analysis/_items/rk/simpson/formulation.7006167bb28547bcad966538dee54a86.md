One-panel Simpson quadrature is $S=(b-a)[f(a)+4f((a+b)/2)+f(b)]/6$. It is exact for polynomials through degree three. Composite Simpson uses equally spaced nodes and an even number of subintervals; with suitable fourth-derivative bounds its global error is $O(h^4)$. Exactness for cubics does not imply exactness for quartics.
