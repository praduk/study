- **(C)** Dijkstra's algorithm is guaranteed to find shortest paths even when some edge weights are negative.
- **(D)** Kruskal's algorithm produces a shortest-path tree rooted at $s$.
- **(E)** Breadth-first search must take $\Theta(n^2)$ time regardless of the number of edges.
