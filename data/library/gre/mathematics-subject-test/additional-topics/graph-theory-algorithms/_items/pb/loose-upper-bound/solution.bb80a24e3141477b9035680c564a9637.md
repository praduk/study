**Pattern cue.** An upper bound can be loose.

**Fast route.** For $n\ge1$, $3n+7\le10n^2$, proving big-O. But $T(n)/n^2=3/n+7/n^2\to0$, ruling out a positive constant lower bound; the tight order is $\Theta(n)$.

**Trap.** Big-O does not mean equality of asymptotic growth.
