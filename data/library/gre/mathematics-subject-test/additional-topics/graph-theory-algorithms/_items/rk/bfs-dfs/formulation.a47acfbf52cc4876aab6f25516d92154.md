Breadth-first search (BFS) in an unweighted graph finds minimum-edge distances from a source by exploring distance layers. Depth-first search (DFS) explores along branches and supports component and cycle detection, but its tree paths need not be shortest. With adjacency lists both take $O(V+E)$ time; scanning an adjacency matrix can require $\Theta(V^2)$.
