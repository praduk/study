For a simple planar graph with $V\ge3$, $E\le3V-6$. If it is also bipartite, $E\le2V-4$. Face-length counts give these bounds: edges contribute twice to face boundaries, and relevant bounded cycle lengths are at least three or four. These inequalities can disprove planarity but cannot certify it.
