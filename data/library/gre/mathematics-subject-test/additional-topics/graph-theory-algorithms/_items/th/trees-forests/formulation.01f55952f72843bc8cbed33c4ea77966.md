A tree is a connected acyclic undirected graph. For a finite graph on $n\ge1$ vertices, being a tree is equivalent to connectedness plus $n-1$ edges, and to having a unique simple path between every two vertices. A forest with $n$ vertices and $c$ components has $n-c$ edges. Adding an edge between two existing vertices of a tree creates exactly one cycle.
