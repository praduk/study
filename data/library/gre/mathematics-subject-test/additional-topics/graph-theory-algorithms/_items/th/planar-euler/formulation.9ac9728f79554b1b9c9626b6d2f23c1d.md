For a finite planar embedding with $c$ connected components, $V-E+F=1+c$, where $F$ includes the unbounded face. For a connected graph this becomes $V-E+F=2$. The formula is about an actual embedding; satisfying the numerical identity does not prove that a given graph is planar.
