A graph is bipartite iff it has no odd cycle. A bipartition separates vertices into two classes with every edge crossing between classes. Color a component by even/odd distance from a root; a conflict reveals an odd cycle. In $K_{a,b}$ all $ab$ crossing pairs are edges and there are no edges inside a part.
