For a finite undirected graph, $\sum_v\deg(v)=2|E|$, counting a loop twice in degree. Consequently the number of odd-degree vertices is even. In a simple graph each vertex degree is at most $|V|-1$. The complete graph $K_n$ has $\binom n2$ edges; the complete bipartite graph $K_{a,b}$ has $ab$.
