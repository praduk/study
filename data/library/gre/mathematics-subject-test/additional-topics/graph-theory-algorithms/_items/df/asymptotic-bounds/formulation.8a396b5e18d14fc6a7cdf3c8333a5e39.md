For eventually nonnegative functions, $f=O(g)$ means $f(n)\le Cg(n)$ for all sufficiently large $n$ and some constant $C>0$. $f=\Omega(g)$ reverses the inequality up to a positive constant; $f=\Theta(g)$ means both. A valid upper bound need not be tight. Typical growth order is $1,\log n,n,n\log n,n^2,n^3,2^n,n!$.
