For a finite-second-moment variable, $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[(X-E[X])^2]=E[X^2]-E[X]^2$. Also $\operatorname{Var}(aX+b)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(X)$: translations do not change spread and scaling is squared. Variance is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when the variable is almost surely constant.
