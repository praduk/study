For an integrable discrete random variable, $E[X]=\sum_xxP(X=x)$; with density $f$, use $\int xf(x)\,dx$. Linearity $E[aX+bY+c]=aE[X]+bE[Y]+c$ requires finite expectations but not independence. Indicator variables satisfy $E[\mathbf1_A]=P(A)$. In general $E[g(X)]\ne g(E[X])$.
