For a finite partition $A_1,\ldots,A_k$ with positive probabilities and $P(B)>0$, $P(B)=\sum_iP(B\mid A_i)P(A_i)$ and $P(A_j\mid B)=P(B\mid A_j)P(A_j)/P(B)$. This reverses conditioning by combining the likelihood with the prior frequency; $P(B\mid A)$ and $P(A\mid B)$ need not be close.
