**Answer.** Not independent; covariance zero.

**Pattern cue.** Symmetry can cancel covariance despite deterministic dependence.

**Fast route.** $E[X]=0$ and $E[XY]=E[X^3]=0$, so covariance is zero. Yet $P(Y=0\mid X=0)=1$ while $P(Y=0)=1/3$. Both variances are positive.

**Trap.** Zero covariance rules out linear association, not all dependence.
