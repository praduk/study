**Answer.** $n\ge400$.

**Pattern cue.** Apply the variance bound to the sample mean.

**Fast route.** The probability is at most $4/(n\cdot1^2)$. Requiring this to be at most $1/100$ gives $n\ge400$.

**Trap.** This is a conservative sufficient guarantee, not necessarily the smallest actual sample size.
