**Pattern cue.** Distinguish a probability conditional on the null from a posterior probability of the null.

**Fast route.** The p-value is the null-model probability of a statistic at least as extreme as observed under the test’s rule. It is not $P(H_0\mid\text{data})$.

**Trap.** Reversing the conditioning requires a different model and, in a Bayesian analysis, a prior.
