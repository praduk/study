If $X_1,\ldots,X_n$ are iid with finite mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$, then $\overline X=n^{-1}\sum_iX_i$ has $E[\overline X]=\mu$, variance $\sigma^2/n$, and standard deviation (standard error) $\sigma/\sqrt n$. A statistic is computed from the sample; the parameter describes the population. Independence matters for the variance formula.
