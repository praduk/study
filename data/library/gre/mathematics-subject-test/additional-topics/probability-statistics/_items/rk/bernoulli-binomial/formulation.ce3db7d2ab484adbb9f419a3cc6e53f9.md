A Bernoulli variable equals one with probability $p$ and zero otherwise; its mean is $p$ and variance $p(1-p)$. The sum of $n$ independent Bernoulli variables with the same $p$ is binomial: $P(X=k)=\binom nkp^k(1-p)^{n-k}$, $E[X]=np$, $\operatorname{Var}(X)=np(1-p)$. Fixed trial count, independence, and a common success probability are the model assumptions.
