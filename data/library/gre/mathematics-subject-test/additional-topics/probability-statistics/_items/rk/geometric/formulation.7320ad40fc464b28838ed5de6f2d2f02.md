For independent trials with constant success probability $0<p\le1$, let $X$ count trials through the first success, so $X\in\{1,2,\ldots\}$. Then $P(X=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p$, $P(X>k)=(1-p)^k$, $E[X]=1/p$, and $\operatorname{Var}(X)=(1-p)/p^2$. Counting failures instead shifts the support and mean by one.
