For iid data with finite variance and $n\ge2$, the sample variance $s^2=\sum_i(X_i-\overline X)^2/(n-1)$ is unbiased for the population variance. The descriptive population variance of an entire listed population instead divides by its size $n$. The loss of one degree of freedom comes from deviations summing to zero after estimating the mean.
