For $X\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda)$, $\lambda>0$, $P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$ for $k=0,1,\ldots$, and $E[X]=\operatorname{Var}(X)=\lambda$. Independent Poisson variables add to a Poisson variable with the sum of their parameters. A Poisson distribution is a model to justify, not a consequence of the word “random.”
