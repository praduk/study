For $X$ uniform on $[a,b]$ with $a<b$, density is $1/(b-a)$ on that interval, mean $(a+b)/2$, and variance $(b-a)^2/12$. A contained interval has probability equal to its length divided by $b-a$. These formulas describe a uniform density in the stated coordinate; nonlinear transformations generally are not uniform.
