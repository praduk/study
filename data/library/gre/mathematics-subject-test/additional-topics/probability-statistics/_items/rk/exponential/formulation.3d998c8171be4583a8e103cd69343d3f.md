For rate $\lambda>0$, an exponential variable has density $\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$ for $x\ge0$, tail $P(X>t)=e^{-\lambda t}$ for $t\ge0$, mean $1/\lambda$, and variance $1/\lambda^2$. For $s,t\ge0$, its memoryless identity is $P(X>s+t\mid X>s)=e^{-\lambda t}$. A rate is the reciprocal of the mean waiting time.
