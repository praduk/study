The arithmetic mean is the sum divided by sample size. The median is the middle order statistic, or the average of the middle two under the usual even-size convention. A single very large observation can move the mean arbitrarily, whereas the median is resistant to a minority of extreme replacements. Resistance is not immunity to changes in many observations.
