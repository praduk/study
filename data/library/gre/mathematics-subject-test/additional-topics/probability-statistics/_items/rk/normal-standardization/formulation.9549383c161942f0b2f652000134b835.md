If $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ with $\sigma>0$, then $Z=(X-\mu)/\sigma\sim N(0,1)$. Write probabilities using the standard normal CDF $\Phi$: $P(X\le x)=\Phi((x-\mu)/\sigma)$. The standard deviation is $\sigma$, not $\sigma^2$. Symmetry gives $\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z)$.
