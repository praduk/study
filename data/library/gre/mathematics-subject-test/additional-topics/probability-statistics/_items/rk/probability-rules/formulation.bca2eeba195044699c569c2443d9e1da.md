For events $A,B$, $P(A^c)=1-P(A)$ and $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$. For finite equally likely outcomes, probability is favorable count divided by total count after fixing one consistent sample space. “At least one” often becomes simpler as one minus none.
