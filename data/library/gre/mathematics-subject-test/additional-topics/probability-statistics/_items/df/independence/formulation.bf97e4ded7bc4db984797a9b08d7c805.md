Events $A,B$ are independent iff $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$. Disjoint positive-probability events are not independent. Mutual independence of a family requires the product rule for every finite subfamily; pairwise independence only checks pairs and need not suffice. Independence of random variables implies zero covariance when second moments exist, but the converse generally fails.
