For positive finite variances, $\rho=\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)/(\sigma_X\sigma_Y)$ lies in $[-1,1]$. It measures linear association, not causation. Zero correlation need not mean independence, and correlation is undefined when either variance is zero. Multiplying one variable by a negative constant reverses the correlation sign; nonzero shifts do not affect it.
