For a real or complex series $\sum a_n$, let $L=\limsup |a_n|^{1/n}$. If $L<1$, the series converges absolutely; if $L>1$, it diverges; $L=1$ is inconclusive. The same trichotomy holds when the limit of $|a_{n+1}/a_n|$ exists and the ratios are eventually defined. Factorials favor the ratio test, and $n$th powers favor the root test.
