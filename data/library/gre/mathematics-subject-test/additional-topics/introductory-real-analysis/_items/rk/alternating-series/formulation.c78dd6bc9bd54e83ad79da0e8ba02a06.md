If $b_n\ge0$ decreases to zero, $\sum_{n=1}^\infty(-1)^{n-1}b_n$ converges and the error after $N$ terms has magnitude at most $b_{N+1}$. Convergence is conditional when the absolute-value series diverges. Absolute convergence always implies convergence, while alternating signs without decreasing magnitudes tending to zero are not enough for this test.
