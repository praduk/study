A power series $\sum a_n(x-c)^n$ converges absolutely for $|x-c|<R$ and diverges for $|x-c|>R$, where $1/R=\limsup|a_n|^{1/n}$ with the usual zero/infinity conventions. At each real endpoint, substitute it and test the resulting numerical series separately. The two endpoints can behave differently.
