**Answer.** Infimum zero, attained at $n=1$; supremum one, not attained.

**Pattern cue.** Separate a least upper bound from membership.

**Fast route.** All terms lie below one and approach it, so one is the supremum. The first term is zero and all subsequent terms are nonnegative.

**Trap.** A supremum need not be a maximum.
