**Answer.** Pointwise limit zero; every integral equals one.

**Pattern cue.** A shrinking spike can carry fixed area.

**Fast route.** For every fixed positive $x$, eventually $1/n<x$, and at zero the value is always zero. But height times width is $n(1/n)=1$, so limit and integral cannot be interchanged.

**Trap.** Pointwise convergence alone does not control mass concentrated in small intervals.
