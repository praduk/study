**Pattern cue.** Check even and odd index subsequences.

**Fast route.** Even terms are one and odd terms minus one. Any convergent subsequence must eventually lie near one of these separated values, and both constant subsequences occur.

**Trap.** Boundedness guarantees a convergent subsequence, not convergence of all terms.
