**Answer.** Uniform limit zero; $f_n'(0)=1$ does not converge to its derivative zero.

**Pattern cue.** Differentiate after checking the size of the original functions.

**Fast route.** $\sup|f_n|\le1/n$, but $f_n'(x)=\cos(nx)$, so every derivative value at zero is one.

**Trap.** Uniform convergence of the functions alone does not permit differentiation of the limit.
