**Answer.** Continuous but not differentiable.

**Pattern cue.** Compare the two one-sided difference quotients.

**Fast route.** $|x|\to0$, so it is continuous. The quotient $|h|/h$ is one for $h>0$ and minus one for $h<0$, so the derivative does not exist.

**Trap.** Continuity does not eliminate a corner.
