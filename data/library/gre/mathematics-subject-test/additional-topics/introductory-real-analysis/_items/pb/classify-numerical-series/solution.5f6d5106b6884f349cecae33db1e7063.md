**Correct choice: (C).**

**Fastest valid route.** The alternating harmonic series converges by the alternating-series test, but its absolute-value series is the divergent harmonic series. That is exactly conditional convergence.

**Verification.** In (C), the magnitudes $1/n$ decrease to $0$, so $\sum(-1)^{n-1}/n$ converges. However,

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\left|\frac{(-1)^{n-1}}{n}\right|
=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac1n
$$

diverges. Choices (A), (D), and (E) converge absolutely; (E) also telescopes because $1/[n(n+1)]=1/n-1/(n+1)$. Choice (B) diverges.
