**Answer.** Yes on $[0,1/2]$; no on $[0,1)$.

**Pattern cue.** A geometric majorant works only away from the endpoint.

**Fast route.** On the smaller interval use $|x^n|\le2^{-n}$. On $[0,1)$, uniform convergence of a series would require its terms to tend uniformly to zero, but $\sup_{x<1}x^n=1$.

**Trap.** Pointwise convergence at every point of an open interval need not be uniform.
