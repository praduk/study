A set $U\subseteq\R^d$ is open iff every point of $U$ has an open ball contained in $U$. A set is closed iff it contains the limits of every convergent sequence of its points, with limits taken in $\R^d$. A set can be both open and closed, or neither; “not open” is not the definition of closed.
