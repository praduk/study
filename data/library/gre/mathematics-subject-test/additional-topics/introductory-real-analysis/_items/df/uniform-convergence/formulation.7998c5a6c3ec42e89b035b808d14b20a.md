Pointwise convergence fixes $x$ and then allows the required index $N$ to depend on $x$. Uniform convergence requires one $N$ to work for all $x$ in the domain; equivalently $\sup_x|f_n(x)-f(x)|\to0$. The supremum need not be attained. Changing the domain can change uniformity without changing the pointwise formula.
