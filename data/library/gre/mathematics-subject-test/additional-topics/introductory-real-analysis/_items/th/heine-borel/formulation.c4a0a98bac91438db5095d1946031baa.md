A subset of $\R^d$ is compact iff it is closed and bounded. Continuous images of compact sets are compact. This equivalence uses finite-dimensional Euclidean space; general metric spaces need completeness and total boundedness instead. Bounded open intervals are not compact, and closed unbounded sets are not compact.
