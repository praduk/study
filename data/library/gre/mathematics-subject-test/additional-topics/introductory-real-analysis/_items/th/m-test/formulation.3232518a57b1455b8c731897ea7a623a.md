If $|f_n(x)|\le M_n$ for all $x\in E$, $M_n\ge0$, and $\sum M_n$ converges, then $\sum f_n$ converges uniformly and absolutely on $E$. The tail is bounded independently of $x$ by $\sum_{n>N}M_n$. A divergent proposed majorant does not prove that the function series diverges; it only makes that bound inconclusive.
