A continuous real-valued function on a nonempty compact space attains a maximum and minimum. On a compact metric domain, a continuous function is uniformly continuous: one input tolerance works at every point. The compactness assumption cannot simply be dropped; continuous functions on open or unbounded domains can fail either property.
