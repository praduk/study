If Riemann-integrable functions $f_n$ on a finite interval $[a,b]$ converge uniformly to $f$, then $f$ is Riemann integrable and $\int_a^bf=\lim_n\int_a^bf_n$. The bound $|\int_a^b(f_n-f)|\le(b-a)\sup|f_n-f|$ controls the difference. Pointwise convergence alone does not justify this interchange.
