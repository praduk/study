A bounded nondecreasing real sequence converges to the supremum of its terms; a bounded nonincreasing sequence converges to their infimum. Every bounded sequence in $\R^d$ has a convergent subsequence (Bolzano–Weierstrass), but the entire sequence may diverge. An increasing sequence bounded above needs no explicit closed form to establish convergence.
