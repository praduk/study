If $f$ is differentiable at $(a,b)$, its linear approximation is

$$
L(x,y)=f(a,b)+f_x(a,b)(x-a)+f_y(a,b)(y-b).
$$

Near $(a,b)$, $f(x,y)\approx L(x,y)$. The tangent plane to the graph is $z=L(x,y)$.

**Memory cue (added).** Base height plus slope times each displacement.
