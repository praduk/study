Let $F$ be continuously differentiable near $p=(a,b,c)$, with $F(p)=k$ and $\nabla F(p)\ne0$. The tangent plane to $F(x,y,z)=k$ at $p$ is

$$
\nabla F(p)\cdot\langle x-a,y-b,z-c\rangle=0.
$$

The level $k$ and the third coordinate $c$ are different pieces of data.

**Memory cue (added).** Gradient is normal; displacement is tangent.
