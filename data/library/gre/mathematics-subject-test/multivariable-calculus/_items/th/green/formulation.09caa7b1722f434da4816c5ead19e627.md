Let $C$ be a positively oriented (counterclockwise), simple, closed, piecewise smooth boundary of a planar region $D$. If $P,Q$ are $C^1$ on an open set containing $D$ and its boundary, then

$$
\oint_C P\,dx+Q\,dy
=\iint_D(Q_x-P_y)\,dA.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Go counterclockwise; Q-x minus P-y.
