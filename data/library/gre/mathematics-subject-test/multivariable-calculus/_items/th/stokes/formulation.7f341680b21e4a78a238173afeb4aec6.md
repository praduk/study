For a compact oriented piecewise smooth surface $S$ with consistently oriented boundary $\partial S$, and a $C^1$ vector field $F$ on a neighborhood of $S$,

$$
\oint_{\partial S}F\cdot dr
=\iint_S(\nabla\times F)\cdot n\,dS.
$$

The unit normal $n$ and boundary direction obey the right-hand rule.

**Memory cue (added).** Boundary circulation equals surface curl.
