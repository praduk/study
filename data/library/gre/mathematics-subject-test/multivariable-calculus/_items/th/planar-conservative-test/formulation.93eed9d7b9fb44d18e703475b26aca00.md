If $P,Q$ are $C^1$ on an open simply connected region in $\R^2$, then $F=\langle P,Q\rangle$ is conservative exactly when

$$
P_y=Q_x
$$

throughout the region. Without the domain condition, the equality alone need not give a global potential.

**Memory cue (added).** Cross partials match, and the domain has no holes.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 14.

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** On a simply connected plane domain, matching cross-partials suggest a potential; check $C^1$ regularity.
