For a bounded solid $E$ with piecewise smooth closed boundary and a $C^1$ vector field $F$ on a neighborhood of $E$ and its boundary,

$$
\iint_{\partial E}F\cdot n\,dS
=\iiint_E\nabla\cdot F\,dV,
$$

where $n$ is the **outward** unit normal.

**Memory cue (added).** Outward flux equals divergence inside.
