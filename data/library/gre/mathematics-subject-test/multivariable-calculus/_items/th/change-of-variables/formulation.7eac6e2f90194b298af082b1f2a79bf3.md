For a one-to-one $C^1$ coordinate change $T$ with nonzero Jacobian on the interior, carrying a region $U$ to $D$ (boundary exceptions of area zero allowed), and integrable $f$ on $D$,

$$
\iint_D f(x,y)\,dA
=\iint_U f(T(u,v))
\left|\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right|\,du\,dv.
$$

Use the transformed region and the **absolute value** of the Jacobian.

**Memory cue (added).** Change the function, the region, and the area scale.
