Suppose $f,g$ are $C^1$ near a constrained local extremum $p$ on $g=c$, and $\nabla g(p)\ne0$. Then for some real $\lambda$,

$$
\nabla f(p)=\lambda\nabla g(p),
\qquad g(p)=c.
$$

These equations find candidates, not automatic extrema; singular constraint points require separate attention.

**Memory cue (added).** At a regular constrained extremum, the gradients line up.
