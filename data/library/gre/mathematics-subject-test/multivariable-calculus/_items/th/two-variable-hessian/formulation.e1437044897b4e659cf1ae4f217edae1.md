Let $f$ be $C^2$ near a point $p$ with $\nabla f(p)=0$. Evaluate at $p$:

$$
D=f_{xx}f_{yy}-(f_{xy})^2.
$$

- $D>0$ and $f_{xx}>0$: strict local minimum.
- $D>0$ and $f_{xx}<0$: strict local maximum.
- $D<0$: saddle point.
- $D=0$: inconclusive.

**Memory cue (added).** Positive determinant: the first diagonal chooses up or down. Negative: saddle. Zero: no verdict.
