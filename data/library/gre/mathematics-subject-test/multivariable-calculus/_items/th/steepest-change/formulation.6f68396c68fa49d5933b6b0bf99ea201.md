If $f$ is differentiable at $a$ and $\nabla f(a)\ne0$, the maximum directional derivative over unit vectors is $|\nabla f(a)|$, attained at

$$
u=\frac{\nabla f(a)}{|\nabla f(a)|}.
$$

The opposite direction gives $-|\nabla f(a)|$, the minimum. Thus the greatest **magnitude** occurs in both directions. If the gradient is zero, every directional derivative is zero.

**Memory cue (added).** Uphill follows the gradient; downhill reverses it.
