For $C^1$ functions $f,g,h$, suppose $p$ is a constrained local extremum on $g=c,\ h=d$ and $\nabla g(p),\nabla h(p)$ are linearly independent. Then

$$
\nabla f(p)=\lambda\nabla g(p)+\mu\nabla h(p)
$$

for some $\lambda,\mu$. Include both constraint equations.

**Memory cue (added).** One normal multiplier per independent constraint.
