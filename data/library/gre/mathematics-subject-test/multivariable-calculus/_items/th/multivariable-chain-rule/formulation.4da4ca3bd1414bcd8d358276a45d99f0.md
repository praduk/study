If $z=f(x,y)$ is differentiable and $x=x(t),y=y(t)$ are differentiable, then

$$
\frac{dz}{dt}=f_x\frac{dx}{dt}+f_y\frac{dy}{dt}.
$$

If $x=x(s,t),y=y(s,t)$, hold $t$ fixed to obtain

$$
\frac{\partial z}{\partial s}
=f_x\frac{\partial x}{\partial s}
+f_y\frac{\partial y}{\partial s}.
$$

Evaluate $f_x,f_y$ at the inner point $(x,y)$.

**Memory cue (added).** Add the products along every dependency path.
