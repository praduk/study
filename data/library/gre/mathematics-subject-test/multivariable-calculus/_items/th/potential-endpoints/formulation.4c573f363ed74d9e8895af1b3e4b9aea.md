If $F=\nabla f$ with $f$ continuously differentiable along an oriented piecewise smooth curve from $A$ to $B$, then

$$
\int_C F\cdot dr=f(B)-f(A).
$$

The value depends only on the endpoints.

**Memory cue (added).** Potential at finish minus potential at start.
