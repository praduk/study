With $r\ge0$ and angle $\theta$ measured from the positive $x$-axis,

$$
x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta,\qquad dA=r\,dr\,d\theta.
$$

Choose bounds covering the intended region once, apart from boundary duplication.
