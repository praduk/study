Use $\rho\ge0$, azimuth $\theta$ in the $xy$-plane, and polar angle $0\le\phi\le\pi$ measured from the positive $z$-axis:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x&=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\\
y&=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\\
z&=\rho\cos\phi.
\end{aligned}
$$

The volume element is

$$
dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Two radius factors; sine of the angle from the pole.
