Cylindrical coordinates use polar coordinates in the $xy$-plane:

$$
x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta,\qquad z=z,
$$

with $r\ge0$ and

$$
dV=r\,dr\,d\theta\,dz.
$$

Integration order may be rearranged with corresponding bounds.

**Memory cue (added).** A polar slice stacked in height keeps the factor r.
