A partial derivative differentiates one coordinate while holding the others fixed. For differentiable $f:\R^3\to\R$,

$$
\nabla f=\left\langle\frac{\partial f}{\partial x},
\frac{\partial f}{\partial y},\frac{\partial f}{\partial z}\right\rangle.
$$

For a function of two variables, use only the first two components.

**Memory cue (added).** Gradient gathers the coordinate slopes.
