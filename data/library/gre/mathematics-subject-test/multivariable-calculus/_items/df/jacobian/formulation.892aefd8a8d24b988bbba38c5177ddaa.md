For a differentiable change $(x,y)=T(u,v)$, the Jacobian determinant is

$$
\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}
=\begin{vmatrix}
x_u&x_v\\
y_u&y_v
\end{vmatrix}
=x_u y_v-x_v y_u.
$$

Outputs label rows; inputs label columns.

**Memory cue (added).** Rows are outputs, columns are inputs; cross products subtract.
