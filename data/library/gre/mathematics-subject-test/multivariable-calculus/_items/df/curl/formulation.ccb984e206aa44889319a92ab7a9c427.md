For a differentiable vector field $F=\langle P,Q,R\rangle$,

$$
\nabla\times F
=\langle R_y-Q_z,\ P_z-R_x,\ Q_x-P_y\rangle.
$$

Curl is a vector. Its determinant mnemonic is

$$
\begin{vmatrix}
\mathbf i&\mathbf j&\mathbf k\\
\partial_x&\partial_y&\partial_z\\
P&Q&R
\end{vmatrix}.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Expand across i, j, k with signs plus, minus, plus.
