If $f=u+iv$ is holomorphic, then both real-valued functions $u,v$ are harmonic:

$$
u_{xx}+u_{yy}=0,\qquad v_{xx}+v_{yy}=0.
$$

Here $v$ is a harmonic conjugate of $u$ with the sign convention $u+iv$ holomorphic.

**Memory cue (added).** Holomorphic splits into two zero-Laplacian components.
