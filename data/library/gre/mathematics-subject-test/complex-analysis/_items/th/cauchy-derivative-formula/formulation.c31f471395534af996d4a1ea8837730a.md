Let $C$ be a positively oriented simple closed piecewise smooth contour, let $f$ be holomorphic on an open neighborhood of $C$ and its interior, and let $a$ lie inside $C$. For every integer $n\ge0$,

$$
f^{(n)}(a)=\frac{n!}{2\pi i}\int_C\frac{f(z)}{(z-a)^{n+1}}\,dz.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Derivative order $n$: factorial $n!$, denominator power $n+1$.
