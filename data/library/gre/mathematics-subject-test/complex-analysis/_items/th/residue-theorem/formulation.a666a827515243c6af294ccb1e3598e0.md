Let $C$ be a positively oriented simple closed piecewise smooth contour. Suppose $f$ is holomorphic on a neighborhood of $C$ and its interior except at finitely many isolated singularities $z_k$ strictly inside. Then

$$
\int_C f(z)\,dz=2\pi i\sum_k\operatorname{Res}(f,z_k).
$$

No singularity may lie on $C$.

**Memory cue (added).** Inside residues only; add them, then multiply by $2\pi i$.
