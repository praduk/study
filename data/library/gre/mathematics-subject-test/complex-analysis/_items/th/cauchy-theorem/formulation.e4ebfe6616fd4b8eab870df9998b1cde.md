If $f$ is holomorphic on a simply connected open domain $D$, then $\int_C f(z)\,dz=0$ for every closed piecewise smooth contour $C$ in $D$.

**Memory cue (added).** Holomorphic plus no holes makes closed integrals zero.

*Source: StudySheet.pdf, p. 25.*

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** For a closed contour, first check whether holomorphy gives Cauchy’s theorem or interior singularities require residues.
