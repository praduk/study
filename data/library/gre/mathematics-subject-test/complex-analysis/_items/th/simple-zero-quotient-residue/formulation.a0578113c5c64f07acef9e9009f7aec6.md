If $g,h$ are holomorphic near $z_0$, $h(z_0)=0$, and $h'(z_0)\ne0$, then

$$
\operatorname{Res}\left(\frac gh,z_0\right)=\frac{g(z_0)}{h'(z_0)}.
$$

If $g(z_0)\ne0$ this is a simple pole. If $g(z_0)=0$, the singularity is removable and the residue is zero.

**Memory cue (added).** At a simple denominator zero: numerator over derivative of denominator.
