Let $f(x+iy)=u(x,y)+iv(x,y)$ on an open set, with $u,v$ having continuous first partial derivatives. Then $f$ is holomorphic if and only if

$$
u_x=v_y,\qquad u_y=-v_x.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** “ux and vy agree; uy and vx oppose.” The second equation carries the minus.
