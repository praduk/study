Let $C$ be a positively oriented simple closed piecewise smooth contour. If $f$ is holomorphic on an open neighborhood of $C$ and its interior and $a$ is inside $C$, then

$$
f(a)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_C\frac{f(z)}{z-a}\,dz.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** One inside value is recovered by the single-pole kernel; positive orientation supplies $2\pi i$.
