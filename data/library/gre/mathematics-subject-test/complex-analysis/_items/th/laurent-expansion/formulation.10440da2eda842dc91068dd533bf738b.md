If $f$ is holomorphic on the annulus $r<|z-z_0|<R$, it has a Laurent expansion there:

$$
f(z)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}a_n(z-z_0)^n.
$$

The negative powers form the principal part. The annulus of validity matters.

**Memory cue (added).** Laurent allows the negative powers Taylor cannot.
