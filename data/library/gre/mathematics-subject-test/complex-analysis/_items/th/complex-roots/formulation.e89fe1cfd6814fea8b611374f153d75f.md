For $z=re^{i\theta}\ne0$ and positive integer $n$, its distinct $n$th roots are

$$
r^{1/n}e^{i(\theta+2\pi k)/n},\qquad k=0,\ldots,n-1.
$$

For $z=0$, the only $n$th root is $0$.

**Memory cue (added).** Root the radius; divide the angle after adding every full-turn choice.
