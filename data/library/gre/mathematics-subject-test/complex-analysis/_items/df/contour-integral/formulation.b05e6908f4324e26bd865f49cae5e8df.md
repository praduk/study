If a piecewise continuously differentiable parametrization $z:[a,b]\to\mathbb C$ traces an oriented contour $C$, and $f$ is continuous along it, then

$$
\int_C f(z)\,dz=\int_a^b f(z(t))z'(t)\,dt.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Substitute the path and multiply by its derivative.
