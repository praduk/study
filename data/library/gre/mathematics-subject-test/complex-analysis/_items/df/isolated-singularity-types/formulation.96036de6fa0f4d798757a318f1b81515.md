At an isolated singularity $z_0$, inspect the Laurent principal part:

- **Removable:** no nonzero negative-power terms.
- **Pole of order $m\ge1$:** finitely many negative-power terms, with lowest exponent $-m$ having nonzero coefficient.
- **Essential:** infinitely many nonzero negative-power terms.

**Memory cue (added).** Negative powers: none, finitely many, infinitely many.
