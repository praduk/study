A nonzero complex number can be written $z=re^{i\theta}=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$, where $r=|z|>0$ and an argument $\theta$ is determined modulo $2\pi$. The argument of $0$ is undefined.

**Memory cue (added).** Length times direction; the direction repeats every full turn.
