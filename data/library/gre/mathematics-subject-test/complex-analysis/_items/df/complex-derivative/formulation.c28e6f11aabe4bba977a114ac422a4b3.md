A function defined near $z_0$ is complex differentiable there if

$$
f'(z_0)=\lim_{\substack{h\to0\\h\in\mathbb C}}\frac{f(z_0+h)-f(z_0)}h
$$

exists. The limit must be the same along every approach, not merely the real and imaginary axes.

**Memory cue (added).** One derivative must work from every complex direction.
