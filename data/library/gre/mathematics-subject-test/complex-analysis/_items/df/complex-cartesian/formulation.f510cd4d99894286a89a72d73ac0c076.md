Every complex number has a unique form $z=x+iy$ with $x,y\in\mathbb R$ and $i^2=-1$. Its real and imaginary parts are $\operatorname{Re}z=x$ and $\operatorname{Im}z=y$; the imaginary part is the real number $y$, not $iy$.

**Memory cue (added).** The imaginary part is the coefficient of $i$.
