**Pattern cue.** Separate consequences of continuity from stronger shape assumptions.

**Fast route.** The intermediate-value theorem guarantees a zero in $(0,1)$. A continuous function need not be differentiable, monotone, or have a unique zero; the endpoint values do not locate the zero.

**Trap check.** Existence is weaker than uniqueness or a specified location.
