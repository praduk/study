**Correct choice: (B).**

**Fast route.** The feasible interval is $0<x<6$, and

$$
V(x)=x(12-2x)^2=4x(6-x)^2.
$$

Differentiating in factored form,

$$
V'(x)=4(6-x)(6-3x).
$$

The interior critical point is $x=2$; the endpoints give zero limiting volume, so $x=2$ is the maximum. Choice (E) is a degenerate box, not a maximizer.
