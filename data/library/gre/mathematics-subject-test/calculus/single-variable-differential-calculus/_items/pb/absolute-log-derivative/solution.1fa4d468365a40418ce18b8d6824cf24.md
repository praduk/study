**Answer.** $-2/3$.

**Pattern cue.** Differentiate a logarithm of an absolute value on its nonzero domain.

**Fast route.** Near $1$, $x^2-4\ne0$. Thus $f^{\prime}(x)=2x/(x^2-4)$, giving $-2/3$. Equivalently near $1$, $f(x)=\ln(4-x^2)$.

**Trap check.** The absolute value makes the logarithm real here; dropping it would make the original logarithm undefined at $1$.
