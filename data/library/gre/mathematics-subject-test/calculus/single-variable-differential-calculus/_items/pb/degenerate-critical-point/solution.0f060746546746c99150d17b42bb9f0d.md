**Answer.** A strict local (and absolute) minimum, not an inflection point.

**Pattern cue.** When the second-derivative test is inconclusive, inspect signs.

**Fast route.** $f^{\prime}(0)=f^{\prime\prime}(0)=0$, but $x^4>0=f(0)$ for $x\ne0$. Also $f^{\prime\prime}(x)=12x^2\ge0$ on both sides, so concavity does not change.

**Trap check.** A zero second derivative proves neither absence of an extremum nor an inflection.
