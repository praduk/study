**Fast route.** Implicit differentiation would give $y^{\prime}=-x/y$, which is undefined at this point. The gradient $(2,0)$ is horizontal and normal to the circle, so the tangent is vertical. Equivalently $x=\sqrt{1-y^2}$ has derivative zero with respect to $y$ there.
