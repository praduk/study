**Fast route.** If $x$ is the foot distance and $y$ the height, $x^2+y^2=25$. Thus $2xx^{\prime}+2yy^{\prime}=0$. At $x=3$, $y=4$, $x^{\prime}=1$, giving $y^{\prime}=-3/4$.

**Trap check.** The vertical rate is signed and the height is $4$, not $5$.
