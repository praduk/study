The derivative is

$$
f'(a)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}h,
$$

when this finite limit exists. Differentiability implies continuity. At an interior piecewise junction, first check continuity and then equality of finite left/right derivatives.

**Pattern.** A corner has unequal finite slopes; a vertical tangent involves an infinite slope and is not a finite derivative. A cusp has opposing infinite one-sided slopes under the usual convention.
