If quantities depend on time and satisfy $F(x(t),y(t))=0$, then $F_x x'+F_y y'=0$. Differentiate the relation before inserting instantaneous values.

**Pattern.** Write the geometric relation, state which quantities are constant, differentiate in time, and keep units. A decreasing length has a negative rate.
