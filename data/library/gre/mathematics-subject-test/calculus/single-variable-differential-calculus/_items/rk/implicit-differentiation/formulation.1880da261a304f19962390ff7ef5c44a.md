If a differentiable curve satisfies $F(x,y(x))=0$, the chain rule gives $F_x+F_y y'=0$. Where $F_y\ne0$,

$$
y'=-F_x/F_y.
$$

For a $C^1$ function $F$, the nonzero $F_y$ condition also guarantees a local differentiable graph through the point.

**Pattern.** Differentiate each occurrence of $y$ with its chain factor; use both factors of a product such as $xy$.
