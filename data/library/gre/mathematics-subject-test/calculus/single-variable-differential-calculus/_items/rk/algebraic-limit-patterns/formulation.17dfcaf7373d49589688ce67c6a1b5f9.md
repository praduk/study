A limit depends on values near the target, not necessarily at it. Cancel a factor only at nearby inputs where it is nonzero. For a radical difference, multiply by its conjugate. For rational functions at infinity, divide by the largest relevant power.

**Trap.** $0/0$ records failure of direct substitution; it is not the value of a limit.
