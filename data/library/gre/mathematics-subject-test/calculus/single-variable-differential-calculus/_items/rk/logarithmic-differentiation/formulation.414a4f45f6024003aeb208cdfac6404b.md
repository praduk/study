For differentiable $u,v$ with $u>0$ and $y=u^v$,

$$
\frac{y'}y=v'\ln u+v\frac{u'}u.
$$

For a product of nonzero differentiable factors, differentiating the logarithm of its absolute value turns products into sums of logarithmic derivatives.

**Pattern.** Use when both base and exponent vary, then multiply back by $y$. Do not take real logarithms of negative quantities without the appropriate absolute-value treatment.
