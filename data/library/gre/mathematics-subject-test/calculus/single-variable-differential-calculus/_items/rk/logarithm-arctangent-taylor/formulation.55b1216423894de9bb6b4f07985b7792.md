For real $-1<x\le1$,

$$
\ln(1+x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^{n+1}x^n}{n}.
$$

For real $-1\le x\le1$,

$$
\arctan x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{2n+1}.
$$

The logarithm series converges conditionally at $1$ and diverges at $-1$; the arctangent series converges conditionally at both endpoints. Both converge absolutely for $|x|<1$.

**Pattern.** Integrate a geometric series inside its radius, then check endpoints separately.
