For every real $x$,

$$
\sin x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n+1}}{(2n+1)!},\qquad\cos x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(-1)^nx^{2n}}{(2n)!}.
$$

**Pattern.** Parity often makes a coefficient vanish before any multiplication. Under substitution $x\mapsto x^k$, multiply each exponent by $k$.
