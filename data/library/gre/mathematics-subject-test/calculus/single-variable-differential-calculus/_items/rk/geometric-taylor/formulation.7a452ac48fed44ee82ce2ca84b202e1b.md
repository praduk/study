For $|x|<1$,

$$
\frac1{1-x}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}x^n,\qquad\frac1{(1-x)^2}=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}nx^{n-1}.
$$

The second identity follows by termwise differentiation inside the radius of convergence.

**Pattern.** Factor a denominator into a constant times $1-u$; keep track of any leading power. Boundary points require separate analysis.
