As $u\to0$ through the real domain,

$$
\frac{\sin u}{u}\to1,\quad\frac{1-\cos u}{u^2}\to\frac12,
$$

$$
\frac{e^u-1}{u}\to1,\quad\frac{\ln(1+u)}u\to1.
$$

Also $(1+1/n)^n\to e$. Angles are in radians.

**Pattern.** Rescale the small argument explicitly; for cancellation beyond first order use enough Taylor terms.
