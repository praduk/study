For every real $x$,

$$
e^x=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{x^n}{n!}.
$$

Replacing $x$ by an expression changes both its powers and its coefficients. In a limit with canceled constant and linear terms, keep the quadratic term (or the first surviving order).

**Pattern.** For coefficients of a product, collect only exponent pairs that sum to the requested power.
