For $|x|<1$, $(\arcsin x)^{\prime}=1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$ and $(\arccos x)^{\prime}=-1/\sqrt{1-x^2}$. For real $x$, $(\arctan x)^{\prime}=1/(1+x^2)$. With $\operatorname{arcsec}$ taking values in $[0,\pi]\setminus\{\pi/2\}$, its derivative is $1/(|x|\sqrt{x^2-1})$ for $|x|>1$.

**Pattern.** Apply the inner chain factor and check the branch/domain. The absolute value in the arcsecant derivative is essential for negative inputs.
