A continuous function on $[a,b]$ attains its absolute extrema. Candidates are the endpoints and all interior points where $f'=0$ or a finite derivative fails to exist while $f$ is defined. Evaluate the function at every candidate.

**Pattern.** In optimization, determine the feasible interval first. An endpoint maximum need not satisfy $f'=0$.
