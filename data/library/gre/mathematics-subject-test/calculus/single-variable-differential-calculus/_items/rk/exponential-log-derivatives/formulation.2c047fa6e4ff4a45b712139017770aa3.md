For $a>0$,

$$
(e^x)^{\prime}=e^x,\qquad(a^x)^{\prime}=a^x\ln a.
$$

For $x\ne0$, $(\ln|x|)^{\prime}=1/x$; hence $(\ln|u(x)|)^{\prime}=u'(x)/u(x)$ where $u\ne0$. For logarithms to base $a>0$, $a\ne1$, $(\log_a x)^{\prime}=1/(x\ln a)$ on $x>0$.

**Trap.** $a^x$ is not differentiated by the power rule for $x^a$.
