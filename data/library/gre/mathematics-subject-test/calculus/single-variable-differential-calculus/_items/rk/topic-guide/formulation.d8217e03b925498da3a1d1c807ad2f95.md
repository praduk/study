## What to know

- Limits, one-sided limits, continuity, removable and jump discontinuities, and standard asymptotic limits.
- Derivative rules for algebraic, trigonometric, exponential, logarithmic, inverse, implicit, and parametric functions.
- The chain rule, inverse-function derivative, higher derivatives, linearization, and differentials.
- Critical points, monotonicity, concavity, extrema, related rates, optimization, Rolle's theorem, and the Mean Value Theorem.

## Fast recognition

Simplify or factor before applying a heavy limit method. For inverse derivatives, find the corresponding input and reciprocate the original derivative. In optimization, identify the feasible interval before solving $f'=0$ and compare boundaries. A theorem question is often asking whether its hypotheses force one precise conclusion.

## Common traps

Continuity does not imply differentiability. A zero derivative need not be an extremum, and an extremum at a boundary need not satisfy $f'=0$. L'Hopital's rule applies only to an appropriate indeterminate quotient after its hypotheses are checked.
