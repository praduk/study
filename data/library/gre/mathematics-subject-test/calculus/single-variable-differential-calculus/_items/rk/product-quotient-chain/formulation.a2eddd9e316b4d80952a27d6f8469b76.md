At points where the indicated functions are differentiable,

$$
(fg)^{\prime}=f'g+fg',\qquad(f/g)^{\prime}=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}\quad(g\ne0),
$$

$$
(f\circ g)^{\prime}(x)=f'(g(x))g'(x).
$$

The power rule is $(x^p)^{\prime}=px^{p-1}$ on intervals where the real power is differentiable (in particular $x>0$ for arbitrary real $p$).

**Pattern.** Identify the outermost operation before differentiating. Keep a factored expression when only a value or sign is needed.
