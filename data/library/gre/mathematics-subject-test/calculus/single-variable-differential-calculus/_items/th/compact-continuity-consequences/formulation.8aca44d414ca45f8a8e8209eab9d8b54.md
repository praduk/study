For real $a<b$, a continuous $f:[a,b]\to\R$ is bounded, attains a maximum and a minimum, is uniformly continuous, and attains every intermediate value between any two of its values.

**Trap.** On an open or unbounded interval these conclusions need not all hold. The intermediate-value property alone does not imply continuity.
