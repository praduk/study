If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$, with $a<b$, some $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies

$$
f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}.
$$

Consequently, $|f'|\le M$ on the interior implies $|f(b)-f(a)|\le M(b-a)$.

**Trap.** The theorem neither locates $c$ nor asserts uniqueness or monotonicity.
