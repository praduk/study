For a differentiable function on an interval: $f'>0$ everywhere implies strictly increasing; $f'<0$ implies strictly decreasing; $f'=0$ everywhere implies constant. A nonnegative derivative implies nondecreasing, not necessarily strictly increasing.

**Pattern.** Monotonicity plus a sign change gives exactly one root; the Intermediate Value Theorem supplies existence, and strict monotonicity supplies uniqueness.
