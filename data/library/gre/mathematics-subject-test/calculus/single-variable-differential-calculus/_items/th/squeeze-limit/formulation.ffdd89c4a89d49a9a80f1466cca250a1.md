If $g(x)\le f(x)\le h(x)$ near $a$ (apart from possibly $a$ itself) and $g,h\to L$, then $f\to L$. In particular, $|f(x)|\le b(x)$ with $b(x)\to0$ forces $f(x)\to0$.

**Pattern.** Bound an oscillating factor by $1$ and inspect its amplitude. An oscillating factor alone need not converge.
