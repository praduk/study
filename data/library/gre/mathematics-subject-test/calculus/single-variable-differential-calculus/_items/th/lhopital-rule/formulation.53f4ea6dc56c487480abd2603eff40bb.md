For a one-sided limit at $a$ (finite or infinite), suppose $f,g$ are differentiable on the corresponding punctured interval, $g'\ne0$ there, and the quotient is of type $0/0$ or $\infty/\infty$ (both magnitudes tend to infinity). If $f'/g'$ has a limit $L$ in the extended real numbers, then $f/g\to L$.

**Pattern.** Verify the indeterminate quotient first. Products and differences must be rewritten; an inconclusive derivative quotient does not establish divergence of the original limit.
