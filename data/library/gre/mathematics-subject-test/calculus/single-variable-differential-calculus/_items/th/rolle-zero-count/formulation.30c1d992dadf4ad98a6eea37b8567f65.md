If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$, differentiable on $(a,b)$, $a<b$, and $f(a)=f(b)$, there is $c\in(a,b)$ with $f'(c)=0$.

**Pattern.** Between consecutive distinct zeros of $f$ lies a zero of $f'$. Repeating the argument for higher derivatives can bound how many zeros $f$ has. Use disjoint intervals to ensure distinct points.
