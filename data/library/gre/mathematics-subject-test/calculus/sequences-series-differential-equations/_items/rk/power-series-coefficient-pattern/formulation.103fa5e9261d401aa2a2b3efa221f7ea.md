Let $k$ be a positive integer. For a convergent power series $f(u)=\sum c_nu^n$, substitution $u=x^k$ produces $\sum c_nx^{kn}$ wherever the substitution remains inside the original radius. A coefficient of a power not divisible by $k$ is zero. In a product, the coefficient of $x^m$ is the sum of products whose exponents add to $m$.

**Pattern.** Identify the possible exponents first; calculate only terms that can contribute. This often avoids high-order differentiation.
