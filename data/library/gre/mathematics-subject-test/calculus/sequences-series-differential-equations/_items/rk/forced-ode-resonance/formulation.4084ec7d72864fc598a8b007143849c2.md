For a linear differential operator $L$, if $L[y_p]=g$, all solutions of $L[y]=g$ are $y_p+y_h$ where $L[y_h]=0$.

For constant-coefficient operators and polynomial/exponential/sine/cosine forcing, undetermined coefficients uses the corresponding finite-dimensional trial family. If its exponential rate is a characteristic root of multiplicity $m$, multiply the trial by $x^m$ to remove the overlap.

**Pattern.** Test the forcing against the homogeneous modes before solving for coefficients.
