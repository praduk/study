For $y'=g(x)h(y)$ with continuous $g,h$ on the relevant intervals, on a region where $h(y)\ne0$, separate and integrate:

$$
\int\frac{dy}{h(y)}=\int g(x)\,dx+C.
$$

Constant solutions $y\equiv c$ with $h(c)=0$ must be checked separately because division removes them. Use the initial condition and verify the resulting function in the original equation.

**Trap.** A separated expression may be valid only on a subinterval of the solution domain.
