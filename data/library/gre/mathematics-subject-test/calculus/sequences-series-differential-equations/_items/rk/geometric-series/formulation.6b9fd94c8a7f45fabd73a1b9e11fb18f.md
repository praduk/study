For $r\ne1$,

$$
\sum_{n=0}^{N}ar^n=\frac{a(1-r^{N+1})}{1-r}.
$$

If $|r|<1$, the infinite sum is $a/(1-r)$. For $a\ne0$ and $|r|\ge1$ the infinite series diverges; the zero series is the exception when $a=0$.

**Pattern.** Identify the actual first term and ratio, especially when indexing starts at $1$.
