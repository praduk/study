For terms $a_n=b_n-b_{n+1}$,

$$
\sum_{n=m}^{N}a_n=b_m-b_{N+1}.
$$

Thus if $b_n\to L$, the infinite sum from $m$ is $b_m-L$. For a larger index gap, several initial and final terms survive.

**Pattern.** Write a finite partial sum before passing to the limit; never cancel an infinite expression formally.
