For $ay''+by'+cy=0$ with real constants and $a\ne0$, solve $ar^2+br+c=0$.

- Distinct real roots $r_1,r_2$: $y=C_1e^{r_1x}+C_2e^{r_2x}$.
- Repeated real root $r$: $y=(C_1+C_2x)e^{rx}$.
- Roots $\alpha\pm i\beta$, $\beta>0$: $y=e^{\alpha x}(C_1\cos\beta x+C_2\sin\beta x)$.

**Pattern.** Classify roots before fitting the two initial conditions; verify both initial values.
