For $\sum c_n(x-a)^n$, convergence is absolute for $|x-a|<R$ and fails for $|x-a|>R$. At $a\pm R$ when $0<R<\infty$, test the resulting numerical series separately.

The root formula is $1/R=\limsup|c_n|^{1/n}$, with conventions $1/0=\infty$ and $1/\infty=0$; a simpler ratio calculation often suffices.

**Trap.** The radius is a distance, while the interval depends on the center and its endpoints.
