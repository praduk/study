A series converges absolutely if $\sum|a_n|$ converges. It converges conditionally if $\sum a_n$ converges while $\sum|a_n|$ diverges. Absolute convergence implies convergence.

**Pattern.** Test absolute values first; alternating signs do not force conditional convergence. A nonnegative convergent series is automatically absolutely convergent.
