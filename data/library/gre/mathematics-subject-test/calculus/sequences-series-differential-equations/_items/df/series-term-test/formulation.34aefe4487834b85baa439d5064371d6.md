The series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ converges iff its partial sums $S_N=\sum_{n=1}^Na_n$ converge to a finite number. Convergence forces $a_n\to0$, since $a_n=S_n-S_{n-1}$.

**Trap.** The converse fails: the harmonic series has terms tending to zero but divergent partial sums. If terms fail to approach zero, stop immediately.
