The $p$-series $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-p}$ converges iff $p>1$. For eventually nonnegative terms $a_n\le b_n$, convergence of $\sum b_n$ forces convergence of $\sum a_n$, while divergence of $\sum a_n$ forces divergence of $\sum b_n$.

If $a_n,b_n>0$ eventually and $a_n/b_n\to c$ with $0<c<\infty$, both series have the same convergence behavior.

**Pattern.** Compare the net powers of $n$; inequalities in the other direction may prove nothing.
