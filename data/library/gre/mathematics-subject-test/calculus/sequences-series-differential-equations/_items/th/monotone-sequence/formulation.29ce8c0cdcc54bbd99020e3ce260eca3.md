Every increasing real sequence bounded above converges to its supremum; every decreasing sequence bounded below converges to its infimum.

For $a_{n+1}=g(a_n)$, first establish a preserved bound and monotonicity. If the resulting sequence converges and $g$ is continuous at its limit, the limit satisfies $L=g(L)$.

**Trap.** A fixed-point equation alone does not prove convergence or select the correct root.
