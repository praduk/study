If $a_n\ne0$ eventually and $|a_{n+1}/a_n|\to L$, then $L<1$ gives absolute convergence and $L>1$ (including infinity) gives divergence. $L=1$ is inconclusive.

**Pattern.** Factorials and exponentials simplify in the ratio. For $L>1$, term magnitudes eventually grow and cannot approach zero.
