A power series can be differentiated or integrated term by term inside its open interval of convergence. The resulting series has the same radius, but its endpoint convergence may change.

**Pattern.** Differentiate a geometric series to introduce a factor $n$, or integrate it to introduce $1/(n+1)$. Recheck endpoints after either operation.
