If $b_n\ge0$ decreases to zero, then $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}(-1)^{n+1}b_n$ converges, and after $N$ terms its error has magnitude at most $b_{N+1}$ and the sign of the first omitted term (unless it is zero).

**Pattern.** Verify both decay to zero and monotonicity before bounding the error; count the first omitted index carefully.
