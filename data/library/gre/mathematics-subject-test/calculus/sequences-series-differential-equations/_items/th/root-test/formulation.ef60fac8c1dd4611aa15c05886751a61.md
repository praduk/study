If $\sqrt[n]{|a_n|}\to L$, then $L<1$ gives absolute convergence and $L>1$ (including infinity) gives divergence. $L=1$ is inconclusive. The same conclusions hold using the limit superior.

**Pattern.** An entire expression raised to the $n$th power often becomes simple after taking the $n$th root.
