If $f$ is continuous, positive, and decreasing on $[N,\infty)$ and $a_n=f(n)$, then $\sum_{n=N}^{\infty}a_n$ converges iff $\int_N^\infty f(x)\,dx$ converges. When they converge,

$$
\int_{M+1}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx\le\sum_{n=M+1}^{\infty}a_n\le\int_M^\infty f(x)\,dx\quad(M\ge N).
$$

**Pattern.** Logarithmic factors often simplify under $u=\ln x$. Eventual monotonicity is enough.
