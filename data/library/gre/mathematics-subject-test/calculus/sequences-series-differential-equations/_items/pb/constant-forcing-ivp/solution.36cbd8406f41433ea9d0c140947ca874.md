**Pattern cue.** Separate a nonresonant particular solution from homogeneous modes.

**Fast route.** A constant particular solution is $1$. The homogeneous solution is $A\cos x+B\sin x$. Initial values give $A=-1$, $B=0$. Substitution gives $y^{\prime\prime}+y=\cos x+1-\cos x=1$.

**Trap check.** The particular solution alone need not satisfy the initial conditions.
