**Answer.** $2$.

**Pattern cue.** Differentiate the geometric series, then multiply by its variable.

**Fast route.** For $|x|<1$, $\sum n x^n=x/(1-x)^2$. At $x=1/2$ this is $(1/2)/(1/2)^2=2$.

**Trap check.** Termwise differentiation is justified inside the radius, not automatically at endpoints.
