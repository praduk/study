**Answer.** It diverges; the even subsequence tends to $1$ and the odd subsequence to $-1$.

**Pattern cue.** Separate parity subsequences.

**Fast route.** $a_{2k}=1+1/(2k)\to1$, while $a_{2k+1}=-1+1/(2k+1)\to-1$. A convergent sequence would give the same limit for both.

**Trap check.** Boundedness alone does not give convergence.
