**Answer.** $y(x)=3\sin2x$.

**Pattern cue.** Nonreal characteristic roots give trigonometric modes.

**Fast route.** Roots are $\pm2i$, so $y=A\cos2x+B\sin2x$. The first condition gives $A=0$; the derivative condition gives $2B=6$. Substitution yields $y^{\prime\prime}=-4y$.

**Trap check.** The derivative condition includes the frequency factor $2$.
