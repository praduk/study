**Pattern cue.** Test the alternating series and its absolute values separately.

**Fast route.** The magnitudes decrease to zero, so the alternating series converges. The absolute series is a $p$-series with $p=1/2$, so it diverges.

**Trap check.** Convergence of the signed series does not imply absolute convergence.
