**Fast route.** The radius is $3$, so first obtain $-1<x<5$. At $x=5$ the series is $\sum1/n$, which diverges. At $x=-1$ it is $\sum(-1)^n/n$, which converges by the alternating-series test. Therefore the interval is $[-1,5)$.

A radius calculation alone never settles the endpoints.
