**Fast route.** $f(x)=1/(x\ln x)$ is positive and decreasing for $x\ge2$. Its integral is $\ln(\ln x)$, unbounded as $x\to\infty$, so the series diverges.

**Trap check.** Being smaller than the harmonic terms does not prove convergence.
