This is the 120-minute calculus portion of @gre:mathematics-subject-test:test-layout-speed:rk:course-map-how-to-use-guide. It selects 25 concepts and two adjacent problems per concept from the larger bank.

**Caps include everything:** recalling the concept, its two attempts, and a short solution comparison. For a five-minute block, aim for about one minute of recall, one minute per attempt, and two minutes to compare. Use the ranges on each item as estimates, not as extra time. Do not add theorem-proof study to this session.

**Adaptive rule:** try both drills when the concept is familiar. If a gap consumes the cap, attempt one, check the decisive step, mark the other for later, and move on. Finishing every drill is not required. If the rule and a fresh problem are immediate and correct, reclaim the remaining minutes for a weak block in this subject. Keep the subject's total fixed. A slow correct answer needs a shorter method; a fast wrong answer needs a repaired condition or setup.

Read each tip with its concept; it is an intentional learning cue. The final mixed checkpoint removes that preceding cue. Return to this index to jump between selected groups; **Next** follows the full authored library, including unselected groups.

## 6. Mean Value Theorem and derivative bounds — 4 min

Concept: @gre:mathematics-subject-test:calculus:single-variable-differential-calculus:th:mean-value-theorem

**Check:** Check closed-interval continuity and interior differentiability. Existence does not locate the point or prove uniqueness.

## 10. Differentiating an integral with moving bounds — 4 min
