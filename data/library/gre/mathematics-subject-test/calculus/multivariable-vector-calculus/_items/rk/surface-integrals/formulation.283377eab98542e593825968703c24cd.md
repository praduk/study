For a regular parametrized surface $\mathbf r(u,v)$,

$$
dS=\|\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v\|\,du\,dv,
$$

$$
\iint_S f\,dS=\iint f(\mathbf r)\|\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v\|\,du\,dv.
$$

For flux with orientation given by $\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v$,

$$
\iint_S\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=\iint\mathbf F(\mathbf r)\cdot(\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v)\,du\,dv.
$$

Use a parametrization covering the surface once except on negligible boundaries.

**Pattern.** Scalar area uses a norm; flux uses the oriented vector. For $z=g(x,y)$, the upward area vector is $(-g_x,-g_y,1)\,dx\,dy$.
