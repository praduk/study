For $C^1$ $F$ and a point $\mathbf a$ with $F(\mathbf a)=k$ and $\nabla F(\mathbf a)\ne0$, the surface $F=k$ has normal $\nabla F(\mathbf a)$ and tangent plane

$$
\nabla F(\mathbf a)\cdot(\mathbf x-\mathbf a)=0.
$$

**Trap.** A zero gradient gives no normal direction by this formula and requires separate analysis.
