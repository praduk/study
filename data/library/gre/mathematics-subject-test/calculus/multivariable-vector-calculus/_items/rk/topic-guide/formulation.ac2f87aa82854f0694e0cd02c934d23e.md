## What to know

- Partial and directional derivatives, gradients, tangent planes, Jacobians, Hessians, and the multivariable chain rule.
- Local extrema, second-derivative tests, and constrained extrema by Lagrange multipliers.
- Double and triple integrals; changing order; polar, cylindrical, and spherical coordinates; Jacobian factors.
- Vector fields, conservative fields, potentials, line and surface integrals, curl and divergence.
- Green's theorem, Stokes' theorem, and the divergence theorem, including orientation.

## Fast recognition

Normalize a direction vector before taking a directional derivative. A linear functional on a sphere is maximized in its coefficient-vector direction. Before integrating a vector field directly, test for a potential or for a theorem that turns a boundary integral into an easier interior integral.

## Common traps

Polar area measure is $r\,dr\,d\theta$, not merely $dr\,d\theta$. Mixed partial equality needs suitable regularity. Flux and circulation use different integrands and orientations. A vanishing gradient identifies a candidate, not automatically an extremum.
