For a differentiable scalar function $f$ at $\mathbf a$ and a unit vector $\mathbf u$,

$$
D_{\mathbf u}f(\mathbf a)=\nabla f(\mathbf a)\cdot\mathbf u.
$$

The maximum over unit directions is $\|\nabla f(\mathbf a)\|$. If the gradient is nonzero this is attained in its direction; if the gradient is zero every directional derivative is zero.

**Pattern.** Normalize the supplied direction vector before the dot product.
