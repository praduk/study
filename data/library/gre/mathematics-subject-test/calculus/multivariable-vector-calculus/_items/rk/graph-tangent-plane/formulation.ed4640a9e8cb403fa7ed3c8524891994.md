If $f$ is differentiable at $(a,b)$, the tangent plane to $z=f(x,y)$ is

$$
z=f(a,b)+f_x(a,b)(x-a)+f_y(a,b)(y-b).
$$

Its right side is also the first-order approximation for $f$ near $(a,b)$.

**Pattern.** Compute the gradient and point value; check the resulting plane passes through the point.
