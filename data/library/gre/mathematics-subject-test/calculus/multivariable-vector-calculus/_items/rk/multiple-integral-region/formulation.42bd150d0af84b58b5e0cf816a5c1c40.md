Let $a<b$, let $g_1,g_2$ be continuous on $[a,b]$ with $g_1\le g_2$, and let $f$ be continuous on the compact region $a\le x\le b$, $g_1(x)\le y\le g_2(x)$. Then the double integral is

$$
\int_a^b\int_{g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)}f(x,y)\,dy\,dx.
$$

To reverse order, describe the same set using horizontal slices, splitting it if necessary. For integrand $1$, the integral is geometric area.

**Pattern.** Translate bounds to inequalities and sketch before changing the order; do not merely swap symbols.
