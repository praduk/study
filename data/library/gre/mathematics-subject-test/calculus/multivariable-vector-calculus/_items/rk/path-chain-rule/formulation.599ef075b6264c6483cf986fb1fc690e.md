For differentiable $f$ and a differentiable path $\mathbf r(t)$,

$$
\frac d{dt}f(\mathbf r(t))=\nabla f(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t).
$$

More generally, Jacobian matrices multiply under composition: $D(f\circ g)=Df(g)Dg$ when the maps are differentiable.

**Pattern.** Distinguish path velocity from a unit direction; do not normalize velocity in a time derivative.
