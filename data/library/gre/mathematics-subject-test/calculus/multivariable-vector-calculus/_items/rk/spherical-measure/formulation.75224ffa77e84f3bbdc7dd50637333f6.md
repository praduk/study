Using radius $\rho\ge0$, polar angle $\phi$ from $+z$ with $0\le\phi\le\pi$, and azimuth $\theta$,

$$
x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\quad y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\quad z=\rho\cos\phi,
$$

$$
dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta.
$$

**Pattern.** A radial function in a full ball becomes $4\pi\int f(\rho)\rho^2d\rho$. Do not interchange the polar and azimuthal angle conventions silently.
