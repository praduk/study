A partial derivative differentiates in one coordinate while the others are held fixed. If the second partial derivatives are continuous on a neighborhood, then $f_{xy}=f_{yx}$ there (and similarly for other coordinate pairs).

**Pattern.** Differentiate in the indicated order and eliminate terms independent of that variable early. Do not infer equality of mixed partials from existence alone.
