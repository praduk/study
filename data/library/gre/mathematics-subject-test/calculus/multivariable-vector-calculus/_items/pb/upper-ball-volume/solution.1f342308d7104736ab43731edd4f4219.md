**Pattern cue.** Translate the half-ball condition to the polar-angle range.

**Fast route.** Use $0\le\rho\le2$, $0\le\phi\le\pi/2$, $0\le\theta\le2\pi$. The integral is $(8/3)(1)(2\pi)=16\pi/3$, half the volume of a radius-two ball.
