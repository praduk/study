**Answer.** $2\pi/3$.

**Pattern cue.** Close the surface with a disk and subtract its flux.

**Fast route.** The divergence is $1$, so total flux through the hemisphere plus its base is the half-ball volume $2\pi/3$. On the base disk $z=0$, the field is zero, so the disk contributes no flux.

**Trap check.** The divergence theorem applies to the closed boundary; an open hemisphere needs the base accounted for.
