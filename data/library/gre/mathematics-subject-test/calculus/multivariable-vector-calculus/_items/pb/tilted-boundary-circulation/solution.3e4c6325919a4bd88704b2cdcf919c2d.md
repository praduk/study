**Answer.** $\pi$.

**Pattern cue.** Use Stokes with a graph area vector.

**Fast route.** Curl is $(0,0,1)$ and the upward area vector is $(-1,-1,1)dxdy$. Their dot product is $1$, so the integral is the projected unit disk area $\pi$.

**Trap check.** The surface’s slant factor cancels against the normal component; do not multiply by its area alone.
