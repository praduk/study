**Correct choice: (D).**

**Fast route.** Since $\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=3$, the divergence theorem gives

$$
\iint_S\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS
=\iiint_B3\,dV
=3\left(\frac{4\pi}{3}\right)=4\pi.
$$

Directly, on the unit sphere $\mathbf F=\mathbf n$, so the integrand is $1$ and the flux equals the surface area $4\pi$; this independently checks the result.
