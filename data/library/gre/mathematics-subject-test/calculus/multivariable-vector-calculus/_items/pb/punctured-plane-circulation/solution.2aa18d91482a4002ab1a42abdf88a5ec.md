**Answer.** Circulation $2\pi$; it is not a gradient on the whole punctured plane.

**Pattern cue.** A domain hole can defeat the global zero-curl test.

**Fast route.** On $(\cos t,\sin t)$ the field equals the velocity $(-\sin t,\cos t)$, so the integral is $\int_0^{2\pi}1dt=2\pi$. A gradient would have zero integral around every closed path. Although $P_y=Q_x$ away from zero, the domain is not simply connected.

**Trap check.** Green’s theorem over the filled disk would cross a singularity.
