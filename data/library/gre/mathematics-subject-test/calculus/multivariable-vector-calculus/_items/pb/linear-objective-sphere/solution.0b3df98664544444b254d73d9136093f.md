**Fast route.** By Cauchy-Schwarz,

$$
2x-y+2z\le\sqrt{2^2+(-1)^2+2^2}\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2}=3\cdot3=9.
$$

Equality is attained when $(x,y,z)$ points in the direction $(2,-1,2)$; that vector already has length $3$ and satisfies the constraint. Lagrange multipliers give the same result more slowly.
