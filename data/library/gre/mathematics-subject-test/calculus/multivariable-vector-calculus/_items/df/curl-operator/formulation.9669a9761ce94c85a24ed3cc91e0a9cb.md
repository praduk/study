For a differentiable vector field $\mathbf F=(P,Q,R)$,

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf F=(R_y-Q_z,\ P_z-R_x,\ Q_x-P_y).
$$

It is a vector. For $C^2$ scalar $\phi$, $\nabla\times\nabla\phi=\mathbf0$ by equality of mixed partials.

**Pattern.** In the plane, $Q_x-P_y$ is the vertical curl component. Zero curl alone does not imply a global potential on a domain with holes.
