For a differentiable vector field $\mathbf F=(P,Q,R)$,

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=P_x+Q_y+R_z.
$$

It is a scalar. For $\mathbf F=(x,y,z)$, divergence is $3$.

**Pattern.** Pair each component with its own coordinate derivative; integrate divergence for closed-surface flux when the divergence theorem applies.
