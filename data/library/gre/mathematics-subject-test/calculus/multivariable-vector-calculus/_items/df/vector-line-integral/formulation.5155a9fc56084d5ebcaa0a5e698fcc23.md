For a piecewise $C^1$ oriented path $\mathbf r:[a,b]\to\R^n$ and continuous vector field $\mathbf F$,

$$
\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\int_a^b\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t))\cdot\mathbf r'(t)\,dt.
$$

Reversing orientation negates the integral.

**Pattern.** Substitute the path into the field, then dot with velocity. Do not replace velocity by its norm.
