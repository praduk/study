For a piecewise $C^1$ path $\mathbf r:[a,b]\to\R^n$ and continuous scalar $f$,

$$
\int_C f\,ds=\int_a^b f(\mathbf r(t))\|\mathbf r'(t)\|\,dt.
$$

It is unchanged by reversing the path orientation; repeated tracing counts repeatedly.

**Pattern.** Use speed, not the velocity vector itself. Setting $f=1$ gives length.
