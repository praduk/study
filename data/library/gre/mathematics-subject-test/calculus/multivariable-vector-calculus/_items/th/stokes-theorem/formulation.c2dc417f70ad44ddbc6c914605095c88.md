Let $S$ be an oriented piecewise smooth surface with piecewise smooth boundary and $\mathbf F$ be $C^1$ on a neighborhood of $S$. Then

$$
\oint_{\partial S}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\iint_S(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot\mathbf n\,dS,
$$

with the boundary orientation induced by the normal via the right-hand rule.

**Pattern.** Replace a difficult spanning surface by an easier one with the same oriented boundary when the field is regular on that replacement. The chosen normal determines the sign.
