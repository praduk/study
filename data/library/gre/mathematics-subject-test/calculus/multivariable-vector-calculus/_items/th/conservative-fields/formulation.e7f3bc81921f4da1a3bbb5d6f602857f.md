If $\mathbf F=\nabla\phi$ for a $C^1$ potential, every piecewise smooth path from $A$ to $B$ satisfies

$$
\int_C\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r=\phi(B)-\phi(A).
$$

A $C^1$ field with zero curl on an open simply connected domain in $\R^3$ is conservative; in the plane the condition is $P_y=Q_x$ on an open simply connected domain.

**Pattern.** Find a potential by integrating one component and correcting with a function of the remaining variables. Check domain holes before using a curl test globally.
