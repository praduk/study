For a bounded solid $E$ with piecewise smooth boundary and a $C^1$ field on a neighborhood of $E$,

$$
\iint_{\partial E}\mathbf F\cdot\mathbf n\,dS=\iiint_E\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV,
$$

where $\mathbf n$ is outward. Boundary components around cavities have normals pointing from the solid into the cavity.

**Pattern.** Use for closed-surface flux, not an open patch without accounting for the missing faces. Constant divergence reduces the computation to volume.
