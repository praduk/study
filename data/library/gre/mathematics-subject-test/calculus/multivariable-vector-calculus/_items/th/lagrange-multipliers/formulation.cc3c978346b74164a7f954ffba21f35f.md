If $f,g$ are $C^1$ near a local extremum of $f$ constrained by $g=k$ and $\nabla g\ne0$ there, then

$$
\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g,\qquad g=k.
$$

Solve this system for candidates, then compare values as appropriate. Separately inspect singular constraint points where $\nabla g=0$, boundaries, and existence of a global extremum.

**Pattern.** For a linear objective on a sphere, Cauchy–Schwarz often gives the extremum and equality case directly.
