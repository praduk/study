Let $D$ be a bounded planar region with piecewise smooth boundary, positively oriented so the region stays on the left (outer boundary counterclockwise, hole boundaries clockwise). If $P,Q$ are $C^1$ on a neighborhood of $D$, then

$$
\oint_{\partial D}P\,dx+Q\,dy=\iint_D(Q_x-P_y)\,dA.
$$

**Pattern.** Constant scalar curl turns circulation into signed area. Singularities inside the region can invalidate the hypotheses.
