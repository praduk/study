For a one-to-one $C^1$ coordinate change on a planar region with nonzero Jacobian in its interior, and the usual piecewise smooth boundary/integrability assumptions,

$$
\iint_D f(x,y)\,dx\,dy=\iint_U f(x(u,v),y(u,v))\left|\det\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right|du\,dv.
$$

Boundary exceptions of area zero are harmless when the formula applies; interior multiple covering must be accounted for.

**Pattern.** Use the determinant in the direction from new coordinates to old, and take its absolute value.
