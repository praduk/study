**Answer.** $1/\sqrt2$.

**Pattern cue.** A tangent substitution turns the quadratic radical into a power of secant.

**Fast route.** With $x=\tan\theta$, $0\le\theta\le\pi/4$, the integrand becomes $\cos\theta\,d\theta$, giving $\sin(\pi/4)=1/\sqrt2$. Differentiating $x/\sqrt{1+x^2}$ confirms the antiderivative.

**Trap check.** Choose a branch with positive cosine before dropping absolute values.
