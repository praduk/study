**Pattern cue.** Reduce the even power to a constant and a cosine.

**Fast route.** $\sin^2x=(1-\cos2x)/2$. The cosine integrates to zero over this interval, leaving $\pi/2$.

**Trap check.** The average of sine squared is $1/2$, not $0$.
