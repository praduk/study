For $a>0$, the substitutions $x=a\sin\theta$, $x=a\tan\theta$, and $x=a\sec\theta$ simplify $\sqrt{a^2-x^2}$, $\sqrt{a^2+x^2}$, and $\sqrt{x^2-a^2}$, respectively, on appropriately restricted branches.

**Pattern.** Choose the angle interval so square roots simplify with the correct sign. For example $\sqrt{a^2-a^2\sin^2\theta}=a|\cos\theta|$, not automatically $a\cos\theta$. A geometric semicircle area may be faster than substitution.
