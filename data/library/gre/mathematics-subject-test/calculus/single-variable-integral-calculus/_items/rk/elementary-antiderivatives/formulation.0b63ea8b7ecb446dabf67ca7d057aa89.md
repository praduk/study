Useful derivative pairs are

$$
\int e^x\,dx=e^x+C,\quad\int\frac{dx}{1+x^2}=\arctan x+C,
$$

$$
\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{1-x^2}}=\arcsin x+C\quad(-1<x<1).
$$

Scaling requires its chain factor: for $a>0$, $\int dx/(a^2+x^2)=a^{-1}\arctan(x/a)+C$.

**Pattern.** Match the shape and scale before launching a longer substitution.
