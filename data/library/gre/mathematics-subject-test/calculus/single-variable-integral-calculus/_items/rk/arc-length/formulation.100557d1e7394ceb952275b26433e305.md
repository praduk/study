For a $C^1$ graph $y=f(x)$ on $[a,b]$,

$$
L=\int_a^b\sqrt{1+(f'(x))^2}\,dx.
$$

For a $C^1$ parametrization $\mathbf r(t)$, $L=\int_\alpha^\beta\|\mathbf r'(t)\|\,dt$; repeated tracing contributes repeatedly.

**Pattern.** Simplify the squared speed before integrating.
