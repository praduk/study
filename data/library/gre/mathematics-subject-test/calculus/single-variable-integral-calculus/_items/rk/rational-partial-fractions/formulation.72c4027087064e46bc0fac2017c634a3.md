For a rational function, divide first if the numerator degree is at least the denominator degree. Over $\R$, factor the denominator into linear and irreducible quadratic factors. A repeated linear factor $(x-a)^m$ needs all terms $A_k/(x-a)^k$ for $1\le k\le m$; a quadratic power needs a linear numerator at each power.

**Pattern.** Distinct linear factors often give logarithms; repeated factors also give negative powers. Recombine the fractions to verify coefficients.
