For a nonnegative $C^1$ graph $y=f(x)$ on $[a,b]$ rotated once about the $x$-axis,

$$
S=2\pi\int_a^b f(x)\sqrt{1+(f'(x))^2}\,dx.
$$

This is curved lateral area; it excludes end caps. More generally use $2\pi$ times distance to the axis times arc-length element, with care about repeated tracing or overlap.

**Pattern.** Distinguish a surface from an enclosed solid before choosing a formula.
