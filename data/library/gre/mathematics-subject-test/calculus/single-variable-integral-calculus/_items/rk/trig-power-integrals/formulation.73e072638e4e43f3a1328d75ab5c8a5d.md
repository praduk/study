For nonnegative integer powers in $\sin^m x\cos^n x$: if $m$ is odd, save one sine factor and convert the rest to cosine; if $n$ is odd, save one cosine factor and convert the rest to sine. If both are even, use

$$
\sin^2x=(1-\cos2x)/2,\quad\cos^2x=(1+\cos2x)/2.
$$

**Pattern.** Different frequencies often favor product-to-sum instead of a power substitution.
