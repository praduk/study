For continuous $f$ and differentiable bounds $u,v$ taking values in its interval of continuity,

$$
\frac d{dx}\int_{u(x)}^{v(x)}f(t)\,dt=f(v(x))v'(x)-f(u(x))u'(x).
$$

**Pattern.** Make a separate contribution from each endpoint; the lower one is subtracted. This formula assumes the integrand itself has no separate dependence on $x$.
