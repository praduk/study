For continuous graphs with upper function $u$ and lower function $l$ on $[a,b]$,

$$
A=\int_a^b(u-l)\,dx.
$$

If order changes, split at crossings or integrate $|f-g|$. A signed integral can cancel while geometric area cannot.

**Pattern.** Solve intersections first, then test which graph is above on each interval.
