On intervals where the integrands are defined,

$$
\int\sin x\,dx=-\cos x+C,\quad\int\cos x\,dx=\sin x+C,
$$

$$
\int\sec^2x\,dx=\tan x+C,\quad\int\sec x\tan x\,dx=\sec x+C,
$$

$$
\int\csc^2x\,dx=-\cot x+C,\quad\int\csc x\cot x\,dx=-\csc x+C.
$$

**Pattern.** Recognize a derivative pair and account for the inner-frequency factor.
