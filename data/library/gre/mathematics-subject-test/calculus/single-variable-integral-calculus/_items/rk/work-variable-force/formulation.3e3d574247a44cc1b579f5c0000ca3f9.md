For motion along an oriented coordinate $x$ and force component $F(x)$ in that direction, work is

$$
W=\int_a^b F(x)\,dx.
$$

A spring with stiffness $k>0$ requires applied force $kx$ to hold displacement $x$ from equilibrium; quasistatic work to stretch it from $a$ to $b$ is $k(b^2-a^2)/2$ for $0\le a<b$.

**Pattern.** Integrate force over displacement, not force over time. Track units and the sign of force relative to motion.
