On an interval where the power is differentiable,

$$
\int x^p\,dx=\frac{x^{p+1}}{p+1}+C\quad(p\ne-1).
$$

For $x\ne0$, $\int dx/x=\ln|x|+C$, with constants chosen separately on disconnected intervals.

**Pattern.** Check the exceptional exponent $-1$ before applying the power rule; verify an antiderivative by differentiating.
