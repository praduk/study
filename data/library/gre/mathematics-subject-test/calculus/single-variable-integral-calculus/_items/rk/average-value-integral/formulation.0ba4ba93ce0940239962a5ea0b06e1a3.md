For integrable $f$ on $[a,b]$, $a<b$,

$$
f_{\rm avg}=\frac1{b-a}\int_a^b f(x)\,dx.
$$

If $f$ is continuous, some $c\in[a,b]$ satisfies $f(c)=f_{\rm avg}$. The point is not generally the midpoint and need not be unique.

**Pattern.** Divide total accumulation by interval length.
