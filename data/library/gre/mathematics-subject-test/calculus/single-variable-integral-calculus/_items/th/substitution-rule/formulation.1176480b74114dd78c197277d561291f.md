For continuous $f$ and a continuously differentiable $g$,

$$
\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)}f(u)\,du.
$$

**Pattern.** An inner expression next to its derivative suggests substitution. Change both limits, or return to the old variable before evaluating; never mix them.
