For continuously differentiable $u,v$ on $[a,b]$,

$$
\int_a^b u(x)v'(x)\,dx=[uv]_a^b-\int_a^b u'(x)v(x)\,dx.
$$

For an indefinite integral, write $\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du$. Choose a factor that simplifies when differentiated. For improper endpoints apply the finite-interval formula first and take justified limits.

**Pattern.** Polynomial-times-exponential and logarithmic factors are common cues.
