**Answer.** $4/5$.

**Pattern cue.** Use a right triangle and the inverse-function range.

**Fast route.** Let $\theta=\arctan(-3/4)$. Then $-\pi/2<\theta<0$, so cosine is positive. A triangle with leg magnitudes $3,4$ has hypotenuse $5$, giving $\cos\theta=4/5$.

**Trap check.** A negative tangent does not make cosine negative on the arctangent branch.
