**Pattern cue.** Combine equal-frequency terms into one sinusoid.

**Fast route.** $f(x)=\sqrt2\cos(3x-\pi/4)$. A full phase change is $2\pi$, so $3T=2\pi$. A shift of $\pi/3$ negates the function and is not a period.

**Trap check.** A sign reversal is not a period unless the function is identically zero.
