**Answer.** The entire line $y=2$.

**Pattern cue.** Replace a polar product by its Cartesian coordinate.

**Fast route.** $r\sin\theta=y$, so every represented point has $y=2$. Conversely, every $(x,2)$ has $r=\sqrt{x^2+4}>0$ and an angle in $(0,\pi)$, so no point of the line is lost.

**Trap check.** Check both directions when claiming equality of loci.
