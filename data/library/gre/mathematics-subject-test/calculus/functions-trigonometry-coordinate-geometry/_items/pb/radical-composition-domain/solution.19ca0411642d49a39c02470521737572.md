**Correct choice: (C).**

**Fast route.** The radical requires

$$
2-\frac{1}{x+1}=\frac{2x+1}{x+1}\ge 0,
$$

with $x\ne-1$. A sign chart at $-1$ and $-\tfrac12$ gives

$$
(-\infty,-1)\cup[-\tfrac12,\infty).
$$

The endpoint $-\tfrac12$ is allowed because the radicand is zero. The point $-1$ is never allowed because $g$ is undefined there.
