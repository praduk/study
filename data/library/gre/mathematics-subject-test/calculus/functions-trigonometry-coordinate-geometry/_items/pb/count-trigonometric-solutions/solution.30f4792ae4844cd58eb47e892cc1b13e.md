**Correct choice: (E).**

**Fast route.** Factor instead of dividing by $\cos x$:

$$
2\sin x\cos x=\cos x
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\cos x(2\sin x-1)=0.
$$

Thus $x=\frac\pi2,\frac{3\pi}{2}$ or $x=\frac\pi6,\frac{5\pi}{6}$. These four values are distinct. Dividing by $\cos x$ would incorrectly discard two solutions.
