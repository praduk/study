For real functions $f,g$, the domain of $f\circ g$ is exactly

$$
\{x\in\operatorname{dom}g:g(x)\in\operatorname{dom}f\}.
$$

Require positive logarithm arguments, nonnegative even-root radicands, and nonzero denominators. A restriction from the original expression survives cancellation.

**Pattern.** Intersect restrictions; use a sign chart for rational inequalities rather than multiplying by a denominator of unknown sign.
