On a domain symmetric about $0$, $f$ is even iff $f(-x)=f(x)$ and odd iff $f(-x)=-f(x)$. A positive number $T$ is a period if the domain is invariant under translation by $T$ and $f(x+T)=f(x)$ throughout the domain.

Sine and tangent are odd; cosine is even. Sine/cosine have least positive period $2\pi$, tangent has $\pi$. For nonzero $b$, the least period of $\sin(bx+c)$ or $\cos(bx+c)$ is $2\pi/|b|$, and that of $\tan(bx+c)$ is $\pi/|b|$.

**Trap.** A shifted even function need not be even about $0$.
