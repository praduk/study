The real principal branches are $\arcsin:[-1,1]\to[-\pi/2,\pi/2]$, $\arccos:[-1,1]\to[0,\pi]$, and $\arctan:\R\to(-\pi/2,\pi/2)$.

**Pattern.** Reduce an angle using its sine/cosine/tangent, then select the representative in the principal range. In general $\arcsin(\sin x)\ne x$.
