For real $x$,

$$
\sin2x=2\sin x\cos x,
$$

$$
\cos2x=\cos^2x-\sin^2x=1-2\sin^2x=2\cos^2x-1.
$$

Therefore $\sin^2x=(1-\cos2x)/2$ and $\cos^2x=(1+\cos2x)/2$.

**Pattern.** Use double angles to factor equations; use power reduction to integrate even powers. Never divide out a potentially zero trigonometric factor.
