For $C^2$ functions $x(t),y(t)$ at a point where $x'(t)\ne0$,

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{y'}{x'},\qquad\frac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\frac1{x'}\frac d{dt}\left(\frac{y'}{x'}\right).
$$

**Pattern.** Compute the first quotient, differentiate that quotient in $t$, then divide by $x'$ again. If $x'=0$, inspect the tangent separately.
