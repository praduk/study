For real $x,y$,

$$
\sin x\cos y=\frac{\sin(x+y)+\sin(x-y)}2,
$$

$$
\cos x\sin y=\frac{\sin(x+y)-\sin(x-y)}2,
$$

$$
\cos x\cos y=\frac{\cos(x+y)+\cos(x-y)}2,
$$

$$
\sin x\sin y=\frac{\cos(x-y)-\cos(x+y)}2.
$$

**Pattern.** A product of different frequencies becomes a sum that integrates directly; derive the sign from the addition formulas if uncertain.
