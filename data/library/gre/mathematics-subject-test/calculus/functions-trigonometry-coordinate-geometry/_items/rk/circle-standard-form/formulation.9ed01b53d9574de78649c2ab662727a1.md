A circle with center $(h,k)$ and radius $r>0$ has equation $(x-h)^2+(y-k)^2=r^2$. At $(x_0,y_0)$ on the circle, its tangent is

$$
(x_0-h)(x-x_0)+(y_0-k)(y-y_0)=0.
$$

**Pattern.** Complete both squares before reading the radius; for a tangent use the radius as the normal.
