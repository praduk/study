Where denominators are nonzero,

$$
\tan x=\frac{\sin x}{\cos x},\quad\cot x=\frac{\cos x}{\sin x},\quad\sec x=\frac1{\cos x},\quad\csc x=\frac1{\sin x}.
$$

The anchor $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$ gives $1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x$ and $1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x$ by division.

**Pattern.** Reduce to sine/cosine, then use one quadratic identity. Squaring loses quadrant signs.
