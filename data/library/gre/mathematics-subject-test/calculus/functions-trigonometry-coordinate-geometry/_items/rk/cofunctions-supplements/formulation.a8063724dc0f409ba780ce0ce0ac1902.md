For real $x$,

$$
\sin(\pi/2-x)=\cos x,\quad\cos(\pi/2-x)=\sin x,
$$

$$
\sin(\pi-x)=\sin x,\quad\cos(\pi-x)=-\cos x.
$$

Thus $\tan(\pi-x)=-\tan x$ wherever defined. Complementary angles interchange sine/cosine; supplementary angles do not.

**Pattern.** Use a unit-circle reflection to determine the sign before simplifying.
