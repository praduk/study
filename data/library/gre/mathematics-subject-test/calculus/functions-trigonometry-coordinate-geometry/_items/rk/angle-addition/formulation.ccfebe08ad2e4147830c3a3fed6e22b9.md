For real $x,y$,

$$
\sin(x+y)=\sin x\cos y+\cos x\sin y,
$$

$$
\cos(x+y)=\cos x\cos y-\sin x\sin y.
$$

Replace $y$ by $-y$ for subtraction. Dividing yields $\tan(x+y)=(\tan x+\tan y)/(1-\tan x\tan y)$ when every term is defined.

**Pattern.** Build $15^\circ$ and $75^\circ$ from $45^\circ$ and $30^\circ$. The cosine addition formula carries the minus sign.
