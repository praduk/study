For a nonnegative continuous radius $r(\theta)$ on $[\alpha,\beta]$, the area swept is

$$
A=\frac12\int_\alpha^\beta r(\theta)^2\,d\theta,
$$

provided the region is swept once (up to boundary overlaps). Integrating repeated sweeps counts area repeatedly.

**Pattern.** Identify a single loop or full tracing interval before integrating; power reduction handles $\sin^2$ and $\cos^2$.
