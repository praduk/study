For $a,b>0$, $x^2/a^2-y^2/b^2=1$ has foci $(\pm\sqrt{a^2+b^2},0)$ and asymptotes $y=\pm(b/a)x$. The positive squared term gives the transverse axis. Translating the center replaces $x,y$ by $x-h,y-k$.

**Pattern.** Focal distance uses a sum; slopes use square roots of the denominators.
