For $a\ge b>0$, the ellipse $x^2/a^2+y^2/b^2=1$ has horizontal semiaxis $a$, vertical semiaxis $b$, and foci $(\pm c,0)$ where $c^2=a^2-b^2$. When $a=b$ it is a circle with coincident foci.

**Pattern.** The larger denominator identifies the major axis; implicit differentiation finds a tangent without solving for $y$. Swap coordinates for a vertical major axis.
