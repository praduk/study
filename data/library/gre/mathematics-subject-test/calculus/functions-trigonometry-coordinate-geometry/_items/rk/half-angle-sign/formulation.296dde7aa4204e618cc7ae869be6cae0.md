For real $x$,

$$
\sin(x/2)=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1-\cos x}{2}},\qquad\cos(x/2)=\pm\sqrt{\frac{1+\cos x}{2}}.
$$

Choose each sign from the quadrant of $x/2$, not the quadrant of $x$.

**Pattern.** First halve the interval containing the angle; only then evaluate the square root.
