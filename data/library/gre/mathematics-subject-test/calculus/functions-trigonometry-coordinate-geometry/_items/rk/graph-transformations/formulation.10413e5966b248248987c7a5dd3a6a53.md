If $(u,f(u))$ lies on a graph, then for $b\ne0$ it becomes

$$
\left(h+\frac{u}{b},\;af(u)+k\right)
$$

on $y=af(b(x-h))+k$. Horizontal lengths scale by $1/|b|$ and vertical lengths by $|a|$; negative factors reflect the corresponding coordinate. For $a=0$ the output is constant on the inherited domain.

**Pattern.** Transform a point or a range directly; avoid guessing the direction of an inside shift.
