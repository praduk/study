At $0,\pi/6,\pi/4,\pi/3,\pi/2$, sine equals $0,1/2,\sqrt2/2,\sqrt3/2,1$; cosine is the reverse list. On the unit circle, cosine is the horizontal coordinate and sine the vertical coordinate. This fixes signs in every quadrant; tangent is their quotient where defined.

**Pattern.** Reduce to a reference angle before using an addition formula; a sign or magnitude check often eliminates choices.
