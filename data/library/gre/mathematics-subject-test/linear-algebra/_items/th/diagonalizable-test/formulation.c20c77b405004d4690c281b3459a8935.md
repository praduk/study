A matrix $A\in M_n(F)$ is diagonalizable over $F$ exactly when:

1. its characteristic polynomial splits into linear factors over $F$; and
2. for each eigenvalue $\lambda$, geometric multiplicity equals algebraic multiplicity.

The splitting condition cannot be omitted.

**Memory cue (added).** All roots available; all eigenspaces full.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 17.

**Trap (sheet, p. 30).** Repeated eigenvalues do not by themselves prevent diagonalization.
