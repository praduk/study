For $n\times n$ matrices over a field,

$$
\det(AB)=\det A\,\det B.
$$

If $A$ is invertible, then $\det(A^{-1})=1/\det A$.

**Memory cue (added).** Determinants turn matrix multiplication into scalar multiplication.
