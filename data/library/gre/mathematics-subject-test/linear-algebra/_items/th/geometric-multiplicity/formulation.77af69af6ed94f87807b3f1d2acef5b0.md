For a matrix $A$ with characteristic polynomial $p_A$ and eigenspace $E_\lambda=\ker(A-\lambda I)$, consider an eigenvalue $\lambda$. Its geometric multiplicity is $\dim E_\lambda$; its algebraic multiplicity is its multiplicity as a root of $p_A$. They satisfy

$$
1\le\dim E_\lambda\le
\operatorname{mult}_{p_A}(\lambda).
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Independent eigenvectors cannot outnumber repeated polynomial roots.
