For $A\in M_n(F)$ over a field,

$$
A\text{ invertible}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\det A\ne0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{rank}A=n.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Square and full rank means invertible.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 16.

**Recognition cue (sheet, p. 30).** For a square matrix, connect determinant, rank, eigenvalues, and invertibility.
