For $A\in M_n(F)$, the following are equivalent:

- $A$ is invertible;
- its columns are linearly independent;
- its columns span $F^n$;
- its rows are linearly independent.

Thus the columns and rows form bases when $A$ is invertible.

**Memory cue (added).** For n vectors in n dimensions, independent and spanning coincide.
