If the eigenvalues of $A\in M_n(F)$ are $\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_n$ in a splitting field, counted with algebraic multiplicity, then

$$
\operatorname{tr}A=\sum_{i=1}^n\lambda_i,
\qquad
\det A=\prod_{i=1}^n\lambda_i.
$$

Diagonalizability is not required.

**Memory cue (added).** Trace adds; determinant multiplies.
