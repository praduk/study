For a linear map $T:V\to W$ with $V$ finite-dimensional,

$$
\dim V=\dim(\im T)+\dim(\ker T)
=\operatorname{rank}T+\operatorname{nullity}T.
$$

For an $m\times n$ matrix $A$, this becomes $\operatorname{rank}A+\dim\ker A=n$.

**Memory cue (added).** Input dimension = visible output directions + lost directions.
