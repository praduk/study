If $u_1,\ldots,u_k$ is an **orthonormal** basis of a subspace $W\subseteq\R^n$, then

$$
\operatorname{proj}_Wv=\sum_{i=1}^k(v\cdot u_i)u_i.
$$

The residual is perpendicular to every vector in $W$.

**Memory cue (added).** Add the independent shadows onto the unit basis directions.
