Starting from linearly independent $v_1,\ldots,v_k$ in $\R^n$, set $u_1=v_1$ and

$$
u_j=v_j-\sum_{i=1}^{j-1}
\frac{v_j\cdot u_i}{u_i\cdot u_i}\,u_i.
$$

Then the $u_j$ are nonzero and mutually orthogonal. Normalize with $e_j=u_j/|u_j|$ for an orthonormal basis of the same span.

**Memory cue (added).** Subtract old shadows, then normalize.
