Vectors $v_1,\ldots,v_k\in\R^n$ form an orthogonal set when $v_i\cdot v_j=0$ for $i\ne j$.

They form an orthonormal set when they are orthogonal and $|v_i|=1$ for every $i$.

**Memory cue (added).** Orthogonal: right angles. Orthonormal: right angles plus unit lengths.
