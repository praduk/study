For $A\in M_n(F)$, a scalar $\lambda\in F$ is an eigenvalue if there exists $v\ne0$ with

$$
Av=\lambda v.
$$

Such a vector is an eigenvector; the eigenspace is $E_\lambda=\ker(A-\lambda I)$.

**Memory cue (added).** A nonzero direction is scaled without turning.
