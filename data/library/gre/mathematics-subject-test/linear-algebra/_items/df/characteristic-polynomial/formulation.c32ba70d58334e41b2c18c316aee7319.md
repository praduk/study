Following the sheet, use

$$
p_A(\lambda)=\det(A-\lambda I).
$$

Its roots are the eigenvalues in a splitting field. The other common convention $\det(\lambda I-A)$ differs by $(-1)^n$ and has the same roots.

**Memory cue (added).** Choose one determinant order; roots do not change.
