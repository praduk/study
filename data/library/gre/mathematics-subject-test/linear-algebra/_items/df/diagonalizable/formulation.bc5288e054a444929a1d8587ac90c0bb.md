A matrix $A\in M_n(F)$ is diagonalizable **over $F$** if there is an invertible $P\in M_n(F)$ and a diagonal $D\in M_n(F)$ such that

$$
A=PDP^{-1}.
$$

Equivalently, $F^n$ has a basis of eigenvectors of $A$. The columns of $P$ are those eigenvectors.

**Memory cue (added).** P stores the eigenvector basis; D stores its scales.

**Source:** StudySheet.pdf, p. 17.
