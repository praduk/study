The sheet’s recovery method is

$$
e^{i(x+y)}=e^{ix}e^{iy}.
$$

Using $e^{it}=\cos t+i\sin t$,

$$
\cos(x+y)+i\sin(x+y)
=(\cos x+i\sin x)(\cos y+i\sin y).
$$

Real parts recover cosine addition; imaginary parts recover sine addition.

**Memory cue (added).** Real gives cosine; imaginary gives sine. The negative cosine cross-term comes from $i^2=-1$.
