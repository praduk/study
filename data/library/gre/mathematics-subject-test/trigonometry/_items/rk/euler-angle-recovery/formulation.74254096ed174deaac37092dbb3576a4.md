The sheet’s recovery method is

$$
e^{i(x+y)}=e^{ix}e^{iy}.
$$

Using $e^{it}=\cos t+i\sin t$,

$$
\cos(x+y)+i\sin(x+y)
=(\cos x+i\sin x)(\cos y+i\sin y).
$$

Real parts recover cosine addition; imaginary parts recover sine addition.

For $n^\text{th}$ angle formulas use
$$
e^{inx} = \left( e^{ix} \right)^n.
$$

For example
$$
\aligned{
\cos(nx) + i\sin(nx) &= (\cos(x) + i\sin(x))^n
}
$$

For $n=2$,
$$
\aligned{
\cos(2x) + i\sin(2x) &= (\cos(x) + i\sin(x))^2 \\
&= ( \cos^2 x - \sin^2 x ) + i(2\sin x\cos x) \\
&= (1 - 2\sin^2 x) + i(2\sin x\cos x) \\
&= (2 \cos^2 x - 1) + i(2\sin x\cos x) \\
}
$$


**Memory cue (added).** Real gives cosine; imaginary gives sine. The negative cosine cross-term comes from $i^2=-1$.
