As stated in the sheet:

- Quadrant I: all six are positive.
- Quadrant II: sine and cosecant are positive.
- Quadrant III: tangent and cotangent are positive.
- Quadrant IV: cosine and secant are positive.

The other functions are negative within each open quadrant; on axes some values vanish or are undefined.
