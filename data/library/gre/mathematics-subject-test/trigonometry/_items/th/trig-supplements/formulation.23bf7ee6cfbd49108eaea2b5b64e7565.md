

$$
\sin(\pi-x)=\sin x,\qquad
\cos(\pi-x)=-\cos x,\qquad
\tan(\pi-x)=-\tan x
$$

where defined.

**Memory cue (added).** Supplement lands across the vertical axis: sine stays, cosine changes, so tangent changes.

Source: StudySheet.pdf, p. 3.

**Trap (sheet, p. 30).** $\sin(\pi-x)=\sin x$; the cosine identity comes from the complementary angle $\pi/2-x$.
