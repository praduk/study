

$$
\tan(x/2)=\frac{\sin x}{1+\cos x}
\quad(1+\cos x\ne0),
$$

$$
\tan(x/2)=\frac{1-\cos x}{\sin x}
\quad(\sin x\ne0).
$$

Each formula holds on its own stated domain; the second loses some points where tangent itself is defined.

**Memory cue (added).** Sine over one-plus-cosine; rationalize to one-minus-cosine over sine.
