For real $x$,

$$
\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1.
$$

Dividing by $\cos^2x$ or $\sin^2x$ gives, where defined,

$$
1+\tan^2x=\sec^2x,\qquad
1+\cot^2x=\csc^2x.
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Keep the unit-circle identity; divide it to recover the other two.
