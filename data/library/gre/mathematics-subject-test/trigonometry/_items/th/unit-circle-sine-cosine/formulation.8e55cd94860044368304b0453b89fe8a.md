In the order $0,\pi/6,\pi/4,\pi/3,\pi/2$,

$$
\sin x:\quad \frac{\sqrt0}{2},\frac{\sqrt1}{2},
\frac{\sqrt2}{2},\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\frac{\sqrt4}{2}.
$$

Cosine has the same list in reverse order.

**Memory cue (added).** Square roots zero through four, all over two; reverse for cosine.
