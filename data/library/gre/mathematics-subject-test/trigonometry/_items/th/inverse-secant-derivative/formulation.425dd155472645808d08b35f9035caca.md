Using $\operatorname{arcsec}x=\arccos(1/x)$ with values in $[0,\pi]\setminus\{\pi/2\}$, for $|x|>1$,

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\operatorname{arcsec}x
=\frac1{|x|\sqrt{x^2-1}}.
$$

Other inverse-secant branch conventions can change the derivative’s sign.

**Memory cue (added).** Secant inverse: outside absolute $x$, inside square-minus-one.
