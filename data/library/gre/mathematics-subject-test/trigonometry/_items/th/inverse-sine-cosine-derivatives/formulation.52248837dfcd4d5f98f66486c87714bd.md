For $-1<x<1$, with principal inverse functions,

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\arcsin x=\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}},\qquad
\frac{d}{dx}\arccos x=-\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}}.
$$

There is no finite derivative at the endpoints.
