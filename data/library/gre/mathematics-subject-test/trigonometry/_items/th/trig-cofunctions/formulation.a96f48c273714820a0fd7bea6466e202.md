

$$
\sin(\pi/2-x)=\cos x,\qquad
\cos(\pi/2-x)=\sin x,
$$

$$
\tan(\pi/2-x)=\cot x, \qquad
\cot(\pi/2-x)=\tan x
$$

where the quotients are defined.  Note

$$
\sin(\pi/2 \pm x)=\cos x,\qquad
\cos(\pi/2 \pm x)=\mp \sin x,
$$

**Memory cue (added).** Complement swaps each function with its cofunction.
