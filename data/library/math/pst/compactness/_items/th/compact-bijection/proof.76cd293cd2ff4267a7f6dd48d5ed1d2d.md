Let $f:X\to Y$ satisfy the hypotheses. A closed $F\subseteq X$ is compact; its image $f(F)$ is compact and therefore closed in the Hausdorff space $Y$. Thus $f$ is a closed bijection. For any closed $F\subseteq X$, the preimage of $F$ under $f^{-1}$ is $f(F)$, which is closed. Therefore $f^{-1}$ is continuous.
