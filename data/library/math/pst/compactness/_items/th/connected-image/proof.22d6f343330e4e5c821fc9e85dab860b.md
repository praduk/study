A separation of the image pulls back to disjoint nonempty open sets covering the domain, a contradiction. For the second assertion, suppose $B=U\cup V$ is a separation in the relative topology. Connectedness forces $A$ wholly into one side, say $U$. For a point $v\in V$, write $V=B\cap W$ with $W$ ambient open containing $v$. Then $W$ misses $A$ because $A\subseteq B$ and $V\cap A=\nulset$. This contradicts $v\in\closure A$.
