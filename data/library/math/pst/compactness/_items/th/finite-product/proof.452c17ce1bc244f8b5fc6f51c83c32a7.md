The result is immediate if a factor is empty. Given an open cover and $(x,y)$, choose an open rectangle $U_{x,y}\times V_{x,y}$ containing $(x,y)$ inside a cover member. Fixing $x$, compactness of $Y$ gives finitely many $V_{x,y_j}$ covering $Y$. Set $U_x=\bigcap_j U_{x,y_j}$. Then $U_x\times Y$ is covered by those finitely many original cover members. Finitely many $U_x$ cover $X$ by compactness of $X$. Combining the corresponding finite collections covers the product.
