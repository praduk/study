Every second-countable regular space, with regularity including $T_1$, admits a metric inducing its topology.

This theorem is used without proof here. Its standard proof separates points from closed sets by continuous functions and embeds the space in a countable product. The $T_1$ hypothesis is essential under conventions that otherwise omit it from regularity. See the inspected [Ivan Khatchatourian notes, Urysohn metrization theorem](https://www.math.toronto.edu/ivan/mat327/docs/notes/15-umt.pdf).
