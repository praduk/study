In set theory with the axiom of choice, an arbitrary product of compact spaces is compact in the product topology.

This is Tychonoff's theorem, used without proof here; the finite-product proof does not by itself justify an arbitrary product. The statement concerns the product topology, not the box topology. See the inspected [Ivan Khatchatourian notes, Tychonoff's theorem](https://www.math.toronto.edu/ivan/mat327/docs/notes/17-tychonoff.pdf).
