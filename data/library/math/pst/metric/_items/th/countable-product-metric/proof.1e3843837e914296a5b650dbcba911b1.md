The series converges uniformly by comparison with $\sum2^{-n}$. Symmetry and definiteness are immediate, and $\min(1,a+b)\le\min(1,a)+\min(1,b)$ gives the triangle inequality. To fit a $D$-ball inside a finite-coordinate neighborhood of $x$, choose sufficiently small positive coordinate radii in the restricted indices; a $D$-distance below the minimum of their weighted truncated radii enforces all restrictions. Conversely, for a prescribed $D$-radius, choose $N$ making the tail sum small, then restrict the first $N$ coordinate distances so their weighted sum is smaller than the remaining allowance. This supplies a product neighborhood inside the ball. The two topologies coincide.
