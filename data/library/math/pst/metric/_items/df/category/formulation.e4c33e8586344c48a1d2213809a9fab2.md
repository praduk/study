A subset $A$ is nowhere dense when $\Int(\closure A)=\nulset$. A meagre set is a countable union of nowhere dense sets. A space is Baire when every countable intersection of dense open sets is dense; equivalently no nonempty open subset is meagre in the ambient space.

Meagre and measure zero are different notions. These definitions concern topological size and do not refer to a measure.
