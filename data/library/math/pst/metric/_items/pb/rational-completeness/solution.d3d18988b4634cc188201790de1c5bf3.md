In the usual topology on $\Q$, each singleton is closed with empty interior, so it is @[nowhere dense]math:pst:metric:df:category. Since $\Q$ is countable, it is a countable union of nowhere dense subsets of itself. It is also a nonempty open subset of itself. Thus it is not Baire. The Baire theorem implies that any topology induced by a complete metric is Baire. Since being Baire depends only on open sets, there can be no compatible complete metric on $\Q$.
