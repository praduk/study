Singletons are closed: if $x\ne a$, the ball about $x$ of radius $d(x,a)/2$ excludes $a$. For disjoint nonempty closed sets $A,B$, the distance functions $d(x,A)=\inf_{a\in A}d(x,a)$ and $d(x,B)$ are continuous, since their value changes by at most $d(x,y)$. At a point of $A$, distance to $B$ is positive because $B$ is closed; similarly with roles reversed. Therefore $U=\{x:d(x,A)<d(x,B)\}$ and $V=\{x:d(x,B)<d(x,A)\}$ are disjoint open sets containing $A,B$. If a set is empty, separation is immediate.
