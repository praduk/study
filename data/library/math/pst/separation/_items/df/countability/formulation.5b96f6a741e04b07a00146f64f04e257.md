A space is first countable if each point has a countable neighborhood basis, second countable if its topology has a countable basis, separable if it has a countable dense subset, and Lindelöf if every open cover has a countable subcover.

These are distinct conditions. @[Metric spaces]math:uganalysis:metric-multivariable:df:metric-completeness are first countable, since the balls of radii $1/n$ form a neighborhood basis. An uncountable discrete space is metrizable but not second countable or separable.
