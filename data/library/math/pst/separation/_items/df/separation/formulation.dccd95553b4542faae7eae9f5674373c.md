A space is $T_1$ if every singleton is closed; it is Hausdorff if distinct points have disjoint open neighborhoods. It is regular here if it is $T_1$ and every point and disjoint closed set have disjoint open neighborhoods. It is normal here if it is $T_1$ and every two disjoint closed sets have disjoint open neighborhoods.

These conventions include $T_1$ in regularity and normality; some texts omit it. A regular space is Hausdorff by applying the definition to a singleton.
