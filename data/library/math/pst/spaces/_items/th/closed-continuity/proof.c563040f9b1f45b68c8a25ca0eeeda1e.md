Suppose $f$ is continuous and $x\in\closure A$. Each open neighborhood $V$ of $f(x)$ has open preimage containing $x$, so that preimage meets $A$. Thus $V$ meets $f(A)$ and the closure test gives the inclusion. Conversely, let $F\subseteq Y$ be closed and set $A=f^{-1}(F)$. The assumed inclusion gives $f(\closure A)\subseteq\closure{f(A)}\subseteq F$. Hence $\closure A\subseteq A$, so $A$ is closed. Preimages of all closed sets are closed, which is continuity.
