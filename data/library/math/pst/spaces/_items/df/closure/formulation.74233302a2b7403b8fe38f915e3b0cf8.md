For $A\subseteq X$, $\Int A$ is the union of open subsets of $A$; $\closure A$ is the intersection of closed subsets containing $A$; and $\partial A=\closure A\setminus\Int A$. A point $x$ is an accumulation point of $A$ when every neighborhood of $x$ meets $A\setminus\{x\}$.

A subset is dense when $\closure A=X$. Accumulation is different from membership in the closure: an isolated point of $A$ lies in its closure but need not be an accumulation point.
