A family $\mathcal B\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$ is a basis if it covers $X$ and, whenever $x\in B_1\cap B_2$ with $B_1,B_2\in\mathcal B$, some $B_3\in\mathcal B$ satisfies $x\in B_3\subseteq B_1\cap B_2$. It generates the unions of its members.

A subbasis is a family whose finite intersections form a basis; the empty finite intersection is $X$. A neighborhood of $x$ is any set containing an open set that contains $x$. A neighborhood basis at $x$ refines every neighborhood of $x$.
