A surjection $q:X\to Y$ is a quotient map when $U\subseteq Y$ is open exactly when $q^{-1}(U)$ is open. Given an @[equivalence relation]math:set:relations-functions:df:equivalence-relation on $X$, its quotient set receives this topology under the map to equivalence classes.

A subset $A\subseteq X$ is saturated if it is a union of fibers, equivalently $q^{-1}(q(A))=A$. Quotient openness is tested by saturated preimages; a continuous surjection alone need not be quotient.
