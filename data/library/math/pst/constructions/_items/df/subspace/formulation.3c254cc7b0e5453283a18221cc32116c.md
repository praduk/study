For $A\subseteq X$, the subspace topology consists of $A\cap U$ with $U$ open in $X$. An embedding $f:Y\to X$ is a homeomorphism from $Y$ onto $f(Y)$ with its subspace topology.

A map $g:Z\to A$ is continuous exactly when its composite with inclusion $A\injectto X$ is continuous. Indeed preimages of $A\cap U$ are precisely preimages of $U$ under the composite. Relative openness need not be openness in the ambient space.
