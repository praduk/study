The product topology on $\prod_{i\in I}X_i$ has basis $\prod_i U_i$, where each $U_i$ is open and $U_i=X_i$ for all but finitely many indices. It is the coarsest topology making every coordinate projection continuous. The box topology allows restrictions in every coordinate and can be strictly finer for infinite products.

The product indexed by the empty set is a one-point space. If a factor is empty, the product is empty.
