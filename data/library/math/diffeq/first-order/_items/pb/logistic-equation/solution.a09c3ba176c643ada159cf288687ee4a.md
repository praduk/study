For $y_0\ne K$, separation gives $\log|y/(K-y)|=rt+C$, yielding

$$
y(t)=\frac{K}{1+(K/y_0-1)e^{-rt}}.
$$

The same formula gives the equilibrium $K$ when $y_0=K$. For $t\ge0$ the denominator is positive: if its coefficient is negative its minimum is at $0$, where it equals $K/y_0>0$. Thus the forward solution exists and tends to $K$. Direct differentiation verifies both equation and initial value. Dividing by $y(1-y/K)$ excludes $y=0$ and $y=K$; both are solutions and must be checked separately.
