For $y''+P(x)y'+Q(x)y=0$, a point $x_0$ is ordinary when $P,Q$ are analytic near $x_0$. A singular point is regular singular if $(x-x_0)P(x)$ and $(x-x_0)^2Q(x)$ extend analytically near $x_0$. Other singular points are irregular. These definitions refer to the normalized equation. For $x^2y''+axy'+by=0$, zero is regular singular unless the normalized coefficients already extend there.
