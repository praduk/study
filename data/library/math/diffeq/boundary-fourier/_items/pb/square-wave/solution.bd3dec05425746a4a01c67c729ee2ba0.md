Oddness gives $a_n=0$. For $n\ge1$,

$$
b_n=\frac2\pi\int_0^\pi\sin(nx)\,dx=\frac{2(1-(-1)^n)}{\pi n}.
$$

Thus the series is $\frac4\pi\sum_{k=0}^\infty\sin((2k+1)x)/(2k+1)$. The piecewise-smooth convergence theorem gives the specified value $f(x)$ between jumps and the mean $0$ at every multiple of $\pi$, regardless of the assigned single-point values of $f$ there. The odd harmonics are essential; even coefficients vanish exactly.
