If $\lambda=0$, the affine solution and both conditions force zero. If $\lambda=-k^2<0$, the solution is $A\cosh kx+B\sinh kx$; the endpoint conditions again force zero. For $\lambda=k^2>0$, $y=A\cos kx+B\sin kx$. The first condition sets $A=0$; a nonzero $B$ requires $kL=n\pi$ for a positive integer $n$. Thus $\lambda_n=(n\pi/L)^2$ and the eigenfunctions are nonzero multiples of $\sin(n\pi x/L)$. These directly satisfy the equation and boundary data.
