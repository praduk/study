On a finite interval $[a,b]$, a regular Sturm–Liouville problem is $-(p y')'+qy=\lambda wy$, where $p\in C^1$, $q,w$ are continuous real functions, $p,w>0$, with separated homogeneous boundary conditions $\alpha_1y(a)+\alpha_2p(a)y'(a)=0$ and $\beta_1y(b)+\beta_2p(b)y'(b)=0$. Each coefficient pair is real and not both zero. An eigenvalue is a scalar $\lambda$ admitting a nonzero solution, called an eigenfunction. The weighted inner product of real functions is $\langle u,v\rangle_w=\int_a^b uvw\,dx$.
