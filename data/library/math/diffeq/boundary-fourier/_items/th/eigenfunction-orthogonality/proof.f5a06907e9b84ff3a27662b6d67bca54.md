Multiply the equation for $u$ by $v$, the equation for $v$ by $u$, and subtract. Integration by parts yields

$$
(\lambda-\mu)\int_a^b uvw\,dx=[p(uv'-u'v)]_a^b.
$$

At either endpoint the two vectors $(u,pu')$ and $(v,pv')$ lie in the same one-dimensional subspace defined by the nonzero boundary coefficient pair; their determinant is therefore zero. The boundary term vanishes, and division by $\lambda-\mu\ne0$ proves orthogonality.
