For $x'=-x^3$ and initial value $x_0$, direct separation gives $x(t)=x_0/\sqrt{1+2x_0^2t}$ for $t\ge0$, including zero. Its magnitude never exceeds $|x_0|$ and tends to zero, so the equilibrium is asymptotically stable. For $x'=x^3$, the solution for nonzero $x_0$ is $x_0/\sqrt{1-2x_0^2t}$ until blow-up. Every positive initial value, however small, eventually leaves any fixed small neighborhood, so zero is unstable. In both cases $f'(0)=0$; the linearization gives no conclusion.
