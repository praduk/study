Along a solution in the ball, the chain rule gives $(e^{ct}V(x(t)))'\le0$, so $V(x(t))\le e^{-ct}V(x(0))$. Choose a smaller closed ball of radius $r$ contained in the domain and take $V(x(0))<ar^2$. The solution cannot first reach its boundary, since that would require $V\ge ar^2$. It stays in a compact subset of the domain; boundedness of $f$ there makes the trajectory uniformly Lipschitz in time, so at a finite maximal endpoint it has a limit, and local existence at that limit extends it. Thus it exists for all forward time. Combining the two quadratic bounds yields the estimate. Making the initial norm small gives stability, and the exponential tends to zero.
