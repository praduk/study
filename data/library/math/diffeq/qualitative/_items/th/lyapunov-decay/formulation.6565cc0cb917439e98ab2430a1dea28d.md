Let $f$ be locally Lipschitz near $0\in\R^n$, with $f(0)=0$. Suppose a $C^1$ function $V$ and positive constants $a,b,c$ satisfy

$$
a\norm{x}^2\le V(x)\le b\norm{x}^2,\qquad DV(x)f(x)\le-cV(x)
$$

on a ball about zero. Then zero is asymptotically stable, and sufficiently small initial states satisfy $\norm{x(t)}\le\sqrt{b/a}\,e^{-ct/2}\norm{x(0)}$.
