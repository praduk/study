Let $\kappa,L>0$ and $f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^N b_n\sin(n\pi x/L)$. The classical solution of $u_t=\kappa u_{xx}$ on $0<x<L$, with $u(0,t)=u(L,t)=0$ and $u(x,0)=f(x)$, is

$$
u(x,t)=\sum_{n=1}^N b_ne^{-\kappa(n\pi/L)^2t}\sin(n\pi x/L).
$$

Uniqueness holds among solutions with continuous $u,u_t,u_x,u_{xx}$ on each closed finite time strip.
