The finite-mode formula yields $u(x,t)=3e^{-t}\sin x-2e^{-9t}\sin3x$. Both endpoint conditions and the initial data follow immediately, and differentiating gives $u_t=u_{xx}$. The relative coefficient magnitude of the third mode to the first is $(2/3)e^{-8t}\to0$. More precisely $e^t u(x,t)\to3\sin x$ uniformly on $[0,\pi]$, because the supremum of the remaining term is at most $2e^{-8t}$.
