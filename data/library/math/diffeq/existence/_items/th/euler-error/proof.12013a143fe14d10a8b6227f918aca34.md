Taylor’s formula with integral remainder bounds the one-step defect by $Kh^2/2$. Thus $e_{j+1}\le(1+hL)e_j+Kh^2/2$, $e_0=0$. Sum the geometric progression to get $e_j\le(Kh/(2L))((1+hL)^j-1)$ when $L>0$, then use $1+hL\le e^{hL}$. When $L=0$, the recursion sums to $jKh^2/2$. Global Lipschitz control on the strip ensures the estimate applies at the numerical values as well as the exact solution.
