A function $f:D\subset\R\times\R^n\to\R^n$ is locally Lipschitz in its state variable uniformly near $(t_0,y_0)$ if some rectangular neighborhood in $D$ and a constant $L\ge0$ satisfy $\norm{f(t,u)-f(t,v)}\le L\norm{u-v}$ for points in that neighborhood with the same time coordinate. A continuous state derivative $D_yf$ implies this property on a sufficiently small compact convex rectangle by integrating the derivative along the segment from $u$ to $v$. Continuity alone is weaker; $f(y)=\sqrt{|y|}$ is continuous but not Lipschitz near zero.
