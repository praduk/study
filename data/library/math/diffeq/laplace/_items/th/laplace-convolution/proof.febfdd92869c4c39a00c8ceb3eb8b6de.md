For large $s$, the double integral of $e^{-s(u+v)}|f(u)g(v)|$ over $u,v\ge0$ is the product of two finite absolute Laplace integrals. Absolute integrability justifies changing variables $t=u+v$ and the order of integration, giving the convolution formula. For delay, the transform integral starts at $a$; substitution $u=t-a$ factors out $e^{-as}$. The value of the step at the single point $a$ does not affect either integral.
