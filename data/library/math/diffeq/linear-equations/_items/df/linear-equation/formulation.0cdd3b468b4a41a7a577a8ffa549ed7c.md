An order-$n$ linear equation on an interval is $a_n(t)y^{(n)}+\cdots+a_0(t)y=g(t)$ with continuous coefficients and $a_n(t)\ne0$. It is homogeneous when $g=0$. The homogeneous solutions form a vector space because differentiation is linear. If $y_p$ is one particular solution, every inhomogeneous solution is $y_p+y_h$ with $y_h$ homogeneous, since applying the operator to a difference subtracts the forcing. Initial data consist of $y(t_0),\ldots,y^{(n-1)}(t_0)$.
