For the polynomial, Cauchy–Riemann requires $v_y=2x$ and $v_x=2y$, giving $v=2xy+C$. Indeed, $u+iv=z^2+iC$.

For $\log|z|$, a hypothetical conjugate would make $F=u+iv$ holomorphic with derivative $u_x-iu_y=(x-iy)/(x^2+y^2)=1/z$. Thus $F$ would be a primitive of $1/z$ on the punctured plane. The unit-circle integral of $1/z$ is $2\pi i$, whereas a derivative's integral on a closed contour is zero. This contradiction excludes a global conjugate.
