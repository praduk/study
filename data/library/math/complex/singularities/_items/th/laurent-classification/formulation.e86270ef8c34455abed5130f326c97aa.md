For a @[holomorphic function]math:complex:holomorphic-functions:df:holomorphic on $0<|z-a|<R$, the singularity at $a$ is removable exactly when every negative Laurent coefficient is zero. It is a pole of order $m$ exactly when $c_{-m}\ne0$ and $c_n=0$ for all $n<-m$. It is essential exactly when infinitely many negative coefficients are nonzero.
