An extension is continuous at $a$, so local boundedness is necessary. Conversely assume $|f|\le M$ near $a$ and define $g(z)=(z-a)^2f(z)$ off $a$, with $g(a)=0$. This function is continuous at $a$, and
$[g(a+h)-g(a)]/h=h f(a+h)\to0$.
Thus $g$ is holomorphic across $a$ with $g(a)=g'(a)=0$. Its Taylor expansion has the form $g(z)=(z-a)^2h(z)$ with $h$ holomorphic. On the punctured disk $h=f$, so $h$ is the required extension.
