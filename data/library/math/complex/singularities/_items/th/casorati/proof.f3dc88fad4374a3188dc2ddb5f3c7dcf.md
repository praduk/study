If the image were not dense, some open disk $D(w,\epsilon)$ would be disjoint from its image. Then $g=1/(f-w)$ would be holomorphic and bounded by $1/\epsilon$ on that punctured disk, so it extends across $a$. If $g(a)\ne0$, the formula $f=w+1/g$ extends $f$ holomorphically. If $g(a)=0$, the extension is not identically zero since $g$ never vanishes off $a$. Its zero has finite order, so $1/g$ and hence $f$ have a pole. Both conclusions contradict essentiality.
