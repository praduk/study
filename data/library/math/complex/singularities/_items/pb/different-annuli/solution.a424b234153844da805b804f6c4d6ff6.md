For $|z|<1$, use $(1-z)^{-1}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}z^n$:

$$
f(z)=-\frac1z\frac1{1-z}=-\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}z^{n-1}.
$$

For $|z|>1$, expand in $1/z$:

$$
f(z)=\frac1{z^2}\frac1{1-1/z}=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}z^{-n-2}.
$$

Each geometric series converges absolutely on its stated annulus and reproduces the rational expression when summed. The pole at $z=1$ separates the two annuli.
