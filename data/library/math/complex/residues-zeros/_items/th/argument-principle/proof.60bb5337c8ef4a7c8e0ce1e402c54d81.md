Near a zero of order $m$, write $f(z)=(z-b)^m h(z)$ with $h$ holomorphic and nonzero. Then $f'/f=m/(z-b)+h'/h$, so its residue is $m$. At a pole of order $m$, the corresponding factor is $(z-b)^{-m}$ and the residue is $-m$. Away from zeros and poles the logarithmic derivative is holomorphic. These sets are finite within the closed disk by compactness and isolated zeros/poles. Apply the residue theorem to $f'/f$.
