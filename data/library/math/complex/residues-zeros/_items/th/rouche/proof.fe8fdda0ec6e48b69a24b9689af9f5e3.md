For $0\le t\le1$, put $F_t=f+tg$. The strict boundary inequality gives $|F_t|\ge|f|-|g|>0$ there, uniformly away from zero by compactness. The argument principle says

$$
N(t)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|z-a|=r}\frac{f'(z)+tg'(z)}{f(z)+tg(z)}\,dz
$$

is the integer counting zeros of $F_t$ inside. The denominator bound makes this integral continuous in $t$. A continuous integer-valued function on the connected interval $[0,1]$ is constant, so $N(0)=N(1)$.
