A contour is a piecewise $C^1$ map $\gamma:[a,b]\to\C$. For a function $f$ continuous on its image,

$$
\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=\int_a^b f(\gamma(t))\gamma'(t)\,dt,
$$

summing over its smooth pieces. Its length is $L(\gamma)=\int_a^b|\gamma'(t)|\,dt$. Reversing orientation negates the integral; concatenating compatible contours adds their integrals. The contour is closed when $\gamma(a)=\gamma(b)$.
