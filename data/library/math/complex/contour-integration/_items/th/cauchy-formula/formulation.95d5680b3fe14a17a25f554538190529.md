Suppose $f$ is holomorphic on an open set containing the closed disk $\overline{D(a,r)}$. For every $z\in D(a,r)$,

$$
f(z)=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|\zeta-a|=r}\frac{f(\zeta)}{\zeta-z}\,d\zeta.
$$

The circle has positive orientation. The proof of this central representation is not included; see Lebl, [Guide to Cultivating Complex Analysis, Section 3.3](https://www.jirka.org/ca/ca.pdf). Subsequent derivative, Taylor, and maximum principles are derived from this exact formula.
