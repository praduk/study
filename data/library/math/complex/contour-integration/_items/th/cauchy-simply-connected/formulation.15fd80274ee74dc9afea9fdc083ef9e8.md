A domain is @[simply connected]math:top:homotopy:df:fundamental-group when every continuous closed loop in it can be continuously contracted to a point within the domain. If $U$ is simply connected and $f$ is holomorphic on $U$, then

$$
\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz=0
$$

for every closed piecewise $C^1$ contour $\gamma$ in $U$. Consequently $f$ has a primitive on $U$, obtained by integrating from a fixed base point.

The extension from triangle integrals to arbitrary contractible contours is not proved here. See Lebl, [Guide to Cultivating Complex Analysis, Chapter 4](https://www.jirka.org/ca/ca.pdf). Holomorphicity only along a curve is not a meaningful substitute for holomorphicity on an open domain.
