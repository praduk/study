If $f$ is holomorphic on $D(a,R)$, then for $|z-a|<R$,

$$
f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}(z-a)^n.
$$

The series converges uniformly on every smaller closed disk. For $0<r<R$, its coefficients satisfy

$$
\frac{f^{(n)}(a)}{n!}=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_{|\zeta-a|=r}\frac{f(\zeta)}{(\zeta-a)^{n+1}}\,d\zeta.
$$
