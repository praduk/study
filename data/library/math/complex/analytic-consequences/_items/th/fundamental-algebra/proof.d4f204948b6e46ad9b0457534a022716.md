For a polynomial $p(z)=a_nz^n+\cdots+a_0$ with $a_n\ne0$ and $n\ge1$, the leading term dominates the finite lower-degree sum for sufficiently large $|z|$, so $|p(z)|\to\infty$. If $p$ had no root, $1/p$ would be entire, bounded outside a large disk, and bounded inside that disk by continuity and compactness. Liouville would make $1/p$ constant, a contradiction. Polynomial division factors out a root; repeating on the lower-degree quotient proves the complete factorization.
