On any disk contained in $U$, join two points by a line segment. The real derivative of $t\mapsto f(z+t(w-z))$ is $f'(z+t(w-z))(w-z)=0$. The real mean value theorem applied separately to real and imaginary parts makes the values equal. Thus $f$ is locally constant. The set where $f$ equals a fixed value is open, as is its complement; connectedness of $U$ forces this nonempty set to be all of $U$.
