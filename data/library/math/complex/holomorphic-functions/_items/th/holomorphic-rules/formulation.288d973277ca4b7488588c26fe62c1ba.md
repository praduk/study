Sums and products of @[holomorphic functions]math:complex:holomorphic-functions:df:holomorphic are holomorphic. Their quotient is holomorphic wherever its denominator is nonzero. If the composition is defined between open sets, a composition of holomorphic functions is holomorphic and

$$
(g\circ f)'(z)=g'(f(z))f'(z).
$$
