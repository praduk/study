Suppose $f=u+iv$ is real differentiable at $a=x+iy$. Then $f$ is complex differentiable at $a$ if and only if

$$
u_x(a)=v_y(a),\qquad u_y(a)=-v_x(a).
$$

In that case $f'(a)=u_x(a)+iv_x(a)$. In particular, if $u,v$ are $C^1$ on an open set and these equations hold everywhere, then $f$ is holomorphic there.
