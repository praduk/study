For an open $U\subseteq\C$ and $a\in U$, the complex derivative is

$$
f'(a)=\lim_{h\to0,\ h\in\C\setminus\{0\}}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}h
$$

when the limit exists. The function is holomorphic on $U$ when it is complex differentiable at every point of $U$, and entire when $U=\C$.

The limit must agree along all complex directions. Differentiability at one point does not mean holomorphicity on a neighborhood. Real differentiability of $f:\R^2\to\R^2$ alone is weaker than complex differentiability.
