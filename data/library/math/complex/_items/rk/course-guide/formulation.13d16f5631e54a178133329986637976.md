Prerequisites: real limits and continuity, uniform convergence of function series, differentiation in $\R^2$, and basic line integrals. This course develops holomorphic functions, Cauchy theory, power and Laurent series, residues, zero counting, conformal maps, and harmonic functions.

The reference inspected is Jiří Lebl's author-written open textbook, [Guide to Cultivating Complex Analysis](https://www.jirka.org/ca/ca.pdf). The entries use original formulations, derivations, and worked examples. Goursat's triangle theorem and the principal consequences of Cauchy's formula have complete proofs. The general simply connected Cauchy theorem, the disk Cauchy formula, Laurent expansion existence, and the Riemann mapping theorem are clearly marked as source-backed results whose full proofs are not included.

A domain means a nonempty connected open subset of $\C$. A disk is $D(a,r)=\{z:|z-a|<r\}$ with $r>0$. Circle integrals are counterclockwise unless stated otherwise. The course concerns one complex variable; several complex variables and Riemann surface theory require additional development.
