Absolute convergence allows the Cauchy product and regrouping by total degree:

$$
e^ze^w=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\sum_{j=0}^n\frac{z^jw^{n-j}}{j!(n-j)!}
=\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}\frac{(z+w)^n}{n!}.
$$

If $z=x+iy$ and $e^z=1$, taking moduli gives $e^x=1$, hence $x=0$. Then $\cos y=1$ and $\sin y=0$, so $y=2\pi k$. Conversely those values give one by the real trigonometric identities.
