Taking moduli gives $|z|=r^{1/n}$. Writing $z=r^{1/n}e^{i\phi}$, the equation is equivalent to $n\phi-\theta\in2\pi\Z$. Thus

$$
z_k=r^{1/n}e^{i(\theta+2\pi k)/n},\qquad k=0,\ldots,n-1.
$$

Each satisfies the equation by direct substitution. Two listed values coincide only when $(k-j)/n$ is an integer, which within the given range forces $k=j$. Any integer in the phase equation is congruent to one of these $k$ modulo $n$, so the list is exhaustive.
