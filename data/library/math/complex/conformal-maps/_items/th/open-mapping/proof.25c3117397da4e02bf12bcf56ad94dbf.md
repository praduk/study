Fix a point $a$ and choose a small closed disk about it inside the domain. By isolated zeros, its radius can be chosen so $f(z)-f(a)$ has no zero on the boundary. Let $d>0$ be the minimum of $|f(z)-f(a)|$ on that boundary. If $|w-f(a)|<d/2$ and $f-w$ had no zero in the disk, $1/(f-w)$ would be holomorphic near its closure. The maximum modulus principle would imply
$|f(a)-w|\ge\min_{\partial D}|f-w|\ge d-|w-f(a)|>d/2$,
a contradiction. Hence every such $w$ is in the image. Applying the argument at each point of an arbitrary open subset proves that its image is open.
