Define $g(z)=f(z)/z$ for $z\ne0$ and $g(0)=f'(0)$; the Taylor series shows this is holomorphic. For $0<r<1$, the boundary bound $|g|\le1/r$ and the @[maximum modulus principle]math:complex:analytic-consequences:th:maximum-modulus give $|g(z)|\le1/r$ for $|z|<r$. Let $r\uparrow1$ to get $|g|\le1$. This gives both inequalities. Either equality hypothesis produces a point inside the disk where $|g|=1$. The maximum modulus principle makes $g$ constant of modulus one, proving the equality case.
