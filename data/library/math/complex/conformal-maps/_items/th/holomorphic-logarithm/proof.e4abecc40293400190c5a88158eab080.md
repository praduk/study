The quotient $f'/f$ is holomorphic, so Cauchy's theorem gives a primitive $G$. Differentiate $fe^{-G}$: its derivative is $e^{-G}(f'-fG')=0$. Thus $fe^{-G}=c$ for a nonzero constant $c$. Choose a complex number $b$ with $e^b=c$, using polar form. Then $L=G+b$ satisfies $e^L=f$. If $L_1,L_2$ are two logarithms, $e^{L_1-L_2}=1$, so their continuous difference takes values in the discrete set $2\pi i\Z$. Connectedness forces it to be constant.
