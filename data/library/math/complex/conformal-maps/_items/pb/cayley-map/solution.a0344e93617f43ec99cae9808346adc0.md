For $z=x+iy$ with $y>0$,
$|z+i|^2-|z-i|^2=(y+1)^2-(y-1)^2=4y>0$,
so $|T(z)|<1$, and the denominator cannot vanish. Solving $w=(z-i)/(z+i)$ gives $z=i(1+w)/(1-w)$. If $|w|<1$, this is defined and its imaginary part is $(1-|w|^2)/|1-w|^2>0$. Both expressions are rational and holomorphic on their respective domains, and direct substitution makes them inverses.
