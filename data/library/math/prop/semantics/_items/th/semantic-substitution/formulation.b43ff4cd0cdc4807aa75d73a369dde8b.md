Let $\sigma$ be a uniform substitution and $v$ a valuation. Define the induced valuation $v_{\sigma}$ by

$$v_{\sigma}(p)=\llbracket\sigma(p)\rrbracket_v.$$

Then every formula $A$ satisfies

$$\llbracket A\sigma\rrbracket_v=\llbracket A\rrbracket_{v_{\sigma}}.$$

Consequently, every uniform substitution instance of a tautology is a tautology.
