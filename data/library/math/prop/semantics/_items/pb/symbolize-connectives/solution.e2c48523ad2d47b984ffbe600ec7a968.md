Suitable translations are:

1. $(p\lor q)\land\neg(p\land q)$.
2. $r\to(p\land q)$.
3. $(\neg p\land\neg q)\to\neg r$.
4. $r\leftrightarrow(p\lor q)$.

The direction in item 2 is the common trap: 'approved only if both submit' makes both submissions necessary for approval, so approval is the antecedent. Item 1 may equivalently be written $(p\land\neg q)\lor(\neg p\land q)$.
