Several uses of the word **complete** must remain separate. @[Functional completeness]math:prop:normal-forms:df:functional-completeness concerns whether connectives express every Boolean function. @[Resolution refutation]math:prop:normal-forms:df:resolution completeness concerns whether one inference method detects every unsatisfiable finite clause set. Strong completeness concerns agreement between @[semantic consequence]math:prop:semantics:df:semantic-consequence and natural-deduction derivability. None is Gödel's incompleteness theorem.

Propositional compactness and completeness are models for later first-order metatheory, but their proofs exploit special features of propositional formulas and truth valuations. First-order incompleteness phenomena for sufficiently strong effectively axiomatized theories, and forcing arguments in set theory, require substantially more syntax, semantics, and set-theoretic machinery. The present results are foundations for those subjects, not substitutes for them.
