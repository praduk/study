Soundness gives derivability only when there is semantic consequence. Conversely, suppose $\Gamma\models A$. By semantic compactness, there is a finite subset $\Gamma_0\subseteq\Gamma$ with $\Gamma_0\models A$. Finite-premise completeness gives $\Gamma_0\vdash A$. Weakening along $\Gamma_0\subseteq\Gamma$ gives $\Gamma\vdash A$. Combining the two directions proves the equivalence for arbitrary $\Gamma$.
