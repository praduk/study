Let $Q(A)$ be a property of formulas. Suppose:

1. $Q(p)$ holds for every atom $p$, and $Q(\true)$ and $Q(\false)$ hold.
2. Whenever $Q(A)$ holds, $Q(\neg A)$ holds.
3. Whenever $Q(A)$ and $Q(B)$ hold, $Q(A\circ B)$ holds for each binary connective $\circ$.

Then $Q(A)$ holds for every formula $A$.
