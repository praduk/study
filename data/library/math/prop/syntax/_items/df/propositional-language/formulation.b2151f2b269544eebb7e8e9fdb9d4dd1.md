A **propositional language** has a countably infinite set $\mathsf{Prop}=\{p_0,p_1,p_2,\ldots\}$ of propositional variables, the nullary logical constants $\true$ and $\false$, the unary connective $\neg$, and the binary connectives $\land$, $\lor$, $\to$, and $\leftrightarrow$.

Propositional variables are also called **atoms**. They have no internal subject-predicate structure in this language. Each atom receives a truth value only when a valuation is supplied.
