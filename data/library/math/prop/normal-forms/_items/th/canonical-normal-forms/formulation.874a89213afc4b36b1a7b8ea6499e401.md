Let $P=\operatorname{At}(A)=\{p_1,\ldots,p_n\}$. Using any fixed order of the finitely many assignments $a:P\to\{0,1\}$, the formula $A$ is logically equivalent both to

$$\bigvee_{a(A)=1}m_a$$

and to

$$\bigwedge_{a(A)=0}k_a.$$

The finite conjunction and disjunction use the convention in @math:prop:normal-forms:df:literal-clause-term. The first formula is the canonical DNF and the second is the canonical CNF.
