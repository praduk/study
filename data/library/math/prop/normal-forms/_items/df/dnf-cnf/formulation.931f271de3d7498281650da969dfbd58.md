A formula is in **disjunctive normal form** (DNF) if it is a finite disjunction of terms. A formula is in **conjunctive normal form** (CNF) if it is a finite conjunction of clauses.

With the empty-operation conventions, $\false$ is an empty DNF and $\true$ is an empty CNF. A DNF or CNF need not be canonical: literals may repeat, clauses or terms may be redundant, and not every atom must occur in every component.
