For $a,b\in\Z$, $a\mid b$ means $b=ak$ for some $k\in\Z$. Thus $0\mid b$ exactly when $b=0$. For integers $a,b$ not both zero, $\gcd(a,b)$ is the greatest positive common divisor. Define $\gcd(0,0)=0$. For nonzero $a,b$, $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ is the least positive common multiple; it is zero if either input is zero. The integers are coprime when their gcd is $1$. A prime is an integer $p>1$ with only positive divisors $1,p$. An integer greater than one that is not prime is composite.
