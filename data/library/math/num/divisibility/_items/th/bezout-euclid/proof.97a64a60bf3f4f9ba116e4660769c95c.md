There is a positive linear combination (choose the sign of a nonzero input). Let $d$ be the least positive combination. Division of $a$ by $d$ gives a remainder that is also a combination; minimality forces that remainder to be zero. Similarly $d\mid b$. Every common divisor divides every combination, hence divides $d$, establishing the assertion. The equation $a=bq+r$ shows that the pairs $(a,b)$ and $(b,r)$ have exactly the same common divisors. Positive remainders strictly decrease, so the algorithm terminates.
