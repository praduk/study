The recurrence multiplies $x_n+y_n\sqrt2$ by $3+2\sqrt2$, whose norm $(3+2\sqrt2)(3-2\sqrt2)$ is $1$. Equivalently expansion gives $(3x+4y)^2-2(2x+3y)^2=x^2-2y^2$. Thus every iterate is an integer solution. We obtain $(3,2)$, $(17,12)$, and $(99,70)$. For $n\ge0$, $x_n\ge1$, $y_n\ge0$, so $y_{n+1}>y_n$, proving distinctness. This construction does not assert that every Pell solution has been classified.
