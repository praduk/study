A divisor independently chooses an exponent from $0$ to $a_i$ for each prime, proving the divisor formulas. Among $1,\ldots,p^a$, exactly $p^{a-1}$ are multiples of $p$, so $\varphi(p^a)=p^a-p^{a-1}$. The Chinese remainder theorem identifies units modulo coprime $m,n$ with pairs of units modulo $m$ and $n$, hence multiplies their counts. This proves the totient formula and multiplicativity; the other formulas also multiply over disjoint prime sets.
