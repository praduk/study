An arithmetic function maps the positive integers to $\C$. It is multiplicative when $f(1)=1$ and $f(mn)=f(m)f(n)$ for coprime positive $m,n$; it is completely multiplicative if this holds without coprimality. Define $(f*g)(n)=\sum_{d\mid n,\,d>0}f(d)g(n/d)$. Let $\mathbf1(n)=1$ and let $\varepsilon(1)=1$, $\varepsilon(n)=0$ for $n>1$. Convolution is associative and commutative, with identity $\varepsilon$, by reindexing the finite sums over factorizations of $n$.
