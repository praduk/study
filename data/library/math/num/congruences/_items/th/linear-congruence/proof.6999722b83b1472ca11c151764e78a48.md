The congruence is the integer equation $ax+my=b$, so necessity follows from divisibility by $d$. If $d\mid b$, multiply a Bézout identity for $a,m$ by $b/d$ to get a particular solution $x_0$. Then $a(x-x_0)$ is divisible by $m$ exactly when $x-x_0$ is divisible by $m/d$, since $a/d$ and $m/d$ are coprime. The distinct classes are $x_0+k(m/d)$ for $0\le k<d$. Taking $b=1$ gives the unit criterion.
