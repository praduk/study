A finite simple continued fraction is $[a_0;a_1,\ldots,a_n]=a_0+1/(a_1+1/(\cdots+1/a_n))$, where $a_0\in\Z$ and $a_i$ are positive integers for $i>0$. To select a unique finite representation of a noninteger rational, require $a_n\ge2$. For an irrational real $\alpha$, put $\alpha_0=\alpha$, $a_k=\lfloor\alpha_k\rfloor$, and $\alpha_{k+1}=1/(\alpha_k-a_k)$ indefinitely. The $k$th convergent is $p_k/q_k=[a_0;\ldots,a_k]$.
