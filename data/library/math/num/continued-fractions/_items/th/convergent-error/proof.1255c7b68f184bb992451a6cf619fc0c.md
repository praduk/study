Substitution of the complete quotient yields $\alpha=(p_k\alpha_{k+1}+p_{k-1})/(q_k\alpha_{k+1}+q_{k-1})$. Subtract $p_k/q_k$ and use the determinant identity. The signed error is $(-1)^k/[q_k(q_k\alpha_{k+1}+q_{k-1})]$. Since $\alpha_{k+1}>a_{k+1}$, its denominator exceeds $q_kq_{k+1}$. The denominators tend to infinity because $q_{k+1}\ge q_k+q_{k-1}$ for $k\ge1$ and are positive from $q_0=1$. Hence the errors tend to zero, with the displayed alternating signs.
