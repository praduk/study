Place the $N+1$ fractional parts of $0,\alpha,\ldots,N\alpha$ in the $N$ half-open intervals of length $1/N$ partitioning $[0,1)$. Two are in the same interval. Their difference gives the first conclusion with $1\le q\le N$. Dividing by $q$ and using $q\le N$ gives error $<1/(qN)\le1/q^2$. For irrational $\alpha$, the possible denominators cannot remain bounded as $N$ tends to infinity: finitely many fixed denominators have a positive minimum of $|q\alpha-p|$ over integers $p$. Thus infinitely many distinct rational approximants occur.
