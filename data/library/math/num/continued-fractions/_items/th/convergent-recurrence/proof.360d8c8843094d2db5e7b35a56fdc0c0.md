The fractional-linear map $t\mapsto a+1/t$ corresponds to the matrix $\begin{pmatrix}a&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$. Multiplying these matrices from $a_0$ to $a_k$ gives $\begin{pmatrix}p_k&p_{k-1}\\q_k&q_{k-1}\end{pmatrix}$ by induction, yielding both the recurrence and the convergent by applying the map at infinity. Each factor has determinant $-1$, so the product has determinant $(-1)^{k+1}=(-1)^{k-1}$. A common divisor of $p_k,q_k$ divides this determinant and must be $1$.
