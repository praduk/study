We have $1/(\sqrt2-1)=\sqrt2+1$, whose integer part is $2$ and whose fractional part is again $\sqrt2-1$. Thus $\sqrt2=[1;2,2,\ldots]$. The recurrence gives $1/1$, $3/2$, $7/5$, $17/12$, followed by $41/29$. Since $17^2-2\cdot12^2=1$, rationalizing gives $17/12-\sqrt2=1/[12(17+12\sqrt2)]$. This is positive and less than $1/(12\cdot29)$ because $17+12\sqrt2>29$, in agreement with the error bound.
