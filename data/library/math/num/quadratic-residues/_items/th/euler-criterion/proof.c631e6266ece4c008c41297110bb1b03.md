Let $g$ generate the nonzero residue classes and write $a=g^k$ when $p\nmid a$. Squares are exactly the even powers. The element $g^{(p-1)/2}$ has order $2$, hence equals $-1$ (the roots of $X^2-1$ are $1,-1$). Thus $a^{(p-1)/2}=(-1)^k$, proving the criterion. Multiplication adds exponents, proving multiplicativity, including zero numerators separately. Apply the criterion directly to $a=-1$ for the last formula.
