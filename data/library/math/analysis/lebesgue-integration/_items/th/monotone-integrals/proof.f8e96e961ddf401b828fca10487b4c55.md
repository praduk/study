Monotonicity gives $L=\lim\int f_n\le\int f$. Fix a nonnegative simple $s\le f$ and $0<c<1$. Put $E_n=\{f_n\ge cs\}$. On $\{s>0\}$ these sets increase to the whole set, because the limit $f\ge s>cs$. Thus $\int_{E_n}s\to\int s$ by measure continuity applied to the finitely many positive level sets of $s$. Since $f_n\ge cs1_{E_n}$, $L\ge c\int s$. Let $c\uparrow1$, then take the supremum over all $s\le f$. This proves $L\ge\int f$, including infinite integrals.
