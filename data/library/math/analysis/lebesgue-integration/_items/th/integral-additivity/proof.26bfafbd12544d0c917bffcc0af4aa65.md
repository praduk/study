For simple functions, take a common finite partition of their level sets; the identity is finite distributivity with nonnegative extended values. Choose increasing simple approximations $s_n\uparrow f$ and $t_n\uparrow g$. Then $s_n+t_n\uparrow f+g$, so monotone convergence proves the nonnegative identity. Positive homogeneity follows in the same way, with the zero case direct. For integrable real functions, split into positive and negative parts; every term is finite, so subtraction is legitimate. Complex linearity follows by real and imaginary parts.
