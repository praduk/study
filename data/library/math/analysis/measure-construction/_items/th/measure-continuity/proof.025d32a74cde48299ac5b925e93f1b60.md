For increasing sets, write $A$ as the disjoint union of $A_1$ and $A_n\setminus A_{n-1}$ for $n\ge2$. Countable additivity makes $\mu(A)$ the limit of the corresponding finite sums, which are $\mu(A_n)$. For decreasing sets, $A_1\setminus A_n\uparrow A_1\setminus A$. Subtract the resulting equality from the finite quantity $\mu(A_1)$. Finiteness is essential: for Lebesgue measure, $A_n=[n,\infty)$ decreases to the empty set but each measure is infinite.
