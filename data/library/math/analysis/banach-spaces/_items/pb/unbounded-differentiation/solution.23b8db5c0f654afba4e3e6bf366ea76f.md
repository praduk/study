For $f_n(x)=\sin(nx)$, $\norm{f_n}_\infty\le1$ but $\norm{f_n'}_\infty=n$, since $f_n'(0)=n$. Thus no uniform operator bound exists. The domain $C^1([0,1])$ with only the supremum norm is not Banach: the smooth functions $\sqrt{(x-1/2)^2+1/n}$ converge uniformly to $|x-1/2|$, which is not differentiable at $1/2$. The closed graph theorem requires completeness of the domain norm. Adding the derivative term, $\norm f_\infty+\norm{f'}_\infty$, produces the usual Banach norm and makes $D$ bounded.
