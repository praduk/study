A norm on a real or complex @[vector space]math:algebra:linear-foundations:df:vector-space satisfies positivity, $\norm{\lambda x}=|\lambda|\norm x$, and the triangle inequality. A Banach space is complete for the metric $d(x,y)=\norm{x-y}$.

For normed spaces $X,Y$, a linear map $T:X\to Y$ is bounded if $\norm{Tx}\le C\norm x$ for some finite $C$ and every $x$. Its operator norm is $\norm T=\sup_{\norm x\le1}\norm{Tx}$. Write $\mathcal B(X,Y)$ for the bounded linear maps and $X^*=\mathcal B(X,\F)$ for the continuous dual, where $\F$ is $\R$ or $\C$.
