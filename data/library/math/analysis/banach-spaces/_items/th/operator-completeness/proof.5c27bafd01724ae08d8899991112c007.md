Let $(T_n)$ be Cauchy in operator norm. For each $x$, $\norm{T_nx-T_mx}\le\norm{T_n-T_m}\norm x$, so $T_nx$ converges in $Y$; call the limit $Tx$. Passing to pointwise limits proves linearity. The Cauchy sequence $(T_n)$ is uniformly bounded in norm by some $M$, giving $\norm{Tx}\le M\norm x$. For large $n,m$, the norm difference is below $\epsilon$; let $m\to\infty$ in the pointwise bound to obtain $\norm{(T_n-T)x}\le\epsilon\norm x$. Hence $\norm{T_n-T}\le\epsilon$.
