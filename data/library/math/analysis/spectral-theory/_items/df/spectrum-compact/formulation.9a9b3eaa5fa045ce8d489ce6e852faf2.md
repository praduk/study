For a bounded operator $T$ on a complex Banach space $X$, the spectrum is

$$
\sigma(T)=\{\lambda\in\C:T-\lambda I\text{ has no bounded everywhere-defined inverse}\}.
$$

An eigenvalue has a nonzero vector $x$ satisfying $Tx=\lambda x$. An operator is compact if it maps every bounded sequence to a sequence with a norm-convergent subsequence. Finite-rank bounded operators are compact by finite-dimensional bounded-subsequence compactness. Not every spectral value is an eigenvalue.
