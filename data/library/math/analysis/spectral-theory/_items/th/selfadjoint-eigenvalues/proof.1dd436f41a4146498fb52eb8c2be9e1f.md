If $Tx=\lambda x$ with $x\ne0$, then
$\lambda\norm x^2=\iprod{Tx}{x}=\iprod{x}{Tx}=\overline\lambda\norm x^2$,
so $\lambda$ is real. If $Ty=\mu y$, both eigenvalues are real, and
$\lambda\iprod{x}{y}=\iprod{Tx}{y}=\iprod{x}{Ty}=\mu\iprod{x}{y}$.
When $\lambda\ne\mu$, the @[inner product]math:analysis:hilbert-spaces:df:hilbert must vanish.
