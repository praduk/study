If $y=0$, the inequality and equality assertion are immediate. Otherwise put $\alpha=\iprod{x}{y}/\norm y^2$. Expanding with the stated inner-product convention gives
$0\le\norm{x-\alpha y}^2=\norm x^2-|\iprod{x}{y}|^2/\norm y^2$.
Rearrangement proves the inequality. Equality holds precisely when $x-\alpha y=0$. Expanding both $\norm{x+y}^2$ and $\norm{x-y}^2$ makes their cross terms cancel and proves the second identity.
