For $y=(y_k)\in\ell^2$, square summability implies $y_n\to0$. Thus $\iprod{e_n}{y}=\overline{y_n}\to0$, proving @[weak convergence]math:analysis:hilbert-spaces:df:weak-convergence by Riesz representation. Norm convergence to zero fails because the norms equal one. Moreover, $\norm{e_n-e_m}=\sqrt2$ for $n\ne m$, so no subsequence is norm Cauchy. A compact @[metric space]math:uganalysis:metric-multivariable:df:metric-completeness has a convergent subsequence from every sequence, hence the norm unit ball is not compact.
