In a normed space $X$, $x_n\rightharpoonup x$ means $\ell(x_n)\to\ell(x)$ for every $\ell\in X^*$. On $X^*$, weak-star convergence $\ell_n\overset{*}{\rightharpoonup}\ell$ means $\ell_n(x)\to\ell(x)$ for every $x\in X$. The weak topology is the coarsest topology making all continuous linear functionals continuous; the weak-star topology on $X^*$ is generated by the evaluations at points of $X$.

Norm convergence implies weak convergence by boundedness of each functional. In a Hilbert space, Riesz representation identifies weak convergence with convergence of $\iprod{x_n}{y}$ for every fixed $y\in H$.
