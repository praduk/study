For fixed $x>0$, $f=\partial_y[y/(x^2+y^2)]$, so $\int_0^1 f(x,y)\,dy=1/(1+x^2)$. The outer integral is $\pi/4$. For fixed $y>0$, $f=-\partial_x[x/(x^2+y^2)]$, so $\int_0^1 f(x,y)\,dx=-1/(1+y^2)$ and the other iterated integral is $-\pi/4$. If $\int|f|$ were finite, Fubini would force equality. Therefore $f$ is not absolutely integrable. Every inner integral in this computation is legitimate for its fixed positive coordinate; the failure concerns the joint integrability hypothesis.
