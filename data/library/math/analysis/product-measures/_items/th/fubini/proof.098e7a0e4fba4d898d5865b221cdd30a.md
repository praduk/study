Tonelli applied to $|f|$ shows that each nonnegative section-integral function has finite integral and is therefore finite almost everywhere. For real $f$, apply Tonelli separately to $f^+$ and $f^-$. Their total integrals are finite, so subtracting the resulting equalities is valid and gives the formula, including measurability of the iterated integrands after the stated null-set convention. Treat the real and imaginary parts separately for complex $f$.
