On sigma-finite measure spaces, if product-measurable $f:X\times Y\to\R$ or $\C$ satisfies $\int|f|\,d(\mu\otimes\nu)<\infty$, then its sections are integrable almost everywhere and

$$
\int f\,d(\mu\otimes\nu)=\int_X\int_Y f\,d\nu\,d\mu=\int_Y\int_X f\,d\mu\,d\nu.
$$

Define the inner integral to be zero on exceptional coordinates where it is not defined.
