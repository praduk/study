For $1\le p<\infty$, $L^p(X,\mu)$ consists of equivalence classes of measurable real or complex functions with

$$
\norm f_p=\left(\int|f|^p\,d\mu\right)^{1/p}<\infty.
$$

Two functions represent the same element exactly when they agree almost everywhere. Define

$$
\norm f_\infty=\inf\{M\ge0:|f|\le M\text{ almost everywhere}\}
$$

and let $L^\infty$ contain the classes with finite essential supremum. Quotienting is necessary: otherwise a nonzero function supported on a null set would have norm zero. Counting measure on the positive integers gives the sequence spaces $\ell^p$.
