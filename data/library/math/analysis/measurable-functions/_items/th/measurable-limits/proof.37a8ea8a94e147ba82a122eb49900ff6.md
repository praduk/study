The set $\{\sup_n f_n>a\}$ equals $\bigcup_n\{f_n>a\}$. Thus the supremum is measurable. Negation converts infima to suprema, proving the infimum case. Since
$\limsup_n f_n=\inf_N\sup_{n\ge N}f_n$
and
$\liminf_n f_n=\sup_N\inf_{n\ge N}f_n$,
both are measurable. An existing pointwise limit agrees with either expression.
