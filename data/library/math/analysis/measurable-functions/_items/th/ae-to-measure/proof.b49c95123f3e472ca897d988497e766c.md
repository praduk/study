Fix $\epsilon>0$ and let $E_N=\bigcup_{n\ge N}\{|f_n-f|>\epsilon\}$. These measurable sets decrease; their intersection is contained in the exceptional null set. Since $\mu(E_1)\le\mu(X)<\infty$, continuity from above gives $\mu(E_N)\to0$. For $n\ge N$, the error set for $f_n$ is contained in $E_N$, proving convergence in measure. Finiteness matters: $1_{[n,n+1]}$ tends pointwise to zero on $\R$ while its error set for $\epsilon=1/2$ always has measure one.
