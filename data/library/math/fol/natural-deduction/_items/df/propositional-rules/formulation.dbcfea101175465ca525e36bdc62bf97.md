Conjunction: from $φ$ and $ψ$ infer $φ∧ψ$; from $φ∧ψ$ infer either conjunct. Disjunction: from either disjunct infer $φ∨ψ$; from $φ∨ψ$, a derivation of $χ$ from temporary assumption $φ$, and a derivation of $χ$ from temporary assumption $ψ$, infer $χ$ while discharging both temporary assumptions.

Implication: if a derivation of $ψ$ uses a marked temporary assumption $φ$, infer $φ→ψ$ and discharge those occurrences; from $φ→ψ$ and $φ$ infer $ψ$. These formulations track occurrences so unrelated assumptions with the same formula need not be discharged.
