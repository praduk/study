Existential introduction: from $φ[t/x]$ infer $∃xφ$, provided $t$ is free for $x$ in $φ$.

Existential elimination: from $∃xφ$ and a subderivation of $ψ$ from temporary assumption $φ[y/x]$, infer $ψ$ and discharge that assumption, provided $y$ is fresh: it occurs free in neither $ψ$ nor any undischarged assumption outside the temporary one, and it does not occur in the existential premise. The name $y$ represents an arbitrary witness and may not escape the subderivation.
