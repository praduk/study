Falsity elimination permits inference of any $φ$ from $⊥$. Negation introduction is implication introduction into $⊥$; negation elimination is implication elimination from $φ$ and $¬φ$.

Classical reductio permits: if, under a marked assumption $¬φ$, one derives $⊥$, infer $φ$ and discharge that assumption. Equivalently one may add double-negation elimination or excluded middle. Without a classical principle, the remaining rules give intuitionistic rather than classical consequence.
