Fix a structure $𝔐$ and assignment $g$. For the universal case, $𝔐,g⊨∀xφ$ exactly when $𝔐,g[x↦a]⊨φ$ for every $a∈M$. By the substitution lemma and freshness of $y$, this is equivalent to $𝔐,g[y↦a]⊨φ[y/x]$ for every $a∈M$: the values of variables other than the renamed bound coordinate agree, and $y$ had no prior occurrence to capture. This is exactly $𝔐,g⊨∀yφ[y/x]$. Replace `every` by `some` for the existential case. Since $𝔐,g$ were arbitrary, both biconditionals are valid.
