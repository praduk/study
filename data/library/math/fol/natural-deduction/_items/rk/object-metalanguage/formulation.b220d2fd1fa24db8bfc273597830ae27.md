Symbols such as $φ$, $Γ$, `is free for`, and `there is a derivation` belong to the metalanguage used to discuss formulas. An eigenvariable condition is likewise a restriction on a derivation, not an additional formula within it.

Quotation or corner brackets may be used when confusion threatens: $φ$ can name a formula schema, while the formula itself is an object-language string. The distinction matters in substitution, induction on formulas, and computability proofs.
