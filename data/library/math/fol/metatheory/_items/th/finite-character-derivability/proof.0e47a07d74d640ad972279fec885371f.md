If $Γ_0⊢φ$ with $Γ_0⊆Γ$, weakening gives $Γ⊢φ$. Conversely, a derivation witnessing $Γ⊢φ$ is a finite tree and therefore has only finitely many undischarged assumption leaves. Let $Γ_0$ be the set of formulas labeling those leaves. Then $Γ_0$ is finite, $Γ_0⊆Γ$, and the same derivation witnesses $Γ_0⊢φ$.
