If $T$ has a model, that same model satisfies every finite subset. Conversely, suppose every finite subset is satisfiable. If $T$ were syntactically inconsistent, finite character of derivability would give a finite $T_0⊆T$ with $T_0⊢⊥$. Soundness would make $T_0$ unsatisfiable, a contradiction. Thus $T$ is consistent, and model existence gives a model of $T$.

For the consequence form, $Γ⊨φ$ means $Γ∪\{¬φ\}$ is unsatisfiable. By the satisfiability form, some finite subset is unsatisfiable. It has the form $Γ_0∪\{¬φ\}$ after enlarging $Γ_0$ if needed, so $Γ_0⊨φ$. Conversely, monotonicity makes any consequence of $Γ_0⊆Γ$ a consequence of $Γ$.
