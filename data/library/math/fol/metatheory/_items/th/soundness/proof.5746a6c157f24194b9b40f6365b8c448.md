Let $D$ be a finite derivation of $φ$ from undischarged assumptions in $Γ$. Fix an arbitrary structure $𝔐$ and assignment $g$ satisfying every member of $Γ$. Induct on the height of nodes in $D$. An undischarged assumption is true by the choice of $𝔐,g$; any zero-premise logical rule is valid by its semantics. At an inference node, the induction hypothesis makes each premise true under every assignment satisfying the currently open assumptions, and local soundness of that rule makes its conclusion true, accounting for any discharged assumptions. Thus the root $φ$ is true under $𝔐,g$. Since the structure and assignment were arbitrary, $Γ⊨φ$.
