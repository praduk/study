The quotient term model $𝔐_Δ$ has as domain the equivalence classes $[t]$ of closed terms under $s∼t$ iff $s=t∈Δ$. Interpret $c$ by $[c]$ and $f$ by $f^{𝔐_Δ}([t_1],…,[t_n])=[f(t_1,…,t_n)]$. Put $([t_1],…,[t_n])∈R^{𝔐_Δ}$ exactly when $R(t_1,…,t_n)∈Δ$.

The equality congruence lemma makes these definitions independent of representatives. The Henkin construction supplies fresh constants—including one for the formula $x=x$—so closed terms exist and the quotient domain is nonempty.
