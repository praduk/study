Convert the formula to NNF and alpha-rename bound variables so that no bound variable is reused and none occurs free outside its own quantified subformula. Induct on the NNF construction. Atomic cases are already prenex. Quantifiers are handled by placing their quantifier before the prenex form of the matrix.

For a binary combination $φ◦ψ$, put both operands in prenex form with disjoint bound variables. Move their leading quantifiers outward one at a time using $(Qxα)◦β↔Qx(α◦β)$ when $x$ is not free in $β$, and symmetrically on the right. The preliminary renaming guarantees every side condition. In NNF, $◦$ is $∧$ or $∨$; under this course's nonempty-domain convention, the displayed equivalences hold for either quantifier. The resulting matrix is quantifier-free, and every step is an equivalence.
