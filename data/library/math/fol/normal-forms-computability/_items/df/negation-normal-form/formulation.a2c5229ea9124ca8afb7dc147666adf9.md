Call atomic formulas and $⊥$ base formulas. A literal is a base formula or the negation of a base formula. A formula is in negation normal form (NNF) when it is built from literals using only $∧$, $∨$, $∀$, and $∃$. Thus both $⊥$ and $¬⊥$ are permitted. Implications and biconditionals have been eliminated, and no negation has any other compound scope.

NNF is a logical-equivalence transformation. It does not, by itself, move quantifiers to the front or remove existential quantifiers.
