For atomic formulas, $𝔐,g⊨R(t_1,…,t_n)$ exactly when $(t_1^𝔐[g],…,t_n^𝔐[g])∈R^𝔐$, and $𝔐,g⊨s=t$ exactly when $s^𝔐[g]=t^𝔐[g]$. No assignment satisfies $⊥$. Connectives have their classical truth conditions.

For quantifiers, $𝔐,g⊨∀xφ$ exactly when $𝔐,g[x↦a]⊨φ$ for every $a∈M$; and $𝔐,g⊨∃xφ$ exactly when this holds for at least one $a∈M$. Quantifiers range only over individuals in $M$, not over subsets, relations, or predicates.
