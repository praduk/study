Prove the term claim by structural induction. Variables use the agreement hypothesis, constants ignore assignments, and the function case follows by applying the same interpreted function to equal argument values.

For formulas, use structural induction. Atomic cases follow from the term claim, and Boolean cases follow immediately from the induction hypotheses. For $φ=∀xψ$, suppose $g$ and $h$ agree on $FV(∀xψ)=FV(ψ)\setminus\{x\}$. For each $a∈M$, the assignments $g[x↦a]$ and $h[x↦a]$ agree on all of $FV(ψ)$; the induction hypothesis therefore equates satisfaction of $ψ$. Quantifying over all $a$ proves the universal case. The existential case is identical with “for some $a$” in place of “for every $a$”.
