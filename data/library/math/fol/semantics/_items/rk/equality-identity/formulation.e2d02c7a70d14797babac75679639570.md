In every structure, the formula $s=t$ is true exactly when the two terms denote the same domain element. Equality is therefore fixed across interpretations, unlike an ordinary binary predicate. Reflexivity and substitution of identicals are sound because actual identity is reflexive and indiscernible.

If a language instead contains a binary relation symbol $E$ intended to resemble equality, its interpretation need not be identity unless axioms force an equivalence relation and congruence. Even then, $E$ can identify distinct domain elements; quotienting by $E$ is a separate construction.
