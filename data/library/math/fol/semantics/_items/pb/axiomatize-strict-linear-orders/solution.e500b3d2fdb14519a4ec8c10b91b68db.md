Use the following three sentences:

1. Irreflexivity: $∀x¬(x<x)$.
2. Transitivity: $∀x∀y∀z(((x<y)∧(y<z))→x<z)$.
3. Total comparability of distinct elements: $∀x∀y(x≠y→((x<y)∨(y<x)))$.

Every strict linear order satisfies them. Conversely, irreflexivity and transitivity make $<$ a strict partial order. If both $x<y$ and $y<x$ held, transitivity would give $x<x$, so asymmetry follows. The third axiom compares every distinct pair, making the order linear. Nonemptiness comes from the course's structure convention, not from an added sentence.
