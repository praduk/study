The kernel and image of an intertwiner are invariant. Irreducibility implies that a nonzero map has zero kernel and full image. For an endomorphism $T$ over an algebraically closed field, the @[characteristic polynomial]math:algebra:linear-foundations:df:determinant-trace has a root $\lambda$, so $T-\lambda I$ has nonzero kernel. It is an intertwiner; the first part forces it to be zero. Thus $T=\lambda I$. Algebraic closure is essential to this second argument.
