Let $N,H$ be groups and $\phi:H\to\operatorname{Aut}(N)$ a homomorphism. Their semidirect product $N\rtimes_\phi H$ is $N\times H$ with

$$
(n,h)(n',h')=(n\phi(h)(n'),hh').
$$

The homomorphism identity for $\phi$ makes multiplication associative. The identity is $(1,1)$ and $(n,h)^{-1}=(\phi(h^{-1})(n^{-1}),h^{-1})$. The copy of $N$ is normal. Trivial $\phi$ gives the direct product. For $C_3\rtimes C_2$ with the nontrivial element acting by inversion, the resulting group is $S_3$, via a 3-cycle and a transposition.
