In the @[class equation]math:algebra:groups-actions:th:orbit-stabilizer every noncentral class has size a positive power of $p$ greater than $1$. Hence $|Z(G)|\equiv |G|\equiv0\pmod p$. For order $p^2$, the center has order $p$ or $p^2$. If it has order $p$, the quotient by the center has order $p$ and is cyclic. Whenever $G/Z(G)$ is generated by $gZ(G)$, every element is $g^iz$ for central $z$, and any two such elements commute. Thus $G$ is abelian in either case.
