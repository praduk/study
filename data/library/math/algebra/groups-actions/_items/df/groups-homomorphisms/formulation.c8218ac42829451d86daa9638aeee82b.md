A group is a set $G$ with associative multiplication, an identity $1$, and an inverse for each element. A subgroup $H\le G$ contains $1$ and is closed under multiplication and inverses. Equivalently, a nonempty subset $H$ is a subgroup when $xy^{-1}\in H$ for every $x,y\in H$.

A homomorphism $f:G\to K$ satisfies $f(xy)=f(x)f(y)$. Its kernel is $\ker f=\{g:f(g)=1\}$ and its image is $f(G)$. It is an isomorphism if it is bijective. A group is abelian when $xy=yx$ for every pair. The trivial group is allowed.
