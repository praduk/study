A subgroup $N\le G$ is normal, written $N\trianglelefteq G$, when $gNg^{-1}=N$ for every $g\in G$. Then $G/N$ is the group of cosets with $(gN)(hN)=ghN$. Normality makes this independent of representatives: replacing $g,h$ by $gn,hm$ multiplies $gh$ on the right by $h^{-1}nhm\in N$.

The projection $q:G\to G/N$ is surjective with kernel $N$. Every homomorphism killing $N$ factors uniquely through $q$, by $gN\mapsto f(g)$.
