A left action of $G$ on a set $X$ assigns $g\cdot x$ with $1\cdot x=x$ and $(gh)\cdot x=g\cdot(h\cdot x)$. Its orbit and stabilizer are $Gx=\{g\cdot x:g\in G\}$ and $G_x=\{g:g\cdot x=x\}$. An action is faithful if only the identity fixes every point, and transitive if $X$ has one orbit.

Conjugation is $g\cdot x=gxg^{-1}$ on $G$. Its stabilizer is the centralizer $C_G(x)=\{g:gx=xg\}$. The center $Z(G)$ consists of elements commuting with all of $G$.
