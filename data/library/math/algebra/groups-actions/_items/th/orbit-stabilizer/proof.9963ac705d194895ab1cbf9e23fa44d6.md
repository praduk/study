Two representatives give the same image precisely when $h^{-1}g\cdot x=x$, equivalently $h^{-1}g\in G_x$, precisely the condition $gG_x=hG_x$. Thus the map is well defined and injective; the definition of an orbit gives surjectivity. Sum the sizes of all conjugacy classes. The singleton classes are exactly the central elements; every other class has the displayed centralizer index.
