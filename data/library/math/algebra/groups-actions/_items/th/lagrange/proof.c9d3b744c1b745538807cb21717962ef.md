Define $x\sim y$ by $x^{-1}y\in H$. The subgroup properties give reflexivity, symmetry, and transitivity, so its equivalence classes partition $G$. The class of $x$ is $xH$. Multiplication $h\mapsto xh$ is a bijection from $H$ to $xH$. Counting the finitely many classes gives the formula. Apply it to the cyclic subgroup generated by an element.
