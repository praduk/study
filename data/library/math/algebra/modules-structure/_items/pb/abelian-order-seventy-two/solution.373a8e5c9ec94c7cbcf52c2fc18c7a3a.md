The PID structure theorem for $\Z$, with the Chinese remainder theorem, decomposes a finite @[abelian group]math:algebra:groups-actions:df:groups-homomorphisms into cyclic prime-power factors. Since $72=2^3 3^2$, the 2-primary part is one of $C_8$, $C_4\times C_2$, $C_2^3$, and the 3-primary part is one of $C_9$, $C_3^2$. Each pairing gives a group, for six classes in total. Within the 2-primary part the exponents $8,4,2$ distinguish the possibilities; the 3-primary exponents $9,3$ distinguish the others. Primary subgroups are intrinsically the elements of prime-power order, so different pairings cannot be isomorphic.
