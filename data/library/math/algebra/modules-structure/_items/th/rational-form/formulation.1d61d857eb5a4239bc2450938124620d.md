For a linear operator $T$ on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$ over a field $k$, give $V$ a $k[x]$-module structure by $xv=T(v)$. There are unique monic nonconstant polynomials $d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_t$ such that

$$
V\cong\bigoplus_i k[x]/(d_i).
$$

In the bases $1,x,\ldots,x^{\deg d_i-1}$ the operator is a sum of companion blocks. Its minimal polynomial is $d_t$ and its characteristic polynomial is $\prod_i d_i$, with both taken as $1$ when $V=0$.
