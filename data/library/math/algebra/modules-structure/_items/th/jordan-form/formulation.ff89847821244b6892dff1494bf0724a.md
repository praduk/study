If the @[characteristic polynomial]math:algebra:linear-foundations:df:determinant-trace of $T\in\End_k(V)$ splits over $k$, then $V$ has a basis in which $T$ is a @[direct sum]math:algebra:modules-structure:df:module of Jordan blocks $J_m(\lambda)$. The multiset of block sizes for each eigenvalue is unique. For every $j\ge1$, the number of $\lambda$-blocks of size at least $j$ equals

$$
\dim\ker(T-\lambda I)^j-\dim\ker(T-\lambda I)^{j-1}.
$$
