Let $R$ be a PID and $M$ a finitely generated $R$-module. There are uniquely determined $r\ge0$ and nonzero nonunit invariant factors $d_1\mid\cdots\mid d_t$, unique up to associates, such that

$$
M\cong R^r\oplus\bigoplus_{i=1}^t R/(d_i).
$$

The case $t=0$ is allowed. The isomorphism itself is generally not canonical.

The full existence-and-uniqueness proof is not included here. This is an explicitly imported theorem, checked against Arvind Nair's [Algebra I, Lecture 1](https://mathweb.tifr.res.in/~arvind/algebra/Lectures_algebra.pdf). Subsequent applications use this theorem as a dependency.
