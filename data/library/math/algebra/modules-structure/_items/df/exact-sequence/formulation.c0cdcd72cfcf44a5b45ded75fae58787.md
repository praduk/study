For module homomorphisms $L\xrightarrow f M\xrightarrow g N$, the sequence is exact at $M$ when $\im f=\ker g$. The sequence $0\to L\xrightarrow f M\xrightarrow g N\to0$ is short exact if $f$ is injective, $g$ is surjective, and $\im f=\ker g$.

The kernel of $f$ is its usual zero fiber; its cokernel is $M/f(L)$. The quotient map satisfies the universal property that every homomorphism out of $M$ killing $f(L)$ factors uniquely through the cokernel.
