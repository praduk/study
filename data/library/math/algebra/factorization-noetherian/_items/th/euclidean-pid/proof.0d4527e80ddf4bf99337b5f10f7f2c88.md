The zero ideal is principal. In a nonzero ideal $I$, choose nonzero $b$ minimizing $d(b)$. For $a\in I$, divide $a=qb+r$. Then $r\in I$. A nonzero remainder would contradict minimality, so $a\in(b)$ and $I=(b)$. Over a field, cancellation of successive leading terms divides one polynomial by a nonzero polynomial and produces either zero remainder or smaller degree, giving the final claim.
