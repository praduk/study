Let $M,N$ be modules over a commutative ring $R$. An $R$-bilinear map is linear in each variable separately. A tensor product consists of an $R$-module $M\tensor_R N$ and a bilinear map $(m,n)\mapsto m\tensor n$ such that every bilinear $b:M\times N\to P$ factors uniquely through a linear map $M\tensor_R N\to P$.

Existence is obtained from the free $R$-module on $M\times N$ by imposing additivity in both slots and $(rm,n)=r(m,n)=(m,rn)$. The images of pairs generate the quotient. This construction verifies the universal property because precisely those relations are killed by every bilinear map. Not every tensor is a single pure tensor.
