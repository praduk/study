A module $P$ is projective if for every surjection $q:M\to N$ and homomorphism $f:P\to N$, there is $g:P\to M$ with $qg=f$. Over a commutative ring, a module $P$ is flat if tensoring with $P$ preserves exact sequences; by right exactness it suffices that tensoring preserve injections.

A free module is projective: choose a preimage in $M$ of the image of each basis element and extend linearly. The assertion for arbitrary bases uses choice. The notions of free, projective, and flat are distinct; this course does not identify them.
