There is a surjection $q:F\to P$ from a @[free module]math:algebra:modules-structure:df:module, for example the free module on the underlying set of $P$. If $P$ is projective, lift $\id_P$ to $s:P\to F$ with $qs=\id_P$. The split exact sequence gives $F\cong\ker q\oplus P$.

Conversely suppose $F=P\oplus Q$ is free. For a surjection $u:M\to N$ and $f:P\to N$, extend $f$ to $F$ by zero on $Q$. Lift that extension using projectivity of the free module, then restrict the lift to $P$. This is exactly the lifting property for $P$.
