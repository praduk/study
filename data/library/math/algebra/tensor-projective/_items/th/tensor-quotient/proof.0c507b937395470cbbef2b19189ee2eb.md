The displayed formula is independent of the representative of $r$ and is bilinear, so induces a @[linear map]math:algebra:modules-structure:df:module. In the other direction send $m+IM$ to $m\tensor(1+I)$. If $m=\sum a_i m_i\in IM$, this tensor is $\sum m_i\tensor(a_i+I)=0$, so the map is well defined.
