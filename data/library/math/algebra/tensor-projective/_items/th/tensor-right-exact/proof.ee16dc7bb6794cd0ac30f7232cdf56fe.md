Since $q$ is surjective, every generating tensor $n\tensor p$ lifts to $m\tensor p$. Let $K$ be the image of $f\tensor\id_P$. The rule $(q(m),p)\mapsto m\tensor p+K$ is well defined: two lifts differ by $f(l)$, whose tensor is in $K$. It is bilinear, so induces $N\tensor P\to(M\tensor P)/K$. This is inverse to the map induced by $q\tensor\id_P$, as checked on generating tensors. Hence the kernel is precisely $K$.

For failure of left exactness, tensor the injection $\Z\xrightarrow{\,2\,}\Z$ with $\Z/2\Z$. It becomes the zero map from $\Z/2\Z$ to itself, which is not injective.
