Send $F$ to $m\mapsto(n\mapsto F(m\tensor n))$. Tensor bilinearity shows that both maps are linear. Conversely, for $g:M\to\Hom_R(N,P)$, the map $(m,n)\mapsto g(m)(n)$ is bilinear and induces a unique $F$. The two assignments are inverse by evaluating on each pair $(m,n)$, and pre- or postcomposing any module homomorphism commutes with the formulas. This also proves naturality in all three variables.
