Tensoring with $\Z/m\Z$ gives $(\Z/n\Z)/m(\Z/n\Z)\cong\Z/d\Z$, where $d=\gcd(m,n)$: the image of multiplication by $m$ has $n/d$ elements. A homomorphism from $\Z/m\Z$ is determined by the image $a$ of $1$, with the necessary and sufficient condition $ma=0$ modulo $n$. These elements form the subgroup generated by $n/d$ modulo $n$, of order $d$. Thus the Hom group is also isomorphic to $\Z/d\Z$, although its universal role differs from that of the @[tensor product]math:algebra:tensor-projective:df:tensor.
