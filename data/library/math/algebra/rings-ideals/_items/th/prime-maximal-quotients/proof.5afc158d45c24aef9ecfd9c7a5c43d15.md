A product $(a+I)(b+I)$ vanishes exactly when $ab\in I$, while its factors vanish exactly when $a\in I$ or $b\in I$. This proves the first equivalence. If $I$ is maximal and $a\notin I$, the ideal $I+(a)$ is $R$, so $1=i+ra$ and $r+I$ inverts $a+I$. Conversely, a nonzero ideal of a field contains an invertible element and therefore $1$, so ideal correspondence shows that a field quotient makes $I$ maximal. Every field is a domain, giving the last claim.
