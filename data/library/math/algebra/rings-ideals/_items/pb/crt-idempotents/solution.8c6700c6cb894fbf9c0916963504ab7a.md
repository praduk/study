The Chinese remainder theorem gives $\Z/12\Z\cong\Z/3\Z\times\Z/4\Z$. In $\Z/3\Z$, $x(x-1)=0$ forces $x=0$ or $1$. Modulo $4$, among consecutive integers one is odd and invertible, so the even factor must be divisible by $4$; again only $0,1$ occur. The four pairs yield residues $0,4,9,1$ modulo $12$. Directly, their squares minus themselves are $0,12,72,0$, all divisible by $12$.
