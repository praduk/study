A field extension $L/K$ means $K$ is a specified subfield of $L$; its degree is $[L:K]=\dim_KL$. An element $\alpha\in L$ is algebraic over $K$ when some nonzero polynomial in $K[x]$ vanishes at it; otherwise it is transcendental.

For algebraic $\alpha$, the kernel of evaluation $K[x]\to L$ is generated by a unique monic irreducible polynomial $m_\alpha$, the minimal polynomial. The quotient $K[x]/(m_\alpha)$ is a field and equals $K[\alpha]=K(\alpha)$. Thus $[K(\alpha):K]=\deg m_\alpha$, with basis $1,\alpha,\ldots,\alpha^{d-1}$. Irreducibility follows because a vanishing product in a field has a vanishing factor.
