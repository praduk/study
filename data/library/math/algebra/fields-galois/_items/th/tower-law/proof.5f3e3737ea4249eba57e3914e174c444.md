Let $(a_i)$ be a $K$-basis of $L$ and $(b_j)$ an $L$-basis of $M$. Expanding coefficients in an expression $\sum_j\ell_j b_j$ shows that the products $a_i b_j$ span $M$ over $K$. If $\sum_{i,j}c_{ij}a_i b_j=0$ with $c_{ij}\in K$, independence of the $b_j$ over $L$ gives $\sum_i c_{ij}a_i=0$ for each $j$, and independence of the $a_i$ gives all $c_{ij}=0$. Thus there are exactly $[M:L][L:K]$ basis elements.
