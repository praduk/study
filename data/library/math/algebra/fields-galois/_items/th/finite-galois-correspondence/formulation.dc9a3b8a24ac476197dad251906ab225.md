Let $L/K$ be finite Galois, with group $G$. Subgroups $H\le G$ correspond bijectively, reversing inclusion, to intermediate fields $K\subseteq E\subseteq L$ by

$$
H\longmapsto L^H=\{a:\sigma(a)=a\text{ for all }\sigma\in H\},\qquad E\longmapsto\operatorname{Gal}(L/E).
$$

Moreover $[L:E]=|H|$, $[E:K]=[G:H]$, and $E/K$ is Galois exactly when $H$ is normal; then $\operatorname{Gal}(E/K)\cong G/H$ by restriction.

The full proof is not included. The statement was checked against Milne's [Fields and Galois Theory, Theorem 3.17](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/FT.pdf). In particular, applying it requires both finiteness and the Galois hypotheses.
