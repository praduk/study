The two hypotheses say exactly that $X$ is inductive. Since $\omega$ is the least inductive set, $\omega\subseteq X$. The assumed inclusion $X\subseteq\omega$ gives the reverse inclusion, so Extensionality yields $X=\omega$.

For a formula $\varphi(n)$, apply this set version to
$X=\{n\in\omega:\varphi(n)\}$, which exists by Separation. This gives the usual induction schema.
