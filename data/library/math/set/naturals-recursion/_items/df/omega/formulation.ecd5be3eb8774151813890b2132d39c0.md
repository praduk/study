For any set $x$, define its **successor** by

$$
S(x)=x\cup\{x\}.
$$

A set $I$ is inductive if $\varnothing\in I$ and $x\in I$ implies $S(x)\in I$. Infinity supplies an inductive set $I$. Define

$$
\omega=\{x\in I:\text{$x$ belongs to every inductive set}\}.
$$

Separation makes $\omega$ a set. Its definition is independent of the initially chosen $I$, because it is characterized as the least inductive set. The members of $\omega$ are the von Neumann natural numbers:

$$
0=\varnothing,\quad 1=\{0\},\quad 2=\{0,1\},\quad\ldots
$$
