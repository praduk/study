**(ZF)** If $\kappa$ is an infinite cardinal, then

$$
\kappa+\kappa=\kappa\cdot\kappa=\kappa.
$$

Consequently, for infinite cardinals $\kappa,\lambda$,
$\kappa+\lambda=\kappa\lambda=\max\{\kappa,\lambda\}$ whenever both are nonzero.

No Choice is required here because the cardinals are initial ordinals and the proof explicitly well-orders the relevant binary products. ZFC is needed to transfer the analogous assertion to arbitrary infinite sets that have not been assumed well-orderable.
