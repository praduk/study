A **cardinal number** is an ordinal $\kappa$ that is not equinumerous with any smaller ordinal. Equivalently, it is an initial ordinal.

If $A$ is well-orderable, its cardinality $|A|$ is the least ordinal equinumerous with $A$. The Axiom of Choice makes every set well-orderable, so in ZFC every set has a cardinal number.

The infinite cardinals are enumerated by the alephs:

$$
\aleph_0=\omega,
$$

$\aleph_{\alpha+1}$ is the least cardinal strictly greater than $\aleph_\alpha$, and for limit $\lambda$,

$$
\aleph_\lambda=\sup_{\alpha<\lambda}\aleph_\alpha.
$$

Hartogs' theorem ensures larger cardinals exist; there is no largest cardinal.
