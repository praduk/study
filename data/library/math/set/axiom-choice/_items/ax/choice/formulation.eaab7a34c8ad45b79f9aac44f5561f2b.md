For every set $\mathcal A$ of nonempty sets, there is a function $c$ with domain $\mathcal A$ such that

$$
c(A)\in A\quad\text{for every }A\in\mathcal A.
$$

Such $c$ is a **choice function**. ZFC is ZF plus this axiom. Choice is unnecessary when a uniform definable selection rule is already available; its force is the simultaneous selection from an arbitrary set-indexed family with no supplied rule.
