For each formula $\varphi(x,p_1,\ldots,p_n)$ in which $B$ is not free, ZF includes the axiom

$$
\forall A\,\exists B\,\forall x\,
\bigl[x\in B\leftrightarrow(x\in A\land\varphi(x,p_1,\ldots,p_n))\bigr].
$$

The resulting subset is written

$$
\{x\in A:\varphi(x,p_1,\ldots,p_n)\}.
$$

Separation forms a subset of a prior set $A$; it is not unrestricted comprehension.
