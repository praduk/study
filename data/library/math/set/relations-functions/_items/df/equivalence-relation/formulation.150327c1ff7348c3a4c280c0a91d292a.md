A relation $E$ on $A$ is an **equivalence relation** when it is

- reflexive: $xEx$ for every $x\in A$;
- symmetric: $xEy\to yEx$;
- transitive: $(xEy\land yEz)\to xEz$.

The equivalence class of $a\in A$ is

$$
[a]_E=\{x\in A:xEa\},
$$

and the quotient is $A/E=\{[a]_E:a\in A\}$. Replacement shows that the quotient is a set.

A **partition** of $A$ is a set $\Pi$ of nonempty, pairwise disjoint subsets of $A$ whose union is $A$.
