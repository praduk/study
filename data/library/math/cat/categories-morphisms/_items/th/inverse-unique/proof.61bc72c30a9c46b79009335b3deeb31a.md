If $l:B\to A$ is a left inverse and $r:B\to A$ is a right inverse, then

$$
l=l\id_B=l(fr)=(lf)r=\id_A r=r.
$$

This also proves uniqueness of a two-sided inverse. The calculations require associativity and identities, not any interpretation of the morphisms as functions. A one-sided inverse alone is insufficient.
