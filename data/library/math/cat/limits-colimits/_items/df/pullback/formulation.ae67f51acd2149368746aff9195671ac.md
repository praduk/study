Given $f:A\to C$ and $g:B\to C$, a pullback is an object $P$ with maps $p:P\to A$, $q:P\to B$ such that $fp=gq$, universal among pairs with this equality. That is, if $fu=gv$ for $u:X\to A,v:X\to B$, there is a unique $h:X\to P$ with $ph=u,qh=v$.

In sets, $A\times_C B=\{(a,b):f(a)=g(b)\}$ with coordinate projections is a pullback. The map from $X$ is $x\mapsto(u(x),v(x))$, which lies in the displayed subset precisely by the assumed equality.
