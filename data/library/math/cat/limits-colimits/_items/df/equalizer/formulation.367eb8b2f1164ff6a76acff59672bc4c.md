An equalizer of parallel arrows $f,g:A\to B$ is a map $e:E\to A$ with $fe=ge$, such that any $h:X\to A$ satisfying $fh=gh$ factors uniquely through $e$. In sets it is the inclusion $\{a:f(a)=g(a)\}\injectto A$.

Every equalizer is monic: if $eu=ev$, both $u$ and $v$ factor the same equalizing map, so uniqueness gives $u=v$. In a category with zero morphisms, a kernel of $f$ is an equalizer of $f$ and zero. In modules this recovers the ordinary kernel submodule.
