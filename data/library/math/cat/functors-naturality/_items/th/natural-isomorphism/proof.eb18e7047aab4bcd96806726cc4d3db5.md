An inverse natural transformation gives inverse components by evaluating the two identity equations. Conversely, suppose every component has an inverse. From $G(f)\alpha_A=\alpha_BF(f)$, compose on the left with $\alpha_B^{-1}$ and on the right with $\alpha_A^{-1}$ to obtain $F(f)\alpha_A^{-1}=\alpha_B^{-1}G(f)$. Thus the inverse components are natural. The composites are identities componentwise.
