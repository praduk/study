For $f:V\to W$ and $\psi\in W^*$,

$$
(f^{**}j_V(v))(\psi)=j_V(v)(f^*\psi)=\psi(f(v))=j_W(f(v))(\psi).
$$

Thus $f^{**}j_V=j_Wf$, the required naturality. The map is linear. For finite-dimensional $V$, if $v\ne0$, extend it to a basis and choose a coordinate functional nonzero on $v$; hence $j_V$ is injective. Since $\dim V^{**}=\dim V$, it is bijective. The pointwise-isomorphism theorem gives a natural isomorphism. This constructs no natural map $V\to V^*$: the double dual has different variance.
