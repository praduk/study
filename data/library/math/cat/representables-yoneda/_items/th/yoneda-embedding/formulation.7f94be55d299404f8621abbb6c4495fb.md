For a @[small category]math:cat:categories-morphisms:df:category $\mathcal C$, define

$$
y:\mathcal C\to[\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}},\Set],\qquad yA=\Hom(-,A),
$$

with $y(f)$ given by postcomposition. Then $y$ is @[fully faithful]math:cat:functors-naturality:df:fully-faithful. In particular, a natural isomorphism $\Hom(-,A)\cong\Hom(-,B)$ implies $A\cong B$.
