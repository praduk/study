Apply the dual of the covariant @[Yoneda lemma]math:cat:representables-yoneda:th:yoneda to the presheaf $\Hom(-,B)$. It gives a bijection

$$
\operatorname{Nat}(\Hom(-,A),\Hom(-,B))\cong\Hom(A,B).
$$

Its inverse takes $f:A\to B$ to the transformation $h\mapsto fh$, exactly $y(f)$. Thus the maps on Hom sets are bijections, proving full faithfulness. If $yA\cong yB$, lift that isomorphism and its inverse through these bijections. Faithfulness turns their inverse equations into inverse equations in $\mathcal C$, proving $A\cong B$.
