A representation is a natural isomorphism $\alpha:\Hom(A,-)\Rightarrow F$. Set $u=\alpha_A(\id_A)$. Yoneda says $\alpha_B(f)=F(f)(u)$, and the bijectivity of each component is precisely the claimed existence and uniqueness. Conversely, an element with this property defines these component maps; functoriality gives naturality, and the hypothesis gives bijectivity. Their componentwise inverses are natural, so they form a representation.
