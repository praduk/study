By the set-theoretic description of a limit, an element of the right side is a compatible family $f_j:A\to D(j)$, namely a cone on $D$ with vertex $A$. The defining property of $L$ identifies each such family with a unique map $f:A\to L$. The identification sends $f$ to its composites with the limit projections, so it is exactly the canonical map. It commutes with maps into $A$ by composition, as well as with the structure maps of the diagram. The empty-diagram case says there is exactly one map from $A$ to a @[terminal object]math:cat:categories-morphisms:df:initial-terminal.
