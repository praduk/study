Suppose a loop $\gamma$ at $e_0$ projects to a based-null-homotopic loop. Lift a null-homotopy of $p\gamma$ starting at $\gamma$ using @math:top:coverings:th:lifting. Each lifted endpoint traces a continuous path in the fiber over $x_0$, a discrete space; it is therefore constant at $e_0$. The final lifted loop projects to a constant path and is itself constant by uniqueness of path lifting. Thus the lifted homotopy contracts $\gamma$ relative to endpoints, so the kernel of $p_*$ is trivial.
