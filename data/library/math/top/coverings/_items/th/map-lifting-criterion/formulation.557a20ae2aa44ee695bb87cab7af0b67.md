Let $p:(E,e_0)\to(X,x_0)$ be a covering and $f:(Y,y_0)\to(X,x_0)$ a based map, where $Y$ is @[path connected]math:pst:compactness:df:connectedness and locally path connected. A based lift of $f$ exists exactly when $f_*\pi_1(Y,y_0)\subseteq p_*\pi_1(E,e_0)$; when it exists it is unique.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §1.3, lifting criterion](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
