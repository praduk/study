Let $X$ be path connected, locally path connected, and semilocally simply connected. Isomorphism classes of based path-connected coverings correspond to subgroups $H\le\pi_1(X,x_0)$ via $H=p_*\pi_1(E,e_0)$. Forgetting basepoints gives conjugacy classes of subgroups. The number of sheets equals the subgroup index. The trivial subgroup gives a universal covering.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §1.3, classification of coverings](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
