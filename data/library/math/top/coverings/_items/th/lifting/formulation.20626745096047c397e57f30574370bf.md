For a covering $p:E\to X$, a path $\alpha:I\to X$ has a unique lift after choosing a point above $\alpha(0)$. More generally, a homotopy $H:Y\times I\to X$ and a lift $\widetilde H_0:Y\to E$ of its initial map have a unique lifted homotopy starting at $\widetilde H_0$.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §1.3, lifting properties](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
