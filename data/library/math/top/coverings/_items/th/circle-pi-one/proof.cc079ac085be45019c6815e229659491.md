The exponential map is a covering: a sufficiently short open arc has disjoint interval lifts translated by integers. Lift each based loop from $0$. Its endpoint is an integer. Lifting a based homotopy leaves this endpoint constant because it moves continuously in the discrete fiber $\Z$. A concatenation lifts by following the first lift, then the translate of the second lift by the first endpoint, so endpoints add. Every integer occurs for $t\mapsto e^{2\pi int}$. If the endpoint is $0$, the lifted loop in $\R$ contracts relative to endpoints by $\widetilde H(t,s)=(1-s)\widetilde\alpha(t)$. Projecting gives a based null-homotopy. Hence the endpoint homomorphism is surjective with trivial kernel.
