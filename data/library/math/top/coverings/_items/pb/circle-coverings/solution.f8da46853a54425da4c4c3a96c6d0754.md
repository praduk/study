By @math:top:coverings:th:circle-pi-one, subgroups of the base fundamental group are $n\Z$ for $n\ge0$, where $0\Z=\{0\}$. The hypotheses for classification hold because short arcs are contractible. Since $\Z$ is abelian, conjugacy does not change subgroups. For $n\ge1$, $z\mapsto z^n$ on $S^1$ realizes $n\Z$ and has $n$ sheets. For $n=0$, $t\mapsto e^{2\pi it}$ realizes the trivial subgroup and has countably infinitely many sheets. These exhaust the classes.
