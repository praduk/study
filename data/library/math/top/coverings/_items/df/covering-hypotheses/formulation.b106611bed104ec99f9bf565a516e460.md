A space is locally path connected if every point has a basis of path-connected neighborhoods. It is semilocally simply connected if each point $x$ has a neighborhood $U$ such that inclusion sends every loop in $U$ based at $x$ to the trivial element of $\pi_1(X,x)$.

The latter condition permits loops that contract in $X$ even if they do not contract inside $U$. It should not be replaced by local simple connectivity without acknowledging the stronger hypothesis.
