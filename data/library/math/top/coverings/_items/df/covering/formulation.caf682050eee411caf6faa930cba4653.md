A surjection $p:E\to X$ is a covering map if each $x\in X$ has an open neighborhood $U$ with $p^{-1}(U)$ a disjoint union of open sets, each mapped homeomorphically onto $U$. These open sets are sheets over $U$.

A deck transformation is a @[homeomorphism]math:pst:spaces:df:continuity $h:E\to E$ satisfying $ph=p$. A universal covering is a covering with @[path-connected]math:pst:compactness:df:connectedness, @[simply connected]math:top:homotopy:df:fundamental-group total space. A local homeomorphism need not be a covering; the simultaneous sheet condition over every basepoint matters.
