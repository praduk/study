The free product $G*H$ is the group with homomorphisms from $G,H$ such that each pair of homomorphisms to a group $K$ extends uniquely to a homomorphism $G*H\to K$. Elements may be represented by reduced alternating words from the factors.

A presentation $\langle S\mid R\rangle$ means the free group on generators $S$, divided by the smallest normal subgroup containing the relators $R$. Normal closure, rather than the subgroup generated without conjugation, is required.
