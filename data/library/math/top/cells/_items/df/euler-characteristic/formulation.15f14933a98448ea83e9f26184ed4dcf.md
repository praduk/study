For a finite CW complex with $c_n$ cells in dimension $n$, define $\chi(X)=\sum_n(-1)^nc_n$. The sum is finite. The homological formula below proves that this value is independent of the chosen finite cell structure.

Counting cells is not the same as counting simplices in an arbitrary drawing. The cell interiors must be disjoint, and attaching maps determine how their boundaries are identified.
