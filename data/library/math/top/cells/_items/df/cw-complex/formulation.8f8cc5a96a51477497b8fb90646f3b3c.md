A CW complex is built from a discrete set $X^0$ by attaching $n$-disks along maps $S^{n-1}\to X^{n-1}$ to form $X^n$. Its underlying set is the union of its skeleta, with the weak topology: a subset is closed when its inverse image under every characteristic map $D^n\to X$ is closed. Characteristic maps restrict to homeomorphisms on disk interiors; the image of an interior is an open cell.

The closure-finite condition requires each closed cell to meet only finitely many open cells. A subcomplex is a union of cells containing their closures. Attaching maps need not be embeddings.
