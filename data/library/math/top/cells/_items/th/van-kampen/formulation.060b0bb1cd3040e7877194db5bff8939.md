Suppose $X=U\cup V$, where $U,V,U\cap V$ are @[path connected]math:pst:compactness:df:connectedness open subsets and $x_0\in U\cap V$. Then $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ is $\pi_1(U,x_0)*\pi_1(V,x_0)$ modulo the normal closure of $i_{U*}(\gamma)i_{V*}(\gamma)^{-1}$ for all $\gamma\in\pi_1(U\cap V,x_0)$.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §1.2, van Kampen theorem](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
