For a connected CW complex, inclusion of the two-skeleton induces an isomorphism on $\pi_1$. A connected graph has free fundamental group on the edges outside a maximal tree. Attaching a two-cell adds the relation represented by its attaching loop; cells of dimension at least three add no relations on $\pi_1$.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §1.2, applications to cell complexes; §1.A, graphs](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
