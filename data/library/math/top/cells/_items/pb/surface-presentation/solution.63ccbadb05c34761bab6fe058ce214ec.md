The one-skeleton has the free group on the $2g$ displayed loops. The single two-cell kills the normal closure of its attaching word. Thus $\pi_1(\Sigma_g)=\langle a_1,b_1,\ldots,a_g,b_g\mid\prod_j[a_j,b_j]=1\rangle$, where $[a,b]=aba^{-1}b^{-1}$. For $g=1$, the sole relation says $a_1b_1=b_1a_1$. Every word reduces to $a_1^mb_1^n$, and mapping these generators to the standard generators of $\Z^2$ shows these exponents have no further relation. Hence the torus group is $\Z^2$.
