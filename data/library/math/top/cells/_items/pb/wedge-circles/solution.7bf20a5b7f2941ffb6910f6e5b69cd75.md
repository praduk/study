Use the graph with one vertex and two loop edges $a,b$. Its maximal tree consists only of the vertex, so the graph theorem gives $\pi_1\isoto F(a,b)$, with no relators. The word $aba^{-1}b^{-1}$ is a nonempty reduced word, so it is not the identity. Hence $ab\ne ba$, and the group is nonabelian. The group $\Z^2$ is abelian, so no isomorphism exists. Equal numbers of generators do not determine a group.
