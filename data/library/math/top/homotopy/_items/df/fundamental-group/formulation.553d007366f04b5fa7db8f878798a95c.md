At $x_0\in X$, a loop is a path $\alpha:I\to X$ with both endpoints $x_0$. The fundamental group $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ consists of loops modulo homotopy relative to endpoints. The product $[\alpha][\beta]$ traverses $\alpha$ first, then $\beta$:

$$
(\alpha*\beta)(t)=\begin{cases}\alpha(2t),&0\le t\le1/2,\\\beta(2t-1),&1/2\le t\le1.\end{cases}
$$

The identity is the constant loop and the inverse uses the reversed path $\bar\alpha(t)=\alpha(1-t)$. A space is simply connected here if it is @[path connected]math:pst:compactness:df:connectedness and its fundamental group is trivial.
