For inclusion $i:A\injectto X$, a retraction is a map $r:X\to A$ with $ri=\id_A$. A strong deformation retraction is such an $r$ with a homotopy $\id_X\simeq ir$ that fixes every point of $A$ throughout.

A strong deformation retract has the same homotopy type as $X$. A retraction by itself need not give a @[homotopy equivalence]math:top:homotopy:df:homotopy; it guarantees only that the induced invariants of $A$ are retracts of those of $X$.
