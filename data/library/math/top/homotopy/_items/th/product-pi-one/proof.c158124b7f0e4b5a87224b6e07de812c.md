A loop in the product is exactly a pair of loops, by the universal property of @[product topology]math:pst:constructions:df:product. A based homotopy in the product is exactly a pair of based homotopies. Thus projection gives a bijection on homotopy classes, with inverse represented by $t\mapsto(\alpha(t),\beta(t))$. Concatenation is coordinatewise, so the bijection and its inverse are @[group homomorphisms]math:algebra:groups-actions:df:groups-homomorphisms.
