Concatenating two endpoint-preserving homotopies on the two halves of $I$ gives a homotopy of concatenations; finite closed pasting proves continuity. Thus multiplication is well-defined. If two parameterizations traverse the same finite list of paths at different positive speeds, interpolate their nondecreasing piecewise-linear time-change functions; composition gives an endpoint-preserving homotopy. This identifies $(\alpha*\beta)*\gamma$ with $\alpha*(\beta*\gamma)$ and removes a stationary interval, proving associativity and the identity law.

For cancellation, set $L_s=1-s$ and define a loop by $\alpha(2tL_s)$ for $t\le1/2$ and $\alpha(2(1-t)L_s)$ for $t\ge1/2$. At $s=0$ it is $\alpha*\bar\alpha$; at $s=1$ it is constant at $x_0$. The same construction with the reversed path proves the other inverse law.
