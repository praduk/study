Composition with $f$ takes based homotopies to based homotopies and satisfies $f\circ(\alpha*\beta)=(f\circ\alpha)*(f\circ\beta)$, giving well-definedness and the homomorphism law. The composition and identity identities follow by applying them to each loop. A based homotopy $H$ gives the loop homotopy $(t,s)\mapsto H(\alpha(t),s)$. Finally $r_*i_*=(ri)_*=\id$, so $i_*[\alpha]=i_*[\beta]$ implies $[\alpha]=[\beta]$ after applying $r_*$.
