Use $H(x,t)=((1-t)+t/\norm{x})x$. Its scalar factor is positive, so the image never reaches zero. At $t=0$ it is $x$, at $t=1$ it is $x/\norm{x}$, and if $\norm{x}=1$ it fixes $x$ for every $t$. Continuity holds on the punctured space. The endpoint formula has different limits along different rays toward zero, so it has no continuous extension there. This verifies failure of this formula, without yet relying on the stronger theorem that no retraction of the closed ball onto its boundary exists.
