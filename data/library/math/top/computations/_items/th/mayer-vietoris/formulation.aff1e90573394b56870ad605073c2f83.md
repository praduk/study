If $X=U\cup V$ is a union of two open sets, there is an exact sequence

$$
\cdots\to H_n(U\cap V)\xrightarrow{(i_*,-j_*)}H_n(U)\oplus H_n(V)\xrightarrow{k_*+l_*}H_n(X)\xrightarrow{\delta}H_{n-1}(U\cap V)\to\cdots.
$$

Here the four named maps arise from inclusions. The minus sign ensures the composite of the first two maps is zero. A reduced version holds when the relevant spaces are nonempty.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §2.2, Mayer–Vietoris sequences](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
