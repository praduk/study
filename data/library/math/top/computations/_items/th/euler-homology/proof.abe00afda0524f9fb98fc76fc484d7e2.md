Use its finite cellular complex over $\Q$. Write $z_n,b_n,c_n,h_n$ for dimensions of cycles, boundaries, chains, and homology. Rank-nullity gives $c_n=z_n+b_{n-1}$, while $h_n=z_n-b_n$. Thus $c_n=h_n+b_n+b_{n-1}$. Alternating sums cancel the two boundary contributions because only finitely many groups are nonzero. The remaining equality is the claimed formula. Homotopy invariance of homology then gives invariance of this integer.
