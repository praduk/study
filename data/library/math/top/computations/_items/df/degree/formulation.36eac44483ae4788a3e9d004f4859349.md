Choose orientations so that $H_n(S^n)\isoto\Z$, with $n\ge1$. For $f:S^n\to S^n$, its degree is the integer satisfying $f_*[S^n]=\deg(f)[S^n]$. Homotopy invariance makes degree constant under homotopy, and functoriality gives $\deg(gf)=\deg(g)\deg(f)$.

Reversing both domain and codomain orientation leaves degree unchanged; reversing just one changes its sign. The definition uses the sphere homology calculation above.
