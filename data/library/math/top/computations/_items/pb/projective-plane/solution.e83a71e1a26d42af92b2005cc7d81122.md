The cellular complex is $0\to\Z\xrightarrow{2}\Z\xrightarrow{0}\Z\to0$, with groups in degrees $2,1,0$. Multiplication by $2$ has zero kernel and image $2\Z$. Therefore $H_2(\RP^2)=0$, $H_1(\RP^2)=\Z/2\Z$, and $H_0(\RP^2)=\Z$. Higher groups vanish because there are no higher cells. The disk model with antipodal boundary identification has this attaching degree: one traversal of its boundary traverses the projective-line one-cell twice in the same orientation.
