Let $U$ be the suspension minus its south pole and $V$ the suspension minus its north pole. These are open contractible sets: slide points toward the remaining pole along suspension lines. Their intersection is homeomorphic to $X\times(-1,1)$ and retracts onto $X\times\{0\}$. The reduced @[Mayer–Vietoris]math:top:computations:th:mayer-vietoris sequence thus has zero groups from $U,V$ on both sides of $\widetilde H_k(SX)\to\widetilde H_{k-1}(U\cap V)$, making that map an isomorphism. Homotopy invariance identifies the target with $\widetilde H_{k-1}(X)$.
