The cellular groups are $C_2=\Z$, $C_1=\Z^{2g}$, and $C_0=\Z$. The one-dimensional boundary is zero because each edge is a loop at the sole vertex. In the attaching word $\prod_j[a_j,b_j]$, each generator occurs once positively and once negatively, so every exponent sum, and therefore every coefficient of $\partial_2$, is zero. Hence $H_0=\Z$, $H_1=\Z^{2g}$, $H_2=\Z$, with all other groups zero. In particular $\chi=1-2g+1=2-2g$.
