A chain complex of @[abelian groups]math:algebra:groups-actions:df:groups-homomorphisms consists of groups $C_n$ and homomorphisms $\partial_n:C_n\to C_{n-1}$ with $\partial_{n-1}\partial_n=0$. Its cycles are $Z_n=\ker\partial_n$, boundaries are $B_n=\im\partial_{n+1}$, and homology is $H_n(C)=Z_n/B_n$.

A chain map $f:C\to D$ satisfies $\partial f=f\partial$ and induces maps on homology, because it preserves both cycles and boundaries. A sequence is exact at a term when the preceding image equals the following kernel.
