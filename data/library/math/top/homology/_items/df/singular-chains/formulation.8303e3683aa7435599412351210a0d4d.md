The standard $n$-simplex is $\Delta^n=\{(t_0,\ldots,t_n):t_i\ge0,\sum t_i=1\}$. A singular $n$-simplex in $X$ is a continuous map $\sigma:\Delta^n\to X$. The group $C_n(X)$ is free abelian on all such maps; chains are finite integer combinations.

For $n\ge1$, set $\partial\sigma=\sum_{i=0}^n(-1)^i\sigma\circ\delta_i$, where $\delta_i$ inserts a zero as the $i$th coordinate. Set $\partial_0=0$ for ordinary homology. Singular homology is the homology of this complex.

For an abelian coefficient group $G$, use $C_n(X;G)=C_n(X)\tensor_{\Z}G$ with boundary $\partial\tensor\id_G$ and denote its homology by $H_n(X;G)$.
