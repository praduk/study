For chain maps $f,g:C\to D$, a chain homotopy is a family $P_n:C_n\to D_{n+1}$ satisfying $g-f=\partial P+P\partial$. A topological homotopy between maps produces such an operator on singular chains by triangulating the prism $\Delta^n\times I$.

The algebraic definition is useful independently of geometry: it is an explicit witness that two chain maps agree after passing to homology.
