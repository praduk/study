If $Z\subseteq A\subseteq X$ and $\closure Z\subseteq\Int_X A$, inclusion of pairs $(X\setminus Z,A\setminus Z)\injectto(X,A)$ induces isomorphisms on @[relative homology]math:top:homology:df:relative-homology in all degrees.

The closure and interior are taken in $X$. Deleting an arbitrary subset of $A$ without the stated condition is not licensed by this theorem.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §2.1, excision](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
