For $A\subseteq X$, inclusion and quotient induce an exact sequence

$$
\cdots\to H_n(A)\to H_n(X)\to H_n(X,A)\xrightarrow{\delta}H_{n-1}(A)\to\cdots.
$$

For a relative cycle represented by $c\in C_n(X)$, $\delta[c]=[\partial c]$. The sequence ends $H_0(X)\to H_0(X,A)\to0$.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §2.1, exact sequences](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
