Expand $\partial(\partial\sigma)$. Every codimension-two face is obtained by deleting two original vertices $i<j$. Deleting $j$ then $i$ gives coefficient $(-1)^j(-1)^i$. Deleting $i$ then the new index $j-1$ gives $(-1)^i(-1)^{j-1}$, its negative. The two face maps agree, so these terms cancel pairwise. These are all terms. In dimensions zero and one the final boundary is zero by the definition of $\partial_0$.
