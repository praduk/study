Homotopic maps induce identical homomorphisms on singular homology. Consequently homotopy-equivalent spaces have isomorphic homology groups, and a nonempty contractible space has $H_0\isoto\Z$ and $H_n=0$ for $n>0$.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §2.1, prism operator and homotopy invariance](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
