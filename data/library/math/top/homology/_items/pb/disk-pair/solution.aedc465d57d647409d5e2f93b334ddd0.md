The disk is contractible. The reduced long exact sequence of the pair identifies $H_k(D^n,S^{n-1})$ with $\widetilde H_{k-1}(S^{n-1})$ for $k\ge1$, because the surrounding reduced disk groups vanish. This is $\Z$ when $k=n$ and zero otherwise. The boundary is nonempty and meets the sole path component of the disk, so $H_0(S^{n-1})\to H_0(D^n)$ is surjective; exactness gives $H_0(D^n,S^{n-1})=0$. For $n=1$, the kernel of $\Z^2\to\Z$ is indeed $\Z$, confirming the low-dimensional endpoint. The sphere calculation is @math:top:computations:pb:spheres.
