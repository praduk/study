For an $n$-manifold $M$ without boundary, an integral orientation is a compatible choice of generator of each local group $H_n(M,M\setminus\{x\};\Z)$. For compact oriented $M$, the fundamental class $[M]\in H_n(M;\Z)$ restricts to these local generators.

The cap product combines a degree-$k$ cohomology class with an $n$-dimensional homology class to produce an $(n-k)$-dimensional class. At simplex level it evaluates the cochain on the first $k+1$ vertices and retains the complementary back face, with the shared vertex included in both. Its induced operation is the one used in duality. For a singular $n$-simplex $\sigma$ and a degree-$k$ cochain $\varphi$, this convention is $\sigma\frown\varphi=\varphi(\sigma|[v_0,\ldots,v_k])\,\sigma|[v_k,\ldots,v_n]$ when $0\le k\le n$.
