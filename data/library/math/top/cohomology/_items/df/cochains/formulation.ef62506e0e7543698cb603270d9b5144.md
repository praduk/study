For an abelian group $G$, define $C^n(X;G)=\Hom(C_n(X),G)$ and $(\delta\varphi)(c)=\varphi(\partial c)$. Since $\partial^2=0$, $\delta^2=0$. Set $H^n(X;G)=\ker\delta_n/\im\delta_{n-1}$.

A map $f:X\to Y$ pulls cochains back by precomposition with its chain map and thus gives $f^*:H^n(Y;G)\to H^n(X;G)$. Cohomology is contravariant. Taking the dual of homology groups alone can miss torsion information.
