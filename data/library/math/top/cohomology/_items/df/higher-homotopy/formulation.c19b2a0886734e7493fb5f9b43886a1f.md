For $n\ge1$, $\pi_n(X,x_0)$ consists of based homotopy classes of maps $(I^n,\partial I^n)\to(X,x_0)$, equivalently based maps $S^n\to X$. Concatenation in one cube coordinate defines its group operation. For $n\ge2$ the groups are abelian, by comparing concatenations in two independent coordinates.

A nonempty space is $r$-connected, for $r\ge0$, when it is path connected and $\pi_i=0$ for $1\le i\le r$. A based map acts on all these groups by composition.
