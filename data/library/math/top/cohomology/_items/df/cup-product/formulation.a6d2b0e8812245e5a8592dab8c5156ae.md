For a commutative coefficient ring $R$, $\varphi\in C^p(X;R)$ and $\psi\in C^q(X;R)$, define

$$
(\varphi\smile\psi)(\sigma)=\varphi(\sigma|[v_0,\ldots,v_p])\,\psi(\sigma|[v_p,\ldots,v_{p+q}]).
$$

Here the face restrictions use the indicated ordered vertices of a singular $(p+q)$-simplex. This operation descends to $H^p(X;R)\times H^q(X;R)\to H^{p+q}(X;R)$, making the direct sum of cohomology groups into a graded ring; the descent and its identities are recorded next.
