A cocycle is a functional on $C_n$ annihilating boundaries $B_n$, so restricting to $Z_n$ defines a functional on $Z_n/B_n=H_n$. Conversely any functional on this quotient extends from $Z_n$ to $C_n$ by choosing a vector-space complement, producing a cocycle; evaluation is surjective. If a cocycle vanishes on $Z_n$, it factors through $C_n/Z_n\isoto B_{n-1}$ via $\partial_n$. Extend that functional from $B_{n-1}$ to $C_{n-1}$; the original cocycle is then its coboundary. Thus the kernel is exactly the coboundaries. This proves the induced bijection without assuming finite dimensionality; in finite dimensions the dimensions of a vector space and its dual agree.
