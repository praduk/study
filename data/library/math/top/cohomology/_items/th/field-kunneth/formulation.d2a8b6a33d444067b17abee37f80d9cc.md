For @[CW complexes]math:top:cells:df:cw-complex $X,Y$ and a field $F$, the homology cross product induces an isomorphism

$$
\bigoplus_{p+q=n}H_p(X;F)\tensor_F H_q(Y;F)\isoto H_n(X\times Y;F).
$$

Over $\Z$, additional Tor terms can occur; the field formula must not be applied unchanged to integral torsion groups.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §3.B, topological Künneth formula](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
