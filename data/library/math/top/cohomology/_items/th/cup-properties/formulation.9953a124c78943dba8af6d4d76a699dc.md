The cup product is associative, has the constant degree-zero class $1$ as unit, commutes with pullback, and satisfies $a\smile b=(-1)^{pq}b\smile a$ for $a\in H^p(X;R)$, $b\in H^q(X;R)$. The cochain identity $\delta(\varphi\smile\psi)=\delta\varphi\smile\psi+(-1)^p\varphi\smile\delta\psi$ makes the product well-defined on cohomology.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §3.2, cup product](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
