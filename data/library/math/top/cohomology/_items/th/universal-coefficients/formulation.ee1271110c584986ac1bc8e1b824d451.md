For every space $X$ and abelian group $G$, there is a natural short exact sequence

$$
0\to\operatorname{Ext}^1_{\Z}(H_{n-1}(X;\Z),G)\to H^n(X;G)\to\Hom(H_n(X;\Z),G)\to0.
$$

It splits as a sequence of abelian groups, but a splitting is not generally natural. Set $H_{-1}=0$ for the degree-zero case.

This structural theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §3.1, universal coefficient theorem](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATplain.pdf).
