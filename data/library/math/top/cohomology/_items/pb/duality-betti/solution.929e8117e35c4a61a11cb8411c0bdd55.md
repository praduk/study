The theorem @math:top:cohomology:th:field-cohomology identifies rational cohomology with the vector-space dual of rational homology, so its dimension is $b_k$. Poincaré duality over $\Q$ identifies that cohomology with $H_{n-k}(M;\Q)$, hence $b_k=b_{n-k}$. For odd $n$, pair the terms of $\chi(M)=\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^kb_k$ with indices $k,n-k$. Their signs are opposite and their dimensions equal. There is no middle unpaired index because $n$ is odd. Each pair cancels, giving zero.
