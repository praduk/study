Let $\Psi_{t,h}$ be the evolution from time $t$ to $t+h$. Local uniqueness gives $\Phi_{t+h}=\Psi_{t,h}\circ\Phi_t$. Thus $\Phi_{t+h}^*\alpha_{t+h}=\Phi_t^*(\Psi_{t,h}^*\alpha_{t+h})$. Differentiating at $h=0$ gives the explicit time derivative plus the @math:diffgeo:forms:df:interior-lie derivative generated at that time by $v_t$. This proves the form identity. Change of variables is the definition of integration by @math:diffgeo:forms:df:forms-pullback; smoothness on a compact domain justifies differentiation under the integral.
