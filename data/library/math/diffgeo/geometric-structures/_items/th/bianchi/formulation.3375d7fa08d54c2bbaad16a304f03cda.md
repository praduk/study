For an endomorphism-valued $k$-form $\beta$ and the local connection form in @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:bundle-connection, define

$$
d_A\beta=d\beta+A\w\beta-(-1)^k\beta\w A.
$$

Curvature satisfies $d_AF_A=0$ and $d_A^2\beta=F_A\w\beta-\beta\w F_A$. The second formula reduces to zero for an abelian scalar-valued connection, but not for a general matrix connection.

The section notation and coefficient-valued exterior calculus are defined in @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:bundle-valued-forms.
