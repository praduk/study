By @math:diffgeo:forms:th:cartan, $\mathcal L_{X_H}\omega=d\iota_{X_H}\omega+\iota_{X_H}d\omega=d^2H=0$. Differentiating pullback along the flow proves preservation of $\omega$, and hence of its wedge powers. Pointwise nondegeneracy makes the top wedge nonzero: a symplectic basis is obtained by successively choosing $v,w$ with $\omega(v,w)=1$ and taking their symplectic orthogonal complement. Also $X_HH=dH(X_H)=\omega(X_H,X_H)=0$.

For the bracket, contraction with a commutator gives $\iota_{[X_f,X_g]}\omega=\mathcal L_{X_f}(\iota_{X_g}\omega)-\iota_{X_g}\mathcal L_{X_f}\omega=d(X_fg)=-d\{f,g\}$; this identity follows by differentiating contractions under pullback. Thus $[X_f,X_g]=-X_{\{f,g\}}$. Apply this equality to $h$: $\{\{h,g\},f\}-\{\{h,f\},g\}=-\{h,\{f,g\}\}$. Antisymmetry rearranges it to the cyclic Jacobi identity. The product rule follows because each $X_g$ is a derivation.
