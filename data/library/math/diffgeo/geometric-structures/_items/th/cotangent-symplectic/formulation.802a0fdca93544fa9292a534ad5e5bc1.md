Let $\pi:T^*Q\to Q$ be the cotangent bundle. Its tautological one-form is $\theta_{\alpha_q}(V)=\alpha_q(d\pi(V))$. Then $\omega=-d\theta$ is symplectic. In induced coordinates,

$$
\theta=p_i\,dq^i,\qquad \omega=dq^i\w dp_i.
$$

For a smooth one-form $\alpha$ on $Q$, its graph is Lagrangian exactly when $d\alpha=0$. In particular every graph of $dS$ is Lagrangian.
