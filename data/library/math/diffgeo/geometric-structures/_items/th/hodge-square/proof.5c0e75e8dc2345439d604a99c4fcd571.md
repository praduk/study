At one point choose an oriented orthonormal coframe with squared norms $\epsilon_i\in\{1,-1\}$, whose product is $(-1)^s$. For an increasing multi-index $I$, let $I^c$ be its complement and let $\sigma_I$ be the sign of the ordering $(I,I^c)$. The defining wedge identity gives $\star e^I=(\prod_{i\in I}\epsilon_i)\sigma_I e^{I^c}$. Applying it twice contributes $(-1)^s\sigma_I\sigma_{I^c}$. Moving the $k$ entries past the $n-k$ complementary entries gives $\sigma_I\sigma_{I^c}=(-1)^{k(n-k)}$. This proves the first formula on a basis.

For the second, the inverse metric rescales by $e^{-2f}$, so the pairing of $k$-forms rescales by $e^{-2kf}$, while the volume rescales by $e^{nf}$. Substitute both factors into the defining wedge identity and use uniqueness of the star. No derivative of $f$ enters this pointwise identity.
