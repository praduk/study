At a point of the fiber choose coordinates with $q$ at zero. Surjectivity supplies $n$ input coordinates for which the corresponding Jacobian minor is invertible. After permuting coordinates, the map $x\mapsto(F(x),x^{n+1},\ldots,x^m)$ has invertible derivative. The Euclidean @[inverse function theorem]math:uganalysis:metric-multivariable:th:inverse-function makes it a local coordinate change. In these new coordinates $F$ is projection onto the first $n$ coordinates, so the fiber is their zero slice. Its tangent vectors are exactly those annihilated by that projection, giving the kernel formula. These local descriptions use the @[subspace topology]math:pst:constructions:df:subspace and cover the fiber.
