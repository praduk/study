A derivation annihilates constants because $v(1)=v(1\cdot1)=2v(1)$. Translate coordinates so $x(p)=0$. For a smooth function $f$ near zero, the fundamental theorem of calculus along radial segments gives

$$
f(x)-f(0)=\sum_i x^i h_i(x),\qquad h_i(x)=\int_0^1\partial_i f(tx)\,dt.
$$

The $h_i$ are smooth and $h_i(0)=\partial_i f(0)$. Applying the derivation and the product rule yields $v(f)=\sum_i v(x^i)\partial_i f(0)$. This proves spanning. Applying a linear relation among the $\partial_i$ to each coordinate function gives zero for every coefficient, proving independence.
