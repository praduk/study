For $v\in T_pM$ and a function germ $h$ at $G(F(p))$, the left side evaluates on $h$ as $v(h\circ G\circ F)$. The right side evaluates as $(dF_pv)(h\circ G)$, the same expression. Equality on all function germs proves equality of tangent vectors. The identity formula follows directly from the definition. Therefore a diffeomorphism has an invertible differential, with inverse the differential of its inverse.
