For $F(x)=\norm{x}^2$, $dF_p(v)=2p\cdot v$, which is surjective to $\R$ on the unit sphere. Thus $T_pS^n=\{v:p\cdot v=0\}$. For $G(A)=A^TA$ valued in symmetric matrices, $dG_A(H)=A^TH+H^TA$. At orthogonal $A$, any symmetric $S$ is obtained by $H=AS/2$, so $I$ is a regular value. At $A=I$ the kernel is the skew-symmetric matrices $H^T=-H$, of dimension $n(n-1)/2$. This verifies the manifold structure through a regular-value argument, not just formal differentiation of a constraint.
