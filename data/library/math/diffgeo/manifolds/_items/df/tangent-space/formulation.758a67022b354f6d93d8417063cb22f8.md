Let $C_p^\infty(M)$ be the algebra of germs of smooth real functions near $p$. A tangent vector at $p$ is an $\R$-linear map $v:C_p^\infty(M)\to\R$ satisfying $v(fg)=f(p)v(g)+g(p)v(f)$. These vectors form $T_pM$.

A smooth curve $\gamma$ through $p$ defines a tangent vector $v(f)=(f\circ\gamma)'(0)$. The coordinate tangent vector $\partial_i|_p$ differentiates $f\circ x^{-1}$ in its $i$th coordinate at $x(p)$.
