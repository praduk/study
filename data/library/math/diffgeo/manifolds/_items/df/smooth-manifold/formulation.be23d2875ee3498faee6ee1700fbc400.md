A topological $n$-manifold is a Hausdorff, @[second-countable]math:pst:separation:df:countability space locally homeomorphic to open subsets of $\R^n$. A chart is such a @[homeomorphism]math:pst:spaces:df:continuity $x:U\to x(U)\subseteq\R^n$. Charts are smoothly compatible if all overlap transition maps are $C^\infty$ @[diffeomorphisms]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map.

A smooth atlas covers the manifold by compatible charts. The maximal compatible atlas is its smooth structure. Different atlases may describe the same maximal atlas. Manifolds with boundary use open subsets of the half-space instead, and smoothness there means local smooth extension across its boundary.
