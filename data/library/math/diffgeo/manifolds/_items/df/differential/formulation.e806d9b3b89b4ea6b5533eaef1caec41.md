For $F:M\to N$, define $dF_p:T_pM\to T_{F(p)}N$ by $(dF_pv)(f)=v(f\circ F)$. The derivation rule shows that this is a tangent vector. In coordinates, $dF_p$ is represented by the Jacobian of the coordinate expression of $F$.

The cotangent space is the dual $T_p^*M=\Hom(T_pM,\R)$. The differentials $dx^i|_p$ are dual to the coordinate tangent basis.
