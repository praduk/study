A map $F:M\to N$ is smooth when for charts $x$ around $p$ and $y$ around $F(p)$, its coordinate expression $y\circ F\circ x^{-1}$ is smooth near $x(p)$. It is enough to verify this on charts from compatible covering atlases, since changes of coordinates are smooth.

A diffeomorphism is a bijective smooth map with smooth inverse. A bijective smooth map need not be a diffeomorphism: $t\mapsto t^3$ on $\R$ has an inverse that is not differentiable at zero.
