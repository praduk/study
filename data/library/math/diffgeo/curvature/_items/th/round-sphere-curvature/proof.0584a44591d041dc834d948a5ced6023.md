Let $p$ denote the position vector and $D$ the Euclidean derivative. Differentiating $g(Z,p)=0$ gives $D_XZ=\nabla_XZ-g(X,Z)p$, where $\nabla$ is tangential projection. Projection is metric compatible and torsion free, so it is the @[Levi–Civita connection]math:diffgeo:connections:th:levi-civita. The tangential part of $D_XD_YZ$ is $\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-g(Y,Z)X$. Subtract the expression with $X,Y$ reversed and subtract the tangential part of $D_{[X,Y]}Z$. Euclidean curvature is zero, yielding $0=R(X,Y)Z-g(Y,Z)X+g(X,Z)Y$. Substitute into the definitions: the sectional numerator is the squared area, and tracing over an orthonormal basis gives $(n-1)g$ and then $n(n-1)$.
