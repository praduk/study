Write $S=\partial_sF$ and $T=\partial_tF$ along the variation. The parameter fields commute, torsion freeness gives $D_sT=D_tS$, and each varied curve satisfies $D_tT=0$. The curvature commutator is $D_sD_tT-D_tD_sT=R(S,T)T$. Its first term is zero, so $-D_tD_sT=R(S,T)T$. Replacing $D_sT$ by $D_tS$ and restricting to $s=0$ yields $D_t^2J+R(J,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma=0$, with the sign matching the stated curvature convention.
