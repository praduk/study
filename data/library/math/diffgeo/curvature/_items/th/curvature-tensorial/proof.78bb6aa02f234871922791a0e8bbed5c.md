For the first slot, expand $R(fX,Y)Z$. The term $\nabla_Y(f\nabla_XZ)$ contributes $-Y(f)\nabla_XZ$; the bracket $[fX,Y]=f[X,Y]-Y(f)X$ contributes the opposite term, leaving $fR(X,Y)Z$. Antisymmetry in $X,Y$ gives linearity in the second slot. For the third slot, expanding $R(X,Y)(fZ)$ produces $fR(X,Y)Z$, two pairs of first-derivative terms that cancel, and $(X(Yf)-Y(Xf)-[X,Y]f)Z=0$. Thus all slots are linear over smooth functions. In a local frame this means evaluation uses only the vector components at the point.
