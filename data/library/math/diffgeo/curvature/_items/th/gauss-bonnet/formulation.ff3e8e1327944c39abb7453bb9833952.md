For a compact oriented Riemannian surface $M$ without boundary,

$$
\int_MK\,dA=2\pi\chi(M),
$$

where $K$ is Gaussian curvature, $dA$ the Riemannian area form, and $\chi(M)$ its Euler characteristic.

This theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Paternain notes, global Gauss–Bonnet theorem](https://www.dpmms.cam.ac.uk/~gpp24/dgnotes/dg.pdf). Surfaces with boundary require additional boundary-curvature terms.
