The sphere curvature formula gives $R(E,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma=E$, since the two vectors are orthonormal. Parallelism gives $D_t^2(fE)=f''E$, so the Jacobi equation reduces to $f''+f=0$. Its solutions are $f(t)=A\cos t+B\sin t$. The prescribed initial data yield $f(t)=\sin t$, whose first positive zero is $\pi$. Thus the antipodal point is conjugate along the great-circle segment. The vanishing at the endpoints comes from neighboring great circles meeting again there, not from a failure of the geodesic initial-value theorem.
