For @[linearly independent]math:algebra:linear-foundations:df:basis-dimension $u,v\in T_pM$, define

$$
K(u\w v)=\frac{g(R(u,v)v,u)}{g(u,u)g(v,v)-g(u,v)^2}.
$$

For an orthonormal basis $e_i$, define $\operatorname{Ric}(v,w)=\sum_i g(R(e_i,v)w,e_i)$ and $\operatorname{Scal}=\sum_j\operatorname{Ric}(e_j,e_j)$. In dimension two sectional curvature is the Gaussian curvature and scalar curvature equals $2K$.

The denominator is strictly positive for independent $u,v$. Sectional curvature is attached to the two-plane rather than its chosen basis.
