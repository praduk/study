For a connection, define

$$
R(X,Y)Z=\nabla_X\nabla_YZ-\nabla_Y\nabla_XZ-\nabla_{[X,Y]}Z.
$$

For the @[Levi–Civita connection]math:diffgeo:connections:th:levi-civita, set $R(X,Y,Z,W)=g(R(X,Y)Z,W)$. This course uses @[sectional curvature]math:diffgeo:curvature:df:sectional-ricci $g(R(u,v)v,u)$ divided by squared area, so a round sphere has positive curvature.

Curvature is independent of coordinate choices. Christoffel coefficients are not tensors, but their derivative and quadratic combinations in $R$ cancel the extra coordinate-change terms.
