Along a geodesic $\gamma$, a Jacobi field is a vector field $J$ satisfying

$$
D_t^2J+R(J,\dot\gamma)\dot\gamma=0.
$$

This is a linear second-order ODE, so $J$ is determined by $J(0)$ and $D_tJ(0)$. Two points on a geodesic are conjugate along that segment if a nonzero Jacobi field vanishes at both endpoints. The definition concerns the chosen geodesic segment, not merely the pair of points.
