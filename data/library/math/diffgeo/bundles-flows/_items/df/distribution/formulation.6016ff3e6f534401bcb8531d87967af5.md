A smooth rank-$r$ distribution is a rank-$r$ vector subbundle $D\subseteq TM$. It is involutive if $[X,Y]$ is a section of $D$ whenever $X,Y$ are local smooth sections of $D$.

An integral manifold is an immersed $r$-manifold whose tangent spaces equal $D$ along its image. Integrability asks for such local integral manifolds through every point. Some sources call involutivity itself integrability; this course distinguishes the algebraic condition from the geometric conclusion.
