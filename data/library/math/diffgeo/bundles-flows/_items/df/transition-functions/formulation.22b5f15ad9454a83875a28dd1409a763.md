If two bundle trivializations overlap, their change of fiber coordinates has the form $(p,v)\mapsto(p,g_{\beta\alpha}(p)v)$ with $g_{\beta\alpha}:U_\alpha\cap U_\beta\to\GL(r,\R)$ smooth. They satisfy $g_{\gamma\beta}g_{\beta\alpha}=g_{\gamma\alpha}$ and $g_{\alpha\alpha}=I$.

Duals, direct sums, tensor products, and exterior powers are obtained by applying the corresponding linear-algebra operation to fibers and transition maps. For $F:N\to M$, the pullback bundle is $F^*E=\{(q,e):F(q)=\pi(e)\}$ over $N$.
