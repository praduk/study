A smooth vector field assigns $X_p\in T_pM$ with smooth coordinate components: $X=\sum_iX^i\partial_i$. It acts on smooth functions by $(Xf)(p)=X_p(f)$ and obeys $X(fg)=fXg+gXf$.

A curve $\gamma:J\to M$ is an integral curve of $X$ when $\dot\gamma(t)=X_{\gamma(t)}$. Here $J$ is an interval. A complete vector field has every maximal integral curve defined on all of $\R$.
