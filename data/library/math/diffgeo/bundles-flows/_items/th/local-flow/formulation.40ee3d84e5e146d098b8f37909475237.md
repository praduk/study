Every smooth vector field has a unique maximal local flow $\Phi:\mathcal D\to M$, where $\mathcal D\subseteq\R\times M$ is open, contains $\{0\}\times M$, and each time fiber is the maximal interval of its integral curve. It satisfies $\Phi_0=\id$ and $\Phi_{s+t}(p)=\Phi_s(\Phi_t(p))$ whenever both sides are defined. Each time map is a diffeomorphism between its domain and image with inverse time $-t$.

This theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [MIT differential-forms notes, §2.2 and §4.3, integral curves and their smooth dependence](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.952/2018SP/files/18.952_book.pdf).
