A smooth constant-rank distribution is locally spanned by the first $r$ coordinate vector fields in suitable charts exactly when it is involutive. Consequently an involutive distribution has local @[integral manifolds]math:diffgeo:bundles-flows:df:distribution obtained by fixing the remaining coordinates.

This theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Dupont notes, Frobenius theorem in chapter 9](https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/dupontnotes.pdf). Constant rank is part of the hypothesis.
