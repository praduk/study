Over a chart, send $v\in T_pM$ to $(p,(v(x^1),\ldots,v(x^n)))$. The coordinate-basis theorem makes this a fiberwise linear bijection. On overlaps, applying a tangent vector to each $y^j$ gives $v(y^j)=\sum_i\partial y^j/\partial x^i\,v(x^i)$ by the chain rule. Thus the transition is smooth and its inverse is the Jacobian of the inverse coordinate change. These compatible trivializations define the smooth bundle structure. The dual transition maps then define the cotangent bundle $T^*M$.
