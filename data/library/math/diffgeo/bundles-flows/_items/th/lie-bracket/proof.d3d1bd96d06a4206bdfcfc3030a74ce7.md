Expanding the commutator on $fg$ shows that the mixed product terms cancel, leaving $[X,Y](fg)=f[X,Y]g+g[X,Y]f$. In coordinates the second derivatives of $f$ cancel because partial derivatives commute, leaving the displayed smooth coefficients. Antisymmetry is immediate. For Jacobi, expand $[X,[Y,Z]]+[Y,[Z,X]]+[Z,[X,Y]]$ as twelve compositions of operators; every ordering of the three operators occurs once with each sign and cancels. Thus the sum is zero on every smooth function.
