The torsion of a connection is $T(X,Y)=\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX-[X,Y]$. The connection is torsion free when $T=0$. A connection is compatible with $g$ if

$$
Xg(Y,Z)=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_XZ)
$$

for all vector fields. Compatibility is equivalently $\nabla g=0$. Torsion freeness and compatibility are separate conditions; together they select the Levi–Civita connection.
