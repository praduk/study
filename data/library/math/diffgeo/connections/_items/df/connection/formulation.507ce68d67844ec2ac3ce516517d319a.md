A connection on $TM$ assigns $\nabla_XY$ to vector fields, is $\R$-bilinear, satisfies $\nabla_{fX}Y=f\nabla_XY$, and $\nabla_X(fY)=X(f)Y+f\nabla_XY$ for smooth $f$.

In coordinates define $\nabla_{\partial_i}\partial_j=\Gamma^k_{ij}\partial_k$. Along a curve, for $V=V^k\partial_k$, the induced derivative is $(D_tV)^k=dV^k/dt+\Gamma^k_{ij}\dot\gamma^iV^j$. The coefficients $\Gamma^k_{ij}$ do not transform as tensor components.

For derivatives of fields taking values in an arbitrary vector bundle, use @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:bundle-connection and @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:bundle-valued-forms. The present definition is their tangent-bundle case.
