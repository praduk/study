A Riemannian metric is a smooth positive-definite symmetric bilinear form $g_p$ on each tangent space. In coordinates $g=g_{ij}\,dx^i\tensor dx^j$, where $(g_{ij})$ is positive definite.

For a piecewise smooth curve, $L(\gamma)=\int\sqrt{g(\dot\gamma,\dot\gamma)}\,dt$. On a connected manifold the Riemannian distance is the infimum of lengths of such curves joining two points. The induced metric topology agrees with the manifold topology, by upper and lower bounds comparing $g$ with the Euclidean metric on sufficiently small relatively compact charts.
