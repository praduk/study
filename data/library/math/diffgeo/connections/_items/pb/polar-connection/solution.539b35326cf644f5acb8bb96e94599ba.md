The metric matrix is $\operatorname{diag}(1,r^2)$, its inverse is $\operatorname{diag}(1,r^{-2})$, and the only nonzero metric derivative is $\partial_r g_{\theta\theta}=2r$. Substitution gives $\Gamma^r_{\theta\theta}=-r$ and $\Gamma^\theta_{r\theta}=\Gamma^\theta_{\theta r}=1/r$. Thus $r''-r(\theta')^2=0$ and $\theta''+2r'\theta'/r=0$. Nonzero Christoffel symbols therefore do not imply nonzero curvature: these are the equations of straight lines expressed in polar coordinates, valid while the curve stays in the chart.
