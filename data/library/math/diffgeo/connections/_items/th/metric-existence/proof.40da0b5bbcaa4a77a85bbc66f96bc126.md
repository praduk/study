Pull the Euclidean @[inner product]math:analysis:hilbert-spaces:df:hilbert back in each coordinate chart to get a local positive-definite metric $g_i$. Choose a smooth @[partition of unity]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity $\rho_i$ subordinate to these charts. Extend each $\rho_i g_i$ by zero outside its chart; containment of the support makes the extension smooth. Local finiteness makes $g=\sum_i\rho_i g_i$ a smooth symmetric tensor. For a nonzero tangent vector $v$, every contribution is nonnegative and at least one has $\rho_i(p)>0$, since their sum is one. That contribution has $g_i(v,v)>0$. Hence $g(v,v)>0$.
