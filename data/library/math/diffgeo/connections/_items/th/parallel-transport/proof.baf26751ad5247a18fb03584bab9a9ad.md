In each coordinate interval, parallelism is the linear ODE $(V^k)'=-\Gamma^k_{ij}(\gamma)\dot\gamma^iV^j$. Continuous smooth coefficients give unique solutions on each compact subinterval. Finitely many curve charts cover the parameter interval, and uniqueness patches the solutions. Dependence on the initial vector is linear; reversing the curve gives the inverse transport. If $V,W$ are parallel, compatibility yields $\frac{d}{dt}g(V,W)=g(D_tV,W)+g(V,D_tW)=0$. Hence their inner product is constant.
