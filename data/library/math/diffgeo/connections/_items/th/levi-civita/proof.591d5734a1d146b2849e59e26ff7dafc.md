For any compatible connection, expand the first three derivatives using compatibility. Replace $\nabla_YX$, $\nabla_ZX$, and $\nabla_ZY$ using torsion freeness. The unwanted covariant derivatives cancel, leaving the displayed formula. Since $g$ is nondegenerate, that formula uniquely determines $\nabla_XY$.

For existence, use its right side to define $\nabla_XY$ by nondegeneracy at each point. The bracket identities $[fX,Y]=f[X,Y]-Y(f)X$ and $[X,fY]=X(f)Y+f[X,Y]$ show, after expansion, that the formula is $C^\infty$-linear in $Z$, linear in $X$ over smooth functions, and obeys $\nabla_X(fY)=X(f)Y+f\nabla_XY$. Its coordinate coefficients are smooth because the inverse matrix of $g$ is smooth. Swapping $X,Y$ in the formula gives $\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX=[X,Y]$; adding the formulas with $Y,Z$ interchanged gives compatibility. Thus it is the required connection.
