Differentiate under the integral on a neighborhood of a fixed $x$; compactness of the parameter interval and openness of the star-shaped domain justify this locally. Closedness means $\partial_ja_i=\partial_ia_j$. Therefore

$$
\partial_jf(x)=\int_0^1\left(a_j(tx)+t\sum_i x^i\partial_ja_i(tx)\right)dt=\int_0^1\frac{d}{dt}\bigl(t a_j(tx)\bigr)dt=a_j(x).
$$

The lower endpoint contributes zero by smoothness at the origin. Thus all coefficients of $df$ equal those of $\alpha$.
