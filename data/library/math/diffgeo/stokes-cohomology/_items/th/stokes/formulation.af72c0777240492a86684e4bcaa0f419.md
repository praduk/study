Let $M$ be an oriented smooth $n$-manifold with boundary, $n\ge1$, and let $\omega$ be a @[compactly supported]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity $(n-1)$-form smooth up to the boundary. Then

$$
\int_Md\omega=\int_{\partial M}i^*\omega,
$$

where $i$ is inclusion and the boundary has the outward-first orientation.
