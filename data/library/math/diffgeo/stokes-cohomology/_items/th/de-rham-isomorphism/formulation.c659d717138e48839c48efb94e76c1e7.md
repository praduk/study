For a @[smooth manifold]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-manifold $M$, integration over smooth @[singular simplices]math:top:homology:df:singular-chains induces a natural isomorphism $H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)\isoto H^k(M;\R)$. Smooth @[singular cohomology]math:top:cohomology:df:cochains agrees with ordinary singular cohomology; this comparison is part of the theorem's construction. The isomorphism takes @[wedge product]math:diffgeo:forms:df:exterior-algebra to @[cup product]math:top:cohomology:df:cup-product.

This theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [John Morgan, Modern Geometry course notes](https://www.math.columbia.edu/~jmorgan/MG_Course_Notes_1.pdf). Real coefficients matter: @[differential forms]math:diffgeo:forms:df:forms-pullback do not retain integral torsion.
