On a star-shaped open subset of $\R^n$, every closed $k$-form with $k\ge1$ is exact. Therefore every closed positive-degree form on a @[smooth manifold]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-manifold is locally exact. Local primitives need not combine to a global primitive.

This theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [MIT differential-forms notes, Poincaré lemma and de Rham cohomology](https://math.mit.edu/classes/18.952/2018SP/files/18.952_book.pdf).
