Every smooth one-form pulls back to $a(t)dt$ with smooth $2\pi$-periodic $a$. There are no two-forms on a one-manifold, so all one-forms are closed. Exactness means $a(t)=f'(t)$ for a smooth periodic function, which forces $\int_0^{2\pi}a(t)dt=0$. Conversely if this integral is zero, $f(t)=\int_0^ta(s)ds$ is periodic and gives a primitive on the circle. Thus integration identifies $H^1_{\mathrm{dR}}(S^1)$ with $\R$; a representative generator is the angular form of integral $2\pi$ or its normalized version of integral $1$. Closed zero-forms are constants because the circle is connected, so $H^0=\R$. Higher groups vanish by dimension.
