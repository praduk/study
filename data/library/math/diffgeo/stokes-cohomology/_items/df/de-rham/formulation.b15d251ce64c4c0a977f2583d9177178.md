A form $\omega$ is closed if $d\omega=0$ and exact if $\omega=d\eta$ for some form $\eta$. Since $d^2=0$, every exact form is closed. Define

$$
H^k_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)=\ker(d:\Omega^k\to\Omega^{k+1})/\im(d:\Omega^{k-1}\to\Omega^k),
$$

with $\Omega^{-1}=0$. Pullback makes this a contravariant invariant, and wedge induces its graded product. In degree zero, it consists of locally constant real functions.
