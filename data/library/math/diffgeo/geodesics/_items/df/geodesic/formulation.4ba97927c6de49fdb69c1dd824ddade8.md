A geodesic for a connection is a curve satisfying $D_t\dot\gamma=0$, equivalently

$$
\ddot\gamma^k+\Gamma^k_{ij}(\gamma)\dot\gamma^i\dot\gamma^j=0.
$$

This second-order equation is a first-order smooth ODE on $TM$. It has a unique maximal solution for each initial position and velocity. For the @[Levi–Civita connection]math:diffgeo:connections:th:levi-civita we call it a Riemannian geodesic. Affine reparameterizations preserve the geodesic equation; arbitrary reparameterizations generally do not.
