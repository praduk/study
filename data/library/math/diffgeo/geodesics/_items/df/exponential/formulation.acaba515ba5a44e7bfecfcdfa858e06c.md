For $p\in M$, let $\gamma_v$ be the geodesic with $\gamma_v(0)=p$, $\dot\gamma_v(0)=v$. Where it exists to time $1$, define $\exp_p(v)=\gamma_v(1)$. This domain contains a neighborhood of $0\in T_pM$.

A neighborhood on which $\exp_p$ is a @[diffeomorphism]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map gives normal coordinates after choosing a basis of $T_pM$. The exponential map is generally not globally injective; completeness concerns whether it is defined on all tangent vectors, not whether it is a global coordinate chart.
