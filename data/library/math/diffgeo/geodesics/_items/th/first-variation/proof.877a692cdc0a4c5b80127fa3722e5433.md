Differentiate the integral using metric compatibility to get $E'(0)=\int g(D_s\partial_tF,\partial_tF)dt$ at $s=0$. Since the parameter vector fields commute and the connection is torsion free, $D_s\partial_tF=D_t\partial_sF$. Thus $E'(0)=\int g(D_tV,\dot\gamma)dt$. The product rule gives $\frac{d}{dt}g(V,\dot\gamma)=g(D_tV,\dot\gamma)+g(V,D_t\dot\gamma)$. Integrating this identity yields the formula. Fixed endpoints give $V(a)=V(b)=0$, and the geodesic equation annihilates the remaining integral.
