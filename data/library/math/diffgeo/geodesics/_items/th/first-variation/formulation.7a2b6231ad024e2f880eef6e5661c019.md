Let $(M,g)$ be a @[Riemannian manifold]math:diffgeo:connections:df:riemannian-metric and take all covariant derivatives with respect to its @[Levi–Civita connection]math:diffgeo:connections:th:levi-civita. For a smooth variation $F(s,t)$ of $\gamma(t)=F(0,t)$ on $[a,b]$, let $V=\partial_sF|_{s=0}$ and $E(s)=\tfrac12\int_a^b|\partial_tF|^2dt$. Then

$$
E'(0)=g(V,\dot\gamma)|_a^b-\int_a^bg(V,D_t\dot\gamma)\,dt.
$$

In particular geodesics are stationary for energy under variations fixing endpoints.
