For a connected finite-dimensional Riemannian manifold without boundary, the following are equivalent: completeness of its Riemannian distance; existence of every maximal geodesic for all real times; compactness of every closed bounded subset. Under these conditions any two points are joined by a length-minimizing geodesic.

This theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Dupont, *Differential Geometry*, chapter 3, Hopf–Rinow theorem](https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/dupontnotes.pdf).
