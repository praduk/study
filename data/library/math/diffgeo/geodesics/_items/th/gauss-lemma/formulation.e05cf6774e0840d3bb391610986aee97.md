Let $(M,g)$ be a @[Riemannian manifold]math:diffgeo:connections:df:riemannian-metric, and let $\exp_p$ be the exponential map of its @[Levi–Civita connection]math:diffgeo:connections:th:levi-civita. Where $\exp_p$ is defined, Gauss lemma gives

$$
g_{\exp_p(v)}(d\exp_p|_v(v),d\exp_p|_v(w))=g_p(v,w).
$$

In a sufficiently small normal ball, radial geodesics minimize length from the center among curves staying in the ball; each sufficiently short segment of a geodesic is minimizing between its endpoints. This is a local assertion and does not make every complete geodesic globally minimizing.

This theorem is used without proof here. See the inspected [Dupont, *Differential Geometry*, chapter 3, exponential map and minimal geodesics](https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/dupontnotes.pdf).
