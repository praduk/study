Smooth dependence of the geodesic ODE on its initial data makes $\exp_p$ smooth near zero. Uniqueness and time rescaling give $\exp_p(tv)=\gamma_v(t)$ for sufficiently small $t$. Differentiate at $t=0$ to get $d(\exp_p)_0(v)=\dot\gamma_v(0)=v$. The @[inverse function theorem]math:uganalysis:metric-multivariable:th:inverse-function now supplies neighborhoods of zero and $p$ on which $\exp_p$ is a @[diffeomorphism]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map.
