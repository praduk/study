Both sides are degree-zero derivations of the exterior algebra: for the right side this follows by combining the graded product rules for $d$ and contraction, with the mixed terms cancelling. On a function $f$, the right side is $\iota_Xdf=Xf$, which is also the derivative of $f\circ\Phi_t$. On $df$, the right side is $d(Xf)$; the left side is the same because pullback commutes with $d$ and differentiation in $t$ commutes with coordinate differentiation. Every form is locally a sum of products $f_0\,df_1\w\cdots\w df_k$, using coordinate functions. Agreement on these generators and the product rule proves the formula.
