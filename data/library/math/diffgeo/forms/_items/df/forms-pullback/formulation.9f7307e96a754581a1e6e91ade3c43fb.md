A differential $k$-form is a smooth section of $\Lambda^kT^*M$; their space is $\Omega^k(M)$. Locally it is $\omega=\sum_{i_1<\cdots<i_k}a_{i_1\cdots i_k}\,dx^{i_1}\w\cdots\w dx^{i_k}$ with smooth coefficients.

For $F:N\to M$, pullback is $(F^*\omega)_p(v_1,\ldots,v_k)=\omega_{F(p)}(dF_pv_1,\ldots,dF_pv_k)$. Pullback preserves wedge products and reverses composition. No inverse of $dF$ is needed, so pullback works for all smooth maps.
