For a vector field $X$ and $k$-form $\omega$, contraction is $(\iota_X\omega)(v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1})=\omega(X,v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1})$, and it is zero on functions. If $\Phi_t$ is the local flow of $X$, the Lie derivative is $\mathcal L_X\omega=\left.\frac{d}{dt}\right|_{t=0}\Phi_t^*\omega$.

The flow definition makes sense locally even when $X$ is not complete. It measures the change of a tensor when it is transported back by the flow.
