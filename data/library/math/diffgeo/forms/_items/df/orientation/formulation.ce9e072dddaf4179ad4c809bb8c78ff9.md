An orientation is a choice of ordered tangent bases up to positive determinant, varying smoothly; equivalently there is an atlas whose transitions have positive Jacobian determinant. An oriented manifold admits a nowhere-zero top-degree form, and such a form specifies an orientation.

On a manifold with boundary, the induced boundary orientation is outward normal first: $(v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})$ is positive on the boundary when $(\nu,v_1,\ldots,v_{n-1})$ is positive in the manifold for an outward-pointing transverse vector $\nu$. A metric is unnecessary for this convention.
