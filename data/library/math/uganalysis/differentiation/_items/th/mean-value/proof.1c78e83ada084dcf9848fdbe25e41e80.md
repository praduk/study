At an interior local maximum or minimum where a derivative exists, the two one-sided difference quotients have opposite weak signs, forcing the derivative to be zero. If $f(a)=f(b)$ and $f$ is not constant, the extreme value theorem gives an interior maximum or minimum with value different from the endpoint value. This proves Rolle's theorem; the constant case is immediate. Apply Rolle to $g(x)=f(x)-f(a)-[f(b)-f(a)](x-a)/(b-a)$ to obtain the displayed formula.
