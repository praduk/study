Let $P(t)=\sum_{k=0}^n f^{(k)}(a)(t-a)^k/k!$ and choose $K=[f(x)-P(x)]/(x-a)^{n+1}$. Then $g(t)=f(t)-P(t)-K(t-a)^{n+1}$ has $g(x)=0$ and $g^{(j)}(a)=0$ for $0\le j\le n$. Rolle first produces a zero of $g'$ between $a$ and $x$. Applying Rolle to $g'$ between $a$ and that zero produces a zero of $g''$. Repeating $n+1$ times gives $g^{(n+1)}(\xi)=0$. Since $P^{(n+1)}=0$, this means $f^{(n+1)}(\xi)=(n+1)!K$, proving the formula.
