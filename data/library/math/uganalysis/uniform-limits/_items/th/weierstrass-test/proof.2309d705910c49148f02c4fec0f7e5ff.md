If $E$ is empty, the conclusion is vacuous. Otherwise, @[absolute convergence]math:uganalysis:series:df:series-convergence at each point follows by comparison. Let $g$ be the pointwise sum. For every $N$, the triangle inequality and the convergence of the nonnegative majorant give
$\sup_{x\in E}|g(x)-\sum_{n=1}^N g_n(x)|\le\sum_{n>N}M_n\to0$.
This is @[uniform convergence]math:uganalysis:uniform-limits:df:uniform-convergence.
