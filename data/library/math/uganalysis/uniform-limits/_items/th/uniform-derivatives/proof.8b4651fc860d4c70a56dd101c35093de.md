The uniform limit $g$ of continuous functions is continuous. Let $\ell=\lim f_n(x_0)$ and define $f(x)=\ell+\int_{x_0}^x g(t)\,dt$. By the fundamental theorem, $f'=g$. For each $x$,
$|f_n(x)-f(x)|\le|f_n(x_0)-\ell|+(b-a)\sup|f_n'-g|$.
The right side is independent of $x$ and tends to zero. Thus convergence is uniform and the stated derivative identity holds.
