Uniform approximation by one bounded $f_n$ makes $f$ bounded. For $\eta=\sup|f-f_n|$, every partition satisfies
$U(f,P)-L(f,P)\le U(f_n,P)-L(f_n,P)+2\eta(b-a)$.
Choose $n$ to make the last term arbitrarily small, then choose a partition for $f_n$ with a small Darboux gap. This proves integrability. Finally, the order bound gives $|\int(f-f_n)|\le(b-a)\sup|f-f_n|\to0$.
