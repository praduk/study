For Riemann integrable $f,g:[a,b]\to\R$ and real $\alpha,\beta$, the function $\alpha f+\beta g$ is integrable and

$$
\int_a^b(\alpha f+\beta g)=\alpha\int_a^b f+\beta\int_a^b g.
$$

If $f\le g$, then $\int_a^b f\le\int_a^b g$. For $a\le c\le b$, the restrictions are integrable and

$$
\int_a^b f=\int_a^c f+\int_c^b f.
$$

Also $|f|$ is integrable and $|\int_a^b f|\le\int_a^b|f|\le(b-a)\sup_{[a,b]}|f|$.
