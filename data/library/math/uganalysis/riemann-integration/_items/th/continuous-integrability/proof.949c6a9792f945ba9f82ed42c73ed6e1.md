The function is bounded and uniformly continuous. If $a=b$, the assertion is immediate. Otherwise, for $\epsilon>0$ choose $\delta>0$ so $|f(x)-f(y)|<\epsilon/[2(b-a)]$ when $|x-y|<\delta$. Choose a partition with mesh smaller than $\delta$. The oscillation on every subinterval is at most $\epsilon/[2(b-a)]$, so the difference of Darboux sums is at most $\epsilon/2<\epsilon$. Apply the Darboux criterion.
