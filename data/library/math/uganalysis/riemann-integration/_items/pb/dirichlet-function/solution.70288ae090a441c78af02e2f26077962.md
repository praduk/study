Every nondegenerate interval contains a rational number. It also contains an irrational: choose a rational $q$ in a suitably smaller interval, then add $\sqrt2/n$ for a sufficiently large positive integer $n$. Thus the infimum on every partition subinterval is zero and the supremum is one. Every lower sum is $0$ and every upper sum is $1$. The two integrals differ, so $f$ is not Riemann integrable.
