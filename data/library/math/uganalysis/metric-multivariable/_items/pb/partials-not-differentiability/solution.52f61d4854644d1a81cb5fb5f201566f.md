The inequality $2|xy|\le x^2+y^2$ gives $|f(x,y)|\le\sqrt{x^2+y^2}/2$, proving continuity. The restrictions to both coordinate axes vanish, so both partial derivatives are zero. Any total derivative would therefore be the zero linear map. Along $(t,t)$, however, $f(t,t)=|t|/\sqrt2$, and $|f(t,t)|/\norm{(t,t)}=1/2$ for $t\ne0$. The required remainder quotient does not tend to zero.
