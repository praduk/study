Polynomials are continuous by the limit laws, and the reciprocal is continuous away from zero. For $x_n=n$, $y_n=n+1/n$, their distance tends to zero while $y_n^2-x_n^2=2+1/n^2\ge2$. For $u_n=1/(n+2)$ and $v_n=1/(n+3)$, both lie in $(0,1)$ and their distance tends to zero, while $|1/u_n-1/v_n|=1$. Either pair contradicts the definition of uniform continuity with a fixed positive error tolerance.
