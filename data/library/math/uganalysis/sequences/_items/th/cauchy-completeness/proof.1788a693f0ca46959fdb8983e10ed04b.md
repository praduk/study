If $x_n\to L$, choose a tail with $|x_n-L|<\epsilon/2$ and use the triangle inequality. Conversely, a Cauchy sequence has a tail within $1$ of one fixed term and is therefore bounded. Bolzano–Weierstrass gives $x_{n_k}\to L$. For a given $\epsilon$, choose $N$ so all tail pairs differ by less than $\epsilon/2$, and choose $n_k\ge N$ with $|x_{n_k}-L|<\epsilon/2$. For every $n\ge N$, $|x_n-L|<\epsilon$.
