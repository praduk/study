Enclose the sequence in a closed interval. Bisect it and retain a closed half containing infinitely many terms; repeat. The resulting nested intervals have lengths tending to zero, so their intersection is a single point $L$. Recursively choose a term in the $k$th interval with index greater than the preceding chosen index. Such a term exists because that interval contains infinitely many terms. Both the chosen term and $L$ lie in the $k$th interval, so their distance tends to zero.
