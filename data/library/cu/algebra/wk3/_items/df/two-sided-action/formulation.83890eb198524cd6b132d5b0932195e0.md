A two-sided action of groups $G$ and $H$ on a set $A$ is a function
$$
\begin{aligned}
G\times A\times H &\to A \\
(g,a,h) &\mapsto gah
\end{aligned}
$$
such that

1. $G\times A \times \set{1} \to A$ is a left action of $G$ on $A$.
2. $\set{1} \times A \times \set{H} \to A$ is a right action of $H$ on $A$.
3. $(ga)h = g(ah)$.
