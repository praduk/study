Let $H\subseteq G$ be a subgroup.  Then all the following are characterizations of normal subgroups

1. $(aH)(bH) = (ab)H \quad a,b\in G$
2. $H = \ker\phi$ for some homomorphism $\phi:G\to H$.
3. For each $h\in H$ and for each $g\in G$, $ghg^{-1}\in H$.
4. $gH = Hg$ for all $g\in G$.
5. $H$ is a union of conjugacy classes in $G$.
