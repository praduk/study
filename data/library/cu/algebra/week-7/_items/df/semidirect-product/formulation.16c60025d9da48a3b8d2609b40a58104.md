Let $H$ and $K$ be groups.  Let $H$ act on $K$ and let $\phi:H \to \Aut(K)$ be the associated group homomorphism.  Then the semidirect product $K\semid_\phi H$ is a group where the set is $K\times H$ and the operation is
$$
(k_1,h_1)(k_2,h_2) = (k_1 (\phi(h_1) k_2),h_1h_2 ).
$$

The semidirect product $H\semidop K$ is a group where the set is $H\times K$ and the operation is
$$
(h_1,k_1)(h_2,k_2) = (h_1h_2,(k_1 \phi(h_2))k_2 ).
$$
