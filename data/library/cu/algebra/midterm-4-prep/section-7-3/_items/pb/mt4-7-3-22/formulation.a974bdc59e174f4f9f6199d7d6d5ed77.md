> Let $R$ be a @[ring with identity]mt4-ring $1\ne0$.

Let $a$ be an element of the ring $R$.

**(a)** Prove that $\{x\in R\mid ax=0\}$ is a @[right ideal]mt4-ideal and $\{y\in R\mid ya=0\}$ is a left ideal (called respectively the right and left *@[annihilators]mt4-annihilator* of $a$ in $R$).

**(b)** Prove that if $L$ is a left ideal of $R$ then $\{x\in R\mid xa=0\text{ for all }a\in L\}$ is a @[two-sided ideal]mt4-ideal (called the left *annihilator* of $L$ in $R$).
