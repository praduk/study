> Let $R$ be a @[ring with identity]mt4-ring $1\ne0$.

Assume $R$ is a @[commutative ring]mt4-ring with $1$. Prove that the @[Binomial Theorem]mt4-binomial-theorem

$$
(a+b)^n=\sum_{k=0}^n\binom nk a^k b^{n-k}
$$

holds in $R$, where the @[binomial coefficient]gre:mathematics-subject-test:discrete-mathematics:th:combination-count $\binom nk$ is interpreted in $R$ as the sum $1+1+\cdots+1$ of the identity $1$ in $R$ taken $\binom nk$ times.
