Linearity of the definite integral gives $\varphi(f+g)=\varphi(f)+\varphi(g)$ for all continuous $f,g$, so $\varphi$ is additive. In the @[pointwise function ring]mt4-function-ring take $f(t)=t$. Then

$$
\varphi(f^2)=\int_0^1 t^2\,dt=\tfrac13,\qquad
\varphi(f)^2=\left(\int_0^1 t\,dt\right)^2=\tfrac14.
$$

The product condition in the @[definition of a ring homomorphism]mt4-ring-homomorphism fails.
