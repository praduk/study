Both coefficient rings are @[integral domains]mt4-integral-domain. By the @[polynomial unit theorem]mt4-polynomial-degree-product, each polynomial @[unit]mt4-unit is an invertible constant. In $\Z$, a product $ab=1$ forces $|a|=|b|=1$, so $\Z[x]^\times=\{1,-1\}$. In the @[field]mt4-field $\Q$ every nonzero element is invertible, so $\Q[x]^\times=\Q^\times$ is infinite. Any @[ring isomorphism]mt4-ring-isomorphism would induce a bijection of their @[unit groups]mt4-units-under-isomorphism. Their different sizes rule out such a map.
