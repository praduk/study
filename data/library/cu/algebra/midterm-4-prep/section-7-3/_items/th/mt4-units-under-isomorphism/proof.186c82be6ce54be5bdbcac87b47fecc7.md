For a @[unit]mt4-unit $u$, use the @[multiplicative law for a ring homomorphism]mt4-ring-homomorphism on $uu^{-1}=u^{-1}u=1_R$. Both products of the images equal $1_S$. If $\varphi$ is a @[ring isomorphism]mt4-ring-isomorphism, every $s$ equals $\varphi(r)$ and hence $\varphi(1_R)s=s=s\varphi(1_R)$. @[Uniqueness of the identity]mt4-ring-arithmetic gives $\varphi(1_R)=1_S$. Applying the first part to $\varphi$ and its inverse gives inverse maps on unit groups, and both preserve multiplication.
