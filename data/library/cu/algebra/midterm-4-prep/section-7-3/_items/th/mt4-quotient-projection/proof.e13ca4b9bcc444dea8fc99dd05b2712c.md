For $\pi(r)=r+I$, the @[quotient operations]mt4-quotient-operations give $\pi(r+s)=\pi(r)+\pi(s)$ and $\pi(rs)=\pi(r)\pi(s)$. These are the two conditions for a @[ring homomorphism]mt4-ring-homomorphism. Every coset $r+I$ is the image of $r$, proving surjectivity. The @[zero coset]mt4-quotient-ring is $I$, so $\pi(r)=0$ exactly when $r\in I$. Hence $\ker\pi=I$.
