Addition is already well-defined in the abelian quotient group. If $i,j\in I$ and $I$ is a @[two-sided ideal]mt4-ideal, then $(r+i)(s+j)-rs=rj+is+ij\in I$. Thus the product is independent of the representatives. The ring identities descend from $R$. Conversely suppose coset multiplication is well-defined. Every $i\in I$ represents zero, so $(r+I)(i+I)=I=(i+I)(r+I)$ gives $ri,ir\in I$. This proves both absorption conditions.
