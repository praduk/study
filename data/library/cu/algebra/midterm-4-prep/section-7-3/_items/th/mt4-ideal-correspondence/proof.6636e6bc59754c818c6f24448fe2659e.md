Images and preimages of subrings under a ring homomorphism are subrings (check zero, subtraction and products). If $A$ contains $I$, then $\pi^{-1}(\pi(A))=A$, since a representative of a class in $\pi(A)$ differs from an element of $A$ by an element of $I$. For any subring $B\subseteq R/I$, surjectivity of the @[natural projection]mt4-quotient-projection gives $\pi(\pi^{-1}(B))=B$. Both assignments preserve inclusion. If $A$ is an ideal, every multiplier of $R/I$ lifts to one in $R$, so $\pi(A)$ absorbs on both sides. If $B$ is an ideal, $\pi(rx)=\pi(r)\pi(x)$ and $\pi(xr)=\pi(x)\pi(r)$ give both absorption properties for $\pi^{-1}(B)$.
