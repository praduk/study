A @[ring homomorphism]mt4-ring-homomorphism is a homomorphism of additive groups. It therefore @[preserves zero and additive inverses]cu:algebra:midterm:theorems:th:mt-homomorphism-facts, and in particular $\varphi(a-b)=\varphi(a)-\varphi(b)$.

The image contains $\varphi(0)=0$. For $s=\varphi(a)$ and $t=\varphi(b)$, both $s-t=\varphi(a-b)$ and $st=\varphi(ab)$ belong to the image. The @[subring test]mt4-subring applies.

The kernel also contains zero. If $i,j$ lie in it, $\varphi(i-j)=0-0=0$. For $r\in R$, the @[zero product identities]mt4-ring-arithmetic give $\varphi(ri)=\varphi(r)0=0$ and $\varphi(ir)=0\varphi(r)=0$. These are the two absorption conditions in the @[ideal test]mt4-ideal.
