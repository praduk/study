A left ideal of a ring $R$ is an additive subgroup $I$ with $ri\in I$ for all $r\in R,i\in I$. A right ideal satisfies $ir\in I$ for all such pairs. A two-sided ideal satisfies both conditions. In a commutative ring the three conditions coincide. By the @[subgroup criterion]cu:algebra:midterm:theorems:th:mt-subgroup-test for $(R,+)$, the additive subgroup condition is equivalent to requiring that $I$ be nonempty and closed under subtraction.
