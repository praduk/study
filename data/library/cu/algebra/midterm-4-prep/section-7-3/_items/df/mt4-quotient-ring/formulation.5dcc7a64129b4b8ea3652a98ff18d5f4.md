For a ring $R$ and a two-sided ideal $I$, $R/I$ consists of the additive cosets $r+I$. Two representatives determine the same @[additive coset]cu:algebra:midterm-3:foundations:df:mt3-index exactly when their difference lies in $I$: if $r=s+i$ with $i\in I$, then $r+I=s+I$. Conversely $r+I=s+I$ implies $r\in s+I$. Writing $\bar r=r+I$, the ring operations are $\bar r+\bar s=\overline{r+s}$ and $\bar r\,\bar s=\overline{rs}$. The @[quotient operations theorem]mt4-quotient-operations shows these formulas are well-defined for two-sided ideals. Its zero is $I$, and $\bar r=0$ exactly when $r\in I$.
