> Let $R$ be a @[ring]mt4-ring with $1$.

The *@[center]mt4-ring-center* of a ring $R$ is $\{z\in R\mid zr=rz\text{ for all }r\in R\}$ (i.e., is the set of all elements which commute with every element of $R$). Prove that the center of a ring is a @[subring]mt4-subring that contains the identity. Prove that the center of a @[division ring]mt4-division-ring is a @[field]mt4-field.
