In the ring of all functions $[0,1]\to\R$, decide separately whether the following sets are subrings.

(a) Functions vanishing at every rational point of $[0,1]$.

(b) Restrictions of real polynomial functions.

(c) Functions with finitely many zeros, together with the zero function.

(d) Functions with infinitely many zeros.

(e) Functions satisfying $\lim_{x\to1^-} f(x)=0$.

(f) Finite rational linear combinations of $\sin(nx)$ and $\cos(mx)$ for integers $m,n\ge0$.
