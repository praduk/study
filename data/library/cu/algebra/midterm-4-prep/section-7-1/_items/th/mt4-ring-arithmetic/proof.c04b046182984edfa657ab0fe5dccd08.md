Distribute $(0+0)a$ and cancel $0a$ in the additive group to get $0a=0$. Distributing $a(0+0)$ gives $a0=0$. Now $(a+(-a))b=0$ and $a(b+(-b))=0$ identify both signed products as the additive inverse of $ab$. Applying this sign rule twice gives the double-negative formula. For identities $e,f$, the product $ef$ equals both $f$ and $e$. The sign rule applied to $1a$ gives the last identity.
