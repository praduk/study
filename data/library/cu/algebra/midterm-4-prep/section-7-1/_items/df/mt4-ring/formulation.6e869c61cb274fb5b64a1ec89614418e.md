A ring $R$ has an abelian group operation $+$ and an associative multiplication. For all $a,b,c\in R$ the two distributive laws are

$$
a(b+c)=ab+ac,\qquad (a+b)c=ac+bc.
$$

The ring is **commutative** if $ab=ba$ for all $a,b$. A **multiplicative identity** is an element $1$ satisfying $1a=a1=a$ for every $a$. Dummit and Foote allow rings without an identity.
