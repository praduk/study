In a ring $R$ with $1\ne0$, an element $u$ is a unit if there exists $v\in R$ with $uv=vu=1$. Then $v=u^{-1}$. The units form a group $R^\times$ under multiplication. In particular, $(uw)^{-1}=w^{-1}u^{-1}$ for units $u,w$.

If $v,w$ are both inverses of $u$, then $v=v(uw)=(vu)w=w$. For units $u,w$, multiplying $uw$ by $w^{-1}u^{-1}$ in either order gives $1$, which proves closure and the inverse formula. Multiplication is associative by the @[ring axioms]mt4-ring, $1$ is its own inverse, and the inverse of $u^{-1}$ is $u$. Thus $R^\times$ satisfies the @[group axioms]cu:algebra:wk1:df:group.
