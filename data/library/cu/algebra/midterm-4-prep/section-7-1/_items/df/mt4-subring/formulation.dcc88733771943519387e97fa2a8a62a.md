A subring $S\subseteq R$ is an abelian subgroup where multiplication is closed.  Equivalently

$$
a,b\in S \implies ab\in S \land a-b\in S.
$$

The @[subgroup criterion]cu:algebra:midterm:theorems:th:mt-subgroup-test applied to $(R,+)$ gives the subtraction test. Closure under products then supplies multiplication on $S$, and the @[ring identities]mt4-ring hold because they hold in $R$.
