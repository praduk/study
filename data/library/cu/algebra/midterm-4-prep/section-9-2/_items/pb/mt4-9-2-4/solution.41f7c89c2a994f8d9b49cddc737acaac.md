The units of $F[x]$ are the nonzero constants by @[the polynomial unit criterion]mt4-polynomial-domains, so each associate class of nonzero polynomials has a unique @[monic]mt4-polynomial-ring member. Assume the complete list of monic primes is $p_1,\ldots,p_k$. The list is nonempty: by @[unique factorization]mt4-field-polynomial-pid-ufd the nonunit $x$ has a prime factor.

The polynomial $h=1+p_1\cdots p_k$ has positive degree, so unique factorization gives it an @[irreducible factor]mt4-irreducible. In a UFD @[every irreducible is prime]mt4-irreducible-prime. Normalize this factor to a monic prime $p$. If $p=p_i$ for any $i$, it divides both $h$ and the product, hence divides their difference $1$, impossible for a prime. This contradicts the completeness of the list. The same argument works over any field.
