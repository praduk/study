The only units of $F[x]$ are the nonzero constants by @[the polynomial unit criterion]mt4-polynomial-domains. Thus two nonzero polynomials are @[associates]mt4-associates precisely when one is a nonzero scalar multiple of the other. Dividing by the leading coefficient gives the unique @[monic]mt4-polynomial-ring member of each class.

Suppose the full list of monic primes were $p_1,\ldots,p_k$. This list is nonempty because $x$ has a prime factor by @[unique factorization in a field polynomial ring]mt4-field-polynomial-pid-ufd. Form $h=1+p_1\cdots p_k$. Each prime has positive degree, so the @[degree formula]mt4-polynomial-domains gives the product positive degree. Adding $1$ leaves its leading term intact. Thus $h$ is a nonunit and has an @[irreducible factor]mt4-irreducible. In a UFD @[every irreducible is prime]mt4-irreducible-prime. Call the monic associate of this factor $p$.

If $p=p_i$ for some $i$, then $p$ divides both $h$ and the product $p_1\cdots p_k$, so it divides their difference $1$. This would make $p$ a unit, contrary to the @[definition of a prime element]mt4-prime-element. Therefore $p$ supplies an additional monic prime, contradicting completeness of the list. The argument works over every field.
