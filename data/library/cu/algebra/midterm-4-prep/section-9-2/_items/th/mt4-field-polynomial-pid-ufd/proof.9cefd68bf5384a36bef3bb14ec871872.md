By @[polynomial division]mt4-division-polynomials, degree makes $F[x]$ Euclidean. Thus every ideal is principal by @[the Euclidean-domain principal ideal theorem]mt4-euclidean-principal, and $F[x]$ is a UFD by @[unique factorization in a PID]mt4-pid-ufd.

The nonzero nonunits are precisely the nonconstant polynomials by @[the polynomial unit criterion]mt4-polynomial-domains. Each therefore has a factorization into irreducibles by @[the definition of a UFD]mt4-ufd, and each irreducible is prime by @[the prime–irreducible equivalence in a UFD]mt4-irreducible-prime.
