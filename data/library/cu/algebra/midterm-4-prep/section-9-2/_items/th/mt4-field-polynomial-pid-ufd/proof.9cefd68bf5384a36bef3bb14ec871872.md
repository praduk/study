By @[polynomial division]mt4-division-polynomials, degree makes $F[x]$ Euclidean. Apply @[the Euclidean-domain principal ideal theorem]mt4-euclidean-principal and then @[unique factorization in a PID]mt4-pid-ufd. Its nonzero nonunits are precisely the nonconstant polynomials by @[the polynomial unit criterion]mt4-polynomial-domains. In a UFD, comparing factorizations shows that an irreducible dividing a product divides one of the factors. This gives the stated prime property.
