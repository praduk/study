Prime implies irreducible by @[Proposition 10]mt4-pid-prime-irreducible. For the converse let $p$ be irreducible and suppose $p\mid ab$. If either factor is zero, $p$ divides that factor. Otherwise $ab=pc$ with $c\ne0$. Factor all three of $a,b,c$ into a unit and irreducibles, allowing empty products for units. By @[unique factorization]mt4-ufd, the factor $p$ matches a factor of $a$ or $b$ up to associates. Thus $p$ divides one of them.
