Suppose $p$ is a @[prime element]mt4-prime-element and $p=ab$. Then $p\mid a$ or $p\mid b$. If $a=pc$ then cancellation in $p=pcb$ gives $cb=1$; the other case is symmetric.

Conversely suppose $p$ is @[irreducible]mt4-irreducible in a PID. Every ideal containing $(p)$ is $(m)$ for some $m$, so $p=mt$. If $m$ is a unit then $(m)=R$; if $t$ is a unit then $(m)=(p)$. Thus $(p)$ is maximal. By @[the maximal ideal quotient criterion]mt4-maximal-quotient-field, its quotient is a field, hence a domain, so @[the prime ideal quotient criterion]mt4-prime-quotient-domain makes $(p)$ prime.
