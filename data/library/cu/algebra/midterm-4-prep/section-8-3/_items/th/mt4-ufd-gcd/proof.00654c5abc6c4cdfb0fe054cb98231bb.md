By @[unique factorization]mt4-ufd and @[the equivalence of primes and irreducibles]mt4-irreducible-prime, factor counts add under multiplication. If $a\mid b$, the @[definition of divisibility]mt4-divisibility-gcd gives $b=ac$ for some $c\ne0$, so $v_p(b)=v_p(a)+v_p(c)$ for every $p$. Conversely, write $a=u\prod p^{e_p}$ and $b=v\prod p^{f_p}$ using the same representatives of the @[associate classes]mt4-associates. If $e_p\le f_p$ for every $p$, then $c=vu^{-1}\prod p^{f_p-e_p}$ is in the ring and satisfies $b=ac$. The componentwise minima therefore give a common divisor divisible by every common divisor, as required by @[the definition of a gcd]mt4-divisibility-gcd.
