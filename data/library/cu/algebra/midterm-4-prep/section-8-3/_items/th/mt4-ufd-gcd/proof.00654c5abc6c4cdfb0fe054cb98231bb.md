By @[unique factorization]mt4-ufd and @[the equivalence of primes and irreducibles]mt4-irreducible-prime, factor counts add under multiplication. If $b=ac$, then $v_p(b)=v_p(a)+v_p(c)$ for every $p$. Conversely, nonnegative exponent differences form the factorization of a quotient, with the unit of $b$ divided by that of $a$. The componentwise minima therefore give a common divisor divisible by every common divisor, as required by @[the definition of a gcd]mt4-divisibility-gcd.
