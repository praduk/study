Write $G=\Z[i]$.

**(a)** Show that $G/(1+i)$ is a field of size $2$.

**(b)** For a rational prime $q\equiv3\pmod4$, prove that $G/(q)$ is a field of size $q^2$.

**(c)** For a rational prime $p\equiv1\pmod4$, take $p=\pi\bar\pi$ as in the Gaussian factorization theorem. Verify the Chinese remainder hypotheses and identify $G/(p)$ with $G/(\pi)\times G/(\bar\pi)$. Find the order of $G/(p)$ and show both factors are fields of order $p$.
