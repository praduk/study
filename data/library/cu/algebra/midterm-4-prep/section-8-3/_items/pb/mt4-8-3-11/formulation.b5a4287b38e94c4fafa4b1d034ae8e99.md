(*Characterization of P.I.D.s*) Prove that $R$ is a @[P.I.D.]mt4-pid if and only if $R$ is a @[U.F.D.]mt4-ufd that is also a @[Bezout Domain]mt4-bezout-domain (cf. @[Exercise 7 in Section 2]mt4-8-2-7). [One direction is given by @[Theorem 14]mt4-pid-ufd. For the converse, let $a$ be a nonzero element of the ideal $I$ with a minimal number of @[irreducible factors]mt4-irreducible. Prove that $I=(a)$ by showing that if there is an element $b\in I$ that is not in $(a)$ then $(a,b)=(d)$ leads to a contradiction.]
