Since $R$ is not a @[field]mt4-field, some nonzero element is a nonunit. The @[well-ordering]math:set:orders-well-orders:df:well-order of the nonnegative integers lets us choose such a $u$ of least @[norm]mt4-euclidean-norm. Divide any $x$ by $u$ using @[Euclidean division]mt4-euclidean-domain. A nonzero remainder of smaller norm cannot be a nonunit. Thus every remainder is zero or a unit, as required by @[the definition of a universal side divisor]mt4-universal-side-divisor.
