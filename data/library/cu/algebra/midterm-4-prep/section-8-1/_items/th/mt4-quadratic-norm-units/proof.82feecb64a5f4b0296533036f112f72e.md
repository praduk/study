Conjugation preserves multiplication, so $(zw)\overline{zw}=(z\bar z)(w\bar w)$. In the standard basis for @[quadratic integers]mt4-quadratic-integers-norm, $N(a+b\omega)=a^2+ab(\omega+\bar\omega)+b^2\omega\bar\omega$ is an integer for $a,b\in\Z$. Indeed the trace and norm of $\omega$ are respectively $1,(1-D)/4$ in the half-integer case and $0,-D$ otherwise. Also conjugation sends $\omega$ to $1-\omega$ or $-\omega$, hence preserves $\mathcal O$. If $zw=1$ there, the two integer norms multiply to $1$. Conversely, $N(z)=\pm1$ gives the inverse $\bar z/N(z)\in\mathcal O$ by the definition of @[a unit]mt4-unit.
