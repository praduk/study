Write $a/b=s+ti$, and choose integers $m,n$ with $|s-m|,|t-n|\le1/2$. Set $q=m+ni$. By @[the Gaussian norm formula]mt4-quadratic-integers-norm, $N(a/b-q)=(s-m)^2+(t-n)^2\le1/2$. Set $r=a-bq=b(a/b-q)$. By @[multiplicativity of the norm]mt4-quadratic-norm-units, $N(r)=N(b)N(a/b-q)\le N(b)/2<N(b)$. This is the required @[Euclidean division]mt4-euclidean-domain.
