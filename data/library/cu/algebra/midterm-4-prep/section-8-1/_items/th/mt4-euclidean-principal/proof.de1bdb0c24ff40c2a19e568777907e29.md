Let $I\ne(0)$ and choose $d\in I\setminus\{0\}$ minimizing its norm. The values lie in the @[well-ordered]math:set:orders-well-orders:df:well-order set of nonnegative integers, so the nonempty set of these values has a least member. Divide any $a\in I$ by $d$ using @[Euclidean division]mt4-euclidean-domain. The remainder $r=a-qd$ belongs to $I$ by @[ideal closure]mt4-ideal. A nonzero remainder would have smaller norm than $d$, so $r=0$. Thus $I\subseteq(d)$, and $d\in I$ gives the reverse inclusion by @[the definition of a principal ideal]mt4-principal-ideal.
