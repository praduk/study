In a division $s=qt+r$, both $t$ and $r=s-qt$ belong to $(s,t)$, while $t$ and $s=qt+r$ belong to $(t,r)$. By the definition of the @[ideal generated by a pair]mt4-principal-ideal, $(s,t)=(t,r)$.

Repeat @[Euclidean division]mt4-euclidean-domain. Each nonzero remainder has smaller norm than the preceding divisor. The nonnegative integers are @[well-ordered]math:set:orders-well-orders:df:well-order, so the norms cannot decrease indefinitely. At the final step the new pair is $(d,0)$. Consequently $(a,b)=(d,0)=(d)$, and the @[gcd generator criterion]mt4-gcd-generated-pair gives both $\gcd(a,b)=d$ up to a unit and an expression $d=ax+by$.
