At a step $s=qt+r$, each pair $s,t$ and $t,r$ belongs to the ideal generated by the other, so $(s,t)=(t,r)$. Under @[Euclidean division]mt4-euclidean-domain, the norms of nonzero remainders form a strictly decreasing sequence of nonnegative integers, so the algorithm terminates. The final ideal is $(d,0)=(d)$, so @[the gcd generator criterion]mt4-gcd-generated-pair gives the gcd and the identity.
