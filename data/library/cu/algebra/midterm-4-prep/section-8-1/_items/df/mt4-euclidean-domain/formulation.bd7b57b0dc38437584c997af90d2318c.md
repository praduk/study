An integral domain $R$ is **Euclidean** if it admits a @[norm]mt4-euclidean-norm $N$ such that, for each $a,b\in R$ with $b\ne0$, there exist $q,r\in R$ satisfying

$$
a=qb+r,\qquad r=0\ \text{or}\ N(r)<N(b).
$$

Here $q$ is a quotient and $r$ a remainder. The definition requires existence for every dividend and nonzero divisor.
