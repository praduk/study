In a commutative ring with identity, $d\mid a$ means $a=dx$ for some $x$ in the ring. By the description of @[principal ideals]mt4-principal-ideal, this says $a\in(d)$. Because $(a)$ is the smallest ideal containing $a$, the condition is equivalent to $(a)\subseteq(d)$.

For $a,b$ not both zero, a **greatest common divisor** is a nonzero $d$ such that $d\mid a$, $d\mid b$, and every common divisor of $a,b$ divides $d$. The pair is **relatively prime** if $1$ is a gcd.
