Let $D\ne0,1$ be a squarefree integer. The quadratic field $K=\Q(\sqrt D)$ has ring of integers $\mathcal O=\Z+\Z\omega$, where $\omega=(1+\sqrt D)/2$ for $D\equiv1\pmod 4$ and $\omega=\sqrt D$ for $D\equiv2,3\pmod 4$. For $z=a+b\sqrt D$ with $a,b\in\Q$, its conjugate is $\bar z=a-b\sqrt D$ and its field norm is $N(z)=z\bar z=a^2-Db^2$. For $D<0$ this is $|z|^2$ in the complex plane. In particular $D=-1$ gives the Gaussian integers $\Z[i]$ with $N(s+ti)=s^2+t^2$.
