A **Dedekind–Hasse norm** on an integral domain $R$ is a positive @[norm]mt4-euclidean-norm $H$ such that for all nonzero $a,b\in R$, either $b\mid a$ or there is $c\in(a,b)$ with $0<H(c)<H(b)$. The @[two-generated ideal]mt4-principal-ideal consists of sums $ua+vb$. Taking $s=u$, $t=-v$ shows that $c\in(a,b)$ is equivalent to $c=sa-tb$ for some $s,t\in R$.
