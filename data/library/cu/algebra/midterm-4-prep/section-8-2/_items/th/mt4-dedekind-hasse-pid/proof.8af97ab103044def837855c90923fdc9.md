Suppose $R$ admits a @[Dedekind–Hasse norm]mt4-dedekind-hasse $H$. In a nonzero ideal $I$, choose $b\ne0$ with $H(b)$ least, using the @[well-ordering]math:set:orders-well-orders:df:well-order of the nonnegative integers. If $a\in I$ lay outside $(b)$, then $a\ne0$ and $b\nmid a$. The norm condition would give $c\in(a,b)$ with $0<H(c)<H(b)$. Since $I$ contains $a,b$, it contains their @[generated ideal]mt4-principal-ideal and hence $c$, contradicting minimality. Thus $I=(b)$. The zero ideal is $(0)$, so $R$ is a @[PID]mt4-pid.

Conversely, suppose $R$ is a PID. By @[Theorem 14]mt4-pid-ufd it has @[unique factorization]mt4-ufd. For $x\ne0$ let $\ell(x)$ count its irreducible factors with multiplicity, assigning units the empty factorization. The count is well defined and $\ell(xy)=\ell(x)+\ell(y)$ for $x,y\ne0$. Set $H(0)=0$ and $H(x)=2^{\ell(x)}$ otherwise. This gives a positive, multiplicative norm, and $H(x)>1$ for each nonzero nonunit.

Now let $a,b\ne0$ with $b\nmid a$, and write $(a,b)=(c)$. Since $b\ne0$, we have $c\ne0$. Write $b=ct$. If $t$ were a unit, $(b)=(c)$ would contain $a$, contradicting $b\nmid a$. Thus $H(t)>1$ and $0<H(c)<H(c)H(t)=H(b)$. This $c\in(a,b)$ satisfies the required condition.
