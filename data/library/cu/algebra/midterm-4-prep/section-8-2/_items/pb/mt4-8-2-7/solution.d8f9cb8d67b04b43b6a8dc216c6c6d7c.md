**(a)** If $R$ is a @[Bézout domain]mt4-bezout-domain, write $(a,b)=(d)$. For a pair not both zero, @[the gcd generator criterion]mt4-gcd-generated-pair gives both the gcd and its linear expression. Conversely a gcd $d$ divides both generators, so $(a,b)\subseteq(d)$. If also $d=ax+by$, then $(d)\subseteq(a,b)$. The zero pair generates $(0)$.

**(b)** By the definition of a @[generated ideal]mt4-principal-ideal, the empty list generates $(0)$, and a one-element list already gives a principal ideal. If the first $n$ generators give $(d)$, adjoining one more generator $c$ gives $(d,c)$, again principal. @[Induction]math:set:naturals-recursion:th:natural-induction now handles every finite list.

**(c)** Take $a/b$ in the @[fraction field]mt4-field-of-fractions with $b\ne0$. By (a) choose $d=ax+by$ generating $(a,b)$. Write $a=da'$, $b=db'$; since $b\ne0$, both $d,b'$ are nonzero. By @[cancellation]mt4-cancellation, this gives $1=a'x+b'y$. Every common divisor of $a',b'$ divides $1$, so they are relatively prime by @[the definition of relative primality]mt4-divisibility-gcd. The fraction equals $a'/b'$.
