Write $S=D^{-1}R$. By @[the construction of the localization]mt4-ring-of-fractions, it is a subring of the fraction field of $R$ containing $1$, hence a domain. Identify $R$ with its image by @[the canonical embedding]mt4-fractions-embedding and let $J$ be any ideal of $S$. Then $I=J\cap R$ is an ideal of $R$, so $I=(a)$ by @[the PID hypothesis]mt4-pid. For $r/d\in J$, multiplication by $d$ shows $r\in J\cap R=(a)$, say $r=at$ with $t\in R$. Thus $r/d=a(t/d)\in aS$. Conversely $a\in J$ implies $aS\subseteq J$. Every ideal $J$ of $S$ therefore has one generator, including $J=(0)$.
