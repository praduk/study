Write $S=D^{-1}R$. Send each localized fraction $r/d$ to the same fraction in @[the fraction field]mt4-field-of-fractions of $R$. The @[localization rules]mt4-ring-of-fractions and fraction-field rules both use the same operations and equality by cross multiplication, so this embeds $S$ as a subring. It contains $1=d/d$ and inherits the zero-product property of the field, so $S$ is an @[integral domain]mt4-integral-domain. Identify $R$ with its image by @[the canonical embedding]mt4-fractions-embedding and let $J$ be any ideal of $S$. The intersection $I=J\cap R$ is an @[ideal]mt4-ideal of $R$: it contains $0$ and is closed under differences and multiplication by elements of $R$. Thus $I=(a)$ by @[the PID hypothesis]mt4-pid. For $r/d\in J$, multiplication by $d$ shows $r\in J\cap R=(a)$, say $r=at$ with $t\in R$. Thus $r/d=a(t/d)\in aS$. Conversely $a\in J$ implies $aS\subseteq J$. Every ideal $J$ of $S$ therefore has one generator, including $J=(0)$.
