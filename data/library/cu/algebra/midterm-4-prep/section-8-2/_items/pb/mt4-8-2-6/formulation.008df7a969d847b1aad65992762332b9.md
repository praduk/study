Suppose $R$ is an integral domain whose prime ideals are all principal.

**(a)** Assuming a nonprincipal ideal exists, prove there is an ideal $I$ maximal among the nonprincipal ideals.

**(b)** Choose $a,b\notin I$ with $ab\in I$. Set $I_a=I+(a)$, $I_b=I+(b)$ and $J=\{r\in R:rI_a\subseteq I\}$. Prove $I_a=(\alpha)$ and $J=(\beta)$ are principal, $I\subsetneq I_b\subseteq J$, and $I_aJ=(\alpha\beta)\subseteq I$.

**(c)** Show that each $x\in I$ has the form $s\alpha$ with $s\in J$. Conclude that $R$ is a PID.
