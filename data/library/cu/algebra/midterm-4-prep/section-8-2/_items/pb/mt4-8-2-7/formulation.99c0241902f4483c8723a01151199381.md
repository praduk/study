Let $R$ be an integral domain.

**(a)** Show that $R$ is a Bézout domain exactly when each pair $a,b$ has a gcd of the form $ax+by$. For the pair $(0,0)$ use $\gcd(0,0)=0$, or handle the zero ideal separately.

**(b)** Show that every finitely generated ideal in a Bézout domain is principal.

**(c)** Show that each element of its fraction field has a representation $a/b$ with $a,b$ relatively prime.
