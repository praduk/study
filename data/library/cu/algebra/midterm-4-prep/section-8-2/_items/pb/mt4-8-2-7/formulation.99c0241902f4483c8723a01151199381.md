An @[integral domain]mt4-integral-domain $R$ in which every @[ideal generated by two elements]mt4-principal-ideal is principal (i.e., for every $a,b\in R$, $(a,b)=(d)$ for some $d\in R$) is called a *@[Bezout Domain]mt4-bezout-domain*. [cf. also @[Exercise 11 in Section 3]mt4-8-3-11.]

**(a)** Prove that the integral domain $R$ is a Bezout Domain if and only if every pair of elements $a,b$ of $R$ has a @[g.c.d.]mt4-divisibility-gcd $d$ in $R$ that can be written as an $R$-linear combination of $a$ and $b$, i.e., $d=ax+by$ for some $x,y\in R$.

**(b)** Prove that every finitely generated ideal of a Bezout Domain is principal. [cf. the exercises in Sections 9.2 and 9.3 for Bezout Domains in which not every ideal is principal.]

**(c)** Let $F$ be the @[fraction field]mt4-field-of-fractions of the Bezout Domain $R$. Prove that every element of $F$ can be written in the form $a/b$ with $a,b\in R$ and $a$ and $b$ @[relatively prime]mt4-divisibility-gcd (cf. Exercise 1).
