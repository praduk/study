> Let $R$ be a @[ring with identity]mt4-ring $1\ne0$.

An element $e\in R$ is called an *@[idempotent]mt4-central-idempotent* if $e^2=e$. Assume $e$ is an idempotent in $R$ and $er=re$ for all $r\in R$. Prove that $Re$ and $R(1-e)$ are @[two-sided ideals]mt4-ideal of $R$ and that $R\cong Re\times R(1-e)$. Show that $e$ and $1-e$ are @[identities]mt4-ring for the @[subrings]mt4-subring $Re$ and $R(1-e)$ respectively.
