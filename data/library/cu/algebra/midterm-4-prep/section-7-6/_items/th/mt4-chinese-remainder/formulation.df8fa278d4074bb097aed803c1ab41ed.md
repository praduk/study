Let $I_1,\ldots,I_k$ be ideals of a commutative ring $R$ with identity, $k\ge1$. The map $r\mapsto(r+I_1,\ldots,r+I_k)$ is a ring homomorphism with kernel $\bigcap_i I_i$. If the ideals are pairwise comaximal, the map is onto and

$$
\bigcap_i I_i=I_1\cdots I_k,\qquad
R/(I_1\cdots I_k)\isoto\prod_{i=1}^k R/I_i.
$$
