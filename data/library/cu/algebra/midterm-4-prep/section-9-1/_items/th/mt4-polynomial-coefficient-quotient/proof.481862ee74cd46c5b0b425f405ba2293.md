Coefficient reduction commutes with the sums and products defining polynomial operations. Every polynomial in $(R/I)[t]$ has a lift, obtained by choosing a representative for each of its finitely many coefficients. Its kernel consists of the polynomials whose coefficients are all in $I$. Each such polynomial is a sum of products $a_it^i$ with $a_i\in I$, and every multiple of a constant in $I$ has all coefficients in $I$. Apply the @[first isomorphism theorem]mt4-first-isomorphism. If $I$ is prime, $R/I$ is a domain by the @[prime ideal quotient criterion]mt4-prime-quotient-domain. Its polynomial ring is a domain by @[Proposition 1]mt4-polynomial-domains, giving the last claim by the same criterion.
