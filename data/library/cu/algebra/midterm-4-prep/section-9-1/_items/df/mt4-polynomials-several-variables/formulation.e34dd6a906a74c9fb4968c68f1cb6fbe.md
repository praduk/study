For a commutative ring $R$ with identity, $R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]$ consists of finite sums of monomials $a x_1^{e_1}\cdots x_n^{e_n}$ with $a\in R$ and $e_i\ge 0$. A polynomial is determined by its coefficient at each exponent tuple. Add coefficients at equal tuples; multiply monomials by multiplying coefficients and adding exponents.

Equivalently, adjoin the variables one at a time, so $R[x_1,\ldots,x_n]=R[x_1,\ldots,x_{n-1}][x_n]$. For an infinite set of variables every polynomial still uses only finitely many, and equality can be checked in a finite-variable subring.
