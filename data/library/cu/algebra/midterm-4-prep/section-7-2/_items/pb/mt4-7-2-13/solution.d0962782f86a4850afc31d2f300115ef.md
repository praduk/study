**(a)** For each $g\in G$, conjugation by $g$ permutes each @[conjugacy class]cu:algebra:week-5:df:conjugacy-classes. Hence $gs_Kg^{-1}=\sum_{h\in K}ghg^{-1}=s_K$, so $gs_K=s_Kg$. Scalars commute with every group element. Distributing in the @[group ring]mt4-group-ring shows $s_K$ commutes with every element of $RG$ and therefore belongs to its @[center]mt4-ring-center.

**(b)** The same argument shows that every $R$-linear combination of the $s_K$ is central. Conversely, write a central element as $z=\sum_{h\in G}a_h h$. For any $g,t\in G$, comparison of the coefficient of $t$ in $z=gzg^{-1}$ gives $a_t=a_{g^{-1}tg}$. Thus the coefficients are constant on each conjugacy class. The classes partition $G$, so grouping terms expresses $z=\sum_K a_K s_K$, where $a_K$ is that common coefficient.
