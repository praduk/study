> Let $R$ be a @[commutative ring]mt4-ring with $1$.

Let $\mathcal K=\{k_1,\ldots,k_m\}$ be a @[conjugacy class]cu:algebra:week-5:df:conjugacy-classes in the finite @[group]cu:algebra:wk1:df:group $G$.

**(a)** Prove that the element $K=k_1+\cdots+k_m$ is in the @[center]mt4-ring-center of the @[group ring]mt4-group-ring $RG$ (cf. @[Exercise 7, Section 1]mt4-7-1-7). [Check that $g^{-1}Kg=K$ for all $g\in G$.]

**(b)** Let $\mathcal K_1,\ldots,\mathcal K_r$ be the conjugacy classes of $G$ and for each $\mathcal K_i$ let $K_i$ be the element of $RG$ that is the sum of the members of $\mathcal K_i$. Prove that an element $\alpha\in RG$ is in the center of $RG$ if and only if $\alpha=a_1K_1+a_2K_2+\cdots+a_rK_r$ for some $a_1,a_2,\ldots,a_r\in R$.
