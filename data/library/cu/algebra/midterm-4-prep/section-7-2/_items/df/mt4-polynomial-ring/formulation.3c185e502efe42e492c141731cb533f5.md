For a commutative ring $R$ with identity, $R[x]$ consists of finite formal sums $\sum_{i\ge0}a_ix^i$ with $a_i\in R$. Equality and addition are coefficientwise. In a product the coefficient of $x^k$ is $\sum_{i=0}^{k}a_i b_{k-i}$. For a nonzero polynomial, its largest exponent with nonzero coefficient is its degree, and that coefficient is the leading coefficient. The zero polynomial has no such exponent. A nonzero polynomial is monic if its leading coefficient is $1$.
