For a field $F$, a formal Laurent series is an expression $\sum_{i\ge N}a_i x^i$ with $a_i\in F$ and some integer lower bound $N$. Only finitely many negative powers can have nonzero coefficients. The set is denoted $F((x))$. Equality and addition are coefficientwise, and products use the usual convolution of coefficients. For each exponent, only finitely many pairs of indices can contribute.
