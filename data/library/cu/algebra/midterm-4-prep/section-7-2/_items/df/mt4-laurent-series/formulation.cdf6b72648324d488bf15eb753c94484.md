For a field $F$, a formal Laurent series is an expression $\sum_{i\ge N}a_i x^i$ with $a_i\in F$ and some integer lower bound $N$. Only finitely many negative powers can have nonzero coefficients. The set is denoted $F((x))$. Equality and addition are coefficientwise, and products use the usual convolution of coefficients. Indeed, if $f$ and $g$ have lower bounds $N$ and $M$, a term $a_i b_{k-i}$ can contribute to the coefficient of $x^k$ only when $N\le i\le k-M$. Thus each coefficient sum is finite, and the product has lower bound $N+M$.
