By the @[coefficient product]mt4-power-series, $a(x)b(x)=1$ forces $a_0b_0=1$. Conversely put $b_0=a_0^{-1}$ and, for $k\ge1$, recursively define

$$
b_k=-a_0^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^k a_i b_{k-i}.
$$

Now the constant coefficient of $a(x)b(x)$ is $1$, and every positive-degree coefficient vanishes. Commutativity gives a two-sided inverse.
