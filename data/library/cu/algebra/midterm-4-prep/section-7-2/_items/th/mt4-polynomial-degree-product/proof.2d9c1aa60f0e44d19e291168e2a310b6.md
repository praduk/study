Let $a$ and $b$ be the leading coefficients of $f$ and $g$, of degrees $m$ and $n$. In the @[polynomial product]mt4-polynomial-ring the coefficient of $x^{m+n}$ is $ab\ne0$, by the @[domain property]mt4-integral-domain, and there are no higher terms. This proves degree additivity and excludes zero divisors. The constants supply $1\ne0$ and multiplication is commutative. For the claim about @[units]mt4-unit, if $fg=1$, the nonnegative degrees must both be zero, so their constant values are inverse in $D$. Conversely, constant inverses in $D$ remain inverses in $D[x]$.
