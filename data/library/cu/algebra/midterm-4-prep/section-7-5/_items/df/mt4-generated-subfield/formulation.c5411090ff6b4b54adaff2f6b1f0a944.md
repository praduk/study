If $A$ is a subset of a field $K$, the **subfield generated by $A$ in $K$** is the intersection of all subfields of $K$ that contain $A$. The family being intersected contains $K$. Its intersection contains $0,1$ and is closed under subtraction, multiplication, and inverses of nonzero elements because every member has these properties. Thus the intersection is a @[field]mt4-field. It contains $A$ and lies in every subfield containing $A$, as required for the smallest such subfield.
