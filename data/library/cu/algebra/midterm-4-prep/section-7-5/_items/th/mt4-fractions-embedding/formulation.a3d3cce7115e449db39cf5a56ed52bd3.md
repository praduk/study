Let $R$ be a commutative ring and let $D\subset R$ be a nonempty multiplicatively closed set of nonzero elements that are not zero divisors. In the ring of fractions $D^{-1}R$, $R$ embeds, every $d\in D$ becomes invertible, and every element has the form $r/d$. If $\phi:R\to S$ embeds $R$ in a commutative ring with identity and every $\phi(d)$ is a unit, then $\phi$ extends uniquely to a unital embedding $D^{-1}R\to S$.
