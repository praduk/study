Since $A$ is an @[integral domain]mt4-integral-domain, $D=A\setminus\{0\}$ is closed under multiplication, and every $d\in D$ is invertible in the @[field]mt4-field $K$. Apply @[the extension theorem for fractions]mt4-fractions-embedding to embed the @[field of fractions]mt4-field-of-fractions in $K$. Its image consists of the elements $ab^{-1}$ with $a,b\in A$ and $b\ne0$. This image is a subfield because the embedding carries sums, products, additive inverses, and inverses of nonzero elements to the corresponding operations in $K$.

The image contains $A$ since $a=a/1$. Conversely, every subfield containing $A$ contains $b^{-1}$ for each nonzero $b\in A$ and hence every $ab^{-1}$. The image is therefore the @[generated subfield]mt4-generated-subfield.
