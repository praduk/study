Apply @[the extension theorem for fractions]mt4-fractions-embedding with $D=A\setminus\{0\}$ to embed the @[field of fractions]mt4-field-of-fractions in $K$. Its image is a field containing $A$. Conversely, every subfield of $K$ containing $A$ contains $b^{-1}$ for $b\in A\setminus\{0\}$ and hence contains $ab^{-1}$ for all $a\in A$. The image is therefore the @[generated subfield]mt4-generated-subfield.
