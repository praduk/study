Use the fraction operations in @[the definition of the ring of fractions]mt4-ring-of-fractions. For any $d\in D$, $rd/d$ represents the same element independently of $d$, by cross multiplication. This formula respects addition and multiplication and has zero kernel: $rd/d=0$ forces $r=0$ by cancellation of regular denominators. It embeds $R$. For $s\in D$ and any $d\in D$, $d/(sd)$ is the inverse of the embedded $s$, so $r/d$ is the product of the embedded $r$ and the inverse of the embedded $d$.

An extension must therefore send $r/d$ to $\phi(r)\phi(d)^{-1}$. If $re=sd$, applying $\phi$ and multiplying by the inverses of $\phi(d)$ and $\phi(e)$ gives equal values. The sum and product formulas give the homomorphism laws, and $d/d$ goes to $1$. Its kernel is zero because $\phi(r)\phi(d)^{-1}=0$ forces $\phi(r)=0$ and hence $r=0$. The prescribed formula gives uniqueness.
