Let $K\subseteq\R$ be a subfield, with identity $e$. By @[the field axioms]mt4-field, $e\ne0$ and $e^2=e$. Thus $e(e-1)=0$ in $\R$. Since @[every field is an integral domain]gre:mathematics-subject-test:abstract-algebra:th:field-domain, $e-1=0$. Hence $1\in K$.

Adding copies of $1$ and taking additive inverses puts every integer in $K$. By @[the fraction field characterization]mt4-fraction-subfield, the @[subfield generated by the integers]mt4-generated-subfield in $\R$ consists of all $m/n$ with $m,n\in\Z$ and $n\ne0$. These quotients are exactly $\Q$, so $\Q\subseteq K$.
