Let $K\subseteq\R$ be a subfield. Its identity $e\ne0$ satisfies $e(e-1)=0$ in $\R$, so $e=1$. Closure under addition and additive inverses now gives $\Z\subseteq K$. By @[the fraction field characterization]mt4-fraction-subfield, the subfield generated by these integers in $\R$ consists of the actual quotients $m/n$ with $m,n\in\Z$ and $n\ne0$. This is $\Q$, and by @[the definition of a generated subfield]mt4-generated-subfield it lies in $K$.
