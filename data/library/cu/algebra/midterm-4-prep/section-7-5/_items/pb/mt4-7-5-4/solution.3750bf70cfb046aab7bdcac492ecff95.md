**Strategy.** Build every rational number inside an arbitrary subfield $K$ of $\R$. First get the real number $1$, then use addition to get the integers and field inverses to get their quotients.

**Identify the identity.** Let $e$ be the multiplicative identity of $K$. By @[the field axioms]mt4-field, $e\ne0$ and $e^2=e$. In $\R$ this reads $e(e-1)=0$. Since @[every field is an integral domain]gre:mathematics-subject-test:abstract-algebra:th:field-domain and $e\ne0$, we get $e=1$. Thus $1\in K$.

**Build the rationals.** Repeated sums of $1$, zero and additive inverses put every integer in $K$. For $m,n\in\Z$ with $n\ne0$, the inverse of $n$ in $K$ is its real reciprocal, so $mn^{-1}\in K$. By @[the fraction field characterization]mt4-fraction-subfield, these quotients form the @[subfield generated by the integers]mt4-generated-subfield in $\R$, namely $\Q$. Hence $\Q\subseteq K$.
