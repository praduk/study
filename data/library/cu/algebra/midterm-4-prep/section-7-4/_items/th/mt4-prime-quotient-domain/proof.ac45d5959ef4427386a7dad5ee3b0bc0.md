The class $a+P$ vanishes exactly when $a\in P$. The quotient has nonzero identity precisely when $P\ne R$. By @[the definition of quotient multiplication]mt4-quotient-ring the product of $a+P$ and $b+P$ is $ab+P$. Thus every zero product has a zero factor precisely when $ab\in P$ forces $a\in P$ or $b\in P$. These are the conditions in @[the definition of a prime ideal]mt4-prime-ideal and @[the definition of an integral domain]mt4-integral-domain.
