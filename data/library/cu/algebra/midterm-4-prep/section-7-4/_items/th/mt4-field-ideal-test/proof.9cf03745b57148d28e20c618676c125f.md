In a @[field]mt4-field every nonzero element is a unit. A nonzero ideal contains such an element, so it equals $R$ by @[the unit ideal test]mt4-unit-ideal-test.

Conversely, suppose the only ideals are $(0)$ and $R$. For $a\ne0$, the @[principal ideal]mt4-principal-ideal $(a)$ contains $a$, hence equals $R$. The unit ideal test gives an inverse for $a$. Thus every nonzero element of the commutative ring $R$ with $1\ne0$ is invertible, as required.
