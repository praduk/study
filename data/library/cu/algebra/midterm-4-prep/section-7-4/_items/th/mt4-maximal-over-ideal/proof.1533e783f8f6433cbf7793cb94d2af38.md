Order the proper ideals containing $I$ by inclusion. This set contains $I$. A nonempty chain has its union as an upper bound: two members of the union lie in one common ideal of the chain, so their difference lies in the union, and left and right absorption follow inside each ideal. The union is proper because $1$ belongs to no member. The empty chain has $I$ as an upper bound. Apply @[Zorn’s lemma]mt4-zorn-lemma. Any larger proper ideal would still contain $I$, contradicting maximality in this poset. Thus the resulting ideal is a @[maximal ideal]mt4-maximal-ideal of $R$.
