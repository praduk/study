When $M=R$, the ideal is improper and the quotient is the zero ring. When $M$ is proper, $R/M$ has nonzero identity. By @[the ideal correspondence theorem]mt4-ideal-correspondence its ideals correspond, preserving inclusion, to the ideals of $R$ between $M$ and $R$. Thus the definition of @[a maximal ideal]mt4-maximal-ideal gives maximality exactly when the only quotient ideals are $(0)$ and $R/M$. Apply @[the field criterion for ideals]mt4-field-ideal-test.
