Assume the axiom of choice. Let $(S,\le)$ be a nonempty partially ordered set. If every chain $C\subseteq S$ has an upper bound in $S$, then $S$ has a maximal element.

A chain is a subset in which every pair is comparable. An upper bound of $C$ is a $u\in S$ with $c\le u$ for every $c\in C$. A maximal element $m$ satisfies $m\le s\implies s=m$ for every $s\in S$.
