Suppose $ab=0$ for nonzero $a,b\in R$. Each annihilates the other, so both are @[zero divisors]mt4-zero-divisor. Every @[ideal]mt4-ideal contains $0$, so $ab\in P$. By @[primality]mt4-prime-ideal, $a\in P$ or $b\in P$, contradicting the hypothesis on $P$. Hence a zero product always has a zero factor. Together with commutativity and $1\ne0$, this makes $R$ an @[integral domain]mt4-integral-domain.
