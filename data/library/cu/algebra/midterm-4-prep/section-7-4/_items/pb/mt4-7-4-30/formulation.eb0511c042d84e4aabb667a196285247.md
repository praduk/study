> Let $R$ be a @[ring with identity]mt4-ring $1\ne0$.

Let $I$ be an @[ideal]mt4-ideal of the @[commutative ring]mt4-ring $R$ and define

$$
\operatorname{rad} I=\{r\in R\mid r^n\in I\text{ for some }n\in\Z^+\}
$$

called the *@[radical]mt4-ideal-radical* of $I$. Prove that $\operatorname{rad} I$ is an ideal containing $I$ and that $(\operatorname{rad} I)/I$ is the @[nilradical]mt4-nilradical of the @[quotient ring]mt4-quotient-ring $R/I$, i.e., $(\operatorname{rad} I)/I=\mathfrak N(R/I)$ (cf. @[Exercise 29, Section 3]mt4-7-3-29).
