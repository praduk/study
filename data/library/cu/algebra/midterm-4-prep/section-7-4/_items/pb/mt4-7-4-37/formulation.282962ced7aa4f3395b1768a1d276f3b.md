> Let $R$ be a @[ring with identity]mt4-ring $1\ne0$.

A @[commutative ring]mt4-ring $R$ is called a *@[local ring]mt4-local-ring* if it has a unique @[maximal ideal]mt4-maximal-ideal. Prove that if $R$ is a local ring with maximal ideal $M$ then every element of $R-M$ is a @[unit]mt4-unit. Prove conversely that if $R$ is a commutative ring with $1$ in which the set of nonunits forms an @[ideal]mt4-ideal $M$, then $R$ is a local ring with unique maximal ideal $M$.
