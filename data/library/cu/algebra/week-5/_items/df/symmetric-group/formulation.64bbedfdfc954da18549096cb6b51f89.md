Let $A$ be a set.  The symmetric group $S_A$ is the group where the set is bijective functions $A\to A$ and the operation is function composition.

A function $f:A\to B$ is bijective if
1. $f(x)=f(y) \implies x=y$ for all $x,y\in A$.
2. For every $b\in B$, there exists an $a\in A$ such that $f(a)=b$.

Let $f:A\to A$ and $g:A\to A$.  The composition of $f$ and $g$ is the function
$$
\begin{aligned}
f\circ g : A&\to A \\
a &\mapsto f(g(a)).
\end{aligned}
$$
