Every subgroup contains $1$ and is closed under $xy^{-1}$, so their intersection does too. The words contain $1$, are closed under concatenation, and are closed under inverses by reversing the word and inverting each letter. Thus they form a subgroup containing $A$. Every subgroup containing $A$ contains all such words, proving equality with the intersection.
Commuting letters have commuting inverses and powers, so two words in pairwise commuting letters can be interchanged by moving one letter at a time. Finally a homomorphism sends each word to the corresponding word in its generator images, so agreement on generators forces agreement everywhere.
