Left cosets partition $G$: $xH=yH$ iff $y^{-1}x\in H$; any intersection forces that equality. Multiplication by $x$ bijects $H$ with $xH$, so each piece has $|H|$ elements. Counting gives the formula.
Apply it to $\langle g\rangle$ to obtain $|g|\mid|G|$, hence $g^{|G|}=1$. If $|G|=p$ is prime, choose $g\ne1$. Its order is a divisor of $p$ greater than one, hence $p$, so $\langle g\rangle=G$.
The converse “every divisor occurs as a subgroup order” is not part of Lagrange's theorem and is false in general.
