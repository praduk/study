An intersection of subgroups of $G$ is a subgroup (the empty intersection is interpreted as $G$). For $A\subseteq G$, the intersection definition of $\langle A\rangle$ equals the set of finite words in $A\cup A^{-1}$, including the empty word.
If the elements of $A$ commute pairwise, $\langle A\rangle$ is abelian. Homomorphisms that agree on $A$ agree on $\langle A\rangle$.
