If $e,f$ are identities, $e=ef=f$. If $u,v$ are inverses of $a$, then $u=u(av)=(ua)v=v$. Both products of $ab$ with $b^{-1}a^{-1}$ give $1$, proving the inverse formula. The defining equations for $a^{-1}$ also show that $a$ is its inverse. Multiplying $ax=b$ on the left by $a^{-1}$ forces $x=a^{-1}b$, which indeed solves it; the other equation is similar. Applying these operations to $ax=ay$ or $xb=yb$ proves cancellation.
