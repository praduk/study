Any central element must commute with the generators $r,s$, and that condition also suffices because all elements are words in them. A rotation $r^i$ commutes with $s$ iff $r^{2i}=1$, or $n\mid2i$. Among $0\le i<n$ this gives $i=0$ and additionally $i=n/2$ when $n$ is even. A reflection $sr^i$ commutes with $r$ only if $r^{i-1}=r^{i+1}$, which would give $r^2=1$, impossible for $n\ge3$.
