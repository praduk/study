The core midterm guide follows the homework list supplied by the user. The three lecture files provide notation and supporting context; they do not establish that every lecture topic is on this exam.

- **Week 1, pp. 3–13:** functions and bijections; group axioms; symmetric groups and cycles; subgroups, generators, cyclic groups; dihedral groups and presentations. Page 4 gives informal category motivation rather than a full category definition.
- **Week 2, pp. 1–6:** homomorphisms, kernel and image, subgroup criterion, isomorphisms, cyclic-group classification, actions, faithfulness, and Cayley's theorem.
- **Week 2, pp. 7–14:** fields and vector spaces; linear actions and matrix groups; words and generators; subgroup lattices and maximal subgroups; pointwise and weak/setwise stabilizers.
- **Week 3, pp. 1–6:** multiplication and conjugation actions; centralizers, centers, normalizers and normality; orbits; right and two-sided actions; cosets, index, and Lagrange.
- **Week 3, pp. 7–13:** permutation-matrix/coset examples, Bruhat decomposition, normality and quotient groups. Quotient-group basics are included below as lecture context. Bruhat decomposition is beyond the listed homework and is not treated as an established midterm requirement here.

The Week 1 file with “(1)” in its filename is byte-for-byte identical to the other Week 1 file and was counted once.

**Notation safeguard:** a normalizer or weak stabilizer requires equality of sets, or both forward and inverse containments. A formula with just $gAg^{-1}\subseteq A$ is insufficient for a general infinite subset. This guide uses the full condition.
