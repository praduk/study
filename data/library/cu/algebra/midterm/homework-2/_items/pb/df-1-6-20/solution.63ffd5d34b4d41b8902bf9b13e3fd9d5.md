If $\alpha,\beta$ are automorphisms, their composite is bijective and

$$
(\alpha\circ\beta)(xy)=\alpha(\beta(x)\beta(y))=(\alpha\circ\beta)(x)(\alpha\circ\beta)(y).
$$

Associativity is inherited from function composition. The identity function is a bijective homomorphism. The inverse function of $\alpha$ is a homomorphism: write $u=\alpha(x),v=\alpha(y)$, then

$$
\alpha^{-1}(uv)=\alpha^{-1}(\alpha(xy))=xy=\alpha^{-1}(u)\alpha^{-1}(v).
$$

Thus the set is closed under composition and inverses and contains the identity.

**Technique:** distinguish an inverse function from the inverse of an individual group element.
