In $D_{2n}$ put $a=s,b=sr$. Then $a^2=1$, $(sr)^2=1$, and $ab=r$, so $(ab)^n=1$. Also $s=a,r=ab$, hence $a,b$ generate.
Conversely, in the presented group put $r=ab,s=a$. Then $r^n=s^2=1$ and

$$
srs=a(ab)a=ba=(ab)^{-1}=r^{-1}.
$$

Thus the defining relations of $D_{2n}$ hold. The two assignments induce homomorphisms in opposite directions whose composites fix the generators, so they are inverse isomorphisms.

**Technique:** change generators and verify the relations both ways. Merely checking that some relations hold proves only a homomorphism from the presented group.
