For a field $F$, let

$$
H(F)=\left\{X(a,b,c)=\begin{pmatrix}1&a&b\\0&1&c\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}:a,b,c\in F\right\}.
$$

Let $X=X(a,b,c)$ and $Y=X(d,e,f)$.

(a) Compute $XY$ and show that $H(F)$ is closed under multiplication. Exhibit explicit $X,Y$ with $XY\ne YX$.

(b) Find an explicit formula for $X^{-1}$ and deduce closure under inverses.

(e) Prove that every nonidentity element of $H(\R)$ has infinite order.

Only parts (a), (b), and (e) are assigned here.
