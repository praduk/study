Let $A$ be nonempty and $1\le k\le|A|$. Let $B$ be the set of $k$-element subsets of $A$, and define

$$
\sigma\cdot\{a_1,\ldots,a_k\}=\{\sigma(a_1),\ldots,\sigma(a_k)\}\qquad(\sigma\in S_A).
$$

(a) Prove this is a group action.

(b) Describe explicitly the permutations induced by $(12)$ and $(123)$ on the six two-element subsets of $\{1,2,3,4\}$.
