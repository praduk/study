If $G$ is abelian and $\varphi$ is surjective, write arbitrary $u,v\in H$ as $u=\varphi(a),v=\varphi(b)$. Then $uv=\varphi(ab)=\varphi(ba)=vu$. Thus surjectivity suffices. For an isomorphism the reverse implication follows by applying the same reasoning to its inverse (or by injectivity).
Without surjectivity the conclusion can fail: the trivial homomorphism from an abelian group into $S_3$ has abelian image but nonabelian codomain. Surjectivity is sufficient, not a necessary condition in every particular example.

**Technique:** lift arbitrary codomain elements to the domain before using the hypothesis.
