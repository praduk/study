If $|F|=q<\infty$, there are $q^{n^2}$ matrices in total, so $\GL_n(F)$ is finite.
Conversely, the injection $F^\times\to\GL_n(F)$ given by $a\mapsto\operatorname{diag}(a,1,\ldots,1)$ shows that finiteness of $\GL_n(F)$ implies finiteness of $F^\times$. Adding the single element $0$ makes $F$ finite. For $n=1$ this is simply $a\mapsto(a)$.

**Technique:** prove one direction by a finite bound and the other by an explicit injection.
