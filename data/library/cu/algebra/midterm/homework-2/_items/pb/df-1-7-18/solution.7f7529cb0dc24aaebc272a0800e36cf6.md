Reflexivity: $a=1a$.

Symmetry: if $a=hb$, then $b=h^{-1}a$.

Transitivity: if $a=hb$ and $b=kc$, then $a=h(kc)=(hk)c$.

These are precisely the three required properties. The equivalence class of $b$ is its orbit $Hb$, so distinct orbits are disjoint and their union is $A$.

**Technique:** identify the identity, inverse, and product that witness the three equivalence-relation axioms.
