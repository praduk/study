(a) The image is independent of the ordering chosen for the set, and injectivity of $\sigma$ preserves cardinality $k$. Identity fixes every subset and $(\sigma\tau)(S)=\sigma(\tau(S))$ element by element. Thus the action is well-defined and satisfies both axioms.

(b) Abbreviate $\{i,j\}$ by $ij$. On the six objects $12,13,14,23,24,34$, the induced permutations are

$$
(12):\ (13\ 23)(14\ 24),\qquad (123):\ (12\ 23\ 13)(14\ 24\ 34).
$$

The first fixes $12$ and $34$. These cycles permute subsets, not the original individual symbols.

**Technique:** check well-definedness before the action axioms; distinguish the original set from the new set being acted on.
