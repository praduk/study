The quaternion group is $Q_8=\{\pm1,\pm i,\pm j,\pm k\}$ with central element $-1$, $(-1)^2=1$, and

$$
i^2=j^2=k^2=-1,\qquad ij=k,\ jk=i,\ ki=j.
$$

Reversing any of the last three products introduces a minus sign. The identity is $1$. In particular $ij\ne ji$, so $Q_8$ is nonabelian. It has one element of order two and six of order four, distinguishing it from $D_8$.
