A **field** $F$ has $0\ne1$, an abelian additive group $(F,+)$, an abelian multiplicative group $F^\times=F\setminus\{0\}$, and distributivity of multiplication over addition.
For a positive integer $n$,

$$
\GL_n(F)=\{A\in M_n(F):\det A\ne0\},\qquad \operatorname{SL}_n(F)=\{A\in\GL_n(F):\det A=1\}.
$$

The group operation is matrix multiplication, not entrywise multiplication. The identity is $I_n$.
For a prime $p$, $\F_p=\Z/p\Z$ is a field. In $\F_2$, $1+1=0$ and $-1=1$. By contrast, $\Z/4\Z$ is not a field because the nonzero class $2$ has no multiplicative inverse.
