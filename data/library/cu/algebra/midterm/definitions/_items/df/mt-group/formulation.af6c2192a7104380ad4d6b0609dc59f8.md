A **group** is a set $G$ with a binary operation $G\times G\to G$ such that multiplication is associative, there is an identity $1$ with $1g=g1=g$, and every $g\in G$ has an inverse $g^{-1}$ with $gg^{-1}=g^{-1}g=1$.
The group is **abelian** exactly when $gh=hg$ for all $g,h\in G$. Its **order** $|G|$ is its cardinality. The identity axiom forces $G$ to be nonempty.
In additive notation use $0,-g,ng$ in place of $1,g^{-1},g^n$.
Examples: $(\Z,+)$ is abelian; $S_3$ is nonabelian. The nonzero integers under multiplication are not a group, because $2$ has no inverse there.
