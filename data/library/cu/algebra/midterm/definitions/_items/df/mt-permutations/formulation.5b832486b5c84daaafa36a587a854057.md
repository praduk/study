For a set $A$, $S_A$ is the group of bijections $A\to A$ under composition. Write $S_n=S_{\{1,\ldots,n\}}$. Its order is $n!$.
The cycle $(a_1\cdots a_m)$ sends $a_i$ to $a_{i+1}$ cyclically and fixes every other symbol. A **transposition** is a two-cycle. Cycles are **disjoint** if their moved symbols are disjoint.
Throughout this guide, $\sigma\tau$ means $\sigma\circ\tau$: apply $\tau$ first. Disjoint cycles commute; overlapping cycles usually do not. The inverse of a cycle reverses its direction. Cycle notation is unchanged by cyclic rotation, not by arbitrary reordering.
