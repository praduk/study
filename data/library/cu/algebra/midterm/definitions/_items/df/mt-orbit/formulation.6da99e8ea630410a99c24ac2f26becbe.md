For an action of $G$ on $A$ and $a\in A$, the **orbit** is

$$
\Orb_G(a)=G\cdot a=\{g\cdot a:g\in G\}.
$$

The relation $a\sim b$ iff $a=g\cdot b$ for some $g$ is an equivalence relation; its equivalence classes are precisely the orbits. Thus orbits partition the set. A nonempty action is transitive exactly when it has one orbit.
