A **left action** of $G$ on a set $A$ is a map $G\times A\to A$, $(g,a)\mapsto g\cdot a$, satisfying

$$
1\cdot a=a,\qquad (gh)\cdot a=g\cdot(h\cdot a).
$$

For fixed $g$, the map $a\mapsto g\cdot a$ is bijective with inverse $a\mapsto g^{-1}\cdot a$. The corresponding **permutation representation** is the homomorphism $\rho:G\to S_A$ defined by $\rho(g)(a)=g\cdot a$.
The action is **trivial** if every $g$ fixes every $a$. It is **faithful** if the only element fixing every $a$ is $1$. It is **transitive** if $A$ is nonempty and every two points are related by the action. Faithful and transitive mean different things.
