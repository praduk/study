A subgroup $M\le G$ is **maximal** exactly when $M\ne G$ and whenever $M\le K\le G$, either $K=M$ or $K=G$.
Maximal does not mean “a unique largest proper subgroup,” and it does not mean a subgroup of maximum size among all proper subgroups. It is a condition on intermediate subgroups above this particular $M$.
Example: $\langle r\rangle$ is maximal in $D_{2n}$. The trivial group has no maximal subgroups and is cyclic.
