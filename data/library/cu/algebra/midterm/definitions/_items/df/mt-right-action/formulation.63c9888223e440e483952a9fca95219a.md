A **right action** is a map $A\times G\to A$, $(a,g)\mapsto a\cdot g$, with $a\cdot1=a$ and $(a\cdot g)\cdot h=a\cdot(gh)$.
It gives a left action by $g*a=a\cdot g^{-1}$. For instance right multiplication $a\cdot g=ag$ gives the left action $g*a=ag^{-1}$.
Commuting left $G$ and right $H$ actions on $A$ form a **two-sided action**: $(g\cdot a)\cdot h=g\cdot(a\cdot h)$. They yield a left action of $G\times H$ by $(g,h)*a=g\cdot a\cdot h^{-1}$.
