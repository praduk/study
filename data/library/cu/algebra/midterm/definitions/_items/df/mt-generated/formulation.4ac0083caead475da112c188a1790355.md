For $A\subseteq G$, the **subgroup generated by $A$**, denoted $\langle A\rangle$, is the smallest subgroup containing $A$:

$$
\langle A\rangle=\bigcap_{A\subseteq H\le G}H.
$$

Equivalently it consists of all finite words in elements of $A\cup A^{-1}$, including the empty word $1$. In particular $\langle\varnothing\rangle=\{1\}$.
A group is **cyclic** if $G=\langle g\rangle$ for some $g$, and **finitely generated** if $G=\langle A\rangle$ for a finite set $A$.
Every cyclic group is abelian. The converse fails: $C_2\times C_2$ is abelian and noncyclic. In a nonabelian group a word in $a,b$ cannot generally be rearranged as $a^ib^j$.
