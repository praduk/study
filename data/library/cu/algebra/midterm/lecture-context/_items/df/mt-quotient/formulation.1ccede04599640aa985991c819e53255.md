For $N\trianglelefteq G$, the **quotient group** $G/N$ consists of left cosets with

$$
(aN)(bN)=(ab)N.
$$

Its identity is $N$ and $(aN)^{-1}=a^{-1}N$. The projection $\pi:G\to G/N$, $g\mapsto gN$, is a surjective homomorphism with kernel $N$.
Normality ensures that the product does not depend on the chosen representatives. A coset is a set, and a representative is one element of that set; a chosen collection of representatives need not be closed under multiplication.
