The identity has determinant one. If $A,B$ have determinant one, then

$$
\det(AB^{-1})=\det(A)\det(B)^{-1}=1.
$$

Thus the subgroup criterion applies. Equivalently this is the kernel of the determinant homomorphism $\GL_n(F)\to F^\times$.

**Technique:** translate the defining condition through a homomorphism. The determinant product and inverse identities are standard linear-algebra prerequisites used by the textbook.
