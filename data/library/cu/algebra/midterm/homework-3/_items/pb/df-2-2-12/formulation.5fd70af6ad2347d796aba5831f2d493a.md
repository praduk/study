Let $R=\Z[x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4]$, the polynomials with integer coefficients in four independent commuting variables. For $\sigma\in S_4$ define

$$
\sigma\cdot p(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)=p(x_{\sigma(1)},x_{\sigma(2)},x_{\sigma(3)},x_{\sigma(4)}).
$$

Put

$$
p=12x_1^5x_2^7x_4-18x_2^3x_3+11x_1^6x_2x_3^3x_4^{23}.
$$

(a) For $\sigma=(1234)$ and $\tau=(123)$ compute $\sigma\cdot p$, $\tau\cdot(\sigma\cdot p)$, $(\tau\sigma)\cdot p$, and $(\sigma\tau)\cdot p$.

(b) Prove this defines a left action of $S_4$ on $R$.

(c) List the stabilizer of $x_4$ and prove it is isomorphic to $S_3$.

(d) List the stabilizer of $x_1+x_2$ and prove it is abelian of order four.

(e) List the stabilizer of $x_1x_2+x_3x_4$ and prove it is isomorphic to $D_8$.

(f) Show that $(x_1+x_2)(x_3+x_4)$ has exactly the same stabilizer as in (e).
