Every element equals its inverse. For arbitrary $a,b\in G$, this applies also to $ab$, so

$$
ab=(ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}=ba.
$$

Thus $G$ is abelian. Do not describe every element as having order two: the identity has order one.

**Technique:** apply a universal hypothesis to a product, then compare two expressions.
