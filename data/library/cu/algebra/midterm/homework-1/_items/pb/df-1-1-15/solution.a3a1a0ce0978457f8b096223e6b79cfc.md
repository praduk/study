For two factors, $(ab)(b^{-1}a^{-1})=1=(b^{-1}a^{-1})(ab)$, so $(ab)^{-1}=b^{-1}a^{-1}$. The assertion for $n=1$ is immediate. If it holds for $n$, then

$$
(a_1\cdots a_na_{n+1})^{-1}=a_{n+1}^{-1}(a_1\cdots a_n)^{-1}=a_{n+1}^{-1}a_n^{-1}\cdots a_1^{-1}.
$$

Induction proves the result. The order reverses; commutativity is never assumed.

**Technique:** induction on word length and a two-factor identity.
