The partitions of five give cycle types

$$
5;\quad4+1;\quad3+2;\quad3+1+1;\quad2+2+1;\quad2+1+1+1;\quad1+1+1+1+1.
$$

Their least common multiples are $5,4,6,3,2,2,1$, respectively. Thus the possible orders are exactly $\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}$.
Witnesses are $1,(12),(123),(1234),(12345),(123)(45)$.

**Technique:** enumerate integer partitions for completeness. Lagrange's theorem alone does not determine which orders occur.
