Write $\sigma=c_1\cdots c_t$, with disjoint cycles of lengths $m_1,\ldots,m_t$. Disjoint cycles commute, so $\sigma^k=c_1^k\cdots c_t^k$. Since their supports are disjoint, this product is the identity exactly when every $c_i^k$ is the identity, exactly when each $m_i\mid k$. The smallest positive such $k$ is $\operatorname{lcm}(m_1,\ldots,m_t)$. The identity has order one; if fixed points are omitted, use the empty least common multiple convention $1$.

**Technique:** turn an identity condition into simultaneous divisibility conditions.
