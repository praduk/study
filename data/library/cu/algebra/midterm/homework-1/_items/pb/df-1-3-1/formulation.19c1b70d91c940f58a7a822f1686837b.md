Let $\sigma,\tau\in S_5$ be defined by

$$
\begin{aligned}
\sigma &: 1\mapsto3,\ 2\mapsto4,\ 3\mapsto5,\ 4\mapsto2,\ 5\mapsto1,\\
\tau &: 1\mapsto5,\ 2\mapsto3,\ 3\mapsto2,\ 4\mapsto4,\ 5\mapsto1.
\end{aligned}
$$

Find the cycle decompositions of $\sigma,\tau,\sigma^2,\sigma\tau,\tau\sigma,\tau^2\sigma$. Products act from right to left.
