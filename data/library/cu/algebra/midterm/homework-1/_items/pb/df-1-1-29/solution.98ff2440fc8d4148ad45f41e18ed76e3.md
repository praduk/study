If both factors are abelian, $(a,b)(c,d)=(ac,bd)=(ca,db)=(c,d)(a,b)$.
Conversely, if the product is abelian, compare $(a,1)(c,1)$ with $(c,1)(a,1)$ to get $ac=ca$ for all $a,c\in A$. Compare $(1,b)(1,d)$ with $(1,d)(1,b)$ to get $bd=db$ for all $b,d\in B$.

**Technique:** prove both implications; isolate one coordinate using an identity in the other.
