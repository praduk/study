The formula for $i=1$ is the definition of the cycle. Applying $\sigma$ advances the index once more, proving the formula by induction. Thus $\sigma^m$ fixes every $a_k$, and it also fixes every symbol outside this cycle. Therefore $\sigma^m=1$.
For $1\le i<m$, $\sigma^i(a_1)=a_{1+i}\ne a_1$, so $\sigma^i\ne1$. Hence the least positive identity power is $m$. For $m=1$ the cycle is the identity and has order one.
