Every element is uniquely $r^i$ or $sr^i$, with $0\le i<n$. A rotation $r^i$ commutes with $s$ exactly when $sr^i=r^is=sr^{-i}$, equivalently $r^{2i}=1$. Thus $n\mid2i$, and oddness gives $n\mid i$.
For a reflection, $r(sr^i)=sr^{i-1}$ whereas $(sr^i)r=sr^{i+1}$. Equality would give $r^2=1$, impossible since $|r|=n\ge3$. Hence no reflection is central and $Z(D_{2n})=\{1\}$.

**Technique:** split into normal forms, test against generators, and solve an exponent congruence.
