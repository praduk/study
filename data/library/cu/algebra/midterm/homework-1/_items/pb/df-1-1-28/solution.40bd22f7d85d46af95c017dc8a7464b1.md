Closure holds because each coordinate product lies in its factor group.

(a) Both parenthesizations of a triple product have first coordinate $a_1\cdot(a_2\cdot a_3)=(a_1\cdot a_2)\cdot a_3$ and second coordinate $b_1\circ(b_2\circ b_3)=(b_1\circ b_2)\circ b_3$.

(b) Coordinatewise multiplication by $(1_A,1_B)$ on either side leaves $(a,b)$ unchanged.

(c) Multiplying $(a,b)$ and $(a^{-1},b^{-1})$ in either order gives $(1_A,1_B)$. Thus all axioms hold.

**Technique:** reduce each assertion to the corresponding assertion in each coordinate.
