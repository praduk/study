The elements are precisely the six $4$-cycles:

$$
\begin{gathered}(1\ 2\ 3\ 4),\quad(1\ 2\ 4\ 3),\quad(1\ 3\ 2\ 4),\\(1\ 3\ 4\ 2),\quad(1\ 4\ 2\ 3),\quad(1\ 4\ 3\ 2).\end{gathered}
$$

Indeed, the order is the least common multiple of disjoint cycle lengths. Partitions of four other than $4$ have least common multiple $1$, $2$, or $3$. Starting each $4$-cycle with $1$ gives exactly $3!=6$ distinct cycles.
