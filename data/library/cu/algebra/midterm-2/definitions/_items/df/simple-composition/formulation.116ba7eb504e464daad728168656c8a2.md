A group $G$ is simple if it is nontrivial and its only normal subgroups are $1$ and $G$. A composition series is a finite strictly increasing chain

$$
1=G_0\nsubg G_1\nsubg\cdots\nsubg G_t=G
$$

whose factors $G_{i+1}/G_i$ are nontrivial simple groups. Normality is required between consecutive terms; $G_i$ need not be normal in $G$.

The factors are called composition factors. Equivalent lists have the same factor isomorphism types with multiplicity, allowing a different order. Jordan–Hölder does not say the intermediate subgroups are unique.

A cyclic group of prime order is simple. $C_4$ is not, because its order-$2$ subgroup is normal. A composition series for $C_4$ is $1<C_2<C_4$, with two $C_2$ factors. The trivial group has the empty composition series and no factors.
