For a prime $p$, a finite $p$-group has order a power of $p$. When $|G|=p^am$ and $p\nmid m$, a Sylow $p$-subgroup has order $p^a$. In the assigned Exercise 3.3.9, such a subgroup is given; its existence need not be proved there.

A Hall subgroup $H$ of a finite group $G$ satisfies $\gcd(|H|,[G:H])=1$. Every Sylow subgroup is Hall, but a Hall subgroup may involve several primes. The trivial subgroup and the whole group are Hall. The subgroup of order $2$ in $C_4$ is not Hall because its index is also $2$.

The results needed for the assigned intersection and image problems follow from Lagrange, the product-order formula, and the isomorphism theorems. The full Sylow theorem is not required for those proofs.
