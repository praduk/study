Let $G$ be a transitive permutation group on a finite nonempty set $A$. A block is a nonempty $B\subseteq A$ such that for every $g\in G$, either $gB=B$ or $gB\cap B=\varnothing$.

(a) If $a\in B$, prove the setwise stabilizer $G_B=\{g:gB=B\}$ is a subgroup containing the point stabilizer $G_a$.

(b) Show the distinct translates of $B$ partition $A$.

(c) A transitive action is primitive if only singletons and $A$ are blocks. Show $S_4$ is primitive in its natural action, whereas $D_8$ on a square's vertices is not.

(d) Prove primitivity is equivalent to each point stabilizer being maximal in $G$.
