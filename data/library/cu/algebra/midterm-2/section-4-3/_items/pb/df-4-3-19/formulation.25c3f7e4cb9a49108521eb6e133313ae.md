Let $H\nsubg G$, let $K$ be a conjugacy class of $G$ contained in $H$, and choose $x\in K$. Prove $K$ is a union of $k=[G:HC_G(x)]$ conjugacy classes of $H$, all of equal size. Deduce an $S_n$-class of even permutations is either one $A_n$-class or two $A_n$-classes of equal size.

For possibly infinite $G$, interpret $k$ as a cardinal number of classes rather than dividing infinite cardinalities.
