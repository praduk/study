Compose the regular representation with the sign homomorphism:

$$
\chi=\sgn\circ\pi:G\to\{1,-1\}.
$$

A composition of homomorphisms is a homomorphism. The identity maps to $1$, and the assumed odd permutation supplies an element mapping to $-1$. Thus the image is all of $\{1,-1\}$. Put $H=\ker\chi$. The First Isomorphism Theorem gives

$$
G/H\cong\{1,-1\}\cong C_2,
$$

so $[G:H]=2$. In fact $H$ is normal, because it is a kernel.

**Technique:** compose an available representation with a simpler homomorphism whose image has the desired size. No explicit list of elements of $H$ is needed.
