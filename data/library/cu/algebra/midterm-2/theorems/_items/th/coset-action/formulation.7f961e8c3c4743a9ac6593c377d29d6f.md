For $H\le G$, left multiplication on the left cosets of $H$ gives $\rho:G\to S_{G/H}$ with kernel

$$
\operatorname{core}_G(H)=\bigcap_{g\in G}gHg^{-1}\le H.
$$

For $H=1$ this is faithful (Cayley's theorem). If $G$ is finite and $p$ is the smallest prime dividing $|G|$, every subgroup of index $p$ is normal.
