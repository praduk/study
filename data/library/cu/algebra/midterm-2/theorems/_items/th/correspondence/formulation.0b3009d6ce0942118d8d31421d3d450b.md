Let $N\nsubg G$ and $\pi:G\to G/N$ be projection. Subgroups $H$ of $G$ containing $N$ correspond bijectively to subgroups $\bar H$ of $G/N$ by

$$
H\mapsto H/N,\qquad\bar H\mapsto\pi^{-1}(\bar H).
$$

This correspondence preserves inclusion, intersections, generated joins, and indices. For $N\le H\le K$, $H\nsubg K$ exactly when $H/N\nsubg K/N$, and then $(K/N)/(H/N)\cong K/H$.
