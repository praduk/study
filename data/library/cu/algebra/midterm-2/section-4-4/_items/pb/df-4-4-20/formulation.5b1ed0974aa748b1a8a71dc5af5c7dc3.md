For a finite group $P$, let $d(P)$ be its minimum number of generators, and let

$$
m(P)=\max\{d(A):A\le P\text{ abelian}\},
$$


$$
J(P)=\langle A:A\le P\text{ abelian and }d(A)=m(P)\rangle.
$$

Take $d(1)=0$.

(a) Prove $J(P)$ characteristic in $P$.

(b) List every abelian subgroup $A$ attaining $d(A)=m(P)$ for $P=Q_8,D_8,D_{16},QD_{16}$, where

$$
QD_{16}=\langle\sigma,\tau\mid\sigma^8=\tau^2=1,\ \sigma\tau=\tau\sigma^3\rangle.
$$

Only parts (a) and (b) are assigned.
