Fix $n\ge2$ with $n\ne6$.

(a) Prove automorphisms of a group permute its conjugacy classes.

(b) Let $K$ be the class of transpositions in $S_n$. Show that any other class $K'$ of elements of order $2$ has $|K'|\ne|K|$, and deduce automorphisms preserve transpositions.

(c) Show any automorphism $\alpha$ has

$$
\alpha((1\ i))=(a\ b_i)\quad(2\le i\le n),
$$

with $a,b_2,\ldots,b_n$ distinct.

(d) Show the transpositions $(1\ i)$ generate $S_n$, and conclude $\operatorname{Aut}(S_n)=\operatorname{Inn}(S_n)$.
