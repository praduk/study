Let $F$ be a field and

$$
G=\left\{\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\0&c\end{pmatrix}:a,b,c\in F,\ ac\ne0\right\}\le\GL_2(F).
$$

(a) Prove that $\varphi\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\0&c\end{smallmatrix}\right)=a$ is a surjective homomorphism $G\to F^\times$. Describe its fibers and kernel.

(b) Do the same for $\psi\left(\begin{smallmatrix}a&b\\0&c\end{smallmatrix}\right)=(a,c)$ with codomain $F^\times\times F^\times$.

(c) Prove that $H=\left\{\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&b\\0&1\end{smallmatrix}\right):b\in F\right\}$ is isomorphic to the additive group of $F$.
