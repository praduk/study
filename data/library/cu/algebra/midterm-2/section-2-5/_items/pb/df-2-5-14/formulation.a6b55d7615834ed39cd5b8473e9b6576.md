Let $M=\langle u,v\mid u^2=v^8=1,\ vu=uv^5\rangle$ be the modular group of order $16$. Its three order-$8$ subgroups are $\langle u,v^2\rangle$, $\langle v\rangle$, and $\langle uv\rangle$, and every proper subgroup is contained in one of them. Prove that these have types $C_2\times C_4,C_8,C_8$, respectively. Show that $M$ has the same subgroup lattice as $C_2\times C_8$ but is not isomorphic to it.
