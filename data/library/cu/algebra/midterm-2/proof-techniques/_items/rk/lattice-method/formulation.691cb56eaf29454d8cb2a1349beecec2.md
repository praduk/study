For the order-$8$ and order-$16$ lattice exercises, first name every maximal subgroup. Build its internal lattice, then identify subgroups shared with other maximal branches. Use element orders to verify the cyclic chains and common involutions.

After constructing the diagram, give a complete cover list. Confirm the total vertex count, and justify that no subgroup is missing: the supplied assertion that every proper subgroup lies in one of the maximal subgroups is enough once their internal lattices are exhausted.

For a quotient lattice, retain only subgroups containing the kernel, replace each by its image quotient, and divide finite subgroup orders by the kernel order. Determine the quotient's group type separately by a map from a familiar group.

**Common mistake:** the modular group and $C_2\times C_8$ have the same lattice; matching a lattice therefore does not establish a group isomorphism.
