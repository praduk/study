Write down the acted-on set before invoking orbit–stabilizer. The same group can act on points, cosets, conjugacy classes, subgroups, or orbits of a normal subgroup, with different stabilizers.

- On points: $G_a$.
- On an element by conjugation: $C_G(x)$.
- On a subgroup by conjugation: $N_G(H)$.
- On the $H$-orbit of a point, when $H\nsubg G$: $HG_a$.
- On the $H$-conjugacy class of $x$, when $H\nsubg G$: $HC_G(x)$.

The last two stabilizer formulas require proof; do not infer them from notation. They explain 4.1.9 and 4.3.19. A coset action instead bounds a quotient of $G$ by a subgroup of $S_n$, as in 4.2.9.
