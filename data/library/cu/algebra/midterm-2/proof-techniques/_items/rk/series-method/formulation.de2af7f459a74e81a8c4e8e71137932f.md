For a composition series through $H\nsubg G$, take a series inside $H$ and a series in $G/H$, pull the quotient series back, and join the two chains at $H$. Verify both adjacent normality and simplicity of every factor.

To find an abelian normal subgroup in a solvable group, form the derived sequence of the given normal subgroup and take its last nontrivial term. Explain separately why all terms remain normal in the ambient group and why the sequence terminates.

For Jordan–Hölder, if two penultimate terms differ, intersect them. The diamond theorem identifies the two extra simple factors in opposite order.

**Common mistake:** a composition-series term may be normal only in its immediate overgroup. Demanding normality in all of $G$ would wrongly discard several valid $D_8$ series.
