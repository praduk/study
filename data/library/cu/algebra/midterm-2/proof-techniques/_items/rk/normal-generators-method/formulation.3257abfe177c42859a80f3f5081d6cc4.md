If $N=\langle S\rangle$, conjugation or an automorphism respects products and inverses. Thus checking the images of $S$ lie in $N$ proves the image of $N$ is contained in $N$. To obtain equality, also apply the inverse transformation, or use equal finite cardinalities when that hypothesis is available.

Use this for 3.1.28 and 3.1.41, the hint in 3.4.11, and characteristicity of the Thompson or commutator subgroup. For characteristicity, show every automorphism preserves the family defining the subgroup, not just inner automorphisms.

**Common mistake:** for an infinite subgroup, a single conjugation mapping generators into the subgroup need not normalize it. Exercise 3.1.28 has an explicit matrix counterexample.
