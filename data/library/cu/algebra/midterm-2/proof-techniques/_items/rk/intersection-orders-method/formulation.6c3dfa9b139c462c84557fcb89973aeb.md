An intersection $H\cap K$ is a subgroup of both factors, so its order divides both when they are finite. Similarly, the image of $H$ in $G/N$ has order dividing both $|H|$ and $[G:N]$.

For prime-power questions, introduce an unknown exponent, e.g. $|P\cap N|=p^c$. Lagrange gives one inequality, and the product-order formula plus divisibility of $|PN|$ gives the other. For Hall subgroups, show the new order and index divide the original coprime order and index.

Examples: 3.2.8, 3.2.18, 3.3.9, and 3.3.10.

**Common mistake:** trivial intersection need not imply coprime orders; $V_4$ supplies a counterexample.
