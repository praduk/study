Define

$$
\aligned{
V&=\{1,(1\ 2)(3\ 4),(1\ 3)(2\ 4),(1\ 4)(2\ 3)\} \\
L &= \genby{(1\ 2)(3\ 4)}.
}
$$

$L$ is normal in $V$ because it has index 2 in $V$.  $V/L$ has order 2, and so is cyclic and hence abelian.  $V$ is normal in $A_4$ because it contains all the double transpositions in $A_4$ and conjugation of $V$ in $A_4$ keeps the same cycle type.  $A_4/V$ is cyclic because it has prime order $12/4=3$, therefore it is abelian.  Hence

$$
\id_{A_4} \nsubg L \subg V \nsubg A_4
$$

is a composition series.
