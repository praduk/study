First $A\cap B\nsubg G$, because conjugation preserves each of $A$ and $B$ and hence their intersection.

For arbitrary $x,y\in G$, commutativity in $G/A$ gives

$$
x^{-1}y^{-1}xy\in A.
$$

The same argument in $G/B$ gives $x^{-1}y^{-1}xy\in B$. Therefore every commutator lies in $A\cap B$.  Then

$$
xy A\cap B = xy y^{-1}x^{-1}yx A \cap B = yx A \cap B.
$$
