A group $H$ is perfect if $H'=H$.

(a) Prove every nonabelian simple group is perfect.

(b) If $H,K$ are perfect subgroups of $G$, prove $\langle H,K\rangle$ perfect. Extend this to a subgroup generated by any collection of perfect subgroups.

(c) Prove every conjugate of a perfect subgroup is perfect.

(d) Prove every group has a unique maximal perfect subgroup and that subgroup is normal.
