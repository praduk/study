Let $G$ be finite and let the prime $p$ divide $|G|$. Define

$$
S=\{(x_1,\ldots,x_p)\in G^p:x_1\cdots x_p=1\}.
$$

Relate two tuples when one is a cyclic rotation of the other.

(a) Show $|S|=|G|^{p-1}$ and $p\mid|S|$.

(b) Show rotations preserve $S$.

(c) Show that this relation is an equivalence relation.

(d) Show singleton classes are exactly the constant tuples $(x,\ldots,x)$ with $x^p=1$.

(e) Show every class has size $1$ or $p$. If there are $k$ singleton classes and $d$ classes of size $p$, deduce $|G|^{p-1}=k+pd$.

(f) Deduce there is a nonidentity element of order $p$.
