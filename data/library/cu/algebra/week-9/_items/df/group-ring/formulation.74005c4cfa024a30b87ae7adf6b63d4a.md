Let $R$ be a ring and $G$ be a group.  Let $\phi:G\to \Aut(R)$ be a group homomorphism.  Then a group ring $R\semid G$ is the ring

$$
R\semid_\phi G = \left\{ \sum_{g\in G} r_g g : r_g\in R \text{ with finitely many $r_g\neq 0$.} \right\}.
$$

with
$$
\aligned{
\sum_{g\in G} r_g g + \sum_{g\in G} s_g g &= \sum_{g\in G} (r_g+s_g) g \\
\left(\sum_{g\in G} r_g g \right) \left(\sum_{h\in G} s_h h\right) &= \sum_{g,h\in G} r_g\ \phi_g(s_h)\ gh. \\
}
$$
