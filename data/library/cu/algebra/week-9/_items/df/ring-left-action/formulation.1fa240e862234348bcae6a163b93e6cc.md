Let $R$ be a ring and $A$ be an abelian group.  A left action of $R$ on $A$ is a function
$$
\aligned{
\cdot : R\times A &\to A \\
(r,a) &\mapsto ra
}
$$
such that for all $r,s\in R$, for all $a,b\in A$,

1. $r(sa) = (ra)a $.
2. $(r+s)a = ra + sa$
3. $r(a+b) = ra + rb$
4. If $1\in R$, then $1a = a$.
