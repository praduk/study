This is original practice, not a prediction of the exam. Use $[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$ and let $a,b$ be generators of $C_7,C_3$ respectively.

**(a), 8 minutes.** Show that $b\cdot a=a^2$ defines an action of $C_3$ on $C_7$. Construct $G=C_7\semid C_3$, write multiplication of $a^ib^j$ and $a^kb^\ell$, and state its order and normal factor.

**(b), 12 minutes.** Compute $Z(G)$ and $G'$. Determine the upper central, lower central, and derived series. Decide whether $G$ is nilpotent and whether it is solvable.
