Let

$$
G=D_{24}=\langle r,s\mid r^{12}=s^2=1,\ srs^{-1}=r^{-1}\rangle
$$

be the dihedral group of **order $24$**, with $r$ of order $12$.

Use $[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}$, $Z_0(G)=1$, $\gamma_1(G)=G$, and $\gamma_{i+1}(G)=[G,\gamma_i(G)]$. For the derived series use $G^{(0)}=G$ and $G^{(i+1)}=[G^{(i)},G^{(i)}]$.

**(a)** Determine every term of the upper and lower central series, and justify where each series stabilizes.

**(b)** Determine the derived series. Is $G$ solvable? Is it nilpotent? Give its derived length if applicable, and explain why the lower central and derived series behave differently here.

*Original practice problem using the techniques in D&F 6.1.7 and 6.1.10; this is not an actual exam question.*
