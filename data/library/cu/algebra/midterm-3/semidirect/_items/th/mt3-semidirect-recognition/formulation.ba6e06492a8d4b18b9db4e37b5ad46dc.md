The operation in @mt3-semidirect makes $N\times H$ a group. Its embedded $N$ is normal, its embedded factors intersect trivially, and its quotient by $N$ is $H$.

Conversely, if $N\trianglelefteq G$ and $H$ is a complement, conjugation defines $\varphi:H\to\Aut(N)$ and

$$
N\semid_\varphi H\longrightarrow G,\qquad(n,h)\longmapsto nh
$$

is an isomorphism. The action is trivial exactly when the two embedded factors commute, in which case the product is direct.
