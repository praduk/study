**Definitions used:** @mt3-semidirect and @mt3-complement.

For the external construction, both associations of three factors have second coordinate $hk\ell$ and first coordinate

$$
n\varphi(h)(m)\varphi(hk)(r),
$$

because $\varphi(h)$ preserves products and $\varphi(hk)=\varphi(h)\varphi(k)$. This proves associativity. Substitution verifies the stated identity and inverse. Projection onto $H$ is a homomorphism with kernel the embedded $N$, proving normality and the quotient claim; intersection of the embedded factors is visibly trivial.

Internally, normality of $N$ makes each conjugation by $h\in H$ an automorphism of $N$, and conjugation respects multiplication of the $h$'s. The displayed map preserves multiplication because

$$
(nh)(mk)=n(hmh^{-1})hk.
$$

It is onto since $NH=G$. If $nh=mk$, then $m^{-1}n=kh^{-1}\in N\cap H=1$, proving injectivity. Finally $\varphi(h)(n)=n$ for all $h,n$ is precisely commutation of the factors.
