**Results used:** @mt3-cyclic-automorphisms and @mt3-semidirect-recognition.

An automorphism of $C_n$ sends $a$ to $a^u$ exactly when $u$ is a unit modulo $n$. A homomorphism from $C_m$ is determined by the image of $b$, whose $m$th power must be the identity. For this automorphism that condition is $a\mapsto a^{u^m}=a$, giving the congruence.

Conversely the congruence ensures the generator assignment respects $b^m=1$, so it defines an action. The external construction gives unique coordinate forms, and moving $a^k$ past $b^j$ using the action gives the multiplication formula.
