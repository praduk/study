Let $N=\langle a\rangle\cong C_n$ and $H=\langle b\rangle\cong C_m$. An action $H\to\Aut(N)$ is specified by a residue $u$ satisfying

$$
\gcd(u,n)=1,\qquad u^m\equiv1\pmod n,
$$

by setting $bab^{-1}=a^u$ in the semidirect product. Its elements have unique forms $a^ib^j$ with $0\le i<n$, $0\le j<m$, and

$$
(a^ib^j)(a^kb^\ell)=a^{i+u^jk}b^{j+\ell}.
$$

Changing generators can identify apparently different actions; see @mt3-classify.
