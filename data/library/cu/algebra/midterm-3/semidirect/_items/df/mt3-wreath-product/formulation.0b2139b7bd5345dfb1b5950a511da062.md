If $K$ acts on a finite set $\Omega$, let $L^\Omega$ be the direct product with coordinates indexed by $\Omega$. The **permutation wreath product** is

$$
L\wr_\Omega K=L^\Omega\semid K,
$$

where $(k\cdot x)_\omega=x_{k^{-1}\omega}$. The inverse in the coordinate index makes this a left action.

For finite $K$, the **regular wreath product** uses the left multiplication action on $\Omega=K$. If $K,L$ are finite, it has order $|L|^{|K|}|K|$, directly from the underlying product set. In particular $C_p\wr C_p=C_p^p\semid C_p$, with the last factor cycling the coordinates; see @mt3-hw1-df-5-5-23.

For the natural action of $S_n$, $C_2\wr S_n$ is the group of signed permutation matrices: the base changes coordinate signs and $S_n$ permutes the coordinates.
