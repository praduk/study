Given groups $N,H$ and a homomorphism $\varphi:H\to\Aut(N)$, the **external semidirect product** $N\semid_\varphi H$ has underlying set $N\times H$ and multiplication

$$
(n,h)(m,k)=(n\varphi(h)(m),hk).
$$

The identity is $(1,1)$ and the inverse is

$$
(n,h)^{-1}=(\varphi(h^{-1})(n^{-1}),h^{-1}).
$$

The construction and its internal recognition are proved in @mt3-semidirect-recognition. The course may write $H\semidop N$ for this convention; specify the normal factor and the action rather than relying on the English word order.
