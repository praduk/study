Let $N\trianglelefteq G$. A subgroup $H\le G$ is a **complement** to $N$ if

$$
N\cap H=1,\qquad NH=G.
$$

Existence of a complement means the extension splits as an internal semidirect product, by @mt3-semidirect-recognition.

An abstract isomorphism $G/N\cong H$ does not alone provide such an embedded complement. For example, the order-$2$ subgroup of $C_4$ has quotient $C_2$, but $C_4$ has no disjoint order-$2$ subgroup.
