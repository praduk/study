For $S\subseteq G$, its **normal closure** $\langle\!\langle S\rangle\!\rangle$ is the smallest normal subgroup of $G$ containing $S$. Equivalently it is generated by all $gsg^{-1}$ with $g\in G$ and $s\in S$: this generated subgroup is preserved by conjugation and lies in any normal subgroup containing $S$. See @mt3-generated-subgroup.
