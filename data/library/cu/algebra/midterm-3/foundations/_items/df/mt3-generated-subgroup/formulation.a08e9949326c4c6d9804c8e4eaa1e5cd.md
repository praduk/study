For a subset $S\subseteq G$, the **generated subgroup** $\langle S\rangle$ is the smallest subgroup containing $S$: its elements are finite products of elements of $S$ and their inverses, including the empty product $1$. A generating set $S$ is **minimal** if $\langle S\setminus\{s\}\rangle\ne G$ for every $s\in S$. Minimal means no redundant member; it does not mean minimum cardinality in an arbitrary group.
