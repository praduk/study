For any group $G$, set

$$
L_0(G)=G,\qquad L_{i+1}(G)=[G,L_i(G)].
$$

The descending sequence $G=L_0(G)\ge L_1(G)\ge\cdots$ is the **lower central series**. Thus $L_1(G)=G'$.

Dummit–Foote writes these terms $G^i$. With the alternative convention $\gamma_1(G)=G$, one has $L_i(G)=\gamma_{i+1}(G)$. The next term always uses commutators with the **whole group**.
