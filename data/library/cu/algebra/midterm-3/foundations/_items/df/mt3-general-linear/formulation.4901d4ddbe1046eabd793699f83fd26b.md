For an $r$-dimensional vector space $V$ over $\F_p$, $\GL(V)$ is the group of invertible linear maps $V\to V$, with composition. After a basis is chosen, it is $\GL_r(\F_p)$, the invertible $r\times r$ matrices.

Changing basis by a matrix $B$ changes the matrix of a transformation from $T$ to $B^{-1}TB$. A diagonal matrix $\diag{u,v}$ multiplies the coordinates by $u,v$; a scalar matrix $uI$ multiplies every vector by the same scalar and preserves every line.
