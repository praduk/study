For a subgroup $H\le G$, its **normalizer** is

$$
N_G(H)=\{g\in G:gHg^{-1}=H\}.
$$

It is a subgroup: conjugations preserving $H$ are closed under composition and inversion. It contains $H$, and $H\trianglelefteq N_G(H)$. Consequently $H\trianglelefteq G$ if and only if $N_G(H)=G$. See @mt3-normal-subgroup; compare @mt3-centralizer.
