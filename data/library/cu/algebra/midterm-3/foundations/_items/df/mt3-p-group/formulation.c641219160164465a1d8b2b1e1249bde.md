For a prime $p$, a finite **$p$-group** is a group of order $p^a$ for an integer $a\ge0$. Thus the trivial group is a $p$-group. A **$p$-subgroup** of a group $G$ is a subgroup whose order is a power of $p$.

By Lagrange, every subgroup and every quotient of a finite $p$-group is again a $p$-group. Here a quotient requires a normal subgroup.
