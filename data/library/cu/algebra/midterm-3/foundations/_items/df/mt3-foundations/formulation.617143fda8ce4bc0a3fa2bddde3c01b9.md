Let $G$ be a group. A **subgroup** $H\le G$ is a subset that is a group under the inherited multiplication. Equivalently, $H$ is nonempty and $xy^{-1}\in H$ for every $x,y\in H$: this gives the identity, inverses, and products, while associativity is inherited.

Normality is defined separately in @mt3-normal-subgroup. The related prerequisites now have individual entries: @mt3-characteristic, @mt3-center, @mt3-centralizer, @mt3-normalizer, @mt3-index, @mt3-lagrange, @mt3-cauchy, @mt3-simple, @mt3-homomorphism, and @mt3-quotient. The product and conjugation tools are @mt3-subgroup-product and @mt3-normalizer-centralizer.
