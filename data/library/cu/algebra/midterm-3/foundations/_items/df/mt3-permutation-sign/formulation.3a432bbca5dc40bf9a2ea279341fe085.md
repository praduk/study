For $\sigma\in S_m$, let $M_\sigma$ be the permutation matrix defined by $M_\sigma e_i=e_{\sigma(i)}$. Its **sign** is $\sgn(\sigma)=\det(M_\sigma)\in\{1,-1\}$. A permutation is **even** if its sign is $1$ and odd if its sign is $-1$.

The **alternating group** is $A_m=\{\sigma\in S_m:\sgn(\sigma)=1\}$. For $m\ge2$, it has order $m!/2$, as proved in @mt3-sign-homomorphism.
