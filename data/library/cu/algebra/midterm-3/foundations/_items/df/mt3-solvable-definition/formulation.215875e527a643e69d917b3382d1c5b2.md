A group $G$ is **solvable** if $G^{(d)}=1$ for some integer $d\ge0$, using @mt3-derived-series. Its **derived length** is the least such $d$.

The trivial group has length $0$; a nontrivial abelian group has length $1$. The equivalent definition by a finite series with abelian factors is proved in @mt3-solvable-series.
