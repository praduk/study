For elements $x,y$ of a group $G$, use the lecture convention

$$
[x,y]=xyx^{-1}y^{-1}.
$$

For subgroups $H,K\le G$, define $[H,K]=\langle[h,k]:h\in H,\ k\in K\rangle$. The **commutator subgroup** or **derived subgroup** is $G'=[G,G]$.

Dummit–Foote uses $x^{-1}y^{-1}xy$; replacing $x,y$ by their inverses shows that both conventions generate the same subgroup $[H,K]$. Element-level formulas still need the chosen convention.

The three separate sequences built from these ideas are @mt3-upper-central, @mt3-lower-central, and @mt3-derived-series.
