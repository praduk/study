A group $G$ is **nilpotent** if $Z_c(G)=G$ for some integer $c\ge0$, using @mt3-upper-central. Its **nilpotency class** is the least such $c$.

The trivial group has class $0$; a nontrivial abelian group has class $1$. The equivalent lower-central criterion is the theorem @mt3-central-series-equivalence, not a different convention.
