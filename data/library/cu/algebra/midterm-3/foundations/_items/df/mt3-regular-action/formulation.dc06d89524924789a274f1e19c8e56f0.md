An action of $G$ on a nonempty set $X$ is **regular** if for each $x,y\in X$ exactly one $g\in G$ satisfies $g\cdot x=y$. Equivalently it is transitive and every point stabilizer is trivial.

The **left regular action** is $G$ acting on itself by $g\cdot x=gx$. The unique element carrying $x$ to $y$ is $yx^{-1}$, so this action is regular and faithful. For finite $G$, its permutation degree is $|G|$.
