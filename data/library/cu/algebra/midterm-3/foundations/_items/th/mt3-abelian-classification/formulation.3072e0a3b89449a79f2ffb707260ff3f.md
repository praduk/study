Every finite abelian group is a direct product of cyclic prime-power groups, uniquely up to reordering the prime-power factors. Equivalently it has a unique invariant-factor description

$$
A\cong C_{n_1}\times\cdots\times C_{n_t},\qquad n_{i+1}\mid n_i,\quad n_i>1.
$$

The trivial group corresponds to an empty product. These are descriptions of isomorphism type, not preferred choices of subgroups or generators.
