Conjugation is an @mt3-automorphism, so characteristicity implies normality. If $H\trianglelefteq G$, conjugation by each $g\in G$ restricts to an automorphism of $H$, hence preserves $K$. For the final assertion, every automorphism of $G$ preserves $H$ and restricts to an automorphism there, so preserves $K$. These arguments use exactly @mt3-characteristic and @mt3-normal-subgroup.
