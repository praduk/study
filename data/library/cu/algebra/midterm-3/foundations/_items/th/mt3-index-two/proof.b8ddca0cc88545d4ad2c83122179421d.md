**Definitions and results used:** @[Coset action and its kernel]mt3-coset-action.

The action on the two left cosets is transitive, so its image is the full group $S_2$. Its kernel $K$ has index $2$ in $G$ and is contained in $H$. Since $H$ also has index $2$, the index identity $[G:K]=[G:H][H:K]$ gives $[H:K]=1$. Hence $H=K$ is normal.
