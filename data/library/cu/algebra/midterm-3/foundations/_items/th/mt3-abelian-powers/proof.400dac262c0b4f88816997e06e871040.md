**Tools used:** @mt3-elementary-abelian and @mt3-first-isomorphism.

Commutativity gives $(ab)^n=a^nb^n$. The kernel and image of $f_n$ are $A[n]$ and $A^n$, so the first isomorphism theorem gives $|A|/|A[n]|=|A^n|$, which rearranges to the stated equality. For $n=p$, every element of the kernel and quotient has exponent dividing $p$. They are finite vector spaces over $\F_p$ with the same number of elements, hence the same dimension and an isomorphism.
