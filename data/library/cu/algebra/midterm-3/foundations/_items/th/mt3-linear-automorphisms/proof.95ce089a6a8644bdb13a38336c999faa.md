**Definitions used:** @mt3-elementary-abelian, @mt3-general-linear.

A group homomorphism preserves integer powers, so as an additive map it also preserves scalar multiplication by residues in $\F_p$. Thus group automorphisms are exactly invertible linear maps.

An invertible matrix is specified by an ordered basis of columns. The first column can be any of $p^r-1$ nonzero vectors. After $i$ independent columns have been chosen, their span has $p^i$ vectors, all forbidden for the next column; there are $p^r-p^i$ choices. Multiply these numbers.
