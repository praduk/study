For every integer $n\ge5$, the alternating group $A_n$ is simple. This is the previously established Dummit–Foote §4.6, Theorem 24, used in the assigned $S_6$ automorphism problem. Its proof is not reproduced here; it is a longer permutation-group argument, not a consequence of the Sylow intersection corollary. The three added prelim solutions use @mt3-simple-actions and do not require this theorem as an input.
