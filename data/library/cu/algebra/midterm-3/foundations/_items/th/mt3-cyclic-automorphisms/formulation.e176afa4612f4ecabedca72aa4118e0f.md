For $C_n=\langle a\rangle$, an automorphism is uniquely determined by $a\mapsto a^u$ with $\gcd(u,n)=1$. Composition multiplies exponents, so

$$
\Aut(C_n)\cong(\Z/n\Z)^\times.
$$

For prime $p$ and $a\ge1$, $|\Aut(C_{p^a})|=p^{a-1}(p-1)$. For prime $q$, this automorphism group is cyclic of order $q-1$.
