**Definitions and result used:** @mt3-abelian and @mt3-abelian-classification.

A character of $C_n=\langle a\rangle$ is uniquely determined by choosing $\chi(a)$ among the $n$th roots of unity. Sending $a$ to $e^{2\pi i/n}$ gives a character generating the dual, so $\widehat{C_n}\cong C_n$.

Restriction to the two factors gives $\widehat{A\times B}\to\widehat A\times\widehat B$. Its inverse sends $(\alpha,\beta)$ to the character $(a,b)\mapsto\alpha(a)\beta(b)$, so this is an isomorphism. Decompose $A$ into cyclic factors and apply these observations. Choosing the cyclic generators and their character values explains why this isomorphism is not canonical.
