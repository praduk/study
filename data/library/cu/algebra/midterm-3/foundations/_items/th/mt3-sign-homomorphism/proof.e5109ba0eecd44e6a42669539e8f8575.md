**Definitions and results used:** @[Sign and the alternating group]mt3-permutation-sign. The linear-algebra input is multiplicativity of determinants.

Since $M_{\sigma\tau}=M_\sigma M_\tau$, multiplicativity of the determinant gives $\sgn(\sigma\tau)=\sgn(\sigma)\sgn(\tau)$. A transposition exchanges two columns of the identity matrix, changing its determinant from $1$ to $-1$. This proves the product formula and surjectivity for $m\ge2$. The kernel is $A_m$, so it is normal and has index $2$.
