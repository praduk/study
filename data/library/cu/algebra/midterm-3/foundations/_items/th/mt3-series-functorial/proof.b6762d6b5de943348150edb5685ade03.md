**Definitions used:** @mt3-series, @mt3-upper-central, @mt3-lower-central, @mt3-derived-series.

Homomorphisms satisfy $f([x,y])=[f(x),f(y)]$, directly by preservation of products and inverses. Applying this to the generators of commutator subgroups and inducting gives the two inclusions. For a surjection, every commutator in the target terms has preimages in the corresponding domain terms, so induction gives equality.

Apply these equalities to an automorphism and its inverse to obtain characteristicity of lower and derived terms. For upper centers, automorphisms preserve the center. Inductively, an automorphism preserving $Z_i(G)$ induces an automorphism of $G/Z_i(G)$ and preserves its center, hence preserves its full inverse image $Z_{i+1}(G)$.
