If $\sigma\in Z(S_n)$, then for each transposition $(ij)$,

$$
(\sigma(i)\ \sigma(j))=\sigma(ij)\sigma^{-1}=(ij).
$$

Thus $\sigma$ preserves every two-element subset. For a fixed $i$, choose distinct $j,k$ different from $i$. Then $\sigma(i)$ lies in both $\{i,j\}$ and $\{i,k\}$, so $\sigma(i)=i$. Every point is fixed, and $\sigma=1$. See @mt3-center.
