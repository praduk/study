For abelian $A$ and integer $n\ge1$, $f_n(a)=a^n$ is a homomorphism. Write

$$
A[n]=\{a:a^n=1\},\qquad A^n=\{a^n:a\in A\}.
$$

If $A$ is finite, then $|A/A^n|=|A[n]|$. For **finite** $A$ and prime $p$, both $A/A^p$ and $A[p]$ are elementary abelian, and therefore are isomorphic (not canonically in general).
