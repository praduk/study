By @mt3-index, cosets partition $G$. The map $h\mapsto gh$ is a bijection from $H$ to $gH$, so each coset has $|H|$ elements. Counting gives the formula. Apply it to the cyclic subgroup generated by an element for its order. For the general index formula, choose left coset representatives $g_i$ for $H$ in $G$ and $h_j$ for $K$ in $H$. Then the $g_i h_j$ represent the left cosets of $K$ in $G$ exactly once, giving the product of the two indices.
