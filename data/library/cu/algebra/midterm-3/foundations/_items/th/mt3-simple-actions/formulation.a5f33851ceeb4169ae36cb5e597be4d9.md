Let $G$ be finite and simple. Every **nontrivial** action $\rho:G\to S_m$ is faithful, so $|G|\mid m!$. If $G$ is nonabelian, its image lies in $A_m$, and therefore

$$
|G|\mid m!/2\qquad(m\ge2).
$$

A transitive action on more than one point is nontrivial. In particular this test applies to the action on multiple Sylow subgroups or on the cosets of a proper subgroup.

The orders used in this obstruction are $|S_m|=m!$ and $|A_m|=m!/2$ for $m\ge2$.
