In an abelian group every subgroup is normal. If $G$ is simple and $x\ne1$, then $\langle x\rangle=G$. An infinite cyclic group has the proper nontrivial subgroup $\langle x^2\rangle$. A finite cyclic group of composite order $n=ab$, with $a,b>1$, has the proper nontrivial subgroup $\langle x^a\rangle$ of order $b$. Thus the order is prime. Conversely @mt3-lagrange gives no proper nontrivial subgroup in a prime-order group.
