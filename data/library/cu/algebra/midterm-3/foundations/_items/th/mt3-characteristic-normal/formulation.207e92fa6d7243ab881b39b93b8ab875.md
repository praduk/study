A characteristic subgroup is normal. If $K\operatorname{char}H$ and $H\trianglelefteq G$, then $K\trianglelefteq G$. If $K\operatorname{char}H\operatorname{char}G$, then $K\operatorname{char}G$.



```tikzcd
\begin{tikzcd}
	G \\
	H \\
	K
	\arrow["{\text{normal}}", no head, from=1-1, to=2-1]
	\arrow["{\text{char}}", no head, from=2-1, to=3-1]
\end{tikzcd}
```
