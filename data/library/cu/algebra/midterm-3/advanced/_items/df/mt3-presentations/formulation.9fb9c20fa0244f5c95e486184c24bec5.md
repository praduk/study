For a set $S$ and words $R\subseteq F(S)$, the presentation

$$
\langle S\mid R\rangle=F(S)/\langle\!\langle R\rangle\!\rangle
$$

means the quotient by the **normal closure** of $R$: the subgroup generated by all conjugates of the relators and their inverses. A displayed equation $u=v$ represents the relator $uv^{-1}$.

A proposed assignment of generator images defines a homomorphism exactly when all defining relations hold, by @mt3-free-maps. It is not automatically an isomorphism; injectivity and surjectivity still require justification.
