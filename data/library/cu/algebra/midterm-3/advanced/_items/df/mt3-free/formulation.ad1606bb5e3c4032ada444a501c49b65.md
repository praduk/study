For a set $S$, the **free group** $F(S)$ is the group of reduced finite words in the formal symbols $s,s^{-1}$, $s\in S$. A word is reduced when adjacent inverse pairs have been cancelled. Multiplication concatenates and reduces; the identity is the empty word, and inversion reverses the word and inverts each symbol.

The generators satisfy no relations beyond cancellation. The resulting universal mapping property is stated and proved separately in @mt3-free-maps. Presentations are defined in @mt3-presentations.
