Let $F=F(S)$ and let $c\ge0$. Then $F/L_c(F)$ is nilpotent of class at most $c$, and every set map from $S$ to a nilpotent group of class at most $c$ extends uniquely to a homomorphism from $F/L_c(F)$.

With indexing $\gamma_1(F)=F$, the denominator is $\gamma_{c+1}(F)$. The statement says **at most** $c$; class exactly $c$ requires a separate argument and enough generators. For one generator and $c\ge1$, this quotient is cyclic.
