**Results used:** @mt3-pgroup-maximal, @mt3-lines-hyperplanes; definitions @mt3-frattini-subgroup and @mt3-elementary-abelian.

Every maximal subgroup $M$ is normal of index $p$, so $P/M\cong C_p$. Therefore $P'$ and every $p$th power lie in $M$, giving $P'P^p\le\Phi(P)$. Both $P'$ and $P^p$ are characteristic, hence normal, so their product is a subgroup.

The quotient $V=P/(P'P^p)$ is abelian of exponent dividing $p$. If $x\notin P'P^p$, its nonzero image in $V$ is excluded by a hyperplane. The inverse image of that hyperplane is a maximal subgroup of $P$ excluding $x$. Thus $x\notin\Phi(P)$, proving equality.

For $f:P\to E$, commutativity and exponent $p$ in $E$ show $P'P^p\le\ker f$. Thus $\overline f(x\Phi(P))=f(x)$ is well defined and gives the required factorization. Surjectivity of $P\to P/\Phi(P)$ gives uniqueness.
