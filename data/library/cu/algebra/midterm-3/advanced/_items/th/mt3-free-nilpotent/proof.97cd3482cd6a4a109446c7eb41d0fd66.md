**Results used:** @mt3-free-maps, @mt3-series-functorial, @mt3-central-series-equivalence.

The quotient map sends $L_c(F)$ onto $L_c(F/L_c(F))$, so the latter is $1$. The equivalence theorem proves nilpotence of class at most $c$.

For a set map $S\to H$ with $L_c(H)=1$, freeness gives a unique homomorphism $f:F\to H$. Functoriality gives $f(L_c(F))\le L_c(H)=1$, so $f$ factors uniquely through $F/L_c(F)$. Any extension from that quotient agrees on the generating images of $S$, hence is the same homomorphism. This proves the universal property.
