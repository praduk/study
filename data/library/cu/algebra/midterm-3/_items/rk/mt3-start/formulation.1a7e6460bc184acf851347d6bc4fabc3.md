### What “know it cold” means

For a theorem: state all hypotheses, give its proof skeleton, and recognize the trigger. For a problem: identify the group action, normal subgroup, or quotient **before** computing. Check whether the conclusion is normality, nonsimplicity, solvability, or classification; these are different goals.
