For a finite group $G$, the following are equivalent:

1. $G$ is nilpotent.
2. Every proper subgroup $H<G$ satisfies $H<N_G(H)$.
3. Every Sylow subgroup of $G$ is normal.
4. $G$ is the internal direct product of its Sylow subgroups.
5. Every maximal subgroup of $G$ is normal.

Finiteness is essential to this list of equivalent criteria. For arbitrary-group closure results, see @mt3-nilpotent-closure.
