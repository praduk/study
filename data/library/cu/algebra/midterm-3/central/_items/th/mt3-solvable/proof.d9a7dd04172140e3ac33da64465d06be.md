**Definitions and result used:** @mt3-solvable-definition, @mt3-derived-series, @mt3-series-functorial.

For $H\le G$, $H^{(i)}\le G^{(i)}$, so a terminating derived series for $G$ forces termination for $H$. A surjection maps each derived term onto the corresponding image term, giving quotient closure.

Let $\pi:G\to G/N$. Since $(G/N)^{(a)}=1$, functoriality gives $G^{(a)}\le N$. Taking $b$ further derived steps yields

$$
G^{(a+b)}=(G^{(a)})^{(b)}\le N^{(b)}=1.
$$

This proves the extension claim and the bound.
