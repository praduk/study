**Definitions and results used:** @mt3-solvable-definition, @mt3-derived-series, @mt3-series-functorial, @mt3-characteristic-normal, and @mt3-abelian-simple.

For simple $G$, the characteristic subgroup $G'$ is either $1$ or $G$. The latter would make every derived term equal to $G\ne1$, contrary to solvability. Thus $G'=1$, so $G$ is abelian and simple, hence cyclic of prime order. Conversely, a cyclic prime-order group is simple and abelian, so is solvable.
