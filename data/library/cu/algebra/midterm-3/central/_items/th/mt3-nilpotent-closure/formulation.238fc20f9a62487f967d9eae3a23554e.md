Subgroups and quotient groups of a nilpotent group are nilpotent, even for infinite groups. A finite direct product of nilpotent groups is nilpotent. If $G/Z(G)$ is nilpotent, then $G$ is nilpotent.

Arbitrary extensions do not preserve nilpotence: $A_3$ and $S_3/A_3$ are nilpotent but $S_3$ is not; see @mt3-series-example.
