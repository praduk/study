Subgroups and quotient groups of a solvable group are solvable. If $N\trianglelefteq G$ and both $N$ and $G/N$ are solvable, then $G$ is solvable.

More precisely, if the derived lengths of $G/N$ and $N$ are at most $a$ and $b$, then the derived length of $G$ is at most $a+b$. No finiteness assumption is needed.
