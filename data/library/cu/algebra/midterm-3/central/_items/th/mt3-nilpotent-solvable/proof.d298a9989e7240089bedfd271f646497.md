**Definitions and result used:** @mt3-derived-series, @mt3-lower-central, @mt3-central-series-equivalence.

Induct on $i$. The case $i=0$ is equality. If $G^{(i)}\le L_i(G)$, then

$$
G^{(i+1)}=[G^{(i)},G^{(i)}]\le[G,L_i(G)]=L_{i+1}(G).
$$

If $G$ has class $c$, then $L_c(G)=1$, hence $G^{(c)}=1$. The group $S_3$ gives the failed converse, as computed in @mt3-series-example.
