**Definitions used:** @mt3-derived-series and @mt3-solvable-definition.

Given such a series, induction shows $G^{(i)}\le H_{d-i}$. The initial case is equality. If it holds for $i$, then

$$
G^{(i+1)}\le[H_{d-i},H_{d-i}]\le H_{d-i-1},
$$

since the quotient by $H_{d-i-1}$ is abelian. Thus $G^{(d)}=1$.

Conversely, each $G^{(i+1)}$ is characteristic, hence normal, in $G^{(i)}$, and its quotient is abelian because all commutators vanish there. A terminating derived series, read in reverse, is the required series.
