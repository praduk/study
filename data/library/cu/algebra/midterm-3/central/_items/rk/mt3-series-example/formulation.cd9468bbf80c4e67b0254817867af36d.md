Let $a=(123)$ and $s=(12)$. Conjugation gives $sas^{-1}=a^{-1}$. Since $S_3/A_3$ is abelian, $S_3'\le A_3$; the commutator $[s,a]=a^{-2}=a$ gives equality.

The @mt3-derived-series is $S_3\trianglerighteq A_3\trianglerighteq1$, so $S_3$ is solvable of length $2$. The @mt3-lower-central has $L_0=S_3$ and $L_i=A_3$ for all $i\ge1$, because commutators with $s$ keep producing $a$. The @mt3-upper-central remains $1$, since $Z(S_3)=1$. Thus $S_3$ is not nilpotent.

**Mnemonic:** lower series commutates with the whole group; derived series commutates with itself. A nilpotent normal subgroup and nilpotent quotient need not give a nilpotent extension.
