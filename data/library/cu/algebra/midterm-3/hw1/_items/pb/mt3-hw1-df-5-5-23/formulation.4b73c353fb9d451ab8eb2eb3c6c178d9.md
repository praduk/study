For an action $\rho:K\to S_n$, let $K$ act on $L^n$ by permuting the factors; the resulting semidirect product is the wreath product $L\wr K$ for this action. When no action is specified, use the left regular action of $K$ on itself.

(a) Find $|L\wr K|$ when $K,L$ are finite and the action is regular. (b) For a prime $p$, prove $C_p\wr C_p$ is nonabelian, has order $p^{p+1}$, and is isomorphic to a Sylow $p$-subgroup of $S_{p^2}$.
