**Technique:** List divisors of the complementary factor; apply the congruence before trying a longer argument.

Factor $312=2^3\cdot3\cdot13$. Sylow gives $n_{13}\mid24$ and $n_{13}\equiv1\pmod{13}$. Among the divisors $1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24$, only $1$ satisfies the congruence. Thus $n_{13}=1$, so the Sylow $13$-subgroup is normal.
