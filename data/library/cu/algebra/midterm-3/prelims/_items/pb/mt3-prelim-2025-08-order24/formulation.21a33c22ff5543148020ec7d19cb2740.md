Let $G$ be a group of order $24$. Prove that $G$ has a normal subgroup of order $4$ or a normal subgroup of order $8$, possibly both.

For this practice, use the corollary of Sylow $(N)^+$ when the Sylow $2$-subgroup is not unique. The original exam does not prescribe a method.

Source: [CU Boulder Algebra prelim, August 2025](https://math.colorado.edu/documents/graduate/prelim/Algebra_Aug_2025.pdf), Problem 1, PDF page 2.
