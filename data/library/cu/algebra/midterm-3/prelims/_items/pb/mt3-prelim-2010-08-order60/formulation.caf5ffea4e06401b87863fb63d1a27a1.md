Prove that every simple group $G$ of order $60$ is isomorphic to $A_5$.

For this practice, use the Sylow $2$-subgroups and apply the corollary of Sylow $(N)^+$ when their number is $15$.

The source problem has two parts, not four. Its part (a) concerns index-$6$ subgroups of $A_6$ and supports a different solution using Sylow $5$-subgroups; the corollary route below is a chosen alternative.

Source: [CU Boulder Algebra prelim, August 2010](https://math.colorado.edu/documents/graduate/prelim/Algebra_Aug_2010.pdf), Problem 1(b), PDF page 2.
