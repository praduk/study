Set

$$
\begin{aligned}
t'_1&=(12)(34)(56),&t'_2&=(14)(25)(36),\\
t'_3&=(13)(24)(56),&t'_4&=(12)(36)(45),\\
t'_5&=(14)(23)(56).
\end{aligned}
$$

Verify $(t'_i)^2=1$, $(t'_it'_j)^2=1$ when $|i-j|\ge2$, and $(t'_it'_{i+1})^3=1$. Deduce that the assignment $(i\ i+1)\mapsto t'_i$ extends to a non-inner automorphism of $S_6$ and that these five images generate $S_6$.
