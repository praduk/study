**Technique:** After embedding, compare normalizers, not just the order of the whole symmetric group.

Assume $G$ is simple. For both $1755=3^3\cdot5\cdot13$ and $5265=3^4\cdot5\cdot13$, Sylow gives $n_3=13$ and $n_{13}=27$. The action on the $13$ Sylow $3$-subgroups embeds $G$ in $S_{13}$.

Let $Q\in\Syl_{13}(G)$. Since $13^2\nmid13!$, $Q$ is also Sylow in $S_{13}$ and is generated by a $13$-cycle. Its centralizer in $S_{13}$ is $Q$: a commuting permutation is determined by the image of one point. Conjugation embeds $N_{S_{13}}(Q)/Q$ in $\Aut(Q)$, which has order $12$. Thus $|N_{S_{13}}(Q)|$ divides $13\cdot12=156$.

But $N_G(Q)\le N_{S_{13}}(Q)$ and

$$
|N_G(Q)|=|G|/27=65\quad\text{or}\quad195.
$$

Both numbers have a factor $5$, while $156$ does not. Contradiction in either case.
