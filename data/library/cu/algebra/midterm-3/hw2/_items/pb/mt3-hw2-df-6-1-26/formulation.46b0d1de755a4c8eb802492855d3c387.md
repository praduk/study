Let $P$ be a finite $p$-group and $\overline P=P/\Phi(P)$.

(a) Prove $\overline P$ is elementary abelian.

(b) If $N\nsubg P$ and $P/N$ is elementary abelian, prove $\Phi(P)\le N$ and formulate the universal property using homomorphisms and a commutative diagram.

(c) If $|\overline P|=p^r$, prove that lifts of a basis generate $P$, every generating set has at least $r$ elements, and an inclusion-minimal generating set corresponds exactly to a basis of $\overline P$.

(d) Prove that cyclic $P/\Phi(P)$ implies cyclic $P$, and deduce the same from cyclic $P/P'$.

(e) If $\alpha\in\Aut(P)$ has prime order $q\ne p$ and fixes a coset $x\Phi(P)$, show that it fixes an element of that coset.

(f) Show that a nonidentity automorphism of order prime to $p$ acts nontrivially on $\overline P$. Deduce that every subgroup of $\Aut(P)$ of order prime to $p$ embeds in $\GL_r(\F_p)$.
