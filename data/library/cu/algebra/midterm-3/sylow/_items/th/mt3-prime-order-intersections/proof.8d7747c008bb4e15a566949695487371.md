**Definitions and results used:** @[Lagrange theorem]mt3-lagrange.

For order-$p$ subgroups $A,B$, the order of $A\cap B$ divides $p$. If it is $p$, then $A\cap B=A=B$, contrary to distinctness. Hence the intersection is trivial, and each subgroup contributes $p-1$ new nonidentity elements.

If $|A|=p$ and $|B|=q$ with distinct primes, the intersection order divides both primes and is again $1$.
