**Definitions and results used:** @[Center]mt3-center.

Let $gN$ generate the cyclic quotient. Every element of $G$ can be written $g^i n$ with $i\in\Z$ and $n\in N$. For any two such elements, centrality of $N$ gives

$$
(g^i n)(g^j m)=g^{i+j}nm=g^{j+i}mn=(g^j m)(g^i n).
$$

Thus all elements commute. Take $N=Z(G)$ for the first statement. The argument works for finite and infinite groups.
