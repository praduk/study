Let $G$ be finite with $|G|=p^a m$ and $p\nmid m$. Sylow $p$-subgroups exist. Every $p$-subgroup is contained in a conjugate of any chosen Sylow $p$-subgroup; in particular, all Sylow $p$-subgroups are conjugate.

For $P\in\Syl_p(G)$,

$$
n_p=[G:N_G(P)],\qquad n_p\mid m,\qquad n_p\equiv1\pmod p.
$$

Normality and uniqueness are separated into @mt3-sylow-normality; the normalizer lemma used in $(N)^+$ is @mt3-sylow-normalizer.
