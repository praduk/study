Let $G$ be finite, $p\mid|G|$, and $k\ge1$. Suppose

$$
p^k\mid [P:P\cap Q]
$$

for **every pair of distinct** $P,Q\in\Syl_p(G)$. Then

$$
n_p\equiv1\pmod{p^k}.
$$

The hypothesis concerns **indices of intersections**, not merely the order of $G$. For $k=1$ it recovers the usual Sylow congruence. For larger $k$ it is an additional-hypothesis theorem.

Prerequisites: @mt3-actions and @mt3-sylow. The key observation is $N_P(Q)=P\cap Q$ whenever $Q$ is Sylow.
