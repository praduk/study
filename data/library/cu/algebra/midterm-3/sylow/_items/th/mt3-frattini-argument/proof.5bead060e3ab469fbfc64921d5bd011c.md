**Definitions and results used:** @[Sylow theorem]cu:algebra:midterm-3:sylow:th:mt3-sylow; @[Normal subgroup]cu:algebra:midterm-3:foundations:df:mt3-normal-subgroup; @[Normalizer]cu:algebra:midterm-3:foundations:df:mt3-normalizer; @[Second isomorphism theorem]cu:algebra:midterm-3:foundations:th:mt3-second-isomorphism; @[Lagrange’s theorem and the index formula]cu:algebra:midterm-3:foundations:th:mt3-lagrange.

For $g\in G$, normality of $N$ puts both $P$ and $gPg^{-1}$ in $\Syl_p(N)$. Sylow conjugacy in $N$ gives $n\in N$ with $gPg^{-1}=nPn^{-1}$. Hence $n^{-1}g\in N_G(P)$ and $g\in NN_G(P)$. This proves the factorization. The index identity follows from the second isomorphism theorem applied to $N_G(P)N/N$.
