**Definitions and results used:** @[Sylow conjugacy]mt3-sylow; @[Automorphisms]mt3-automorphism; @[Characteristic subgroups and normality]mt3-characteristic-normal.

A unique Sylow subgroup is preserved by conjugation, so it is normal. Conversely, if $P$ is normal, every Sylow subgroup is a conjugate of $P$ and hence equals $P$.

Every automorphism sends a subgroup of order $|P|$ to another subgroup of that order, so it permutes the Sylow $p$-subgroups. If $P$ is unique, it is therefore characteristic. Characteristic subgroups are normal, completing the equivalence.
