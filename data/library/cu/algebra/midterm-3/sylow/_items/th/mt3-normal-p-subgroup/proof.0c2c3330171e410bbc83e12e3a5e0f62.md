**Definitions and results used:** @[Sylow subgroup order]mt3-sylow-subgroup; @[Subgroup products]mt3-subgroup-product.

Normality of $K$ makes $KP$ a subgroup. Its order $|K||P|/|K\cap P|$ is a power of $p$. Since $KP$ contains a Sylow $p$-subgroup, its order cannot be larger than $|P|$. Hence $KP=P$ and $K\le P$.
