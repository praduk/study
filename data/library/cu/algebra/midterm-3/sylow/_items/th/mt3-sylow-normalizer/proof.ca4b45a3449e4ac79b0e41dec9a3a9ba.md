**Definitions and results used:** @[Normalizer]mt3-normalizer; @[Sylow subgroup order]mt3-sylow-subgroup; @[Subgroup product formula]mt3-subgroup-product.

Because $K$ normalizes $P$, the product $PK$ is a subgroup and

$$
|PK|=\frac{|P||K|}{|P\cap K|}
$$

is a power of $p$. It contains the Sylow subgroup $P$, whose order already is the largest power of $p$ dividing $|G|$. Thus $PK=P$, so $K\le P$.

Apply this to $K=Q\cap N_G(P)$ to get $Q\cap N_G(P)\le Q\cap P$. The reverse inclusion holds because $P\le N_G(P)$. Interchanging the Sylow names gives the final formula.
