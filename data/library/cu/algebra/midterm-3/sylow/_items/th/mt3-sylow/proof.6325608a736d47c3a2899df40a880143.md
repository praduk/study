**Definitions and results used:** @[Normalizers, characteristic subgroups, and subgroup products]mt3-foundations; @[Orbit-stabilizer and the fixed-point congruence]mt3-actions.

The standard Sylow theorem supplies existence, containment and conjugacy. Conjugation has stabilizer $N_G(P)$, giving the index formula; since $P\le N_G(P)$, the index divides $m$. Uniqueness is equivalent to normality by conjugacy, and automorphisms permute Sylow subgroups, giving characteristicity when unique.

For the normalizer lemma, $P\trianglelefteq N_G(P)$. Hence $PK$ is a subgroup with $p$-power order. Maximality of the order of $P$ gives $PK=P$, so $K\le P$.

Let $P$ act on $\Syl_p(G)$. If $P$ fixes $Q$, then $P\le N_G(Q)$, so the lemma gives $P\le Q$, hence $P=Q$. There is exactly one fixed point, and the fixed-point congruence gives $n_p\equiv1\pmod p$.
