**Definitions and results used:** @[Nontrivial p-group center]mt3-pgroups; @[Cyclic central quotient]mt3-cyclic-central-quotient; @[Groups of order p²]mt3-p-square-abelian.

The center is nontrivial, but cannot have order $p^3$ because $P$ is nonabelian. If it had order $p^2$, the quotient would have order $p$ and be cyclic, again forcing abelianity. Thus $|Z(P)|=p$.

The quotient has order $p^2$, so it is $C_{p^2}$ or $C_p^2$. The cyclic case again forces $P$ abelian; only $C_p^2$ remains.
