**Definitions and results used:** @[Strict normalizer growth]mt3-pgroup-normalizer; @[Normalizer]mt3-normalizer; @[Quotient correspondence]mt3-quotient-correspondence; @[Cauchy theorem]mt3-cauchy.

Strict normalizer growth gives $M<N_P(M)\le P$. Maximality forces $N_P(M)=P$, so $M$ is normal. The nontrivial $p$-group $P/M$ has no proper nontrivial subgroup by correspondence. Cauchy gives a subgroup of order $p$ in $P/M$, which must therefore be the whole quotient. Hence $[P:M]=p$.
