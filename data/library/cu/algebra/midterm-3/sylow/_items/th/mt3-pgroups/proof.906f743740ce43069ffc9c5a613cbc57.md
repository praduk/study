**Definitions and results used:** @[Center]mt3-center; @[Conjugation actions]mt3-group-action; @[Fixed-point congruence]mt3-fixed-points; @[Lagrange theorem]mt3-lagrange.

Let $P$ act on $N$ by conjugation; normality makes this an action on $N$. The fixed points are precisely $N\cap Z(P)$. Since $N$ is a nontrivial subgroup of a finite $p$-group, $p\mid|N|$. Hence

$$
|N\cap Z(P)|\equiv|N|\equiv0\pmod p.
$$

The fixed-point set contains $1$, so its size is a positive multiple of $p$ and is at least $p$. Taking $N=P$ proves the center statement.
