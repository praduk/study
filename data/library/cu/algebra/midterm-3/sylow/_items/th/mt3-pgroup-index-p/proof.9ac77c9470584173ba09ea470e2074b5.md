**Definitions and results used:** @[Coset action]mt3-coset-action; @[Orbit–stabilizer]mt3-actions; @[Lagrange theorem]mt3-lagrange.

Let $P$ act on its $p$ left cosets of $H$, giving $\rho:P\to S_p$. The image has $p$-power order because it is a quotient of $P$. Its order also divides $p!$, whose largest dividing power of $p$ is $p$. Transitivity on $p$ points makes its order divisible by $p$, so $|\rho(P)|=p$.

The kernel $K\le H$ therefore has index $p$ in $P$. Since $H$ also has index $p$, the equality $[P:K]=[P:H][H:K]$ forces $K=H$. A kernel is normal.
