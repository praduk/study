Let $G$ be finite and let $p$ be prime. Write $|G|=p^a m$ with $p\nmid m$. A **Sylow $p$-subgroup** of $G$ has order $p^a$, the full $p$-part of $|G|$. It is therefore a @mt3-p-group of the largest order permitted by Lagrange.

The set of these subgroups is $\Syl_p(G)$, and its size is $n_p(G)$, or $n_p$ when $G$ is understood. If $p\nmid|G|$, the trivial subgroup is the unique Sylow $p$-subgroup.
