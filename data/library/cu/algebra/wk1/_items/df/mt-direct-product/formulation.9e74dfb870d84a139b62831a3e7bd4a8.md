For groups $A,B$, the **direct product** $A\times B$ is the Cartesian product with $(a,b)(c,d)=(ac,bd)$. Its identity is $(1_A,1_B)$ and $(a,b)^{-1}=(a^{-1},b^{-1})$.
If $a,b$ have finite orders, $|(a,b)|=\operatorname{lcm}(|a|,|b|)$: the $k$th power is the identity precisely when both coordinate powers are the identity. If either coordinate has infinite order, so does the pair.
