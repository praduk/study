A group is a set $G$ with an associative binary operation $G\times G\to G$ such that

1. (Left Identity) There exists an $e\in G$ such that for all $g\in G$, $eg=g$.
2. (Left Inverse) For each $g\in G$ there exists a $g^{-1}\in G$ so that $g^{-1}g=e$.

A group is *abelian* if $\forall g,h\in G, gh=hg$.

The *order of a group* is the cardinality of $G$.
