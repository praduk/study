For $n\ge3$, $D_{2n}$ is the symmetry group of a regular $n$-gon and has **order $2n$**. Use

$$
D_{2n}=\langle r,s\mid r^n=s^2=1,\ srs=r^{-1}\rangle.
$$

Here $r$ is rotation by one vertex and $s$ is a reflection. The distinct elements are $r^i,sr^i$ for $0\le i<n$. One may move $s$ past powers using $sr^i=r^{-i}s$; every $sr^i$ has order two.
A **presentation** specifies generators and a complete set of defining relations: every relation must follow from them. Checking a few relations in a target group provides a homomorphism from the presented group; it does not alone establish an isomorphism.
