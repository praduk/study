For a homomorphism $\varphi:G\to H$,

$$
\ker\varphi=\{g\in G:\varphi(g)=1_H\},\qquad\im\varphi=\{\varphi(g):g\in G\}.
$$

They are subgroups of $G$ and $H$, respectively. A trivial kernel means $\ker\varphi=\{1_G\}$, not an empty set. The image need not be the whole codomain.
Example: the projection $A\times B\to A$, $(a,b)\mapsto a$, has kernel $\{1_A\}\times B$ and image $A$.
