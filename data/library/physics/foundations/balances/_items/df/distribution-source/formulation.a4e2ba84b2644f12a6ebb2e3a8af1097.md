A point source of strength $q$ at $a\in\R^3$ is represented by $q\delta_a$, meaning $\langle q\delta_a,f\rangle=qf(a)$ for a smooth compactly supported test function $f$. Distributional derivatives are defined by integration by parts, for example $\langle\partial_i T,f\rangle=-\langle T,\partial_i f\rangle$.

A delta source is not an ordinary function with an infinite value at one point. An equation with a delta is tested after smearing. Products of general distributions require additional definitions and cannot be manipulated as ordinary products.
