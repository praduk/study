Let $\rho(t,x)$, a density, be $C^1$, let $j(t,x)$, its flux, be $C^1$, and let $s(t,x)$, its source density, be continuous. If for every fixed smooth bounded volume $V$,

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\int_V\rho\,d^3x=-\int_{\partial V}j\cdot n\,dA+\int_Vs\,d^3x,
$$

then $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot j=s$. Conversely the local equation implies every such integral balance. Here $n$ is outward normal; flux out reduces the stored quantity.
