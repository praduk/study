Cartan's formula gives $\mathcal L_j\Omega=d\iota_j\Omega+\iota_jd\Omega=d\mathcal C$, since an $(n+1)$-form on $M$ vanishes. This proves the identity. The isomorphism follows in a basis: contracting a nonzero top form sends the $n$ basis vectors to independent complementary $(n-1)$-forms. For a compact region $W$ with $\partial W=\Sigma_1-\Sigma_0+B$, @math:diffgeo:stokes-cohomology:th:stokes gives $\int_{\Sigma_1}\mathcal C-\int_{\Sigma_0}\mathcal C+\int_B\mathcal C=\int_Wd\mathcal C$. A zero divergence therefore conserves flux precisely with that boundary accounting.
