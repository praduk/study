Let $M$ be an oriented $n$-manifold with nowhere-zero volume form $\Omega$ and smooth vector field $j$. Define divergence by $\mathcal L_j\Omega=(\operatorname{div}_\Omega j)\Omega$. The flux $(n-1)$-form $\mathcal C=\iota_j\Omega$ satisfies

$$d\mathcal C=(\operatorname{div}_\Omega j)\Omega.$$

Consequently divergence-free currents have equal flux through homologous compact oriented hypersurfaces, with any side-boundary flux included. The correspondence $j\mapsto\iota_j\Omega$ is a pointwise isomorphism. It needs a volume form but no metric.
