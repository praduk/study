The sample mean is $\bar Y=\mu+B+n^{-1}\sum_i\epsilon_i$. Independence gives $\operatorname{Var}(\bar Y)=\tau^2+\sigma^2/n$. For the difference the common offset cancels, so $\operatorname{Var}(Y_1-Y_2)=2\sigma^2$. More repeated measurements cannot determine the unknown common offset from this data alone. The example separates precision of differences from uncertainty of an absolute value.
