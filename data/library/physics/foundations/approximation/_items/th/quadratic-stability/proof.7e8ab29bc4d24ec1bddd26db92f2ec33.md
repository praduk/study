For $V(x)=x^TPx$, $\dot V=-|x|^2+2x^TPr(x)$. Choose a ball where $2\|P\||r(x)|\le|x|/2$. There $\dot V\le-|x|^2/2\le-V/(2\lambda_{\max}(P))$. A sufficiently small sublevel ellipsoid lies inside the ball and is forward invariant. The local solution therefore stays in a compact subset of the domain, extends for all positive time, and satisfies $V(t)\le V(0)e^{-t/(2\lambda_{\max}(P))}$. The eigenvalue bounds on $P$ prove both Lyapunov stability and convergence to zero.
