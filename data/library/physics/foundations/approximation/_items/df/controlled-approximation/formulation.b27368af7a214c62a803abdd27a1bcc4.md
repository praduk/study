An assertion $f(\epsilon)=f_0+\epsilon f_1+O(\epsilon^2)$ as $\epsilon\to0$ means that a norm and a domain have been specified on which the remainder is bounded by $C\epsilon^2$. If other variables such as time or position are present, say whether the bound is uniform in them.

Small coefficients do not guarantee small accumulated effects over long times. A regular expansion may fail near a turning point, resonance, boundary layer, or phase transition. An approximation is informative only together with its controlling dimensionless parameter and range of validity.
