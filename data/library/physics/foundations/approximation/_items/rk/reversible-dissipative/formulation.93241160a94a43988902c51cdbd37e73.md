A dissipative equation can describe selected variables while energy flows into omitted variables. For example, $m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=0$ gives $d(m\dot x^2/2+kx^2/2)/dt=-b\dot x^2$. This says the oscillator energy decreases; it does not say total energy of oscillator and environment is destroyed.

Deriving irreversible macroscopic behavior from a reversible microscopic model requires specifying coarse graining, initial preparation, and often limits of scale or particle number. Merely forgetting variables is not a proof of a particular friction or entropy-production law.
