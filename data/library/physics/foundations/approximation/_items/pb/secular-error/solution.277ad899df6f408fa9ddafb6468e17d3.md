The mean value theorem and $|\sin|\le1$ give the bound. Hence convergence is uniform on any fixed bounded time interval. For $\epsilon_n=1/(2n)$ and $t_n=2\pi n$, $x_0(t_n)=1$ while $x_{\epsilon_n}(t_n)=\cos(2\pi n+\pi)=-1$. The difference is two, so convergence on the entire half-line is not uniform. A small frequency shift becomes an order-one phase shift over sufficiently long time.
