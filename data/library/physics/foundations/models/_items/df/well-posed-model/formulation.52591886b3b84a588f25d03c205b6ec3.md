An initial-value model is well posed in specified function spaces and on a specified time interval when admissible data give an existing unique solution depending continuously on those data. A physical prediction also requires an observation map continuous enough for the intended accuracy.

For constrained systems, not every coordinate tuple is admissible: Maxwell initial fields must satisfy divergence constraints, and incompressible fluid velocities must have zero divergence. A boundary condition is part of the problem, not an optional detail added after solving its differential equation.
