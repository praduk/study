Solving the linear equation gives $v(t)=(F/b)(1-e^{-bt/m})$. The terminal value $v_\infty=F/b$ identifies $b=F/v_\infty$, but contains no information about $m$: every positive $m$ gives the same terminal speed. If the measured relaxation time is $\tau=m/b$, then $m=b\tau=F\tau/v_\infty$. Thus a steady observable and a transient observable constrain different parameter combinations. Differentiation and the initial condition verify the solution.
