A declared convention $c=\hbar=1$ measures time, length, and inverse energy in compatible units; it does not assert that seconds and joules are the same kind of quantity. For a particle of rest mass $m$, the natural-unit inverse length $m$ corresponds in SI to $mc/\hbar$, and its inverse time to $mc^2/\hbar$.

Keep a dictionary when returning to SI. For example, the reduced Compton length is $\hbar/(mc)$, and an energy gap $\Delta E$ produces angular frequency $\Delta E/\hbar$. A factor of $2\pi$ distinguishes angular frequency from cycles per unit time.
