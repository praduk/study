Write $\mathbf a=\ddot{\mathbf p}(t_r)$. Transversality gives $\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf n=\epsilon_0c|\mathbf E_{\rm rad}|^2$. Since $|\mathbf n\times(\mathbf n\times\mathbf a)|^2=|\mathbf a|^2-(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf a)^2$, rotate polar coordinates to put $\mathbf a$ on the polar axis. Then

$$
\int_{S^2}\bigl(|\mathbf a|^2-(\mathbf n\cdot\mathbf a)^2\bigr)d\Omega=2\pi|\mathbf a|^2\int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\,d\theta=\frac{8\pi}{3}|\mathbf a|^2.
$$

Multiplying by $\epsilon_0c/(16\pi^2\epsilon_0^2c^4)$ gives the claimed factor. If $\mathbf a=0$, both sides vanish without choosing a polar axis.
