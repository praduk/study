Put $u=\phi_1-\phi_2$. Then $\Delta u=0$. The product rule and divergence theorem give

$$
\int_\Omega |\nabla u|^2\,dV=\int_{\partial\Omega}u\,\partial_nu\,dS-\int_\Omega u\Delta u\,dV.
$$

Either boundary hypothesis makes the right side zero. Continuity and nonnegativity give $\nabla u=0$ everywhere. Connectedness makes $u$ constant. Under Dirichlet data that constant is zero. Under Neumann data any constant is indeed allowed.
