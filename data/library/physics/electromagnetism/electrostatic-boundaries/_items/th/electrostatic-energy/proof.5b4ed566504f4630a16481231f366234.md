On a radius-$R$ ball containing the source, integration by parts gives $\int\rho\phi=\epsilon_0\int|\nabla\phi|^2-\epsilon_0\int_{\partial B_R}\phi\partial_n\phi$. The boundary integral is $O(R^2R^{-1}R^{-2})=O(R^{-1})$ and tends to zero. The interior integrand is bounded locally, and the tail of the field integral is bounded by a constant times $\int_R^\infty r^{-2}dr$. Let $R\to\infty$ and divide by two.
