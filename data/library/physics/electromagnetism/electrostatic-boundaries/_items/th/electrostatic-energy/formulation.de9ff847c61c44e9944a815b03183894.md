Let a smooth @[compactly supported]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity charge density generate a smooth potential with $\Delta\phi=-\rho/\epsilon_0$, $\phi=O(r^{-1})$, and $\nabla\phi=O(r^{-2})$ at infinity. Then

$$
\frac12\int_{\R^3}\rho\phi\,d^3x=\frac{\epsilon_0}{2}\int_{\R^3}|\nabla\phi|^2\,d^3x.
$$

These are finite under the stated assumptions. Point-charge self-energy is outside them.
