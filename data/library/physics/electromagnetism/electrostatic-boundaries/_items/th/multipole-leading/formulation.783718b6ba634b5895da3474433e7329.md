Let $\rho\in L^1(\R^3)$ be supported in $|\mathbf y|\leq a$. For $r=|\mathbf x|>2a$, define $Q=\int\rho\,d^3y$, $\mathbf p=\int\mathbf y\rho\,d^3y$, and $\mathbf n=\mathbf x/r$. The Coulomb potential satisfies

$$
\phi(\mathbf x)=\frac1{4\pi\epsilon_0}\left(\frac Qr+\frac{\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf n}{r^2}\right)+O(r^{-3}),
$$

with a remainder bounded by a constant times $a^2\|\rho\|_{L^1}/(\epsilon_0r^3)$. Under an origin shift $\mathbf y'=\mathbf y-\mathbf b$, the dipole becomes $\mathbf p'=\mathbf p-Q\mathbf b$.
