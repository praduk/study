Use $\nabla\cdot(\mathbf E\times\mathbf B)=\mathbf B\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf E)-\mathbf E\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf B)$. Substitute Maxwell's curl equations and divide by $\mu_0$ to obtain

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf S=-\frac{\mathbf B\cdot\partial_t\mathbf B}{\mu_0}-\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf E-\epsilon_0\mathbf E\cdot\partial_t\mathbf E=-\partial_tu-\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf E.
$$

Integrate over the volume, differentiate under the integral, and apply the divergence theorem. No time averaging or periodicity is needed.
