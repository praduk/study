In vacuum define energy density $u=\epsilon_0|\mathbf E|^2/2+|\mathbf B|^2/(2\mu_0)$ and Poynting vector $\mathbf S=\mathbf E\times\mathbf B/\mu_0$. Their units are $\mathrm{J/m^3}$ and $\mathrm{W/m^2}$. The power crossing an oriented surface is $\int_S\mathbf S\cdot\mathbf n\,dS$.

The field-to-matter work rate per volume is $\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf E$. It can be negative, as in a driven source supplying energy to a field. The magnetic part of the Lorentz force changes a particle's direction but contributes no instantaneous work because $(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot\mathbf v=0$.
