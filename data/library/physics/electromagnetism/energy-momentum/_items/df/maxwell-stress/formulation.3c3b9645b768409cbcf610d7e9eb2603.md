Define momentum density $\mathbf g=\epsilon_0\mathbf E\times\mathbf B=\mathbf S/c^2$ and the Maxwell stress

$$
\sigma_{ij}=\epsilon_0(E_iE_j-\tfrac12|\mathbf E|^2\delta_{ij})+\mu_0^{-1}(B_iB_j-\tfrac12|\mathbf B|^2\delta_{ij}).
$$

Repeated spatial indices are summed from $1$ to $3$. The traction $\sigma\mathbf n$ has force-per-area units. The field momentum-flux tensor is $-\sigma$ with these conventions. This sign distinction matters when comparing a mechanics stress tensor with the spatial components of relativistic stress-energy.
