Since $\hat{\mathbf s}\times\hat{\boldsymbol\varphi}=\hat{\mathbf z}$,

$$
\mathbf S=\frac{VI}{2\pi s^2\log(b/a)}\hat{\mathbf z}.
$$

Integrating gives $P=\int_a^bS_z2\pi s\,ds=VI$. Its sign reverses if the current reverses for fixed voltage. The energy propagates through the electromagnetic field in the dielectric region; the conducting boundaries establish the fields and guide that transport. The given transverse model neglects loss and end effects.
