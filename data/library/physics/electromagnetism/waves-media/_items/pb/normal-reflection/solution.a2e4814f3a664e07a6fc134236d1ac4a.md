Tangential $E$ continuity gives $1+r=t$. The reflected magnetic field reverses sign because its propagation direction reverses, so $H$ continuity gives $(1-r)/Z_1=t/Z_2$. Solving,

$$
r=\frac{Z_2-Z_1}{Z_2+Z_1},\qquad t=\frac{2Z_2}{Z_1+Z_2}.
$$

Intensity is proportional to $|E_0|^2/(2Z)$, hence $R=r^2$ and $T=(Z_1/Z_2)t^2=4Z_1Z_2/(Z_1+Z_2)^2$. The numerator of $R+T$ is $(Z_2-Z_1)^2+4Z_1Z_2=(Z_1+Z_2)^2$, giving one. Equal impedances eliminate reflection even if wave speeds differ.
