In a source-free region, smooth Maxwell fields satisfy $(\Delta-c^{-2}\partial_t^2)\mathbf E=(\Delta-c^{-2}\partial_t^2)\mathbf B=0$. A nonzero monochromatic plane wave with real $\mathbf k\ne0$ and $\omega>0$ has complex amplitudes obeying

$$
\omega=c|\mathbf k|,\quad \mathbf k\cdot\mathbf E_0=\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf B_0=0,\quad \mathbf B_0=\omega^{-1}\mathbf k\times\mathbf E_0.
$$

The physical fields are real parts of amplitudes times $e^{i(\mathbf k\cdot\mathbf x-\omega t)}$.
