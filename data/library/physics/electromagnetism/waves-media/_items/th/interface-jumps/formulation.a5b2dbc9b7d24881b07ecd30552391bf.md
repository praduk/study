Across a fixed smooth interface with normal $\mathbf n$ from medium 1 to 2, assume piecewise smooth finite fields, free surface charge $\sigma_f$, free surface current $\mathbf K_f$, and no delta-function magnetic flux or electric displacement sheets. Maxwell's equations imply

$$
\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf D_2-\mathbf D_1)=\sigma_f,\qquad\mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf B_2-\mathbf B_1)=0,
$$

$$
\mathbf n\times(\mathbf E_2-\mathbf E_1)=0,\qquad\mathbf n\times(\mathbf H_2-\mathbf H_1)=\mathbf K_f.
$$
