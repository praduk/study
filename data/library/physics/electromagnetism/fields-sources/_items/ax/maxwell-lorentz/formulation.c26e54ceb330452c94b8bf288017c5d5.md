The classical vacuum field model with prescribed total sources is

$$
\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\rho/\epsilon_0,\qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0,
$$

$$
\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t\mathbf B,\qquad
\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf j+c^{-2}\partial_t\mathbf E.
$$

Its force law for a test charge is $\mathbf f=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)$, with power $\mathbf f\cdot\mathbf v=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v$. For a smooth charged continuum the force density is $\rho\mathbf E+\mathbf j\times\mathbf B$. These are physical laws, not consequences of vector calculus. They omit magnetic charges. Matter motion requires its own dynamical equations; prescribing $\rho,\mathbf j$ is an external-source approximation.

Their exact component equivalence is proved in @physics:electromagnetism:spacetime-forms:th:maxwell-forms. The operator $d$ expresses the differential and topological content, while the @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:hodge-star inserts the metric-dependent vacuum response. This separates geometry, sources, and constitutive physics.
