Rotational symmetry forces $\mathbf E=E_r(r)\hat{\mathbf r}$. On a sphere of radius $r$, Gauss' law gives $4\pi r^2E_r=Q(r)/\epsilon_0$. For $r<a$, $Q(r)=4\pi\rho_0r^3/3$, so $\mathbf E=\rho_0\mathbf x/(3\epsilon_0)$. For $r>a$, $Q=4\pi\rho_0a^3/3$, so $\mathbf E=\rho_0a^3\hat{\mathbf r}/(3\epsilon_0r^2)$. Both values at $a$ equal $\rho_0a/(3\epsilon_0)$, as required without a surface sheet. At the center the vector formula gives zero. The flux at infinity recovers $Q$, and both fields have zero curl away from the interface.
