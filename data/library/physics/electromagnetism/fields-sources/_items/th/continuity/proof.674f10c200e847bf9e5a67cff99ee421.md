Take the divergence of Ampère–Maxwell: $0=\mu_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf j+c^{-2}\partial_t(\rho/\epsilon_0)$. Since $c^{-2}=\epsilon_0\mu_0$, division by $\mu_0$ proves continuity. Integrate and apply the divergence theorem for the volume statement.

Without assuming the divergence constraints at later times, the curl evolution gives $\partial_t(\nabla\cdot\mathbf B)=0$ and $\partial_t(\nabla\cdot\mathbf E-\rho/\epsilon_0)=-\mu_0c^2\nabla\cdot\mathbf j-\partial_t\rho/\epsilon_0=0$. An initially zero constraint is therefore zero throughout the smooth evolution.
