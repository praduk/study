If the Maxwell fields and sources are sufficiently differentiable to commute the derivatives below, then

$$
\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0.
$$

For every fixed bounded smooth volume $V$, this gives $\frac{d}{dt}\int_V\rho\,dV=-\int_{\partial V}\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf n\,dS$. Moreover, if the two divergence equations hold initially and the curl equations hold during evolution with a conserved source, the divergence equations remain true.

The coordinate-free content is $d\mathcal J=d^2\mathcal H=0$, proved with its integration and boundary conditions in @physics:electromagnetism:spacetime-forms:th:charge-topology. It makes charge conservation a compatibility condition of the exterior field equation.
