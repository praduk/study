The divergence of $-\nabla\phi-\partial_t\mathbf A$ is $-\Delta\phi+c^{-2}\partial_t^2\phi$, so Gauss' equation is exactly the scalar wave equation. Using $\nabla\times(\nabla\times\mathbf A)=\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf A)-\Delta\mathbf A$, Ampère–Maxwell becomes

$$
\nabla(\nabla\cdot\mathbf A+c^{-2}\partial_t\phi)-\Delta\mathbf A+c^{-2}\partial_t^2\mathbf A=\mu_0\mathbf j.
$$

The gauge removes its first term, leaving the vector wave equation. These algebraic steps are reversible. Finally $\nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf A)=0$ and $\nabla\times\mathbf E=-\partial_t(\nabla\times\mathbf A)$ prove the homogeneous equations.
