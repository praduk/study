Cylindrical symmetry with the usual reflection symmetries leaves an azimuthal field $B_\varphi(s)\hat{\boldsymbol\varphi}$. Ampère's integral around a circle gives $2\pi sB_\varphi=\mu_0I_{\rm enclosed}$. For $s<a$, $I_{\rm enclosed}=Is^2/a^2$, so $B_\varphi=\mu_0Is/(2\pi a^2)$. For $s>a$, $B_\varphi=\mu_0I/(2\pi s)$. Both approach $\mu_0I/(2\pi a)$ at the surface. The direction follows the orientation of positive circulation about $+z$. The exterior magnetic energy per length grows logarithmically with outer cutoff; a real return path is needed to make that global question finite.
