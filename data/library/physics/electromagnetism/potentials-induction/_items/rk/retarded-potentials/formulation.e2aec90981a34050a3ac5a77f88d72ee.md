For suitably localized conserved sources, the retarded Lorenz-gauge potentials in unbounded vacuum are

$$
\phi(\mathbf x,t)=\frac1{4\pi\epsilon_0}\int\frac{\rho(\mathbf y,t-|\mathbf x-\mathbf y|/c)}{|\mathbf x-\mathbf y|}\,d^3y,
$$

$$
\mathbf A(\mathbf x,t)=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int\frac{\mathbf j(\mathbf y,t-|\mathbf x-\mathbf y|/c)}{|\mathbf x-\mathbf y|}\,d^3y.
$$

This is a source-backed Green-function representation, used here without proving the distributional fundamental-solution identity. Sufficiently smooth sources of compact spatial support and compatible past behavior make the formulas well defined. Retarded time encodes propagation delay; selecting this solution also selects no additional incoming homogeneous field. The differential equations alone permit homogeneous waves and advanced Green functions. A boundary condition or initial-value problem supplies the missing physical choice.
