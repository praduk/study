Let $A$ be a real one-form on a fixed oriented Lorentzian four-manifold, $F=dA$, and let the prescribed current three-form $\mathcal J$ be closed. With @[compactly supported]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity variations, the SI action

$$
S[A]=-\frac1{2\mu_0c}\int_M F\w\star F+\int_M A\w\mathcal J
$$

is stationary exactly when $d\star F=\mu_0c\mathcal J$. Gauge changes $A\mapsto A+d\chi$ with a smooth compactly supported function $\chi$ leave the action unchanged. Integrals must exist; alternatively define the action on a compact region with variations supported in its interior.
