In four spacetime dimensions, keep the orientation and replace a Lorentzian metric $g$ by $\widetilde g=e^{2f}g$. The same two-form $F$ solves $dF=0$, $d\star_gF=0$ exactly when it solves $dF=0$, $d\star_{\widetilde g}F=0$.

With sources the same statement holds if the charge-current three-form $\mathcal J$ is held fixed. It does not mean that measured field components or a fixed current vector are unchanged by rescaling the metric.
