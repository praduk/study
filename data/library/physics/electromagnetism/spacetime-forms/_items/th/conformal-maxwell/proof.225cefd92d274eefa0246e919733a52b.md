By @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:th:hodge-square, the factor multiplying the star on two-forms is $e^{(4-4)f}=1$. Thus the differential equations are identical. For fixed $\mathcal J$, the sourced equation is also unchanged. But $\mathcal J=c^{-1}\iota_J\operatorname{vol}_g$ and the volume scales by $e^{4f}$; representing that same three-form by a vector therefore requires $\widetilde J=e^{-4f}J$. Observer normalization and the musical maps also depend on the metric. This distinguishes an equation's conformal covariance from unchanged measured quantities.
