With the smooth fields and conventions of @physics:electromagnetism:spacetime-forms:df:field-current-forms on Minkowski spacetime, the four vacuum Maxwell equations with electric sources are equivalent to

$$
dF=0,\qquad d\mathcal H=\mathcal J,
\qquad \mathcal H=\frac1{\mu_0c}\star F.
$$

Equivalently $d\star F=\mu_0c\,\mathcal J=\mu_0\star J^\flat$. The same intrinsic equations define the minimally coupled Maxwell model on a curved oriented Lorentzian spacetime; that extension is a physical modeling choice. In components it is $\nabla_\nu F^{\mu\nu}=\mu_0J^\mu$ together with $\nabla_{[\alpha}F_{\beta\gamma]}=0$.
