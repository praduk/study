The form Maxwell model implies $d\mathcal J=0$. Charges on compact homologous oriented spacelike surfaces agree when they bound a compact connecting spacetime region with no side-boundary charge flux. The closed field $F$ has local one-form potentials $F=dA$. A single global potential exists exactly when $[F]=0$ in $H^2_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)$.

For a global $A$, $A\mapsto A+d\chi$ leaves $F$ unchanged. A nonzero flux $\int_\Sigma F$ through any closed oriented two-surface obstructs a global potential. This is a classical real-cohomology statement; it does not assert quantization of flux.
