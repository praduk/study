Apply @math:diffgeo:forms:th:d-squared to $d\mathcal H=\mathcal J$ to obtain $d\mathcal J=0$. For a compact connecting region $W$ with boundary $\Sigma_1-\Sigma_0+B$, @math:diffgeo:stokes-cohomology:th:stokes gives $\int_{\Sigma_1}\mathcal J-\int_{\Sigma_0}\mathcal J=-\int_B\mathcal J$. This proves the charge assertion under its stated flux condition. In the SI split, direct differentiation gives $d\mathcal J=dt\w(\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j)\operatorname{vol}_3$.

Local potentials follow from @math:diffgeo:stokes-cohomology:th:poincare-lemma. By @math:diffgeo:stokes-cohomology:df:de-rham, vanishing of the class of a closed form means exactly that it is globally exact. Gauge invariance follows from $d^2\chi=0$. If $F=dA$ globally, Stokes on a closed surface gives $\int_\Sigma F=\int_{\partial\Sigma}A=0$, proving the obstruction. No assumption that the surface bounds a three-region is needed for this last argument.
