If $K$ is Killing, $\nabla_\mu K_\nu+\nabla_\nu K_\mu=0$, and $u$ is an affine geodesic tangent, then $g(K,u)$ is constant along the geodesic. This includes both timelike and null geodesics.

The symmetry field used here is defined by @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:killing. The corresponding stress-energy result is @physics:gravitation:intrinsic-fields:th:killing-current; one concerns a geodesic observable, the other a hypersurface flux.
