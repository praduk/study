Metric compatibility gives

$$
\frac d{d\lambda}g(K,u)=u^\mu u^\nu\nabla_\mu K_\nu+g(K,\nabla_u u).
$$

The second term vanishes by the affine geodesic equation. The first vanishes because $u^\mu u^\nu$ is symmetric and $\nabla_\mu K_\nu$ is antisymmetric. No positivity is needed. A non-affine parametrization adds a tangent term to the geodesic equation, so the unmodified expression need not then be constant.
