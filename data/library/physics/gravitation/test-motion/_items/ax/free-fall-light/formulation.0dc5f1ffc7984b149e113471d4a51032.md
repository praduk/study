In the test-body approximation an uncharged structureless massive body in free fall follows $\nabla_UU=0$, $g(U,U)=-c^2$. In coordinates,

$$
\frac{d^2x^\mu}{d\tau^2}+\Gamma^\mu_{\alpha\beta}\frac{dx^\alpha}{d\tau}\frac{dx^\beta}{d\tau}=0.
$$

Vacuum light in geometric optics follows future null geodesics with affine tangent or wavevector $k$, $\nabla_k k=0$ and $g(k,k)=0$. Wavelength must be small relative to curvature and background-variation scales. Diffraction requires the field equations. Charged particles instead obey $m(\nabla_UU)^\mu=qF^\mu{}_{\nu}U^\nu$, with the electromagnetic convention of @physics:relativity:covariant-fields:df:field-tensor.
