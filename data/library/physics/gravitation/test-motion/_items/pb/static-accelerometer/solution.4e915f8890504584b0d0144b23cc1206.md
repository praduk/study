Proper time gives $U^t=1/N$ and $U^i=0$. Staticity makes $A^t=0$. Since $\Gamma^i_{tt}=c^2Nh^{ij}\partial_jN$,

$$
A^i=c^2h^{ij}\partial_j\log N,\qquad
\alpha=c^2\sqrt{h^{ij}\partial_i\log N\partial_j\log N}.
$$

For $N=a\xi/c^2$ with $\xi>0$, this yields $\alpha=c^2/\xi$, the Rindler value in flat spacetime. An accelerometer measures deviation from free fall and need not measure curvature. A static observer in a gravitational field generally needs a supporting force.
