Differentiate $\partial_i\Phi=GMx_i/r^3$:

$$
\partial_i\partial_j\Phi=\frac{GM}{r^3}(\delta_{ij}-3n_in_j),\qquad\mathbf n=\mathbf x/r.
$$

The @[Hessian]math:uganalysis:metric-multivariable:df:hessian eigenvalue is $-2GM/r^3$ radially and $GM/r^3$ in each transverse direction. Relative acceleration therefore stretches radial separation at rate $2GM\xi_r/r^3$ and compresses transverse separation at rate $-GM\xi_\perp/r^3$. Their trace is zero in vacuum, consistent with $\Delta\Phi=0$ outside the source. Free fall removes the acceleration of a frame's origin; the spatial gradient of acceleration remains measurable through relative motion.
