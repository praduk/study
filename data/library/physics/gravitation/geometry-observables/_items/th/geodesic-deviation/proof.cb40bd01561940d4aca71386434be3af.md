Torsion freeness gives $\nabla_\xi U=\nabla_U\xi$. Since each curve is geodesic, $\nabla_UU=0$. The curvature definition yields $R(U,\xi)U=\nabla_U\nabla_\xi U-\nabla_\xi\nabla_UU=\nabla_U^2\xi$. Antisymmetry in the first two curvature arguments proves the first equation. In a parallel orthonormal frame, covariant derivatives of vector components are ordinary derivatives. Insert $U^0=c$, $U^i=0$, and $\xi^0=0$ into $R(\xi,U)U$: its $i$ component is $c^2R^i{}_{0j0}\xi^j$, proving the sign and factor.
