Let a smooth two-parameter family of affinely parametrized geodesics have tangent $U$ and connecting vector $\xi$, with $[U,\xi]=0$. Then

$$
\nabla_U\nabla_U\xi+R(\xi,U)U=0.
$$

For proper-time parametrization, a parallel orthonormal frame with $U=c e_0$ gives the spatial tidal equation $d^2\xi^i/d\tau^2=-c^2R^i{}_{0j0}\xi^j$ for a spatial connecting field.
