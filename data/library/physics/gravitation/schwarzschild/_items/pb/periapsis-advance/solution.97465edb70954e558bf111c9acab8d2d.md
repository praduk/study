Write $m_g=GM/c^2$, a length. To first order, $u_1''+u_1=3m_gu_0^2$. Expanding the square gives a constant, a double-frequency term, and resonant term $(6m_ge_{\rm orb}/p^2)\cos\phi$. Since $(d^2/d\phi^2+1)(\phi\sin\phi)=2\cos\phi$, the secular term is $u_{1,\rm sec}=3m_ge_{\rm orb}\phi\sin\phi/p^2$. Constant and double-frequency terms have bounded particular solutions and do not create the leading secular phase.

Compare with $(e_{\rm orb}/p)\cos((1-\delta)\phi)=(e_{\rm orb}/p)(\cos\phi+\delta\phi\sin\phi)+O(\delta^2\phi^2)$. Thus $\delta=3m_g/p$. Periapses have angular separation $2\pi/(1-\delta)$, giving advance $2\pi\delta+O(\delta^2)=6\pi GM/[a(1-e_{\rm orb}^2)c^2]$ to leading order. This phase interpretation is perturbative over orbital scales; the truncated secular expression is not uniformly valid for arbitrarily many orbits.
