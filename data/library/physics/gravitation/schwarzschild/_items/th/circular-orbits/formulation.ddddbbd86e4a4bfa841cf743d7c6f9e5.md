Circular timelike Schwarzschild geodesics exist for radii $r>3GM/c^2$, with

$$
\ell^2=\frac{GM r^2}{r-3GM/c^2}.
$$

They are strict minima of the radial effective potential for $r>6GM/c^2$ and strict maxima for $3GM/c^2<r<6GM/c^2$. At $r=6GM/c^2$ the quadratic restoring coefficient vanishes, marking the innermost stable circular-orbit threshold.
