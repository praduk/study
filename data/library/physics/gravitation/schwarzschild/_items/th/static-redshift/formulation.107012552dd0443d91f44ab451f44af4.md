In a static region $ds^2=-N^2c^2dt^2+h_{ij}dx^idx^j$, $N>0$, a geometric-optics ray connecting fixed-coordinate observers has frequencies satisfying

$$
\frac{\omega_{\rm obs}}{\omega_{\rm emit}}=\frac{N_{\rm emit}}{N_{\rm obs}}.
$$

For Schwarzschild static observers this is $\sqrt{f(r_{\rm emit})/f(r_{\rm obs})}$.
