For an equatorial timelike Schwarzschild geodesic parametrized by proper time, $e=f\,dt/d\tau$ and $\ell=r^2d\phi/d\tau$ are constant, and

$$
\left(\frac{dr}{d\tau}\right)^2+V_\ell(r)=e^2c^2,\qquad V_\ell(r)=f(r)\left(c^2+\frac{\ell^2}{r^2}\right).
$$

The radial equation is $d^2r/d\tau^2=-V_\ell'(r)/2$, including at turning points. Here $e$ is dimensionless and $\ell$ has units $\mathrm{m^2/s}$.
