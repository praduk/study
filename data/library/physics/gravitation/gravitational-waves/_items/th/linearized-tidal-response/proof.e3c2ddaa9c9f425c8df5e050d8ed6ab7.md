At first order TT gauge gives $\Gamma^i_{0j}=\tfrac12\partial_0h_{ij}$ and $\Gamma^i_{00}=0$. Connection products are second order, so $R^i{}_{0j0}=\partial_j\Gamma^i_{00}-\partial_0\Gamma^i_{j0}=-\tfrac12\partial_0^2h_{ij}$. Since $\partial_0=c^{-1}\partial_t$, the first formula follows. Substitute in geodesic deviation and use background separation on the right: multiplying curvature by a first-order separation correction would be second order.

For strain introduce an amplitude parameter $s$: $g_{ij}(s)=\delta_{ij}+s h_{ij}+O(s^2)$. An infinitesimal coordinate separation $L\mathbf e$ has proper length $L_s=L\sqrt{1+s h_{ij}e^ie^j+O(s^2)}$. Differentiation at zero gives $L'_0/L=\tfrac12h_{ij}e^ie^j$. Finite interferometer arms comparable to wavelength need a full light travel-time response; this conclusion is local.
