Within the first-order TT perturbation model,

$$
R^i{}_{0j0}=-\frac1{2c^2}\partial_t^2h_{ij},\qquad
\delta\ddot\xi^i=\frac12\partial_t^2h_{ij}\xi_0^j
$$

for a background comoving freely falling detector congruence. Equalities concern the linear coefficients; $\boldsymbol\xi_0$ is background separation. An infinitesimal comoving arm in spatial unit direction $\mathbf e$ has fractional length variation $\delta L/L=\tfrac12h_{ij}e^ie^j$.
