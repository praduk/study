A vacuum plane wave traveling in $+z$ admits a transverse-traceless representation with $h_{0\mu}=h_{z\mu}=0$ and transverse matrix

$$
(h_{ab})_{a,b=x,y}=\begin{pmatrix}h_+&h_\times\\h_\times&-h_+\end{pmatrix},
$$

where $h_+,h_\times$ are functions of $t-z/c$. These dimensionless functions are plus and cross polarizations. The matrix is symmetric, trace-free, and transverse, leaving two components. The reduction to TT gauge for a propagating vacuum plane wave is a source-backed gauge result. It does not claim arbitrary sourced or near-zone fields can globally take this form. Observable effects are relative motion and changes in light propagation, not a preferred numerical gauge component in every chart.
