For an isolated weakly self-gravitating slow source, define trace-free mass quadrupole

$$
Q_{ij}(t)=\int\rho(\mathbf x,t)\left(x_ix_j-\frac13|\mathbf x|^2\delta_{ij}\right)d^3x.
$$

Leading far-zone radiation and mean luminosity are

$$
h^{\rm TT}_{ij}=\frac{2G}{c^4r}\ddot Q^{\rm TT}_{ij}(t-r/c),\qquad
\langle P\rangle=\frac{G}{5c^5}\left\langle\dddot Q_{ij}\dddot Q_{ij}\right\rangle.
$$

TT projects transverse to the observation direction and removes the transverse trace. These are source-backed asymptotic formulas used without deriving the radiation stress-energy calculation. They are not exact strong-field merger formulas. Mass-energy and momentum conservation prevent a freely moving center of mass from supplying an independent leading dipole-radiation channel. Energy loss changes the source's motion; long evolutions need consistent radiation reaction.
