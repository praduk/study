About Minkowski spacetime with $\Lambda=0$, write $g_{\mu\nu}=\eta_{\mu\nu}+h_{\mu\nu}$ and retain terms linear in $h$. Define $h=\eta^{\mu\nu}h_{\mu\nu}$ and $\bar h_{\mu\nu}=h_{\mu\nu}-\eta_{\mu\nu}h/2$. Lorenz gauge $\partial^\mu\bar h_{\mu\nu}=0$ gives

$$
\Box\bar h_{\mu\nu}=-\frac{16\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu},\qquad\Box=\Delta-c^{-2}\partial_t^2.
$$

This source-backed first-order reduction is used without the complete component calculation. A passive infinitesimal coordinate change generated by $\xi$ gives $h_{\mu\nu}\mapsto h_{\mu\nu}-\partial_\mu\xi_\nu-\partial_\nu\xi_\mu$. Residual gauge changes satisfy $\Box\xi_\mu=0$. Small coordinate components only have meaning in a specified background chart and gauge.
