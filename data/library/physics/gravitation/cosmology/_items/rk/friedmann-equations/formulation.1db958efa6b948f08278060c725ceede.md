For FLRW perfect-fluid matter, Einstein's equation reduces to

$$
H^2=\frac{8\pi G\epsilon}{3c^2}+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3}-\frac{kc^2}{a^2},\qquad H=\frac{\dot a}{a},
$$

$$
\frac{\ddot a}{a}=-\frac{4\pi G}{3c^2}(\epsilon+3p)+\frac{\Lambda c^2}{3},\qquad\dot\epsilon+3H(\epsilon+p)=0.
$$

These exact equations within the ansatz are quoted as a source-backed reduction without the full Ricci calculation. The first is the Friedmann constraint, the second the acceleration equation, and the third matter energy balance. They are related by the Bianchi identity; initial data must satisfy the constraint. Interacting components need not obey the last equation individually, though the total does.

Pressure contributes through $\epsilon+3p$. Positive $\Lambda$ can drive accelerated expansion. Neither conclusion requires inserting observational parameter values.
