In spatially flat noncompact FLRW with cosmic time interval $(t_B,t_F)$, radial light travels comoving distance $\int c\,dt/a(t)$. When finite, define proper horizon distances

$$
d_{\rm particle}(t)=a(t)\int_{t_B}^{t}\frac{c\,dt'}{a(t')},\qquad
d_{\rm event}(t)=a(t)\int_t^{t_F}\frac{c\,dt'}{a(t')}.
$$

They ask how far signals could travel from the past boundary and how far future communication can reach. A divergent integral means no finite horizon distance of that type in this model. The Hubble radius $c/|H|$ for $H\ne0$ is an expansion scale, not necessarily either causal horizon. Global topology and extensions of the cosmological model affect global causal questions.
