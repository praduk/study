The FLRW ansatz models homogeneous isotropic space on the scales being described, with comoving perfect-fluid matter:

$$
ds^2=-c^2dt^2+a(t)^2\left(\frac{dr^2}{1-kr^2}+r^2d\Omega^2\right),\qquad k\in\{-1,0,1\}.
$$

Here $a(t)>0$ has length units, $r$ is dimensionless, $d\Omega^2=d\theta^2+\sin^2\theta\,d\phi^2$, and comoving clocks measure cosmic time $t$. Spatial sectional curvature is $k/a^2$. For $k=1$ the radial chart covers only suitable parts of the spatial geometry. Curvature sign does not determine global topology.

This is an idealized large-scale model, not a claim that every bound system expands uniformly. Matter, equation of state, and initial data remain inputs. No numerical present-day cosmological parameters are assumed here.
