Put $n=3(1+w)>0$. Density scaling gives $\epsilon=C a^{-n}$ with $C>0$. The expanding root of Friedmann is $\dot a/a=B a^{-n/2}$, $B=\sqrt{8\pi GC/(3c^2)}>0$. Hence $d(a^{n/2})/dt=nB/2$ and

$$
a(t)=A(t-t_B)^q,\qquad q=\frac2{3(1+w)},\qquad t>t_B,
$$

with $A>0$ compatible with $C$. Substitution gives $\epsilon=3c^2q^2/[8\pi G(t-t_B)^2]$, checking the constraint. Dust has $q=2/3$; radiation $q=1/2$. Since $\ddot a=Aq(q-1)(t-t_B)^{q-2}$, acceleration requires $q>1$, equivalently $-1<w<-1/3$. The excluded $w=-1$ positive-density case instead admits exponential expansion.
