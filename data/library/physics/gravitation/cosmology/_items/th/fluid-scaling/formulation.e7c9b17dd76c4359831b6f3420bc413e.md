Let differentiable $a(t)>0$ and $\epsilon(t)$ on an interval obey the continuity equation for a separately conserved fluid, with $p=w\epsilon$ and constant $w\in\R$. Then

$$
\epsilon(t)a(t)^{3(1+w)}=C
$$

for a constant $C$. Dust scales as $a^{-3}$, radiation with $w=1/3$ as $a^{-4}$, and a $w=-1$ component has constant density.
