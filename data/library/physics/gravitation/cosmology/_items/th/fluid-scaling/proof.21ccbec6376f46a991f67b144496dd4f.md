The product rule gives

$$
\frac d{dt}(\epsilon a^{3(1+w)})=a^{3(1+w)}\left(\dot\epsilon+3(1+w)\frac{\dot a}{a}\epsilon\right)=0.
$$

Thus the product is constant. Conversely a constant product differentiates to the continuity equation. No division by $\epsilon$ is used, so zero density is included. Insert the three specified $w$ values to get their powers. For radiation, the extra power beyond volume dilution represents redshifting of individual energies.
