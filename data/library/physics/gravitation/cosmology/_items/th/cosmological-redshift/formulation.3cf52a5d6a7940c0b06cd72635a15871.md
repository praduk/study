For a geometric-optics ray between fixed comoving emitter and observer along the same FLRW spatial path,

$$
1+z:=\frac{\omega_{\rm emit}}{\omega_{\rm obs}}=\frac{a(t_{\rm obs})}{a(t_{\rm emit})}.
$$

This concerns infinitesimally separated wavefronts in geometric optics. Peculiar velocities add local Doppler factors.
