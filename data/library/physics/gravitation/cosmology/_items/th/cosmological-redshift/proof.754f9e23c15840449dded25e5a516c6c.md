Along the path choose radial comoving coordinate $\chi$, with $d\chi=dr/\sqrt{1-kr^2}$ in a suitable chart. The null equation is $d\chi=\pm c\,dt/a(t)$. Fixed comoving endpoints make $\int_{t_e}^{t_o}c\,dt/a(t)=\Delta\chi$ the same for successive wavefronts. Vary their endpoint times to obtain $\delta t_o/a(t_o)=\delta t_e/a(t_e)$. Comoving proper time equals cosmic time, and frequency is inverse infinitesimal period. Therefore $\omega_e/\omega_o=\delta t_o/\delta t_e=a(t_o)/a(t_e)$. Homogeneity permits this radial description without privileging an origin.
