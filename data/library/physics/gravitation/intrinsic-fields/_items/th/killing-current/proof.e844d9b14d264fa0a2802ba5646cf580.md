Metric compatibility and the product rule give $\nabla_aj^a=(\nabla_aT^{ab})K_b+T^{ab}\nabla_aK_b$. The first term is zero; symmetry of $T$ reduces the second to $\tfrac12T^{ab}(\nabla_aK_b+\nabla_bK_a)=0$. In oriented coordinates, $\nabla_aj^a=|\det g|^{-1/2}\partial_a(|\det g|^{1/2}j^a)$, using $\Gamma^b_{ba}=\partial_a\log\sqrt{|\det g|}$. This is the volume divergence.
