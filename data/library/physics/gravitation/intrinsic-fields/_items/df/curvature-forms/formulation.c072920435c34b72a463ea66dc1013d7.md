Choose a local frame $e_a$ of $TM$ and its dual coframe $\theta^a$. Define connection one-forms by $\nabla_Xe_b=\omega^a{}_b(X)e_a$. The matrix of curvature two-forms and the torsion two-forms are

$$
\Omega^a{}_b=d\omega^a{}_b+\omega^a{}_c\w\omega^c{}_b,\qquad
\Theta^a=d\theta^a+\omega^a{}_b\w\theta^b.
$$

Here $R(X,Y)e_b=\Omega^a{}_b(X,Y)e_a$, so in a coordinate coframe $\Omega^a{}_b=\tfrac12R^a{}_{bij}\,dx^i\w dx^j$ with the convention in @physics:gravitation:geometry-observables:df:connection-curvature. For the Levi-Civita connection $\Theta^a=0$; in an orthonormal frame $\omega_{ab}+\omega_{ba}=0$, lowering a frame index with the constant signature matrix. These follow by expanding the definitions of torsion and metric compatibility.

The identity $d_\omega\Omega=0$ is the bundle @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:th:bianchi applied to $TM$. Changing a frame changes $\omega$ by the connection gauge rule and conjugates $\Omega$; the curvature tensor is unchanged as a geometric object. A curved metric can have a frame connection vanish at one point without having zero curvature there.
