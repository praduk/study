Set $\kappa=8\pi G/c^4$ and $T=g^{\mu\nu}T_{\mu\nu}$. Contraction gives $-R+4\Lambda=\kappa T$, hence $R=4\Lambda-\kappa T$. Substitution yields

$$
R_{\mu\nu}=\kappa\left(T_{\mu\nu}-\frac12Tg_{\mu\nu}\right)+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}.
$$

For a perfect fluid, $R=4\Lambda+\kappa(\epsilon-3p)$. Radiation has $R=4\Lambda$, so with $\Lambda=0$ its scalar curvature is zero. Yet $R_{\mu\nu}=\kappa T_{\mu\nu}$ can be nonzero. Even vanishing Ricci curvature does not force full Riemann curvature to vanish in four dimensions. Scalar curvature alone cannot diagnose flatness.
