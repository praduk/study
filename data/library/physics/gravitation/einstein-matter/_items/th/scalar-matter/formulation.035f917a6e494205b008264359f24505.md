Let a real scalar $\varphi$ have smooth potential $V$ and action

$$
S_m=-\frac1{2c}\int\left(g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu\varphi\partial_\nu\varphi+2V(\varphi)\right)\sqrt{-g}\,d^4x,
$$

normalized so the parenthesized expression has energy-density units. Compact variations give

$$
\Box_g\varphi-V'(\varphi)=0,\qquad
T_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu\varphi\partial_\nu\varphi-g_{\mu\nu}\left(\tfrac12\partial_\alpha\varphi\partial^\alpha\varphi+V(\varphi)\right),
$$

where $\Box_g=\nabla_\mu\nabla^\mu$. On solutions, $\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0$.
