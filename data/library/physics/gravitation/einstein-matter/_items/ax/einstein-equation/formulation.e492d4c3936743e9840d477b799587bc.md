Classical GR models the metric by

$$
G_{\mu\nu}+\Lambda g_{\mu\nu}=\frac{8\pi G}{c^4}T_{\mu\nu},\qquad G_{\mu\nu}=R_{\mu\nu}-\frac12Rg_{\mu\nu}.
$$

The matter equations must be specified alongside it. In length-based coordinates, both sides have inverse-length-squared units. The equation is nonlinear in the metric and has @[diffeomorphism]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map gauge redundancy. Initial or boundary data must be consistent; one cannot freely specify every metric component and derivative.

In a weak nearly static field with slow test motion, $g_{00}=-(1+2\Phi/c^2)$ to leading order gives $\ddot x^i\simeq-\partial_i\Phi$. The Newtonian limit fixes the coupling so nonrelativistic sources recover $\Delta\Phi=4\pi G\rho$ when cosmological effects are negligible. The field equation is a physical model, not a deduction solely from equivalence or covariance.
