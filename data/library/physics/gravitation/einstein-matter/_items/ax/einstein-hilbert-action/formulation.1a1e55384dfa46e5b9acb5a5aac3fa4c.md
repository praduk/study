For length-based coordinates take

$$
S_g=\frac{c^3}{16\pi G}\int(R-2\Lambda)\sqrt{-\det g}\,d^4x.
$$

Define matter stress-energy by compactly supported inverse-metric variation,

$$
\delta S_m=-\frac1{2c}\int T_{\mu\nu}\delta g^{\mu\nu}\sqrt{-\det g}\,d^4x.
$$

The classical action principle requires stationarity of $S_g+S_m$ under admissible variations. This chooses the physical dynamics. Variations here have compact support in the interior. A problem with actual spacetime boundary generally needs an appropriate boundary term and boundary conditions: fixing only the metric value does not eliminate all normal-derivative variations of the Einstein–Hilbert integral.
