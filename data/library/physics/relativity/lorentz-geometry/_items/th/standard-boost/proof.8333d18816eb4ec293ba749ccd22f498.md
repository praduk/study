For any increments $a=\Delta(ct)$ and $b=\Delta x$, expand $-\gamma^2(a-\beta b)^2+\gamma^2(b-\beta a)^2=\gamma^2(1-\beta^2)(-a^2+b^2)=-a^2+b^2$. The transverse terms are unchanged, so polarization gives preservation of all inner products. The determinant of the $2\times2$ block is $\gamma^2(1-\beta^2)=1$. Multiplying its $\beta$ and $-\beta$ matrices gives the identity. For future causal vectors $a\geq|b|$ with $a>0$, $a-\beta b\geq a(1-|\beta|)>0$, proving time orientation. Finally $x'=0$ is equivalent to $x=vt$.
