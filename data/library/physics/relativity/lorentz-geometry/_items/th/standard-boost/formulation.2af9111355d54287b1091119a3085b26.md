For $|v|<c$, set $\beta=v/c$ and $\gamma=(1-\beta^2)^{-1/2}$. The passive boost

$$
ct'=\gamma(ct-\beta x),\qquad x'=\gamma(x-\beta ct),\qquad y'=y,\quad z'=z
$$

is proper and orthochronous, preserves the Minkowski metric, and has inverse given by $v\mapsto-v$. The $S'$ origin moves along $x=vt$ in $S$.
