Rotate axes so $\Delta\mathbf x=(L,0,0)$ with $L\geq0$. If spacelike, $L>c|\Delta t|$; choose $v=c^2\Delta t/L$, which has $|v|<c$, to get $\Delta t'=\gamma(\Delta t-vL/c^2)=0$. Slightly increasing or decreasing $v$ gives opposite signs. If timelike and $L>0$, choose $v=L/\Delta t$; then $|v|<c$ and $\Delta x'=0$. If $L=0$, the original frame suffices. Finally a future causal vector has $c\Delta t\geq L$ and $\Delta t>0$; the boost estimate in @physics:relativity:lorentz-geometry:th:standard-boost keeps its time component positive, while rotations do not change it. Preservation of the future cone is also exactly the orthochronous condition.
