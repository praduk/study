At an event of an oriented Lorentzian four-manifold with signature $(-+++)$, let $u=U/c$ be a future unit timelike observer, so $g(u,u)=-1$. Its rest space $u^\perp$ has the positive metric induced by $g$; the projection is $v\mapsto v+g(u,v)u$. See @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:lorentzian-causality.

For the field two-form in @physics:electromagnetism:spacetime-forms:df:field-current-forms, define spatial covectors

$$e_u=-c\,\iota_uF,\qquad b_u=\iota_u\star F.$$

Both annihilate $u$. Their metric duals in $u^\perp$ are the measured electric and magnetic vectors. In an adapted oriented orthonormal coframe with $u=\partial_0$ they are $E_i\,dx^i$ and $B_i\,dx^i$. Thus observer changes are changes of the time-space split of the same $F$; $\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf B$ are not individually four-vectors.
