For a photon or plane wave with frequency $\omega$ and direction cosine $n_x$ in $S$, the standard boost gives

$$
\omega'=\gamma\omega(1-\beta n_x),\qquad n'_x=\frac{n_x-\beta}{1-\beta n_x}.
$$

For light propagating along $+x$ and an observer moving at $+v$, the frequency ratio is $\sqrt{(1-\beta)/(1+\beta)}$.
