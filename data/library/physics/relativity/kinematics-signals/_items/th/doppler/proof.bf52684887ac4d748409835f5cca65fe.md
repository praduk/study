The time component of the transformed wavevector is $k'^0=\gamma(k^0-\beta k^1)=\gamma\omega(1-\beta n_x)/c$. Its $x$ component is $k'^1=\gamma\omega(n_x-\beta)/c$. Divide $ck'^1$ by $\omega'$ to obtain $n'_x$; the denominator is positive because $|\beta|<1$ and $|n_x|\leq1$. For $n_x=1$, $\gamma(1-\beta)=\sqrt{(1-\beta)/(1+\beta)}$. This is a comparison of frequencies at specified emission and reception directions, not a statement about simultaneous source positions.
