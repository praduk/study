Differentiate the boost along the worldline: $dt'=\gamma(1-vu_x/c^2)dt$, $dx'=\gamma(u_x-v)dt$, $dy'=u_y dt$, and $dz'=u_z dt$. Divide the spatial differentials by $dt'$ to obtain the formulas. Since $|vu_x|/c^2<1$, this division is legitimate. Direct expansion gives

$$
1-|\mathbf u'|^2/c^2=\frac{1-|\mathbf u|^2/c^2}{\gamma^2(1-vu_x/c^2)^2}.
$$

Thus the sign, including the zero case, is unchanged.
