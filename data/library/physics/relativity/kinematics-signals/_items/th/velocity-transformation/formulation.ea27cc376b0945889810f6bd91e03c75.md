Under the standard $x$ boost with speed $v$, a particle with velocity $\mathbf u$ in $S$ has

$$
u'_x=\frac{u_x-v}{1-vu_x/c^2},\qquad
u'_y=\frac{u_y}{\gamma(1-vu_x/c^2)},\qquad
u'_z=\frac{u_z}{\gamma(1-vu_x/c^2)}.
$$

For $|\mathbf u|\leq c$ the denominator is positive, and timelike or null speed character is preserved.
