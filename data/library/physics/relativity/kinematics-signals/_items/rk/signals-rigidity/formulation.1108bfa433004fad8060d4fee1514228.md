The usual causal interpretation assumes controllable physical signals follow future causal curves. Lorentz invariance of the cones then makes their causal direction consistent across inertial frames. A spacelike pair has frame-dependent temporal order; turning a spacelike correlation into an operational signal requires additional physical assumptions, not merely a nonzero mathematical correlation.

Instantaneously rigid extended bodies are incompatible with finite propagation of disturbances. A prescribed family of worldlines can maintain fixed local proper separations in special situations, but that is a constrained motion, not a material response law. For example, observers at different positions in a uniformly accelerating rigid frame require different proper accelerations. Rotations and acceleration cannot be modeled by copying one instantaneous rest frame globally without checking consistency.
