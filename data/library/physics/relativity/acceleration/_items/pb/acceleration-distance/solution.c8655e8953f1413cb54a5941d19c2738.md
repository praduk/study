Its final rapidity is $\chi=\operatorname{artanh}(3/5)=\frac12\log4=\log2$. Thus $\tau=(c/\alpha)\log2$. The associated $\gamma=5/4$ and $\sinh\chi=\gamma\beta=3/4$, so $t=3c/(4\alpha)$ and $x=c^2/(4\alpha)$. The kinetic energy is $(\gamma-1)mc^2=mc^2/4$. For this one-dimensional motion $dp/dt=m\alpha$ is constant; its work $m\alpha x=mc^2/4$ independently verifies the energy result.
