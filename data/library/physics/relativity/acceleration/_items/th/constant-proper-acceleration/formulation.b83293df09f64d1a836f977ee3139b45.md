For $\alpha>0$, the worldline starting at rest at the origin and accelerated in the $+x$ direction with constant signed proper acceleration has

$$
ct(\tau)=\frac{c^2}{\alpha}\sinh(\alpha\tau/c),\qquad
x(\tau)=\frac{c^2}{\alpha}\bigl(\cosh(\alpha\tau/c)-1\bigr).
$$

Its laboratory speed is $c\tanh(\alpha\tau/c)$ and its acceleration magnitude is $\alpha$.
