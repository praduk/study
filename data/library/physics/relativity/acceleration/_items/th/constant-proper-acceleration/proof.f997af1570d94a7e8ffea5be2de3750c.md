For motion along one axis write $U=c(\cosh\chi,\sinh\chi,0,0)$. Then $A=c\dot\chi(\sinh\chi,\cosh\chi,0,0)$, so $A\cdot A=c^2\dot\chi^2$. Constant positive signed proper acceleration means $\dot\chi=\alpha/c$, and the initial rest condition gives $\chi=\alpha\tau/c$. Integrate $dx^0/d\tau=c\cosh\chi$ and $dx/d\tau=c\sinh\chi$ with zero initial coordinates to get the formulas. Taking their derivative ratio gives the speed. This also directly checks the normalization $U\cdot U=-c^2$.
