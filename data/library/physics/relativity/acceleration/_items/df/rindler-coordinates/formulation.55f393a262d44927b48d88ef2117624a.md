On the wedge $x>|ct|$ introduce $\xi>0$ and time $\eta$ by $ct=\xi\sinh(a\eta/c)$, $x=\xi\cosh(a\eta/c)$, with constant scale $a>0$. Differentiating these expressions gives

$$
ds^2=-\left(\frac{a\xi}{c^2}\right)^2c^2d\eta^2+d\xi^2+dy^2+dz^2.
$$

A worldline at fixed $\xi,y,z$ has proper time $d\tau=(a\xi/c^2)d\eta$ and proper acceleration $c^2/\xi$. Thus the congruence is not a set of clocks with equal proper acceleration. The metric is flat because it is a coordinate transform of Minkowski space; nonzero Christoffel symbols alone do not demonstrate curvature.
