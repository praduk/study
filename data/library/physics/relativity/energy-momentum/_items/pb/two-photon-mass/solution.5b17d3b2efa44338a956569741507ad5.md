Each photon momentum is $p_a=(E_a/c,E_a\mathbf n_a/c)$ with $|\mathbf n_a|=1$. Hence

$$
M^2c^4=(E_1+E_2)^2-|E_1\mathbf n_1+E_2\mathbf n_2|^2=2E_1E_2(1-\cos\theta).
$$

For opposite equal energies $E$, the spatial sum vanishes and $M=2E/c^2$. For parallel photons, $M=0$ and the total momentum is null, so there is no rest frame for the pair. The formula describes kinematics; it does not predict photon-photon interaction probabilities.
