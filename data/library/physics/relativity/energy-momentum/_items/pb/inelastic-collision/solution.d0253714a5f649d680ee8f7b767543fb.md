Their momenta are $(\gamma mc,\pm\gamma mv,0,0)$, so total momentum is $(2\gamma mc,0,0,0)$. Conservation therefore leaves the composite at rest with mass $M=2\gamma m$. Its rest energy exceeds $2mc^2$ by $2(\gamma-1)mc^2$, exactly the initial kinetic energy. It may appear as internal excitation or thermal energy within the idealized composite. If radiation escaped, its four-momentum would have to be subtracted before assigning the remnant mass.
