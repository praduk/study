For a system with total future timelike four-momentum $P$, define its invariant mass $M=\sqrt{-P\cdot P}/c$. In the frame where its spatial momentum vanishes, total energy is $Mc^2$. Its center-of-momentum velocity relative to any inertial frame is $\mathbf V=c^2\mathbf P/E_{\rm total}$.

System mass is not generally the sum of constituent rest masses: relative kinetic energy, binding energy, and field energy contribute to the total. A system of massless particles can have positive invariant mass. If total momentum is null, no center-of-momentum rest frame exists.
