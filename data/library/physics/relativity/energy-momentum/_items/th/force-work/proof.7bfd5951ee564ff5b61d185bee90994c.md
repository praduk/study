Differentiate the mass-shell identity at constant $m$: $2E\,dE/dt=2c^2\mathbf p\cdot d\mathbf p/dt$. Since $c^2\mathbf p/E=\mathbf v$, this yields the power equation. Multiplying $dp/dt$ by $dt/d\tau=\gamma$ gives the displayed four-force. Orthogonality follows either by substituting its components or by differentiating $p\cdot p=-m^2c^2$ and using $p=mU$. If the invariant mass varies, the orthogonality conclusion is no longer automatic.
