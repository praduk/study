For a smooth timelike worldline parametrized by proper time, $U^\mu=dx^\mu/d\tau=(\gamma c,\gamma\mathbf v)$ and $A^\mu=dU^\mu/d\tau$. Their identities are $U\cdot U=-c^2$ and $U\cdot A=0$, the latter obtained by differentiation.

An observer's instantaneous rest space is $U^\perp$. Its induced metric is @[positive definite]math:algebra:linear-foundations:df:bilinear-forms: in a rest frame it consists of vectors $(0,\mathbf w)$. The orthogonal projector is $h^\mu{}_{\nu}=\delta^\mu{}_{\nu}+U^\mu U_\nu/c^2$. Proper acceleration is $\alpha=\sqrt{A\cdot A}\geq0$. It is what an ideal accelerometer measures, unlike the coordinate acceleration, which depends on the frame.
