For a future-directed piecewise smooth timelike worldline $x(t)$ with $|\mathbf v(t)|<c$, its proper time is

$$
\tau_2-\tau_1=\frac1c\int\sqrt{-\eta_{\mu\nu}\,dx^\mu dx^\nu}=\int_{t_1}^{t_2}\sqrt{1-|\mathbf v(t)|^2/c^2}\,dt.
$$

The first integral means the positive length functional evaluated with a parameter. It is invariant under proper orthochronous Poincaré changes of coordinates. Ideal clocks realize this mathematical functional by the clock hypothesis. Null curves have zero proper time; no timelike observer rest frame or massive-particle four-velocity can be obtained by simply substituting $v=c$.
