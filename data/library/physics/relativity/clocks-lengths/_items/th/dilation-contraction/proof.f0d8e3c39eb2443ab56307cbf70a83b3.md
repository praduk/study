Along the moving clock, $|\mathbf v|$ is constant, so the proper-time integral is $\Delta t\sqrt{1-v^2/c^2}=\Delta t/\gamma$. For the rod choose two endpoint events with $\Delta t=0$ in $S$. The boost gives $\Delta x'=\gamma\Delta x$. Because each endpoint has a fixed $x'$ coordinate in the rod's rest frame, their $\Delta x'$ is $L_0$ even though the events are not simultaneous there. Thus $L=\Delta x=L_0/\gamma$. Taking instead $\Delta t'=0$ would be a different measurement protocol.
