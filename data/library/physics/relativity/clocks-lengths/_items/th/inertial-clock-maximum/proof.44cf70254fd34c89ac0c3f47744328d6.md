Choose the inertial rest frame of the endpoint separation; the endpoints then have the same spatial position and time difference $T>0$. Every competing future timelike curve can be parametrized by this coordinate time, since its time coordinate increases strictly. Its elapsed time is $\int_0^T\sqrt{1-|\mathbf v|^2/c^2}\,dt\leq T$. The straight stationary segment attains $T$. Equality requires $\mathbf v=0$ almost everywhere. Piecewise smoothness then makes each smooth piece spatially constant and continuity joins those constants, giving the same straight segment. Conversely that segment attains equality. This is a global flat-spacetime statement; curved-spacetime geodesics need not globally maximize proper time.
