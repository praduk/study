The Lorentz factor is $5/3$, so $\Delta t=5\tau_0/3$ and $L=v\Delta t=4c\tau_0/3$. The laboratory segment between the creation and decay positions has rest length $L$ in the lab; simultaneous endpoint measurement in the particle frame gives $L'=L/\gamma=4c\tau_0/5$. That segment moves past the particle at speed $4c/5$ and crosses it in time $L'/v=\tau_0$. This is consistent with the particle clock. A population with exponentially distributed lifetimes needs a separate decay model to compute survival probabilities.
