Set $A^\mu=(\phi/c,\mathbf A)$ and $A_\mu=(-\phi/c,\mathbf A)$, and define

$$
F_{\mu\nu}=\partial_\mu A_\nu-\partial_\nu A_\mu,\qquad \partial_0=c^{-1}\partial_t.
$$

Then $F_{0i}=-E_i/c$, $F_{ij}=\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$, $F^{0i}=E_i/c$, and $F^{i0}=-E_i/c$, where $\epsilon_{123}=1$. All components have magnetic-field units. The associated two-form is $F=\tfrac12F_{\mu\nu}dx^\mu\w dx^\nu$. A gauge change $A_\mu\mapsto A_\mu+\partial_\mu\chi$ leaves $F$ unchanged. Electric and magnetic components are an observer-dependent splitting of one tensor.
