With $x^0=ct$ and prescribed conserved current, a compatible flat-space Maxwell action is

$$
S[A]=\frac1c\int\left[-\frac{F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}}{4\mu_0}+J^\mu A_\mu\right]d^4x.
$$

The source coupling is $-\rho\phi+\mathbf j\cdot\mathbf A$ per spatial volume and time. @[Compactly supported]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity variation of $A_\nu$ gives $(\partial_\mu F^{\mu\nu}/\mu_0+J^\nu)\delta A_\nu$ after integration by parts, hence the source equation with our stated sign. Antisymmetry supplies the factor two in varying $F^2$.

A massive charged particle has action $-mc^2\int d\tau+q\int A_\mu dx^\mu$. Gauge changes add an endpoint term to this particle action. Lorentz covariance constrains admissible theories but does not single out these dynamics by itself; their physical content remains the Maxwell and Lorentz-force model.

The same SI field and source terms are written without coordinates, and varied explicitly, in @physics:electromagnetism:spacetime-forms:th:maxwell-action. Its compact-support hypothesis identifies exactly which boundary terms have been discarded.
