With $J^\mu=(c\rho,\mathbf j)$ and the field tensor in @physics:relativity:covariant-fields:df:field-tensor, the four SI Maxwell equations are equivalent to

$$
\partial_\nu F^{\mu\nu}=\mu_0J^\mu,\qquad
\partial_\alpha F_{\beta\gamma}+\partial_\beta F_{\gamma\alpha}+\partial_\gamma F_{\alpha\beta}=0.
$$

Their form is invariant under Poincaré transformations. Notice the order of the two indices in the source equation.
