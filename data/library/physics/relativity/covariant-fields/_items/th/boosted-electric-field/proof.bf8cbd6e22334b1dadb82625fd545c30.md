The only independent nonzero contravariant component initially is $F^{02}=E/c$. Tensor transformation gives $F'^{02}=\Lambda^0{}_0F^{02}=\gamma E/c$ and $F'^{12}=\Lambda^1{}_0F^{02}=-\gamma\beta E/c$. Since $F'^{02}=E'_y/c$ and $F'^{12}=B'_z$, these are exactly the claimed fields; all other independent components vanish. As a check, $B'^2-E'^2/c^2=\gamma^2E^2(\beta^2-1)/c^2=-E^2/c^2$, unchanged from the original frame.
