Suppose in $S$ the field is $\mathbf E=E\hat{\mathbf y}$ and $\mathbf B=0$. Under the standard $x$ boost,

$$
\mathbf E'=\gamma E\hat{\mathbf y},\qquad \mathbf B'=-\gamma\frac{vE}{c^2}\hat{\mathbf z}.
$$

Thus a field that is purely electric for one inertial observer need not be purely electric for another.
