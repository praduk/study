Because $F^i{}_{0}=E_i/c$ and $F^i{}_{j}=\epsilon_{ijk}B_k$, the spatial right side is $q\gamma(E_i+(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)_i)$. Divide by $\gamma=dt/d\tau$. Since $F^0{}_{i}=E_i/c$, the time component is $q\gamma\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v/c$, giving the power equation. Finally $U_\mu F^\mu{}_{\nu}U^\nu=U^\alpha F_{\alpha\nu}U^\nu=0$ by antisymmetry. Thus the force is compatible with a fixed mass shell.
