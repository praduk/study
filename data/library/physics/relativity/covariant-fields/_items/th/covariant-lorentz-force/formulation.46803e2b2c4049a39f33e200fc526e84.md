The equation $dp^\mu/d\tau=qF^\mu{}_{\nu}U^\nu$ for a constant-mass test charge is equivalent to $d\mathbf p/dt=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)$ together with $dE/dt=q\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf v$. Its right side is orthogonal to $U$.

Intrinsically the force covector is $-q\iota_UF$ with the conventions of @physics:relativity:observer-geometry:th:observer-reconstruction. Its orthogonality to $U$ follows directly from the alternating two-form, without a component expansion.
