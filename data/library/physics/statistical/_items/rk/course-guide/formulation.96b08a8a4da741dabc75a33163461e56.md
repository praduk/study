Assume the Mathematics curriculum, including probability, asymptotics, and Hilbert spaces. Read in authored order: ensemble assumptions, canonical observables, classical gases, particle exchange, quantum statistics, phase transitions, and the limits of equilibrium inference. Every theorem is conditional on its stated model and includes its derivation. A probability distribution is not by itself a dynamical explanation of equilibration.

Use SI units, $\beta=1/(k_BT)$, particle count $N$, and energy-per-particle chemical potential $\mu$. Distinguish finite-system identities from thermodynamic-limit conclusions. Quantum state counts are physical modeling inputs. Ordinary temperatures here are positive unless an explicitly bounded-spectrum example says otherwise.

Inspected primary teaching sources are Mehran Kardar's MIT notes: [ensembles](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/0cb99318c2ed889316cdcff839759e8d_MIT8_333F13_Lec12.pdf), [classical gas](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/0cdb670a825c6aaea7e229ac40776cbf_MIT8_333F13_Lec13.pdf), [particle exchange](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/89259a8fd183acfaec06922c88a6024b_MIT8_333F13_Lec14.pdf), [interactions](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/6739dd6055176b78b9103e2de8850978_MIT8_333F13_Lec17.pdf), [quantum modes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/368b2180e6efa1417ab336d79b2df6db_MIT8_333F13_Lec20.pdf), [identical particles](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/7f26f530bdda5d69c399057bd4b1bd28_MIT8_333F13_Lec22.pdf), [occupations](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/97bfdfb93240e2f48984629ad0c037de_MIT8_333F13_Lec23.pdf), [Fermi gas](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/9d5207eef23c4d6e18ef3afbca5d9d81_MIT8_333F13_Lec24.pdf), and [Bose gas](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/8-333-statistical-mechanics-i-statistical-mechanics-of-particles-fall-2013/85cd383f3ca4573c3d578db435257c5a_MIT8_333F13_Lec25.pdf). Exposition, finite examples, and proofs are independently authored; model assumptions are explicitly separated from their consequences.

This is a core course, not a full treatment of renormalization, rigorous interacting thermodynamic limits, transport, nonequilibrium quantum systems, or simulation algorithms. Mean-field and continuum formulas are marked as models or approximations. In particular, ensemble equivalence is not asserted for arbitrary interactions or constraints.
