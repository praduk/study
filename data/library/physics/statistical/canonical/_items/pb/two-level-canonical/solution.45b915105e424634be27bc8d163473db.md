With $x=\epsilon/(k_BT)$, $Z=1+e^{-x}$ and $U=\epsilon/(1+e^x)$. Differentiation gives


$$
C_V=k_Bx^2\frac{e^x}{(1+e^x)^2}.
$$


As $T\downarrow0$, $U\sim\epsilon e^{-x}$ and $C_V\sim k_Bx^2e^{-x}$. As $T\to\infty$, $U\to\epsilon/2$ and $C_V\sim k_Bx^2/4$. The vanishing high-temperature capacity reflects saturation of a bounded spectrum, not the behavior of a translational ideal gas. The partition function satisfies $Z\to1$ as $T\downarrow0$ and $Z\to2$ as $T\to\infty$.
