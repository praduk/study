For finitely many energies and $T>0$, define $\mathcal F[p]=\sum_i p_i E_i-TS[p]$. The unique minimizer is the canonical vector $q_i=e^{-\beta E_i}/Z$, and


$$
\mathcal F[p]=-k_BT\log Z+k_BT\sum_i p_i\log\frac{p_i}{q_i}.
$$

For nearby equilibrium distributions, @physics:statistical:geometry:th:relative-entropy-metric identifies the second variation of the relative-entropy term with the Fisher metric. In this statement the free-energy functional keeps its reference temperature and energies fixed; the comparison distribution may vary. The exact relative-entropy formula supplies the global nonnegative gap, while the metric describes only its local quadratic behavior.
