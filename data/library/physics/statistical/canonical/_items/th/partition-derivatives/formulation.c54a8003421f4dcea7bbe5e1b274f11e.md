For a finite set of fixed energies, canonical weights satisfy


$$
U=-\partial_\beta\log Z,\quad \partial_\beta^2\log Z=\operatorname{Var}(E),\quad C_V=\frac{\operatorname{Var}(E)}{k_BT^2}\ge0.
$$


Parameters other than $T$ are held fixed.

Geometrically $\partial_\beta^2\log Z$ is the pullback Fisher coefficient $G_{\beta\beta}$ in @physics:statistical:geometry:th:fisher-covariance. Under the temperature coordinate it becomes $G_{TT}=C_V/(k_BT^2)$, with the square of the parameter Jacobian included. @physics:statistical:geometry:pb:temperature-information checks the metric and its units for two levels.
