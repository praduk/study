Differentiate the finite sum: $Z'=-\sum E_i e^{-\beta E_i}$ and $Z''=\sum E_i^2e^{-\beta E_i}$. Thus $-Z'/Z=\langle E\rangle$ and $(\log Z)''=Z''/Z-(Z'/Z)^2=\langle E^2\rangle-\langle E\rangle^2$. Consequently $\partial_\beta U=-\operatorname{Var}(E)$. Since $d\beta/dT=-1/(k_BT^2)$, the chain rule proves the heat-capacity formula. Countably infinite models need convergence permitting these derivatives; the finite proof alone does not supply that justification.
