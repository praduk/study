For an isolated equilibrium model with a finite nonempty accessible set of $M$ microscopic states, assign probability $1/M$ to each. For classical phase space, choose the normalized Liouville measure on a specified finite-volume energy shell, with all other conserved quantities and constraints imposed. This is an ensemble assumption. Hamiltonian volume preservation alone does not establish equal sampling, ergodicity, or a relaxation time. Energy-shell width and reference phase-space cell must be stated when entropy is computed.
