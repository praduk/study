Let $q_i=1/M$. For $p_i>0$, the inequality $\log x\le x-1$ gives $p_i\log(q_i/p_i)\le q_i-p_i$. Sum over the support of $p$. Its right side is at most zero, so $\sum_i p_i\log(p_i/q_i)\ge0$. Expanding this expression gives $\log M-S[p]/k_B\ge0$. Equality requires full support and $q_i/p_i=1$ for every $i$, precisely the uniform distribution. The logarithm inequality follows because $x-1-\log x$ has its unique minimum zero at $x=1$.
