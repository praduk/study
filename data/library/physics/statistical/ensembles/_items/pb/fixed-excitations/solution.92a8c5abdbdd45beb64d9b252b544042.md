Choose two excited sites: $M=\binom42=6$. Three choices contain site 1, so its excitation probability is $1/2$. The entropy is $k_B\log6$. Only one choice excites both 1 and 2, so the joint probability is $1/6\ne1/4$. The fixed-energy constraint correlates otherwise noninteracting sites; an additive Hamiltonian does not by itself imply independent occupations in a microcanonical ensemble.
