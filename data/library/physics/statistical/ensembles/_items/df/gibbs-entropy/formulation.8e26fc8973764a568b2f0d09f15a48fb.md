For a finite probability vector $(p_i)$ define


$$
S[p]=-k_B\sum_i p_i\log p_i,\qquad 0\log0:=0.
$$


This measures distributional uncertainty in J/K. Identifying it with thermodynamic entropy is an additional physical correspondence for an appropriate equilibrium ensemble and state counting. Uniform weight on $M$ states gives $k_B\log M$. A classical differential entropy requires a reference measure and changes under a change of that reference.
