A coarse graining partitions microscopic states into cells $C_a$ and retains only their probabilities $P_a$. It discards conditional information within each cell. For a finite distribution, the dimensionless Shannon entropy satisfies $H(X)=H(a)+\sum_aP_aH(X\mid a)$, by substituting $p_i=P_a p(i\mid a)$ into its defining sum. Coarse graining alone does not create a physical arrow of time. A claim of increasing thermodynamic entropy additionally needs a preparation, a chosen macroscopic description, and assumptions about the dynamics or discarded correlations.
