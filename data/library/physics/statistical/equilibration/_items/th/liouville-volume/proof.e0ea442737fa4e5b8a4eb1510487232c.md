The vector field is $X_H=(H_p,-H_q)$, so its divergence is $\sum_j(H_{q_jp_j}-H_{p_jq_j})=0$ by equality of mixed derivatives. The variational equation is $\dot M=DX_H(\Phi_t)M$ for $M=D\Phi_t$ with $M(0)=I$. Jacobi's determinant formula, initially and then throughout the invertible flow, gives $d(\det M)/dt=(\operatorname{div}X_H)(\Phi_t)\det M=0$. Therefore $\det M=1$. Volume preservation permits stationary ensembles but does not prove mixing or thermalization.
