Put $r_i=p_i/\pi_i$ and $a_{ij}=w_{ij}\pi_j=a_{ji}$. Conservation of probability cancels the derivative of the linear terms, so


$$
\dot D=\sum_{i,j\ne i}a_{ij}(r_j-r_i)\log r_i=-\frac12\sum_{i,j\ne i}a_{ij}(r_i-r_j)(\log r_i-\log r_j)\le0.
$$


The logarithm is strictly increasing. Each nonzero-rate summand vanishes precisely when $r_i=r_j$. Connectivity therefore forces a common ratio, and normalization makes it one. This establishes monotonicity for the stated stochastic model, without deriving the model from microscopic mechanics.
