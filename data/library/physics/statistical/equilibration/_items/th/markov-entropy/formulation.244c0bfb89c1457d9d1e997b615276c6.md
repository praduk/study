For a finite detailed-balance Markov model with $\pi_i>0$ and $\sum_i\pi_i=1$, along a differentiable solution with every $p_i>0$,


$$
D(p\Vert\pi)=\sum_i p_i\log(p_i/\pi_i)
$$


is nonincreasing. If the undirected positive-rate graph is connected, equality of its time derivative occurs only at $p=\pi$.
