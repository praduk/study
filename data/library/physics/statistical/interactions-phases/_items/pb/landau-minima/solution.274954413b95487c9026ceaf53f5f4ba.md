Set $r=a(T-T_c)$. If $r\ge0$, both added terms are nonnegative and vanish together only at $m=0$, giving minimum $f_0$, including $r=0$. If $r<0$, complete the square:


$$
f(m)=f_0-\frac{r^2}{4b}+\frac b4\left(m^2+\frac rb\right)^2.
$$


The two global minima are $m=\pm\sqrt{-r/b}$, with value $f_0-r^2/(4b)$. Their square-root onset and the discontinuity of the second temperature derivative are properties of this polynomial model. Fluctuations omitted by it can change physical critical exponents.
