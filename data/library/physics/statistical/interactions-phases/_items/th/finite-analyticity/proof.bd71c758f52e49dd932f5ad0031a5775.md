Exponential and reciprocal are real analytic where $T>0$, so every Boltzmann factor and their finite sum are real analytic. At each real parameter point the partition function is strictly positive. Continuity gives a neighborhood where its values stay positive, and the real logarithm is analytic there; composition and multiplication prove the assertion. A sequence of analytic free energies can converge nonanalytically as system size diverges, so the proof does not forbid thermodynamic-limit phase transitions.
