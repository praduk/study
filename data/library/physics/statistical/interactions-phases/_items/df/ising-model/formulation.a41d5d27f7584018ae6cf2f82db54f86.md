On a finite graph with $N\ge1$ vertices labeled $i$ and edges $\langle ij\rangle$, an Ising configuration has $s_i=\pm1$ and model energy


$$
H(s)=-J\sum_{\langle ij\rangle}s_is_j-h\sum_i s_i.
$$


Here $J$ and $h$ have units J, and each edge is counted once. For $J>0$, aligned neighbors lower the interaction energy. The order parameter $m=N^{-1}\langle\sum_i s_i\rangle$ is dimensionless. A physical magnetic moment per site converts this spin average into magnetization. The model suppresses continuous spin directions and microscopic material details; parameters must be fitted or derived for an application.
