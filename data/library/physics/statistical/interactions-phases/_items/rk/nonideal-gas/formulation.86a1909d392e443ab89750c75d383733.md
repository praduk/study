The van der Waals model writes $p=Nk_BT/(V-Nb)-aN^2/V^2$ for $V>Nb$, with $b$ a volume per particle and $a$ having units $\mathrm{J\,m^3}$ under particle counting. Excluded volume raises pressure; the attractive correction lowers it. A branch with $(\partial p/\partial V)_T>0$ has negative isothermal compressibility and cannot represent a stable unconstrained bulk equilibrium. Phase separation replaces such a branch when an appropriate free-energy construction permits it. This approximation does not supply exact coexistence data for a real fluid.
