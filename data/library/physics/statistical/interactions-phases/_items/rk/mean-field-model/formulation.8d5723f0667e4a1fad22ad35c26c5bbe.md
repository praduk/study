Replacing each neighbor spin by the mean $m$ on a regular graph of coordination number $z$ yields the effective field $h+zJm$ and the approximate equation


$$
m=\tanh[\beta(h+zJm)].
$$


This discards connected neighbor correlations. It is useful for studying an approximate broken-symmetry branch but gives no uniform error bound near criticality or in arbitrary dimension. At $h=0$, its linearization predicts $k_BT_c=zJ$. The prediction belongs to the mean-field model; it is not the exact critical temperature of every finite-range Ising system.
