A Bernoulli variable satisfies $n^2=n$, so the fermionic variance is $\bar n-\bar n^2=\bar n(1-\bar n)$. For a boson, differentiating the convergent geometric series twice gives $\sum n(n-1)x^n=2x^2/(1-x)^3$. After normalization, $\langle n(n-1)\rangle=2x^2/(1-x)^2=2\bar n^2$. Thus $\langle n^2\rangle-\bar n^2=\bar n+\bar n^2$. These are mode-number fluctuations of ideal grand canonical states; they are not universal statements about every spatial counting experiment.
