Let $x=\beta\hbar\omega>0$. Geometric summation gives $Z=e^{-x/2}/(1-e^{-x})$. Differentiating its logarithm gives $U=\hbar\omega/2+\hbar\omega/(e^x-1)$. Differentiating $U$ yields


$$
C_V=k_Bx^2\frac{e^x}{(e^x-1)^2}.
$$


As $T\downarrow0$, $U\to\hbar\omega/2$ and $C_V\sim k_Bx^2e^{-x}$. As $T\to\infty$, $U\sim k_BT$ and $C_V\to k_B$, recovering two classical quadratic terms. The zero-point energy is temperature-independent and contributes no heat capacity. The partition function satisfies $Z\sim e^{-x/2}$ as $T\downarrow0$, while $Z\sim1/x=k_BT/(\hbar\omega)$ as $T\to\infty$.
