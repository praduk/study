For the homogeneous, spinless, noninteracting three-dimensional Bose gas with quadratic dispersion and ground energy zero, the continuum excited-state density is bounded by $\zeta(3/2)/\lambda_T^3$. Thus the ideal thermodynamic-limit model has


$$
T_c=\frac{2\pi\hbar^2}{mk_B}\left(\frac{n}{\zeta(3/2)}\right)^{2/3},\qquad \frac{N_0}{N}=1-\left(\frac{T}{T_c}\right)^{3/2}\quad(0<T<T_c).
$$


The excess particles occupy a single lowest mode macroscopically. In a finite grand canonical box $\mu<\epsilon_0$; setting $\mu=\epsilon_0$ prematurely makes its partition factor diverge. Condensation is not by itself a derivation of superfluidity. These ideal-gas results and their thermodynamic-limit discussion are source-backed model facts; the continuum-limit proof is not included.
