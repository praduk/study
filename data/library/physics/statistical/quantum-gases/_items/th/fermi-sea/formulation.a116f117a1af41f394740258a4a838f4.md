In the continuum thermodynamic-limit model with spin degeneracy $g$, particle density $n>0$, dispersion $\epsilon(k)=\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$, and occupation one per spin mode for $k<k_F$,


$$
k_F=\left(\frac{6\pi^2 n}{g}\right)^{1/3},\qquad E_F=\frac{\hbar^2k_F^2}{2m},\qquad \frac UV=\frac35nE_F,\qquad p=\frac25nE_F.
$$


Pressure is defined by $p=-\partial_VU|_N$ in this model.
