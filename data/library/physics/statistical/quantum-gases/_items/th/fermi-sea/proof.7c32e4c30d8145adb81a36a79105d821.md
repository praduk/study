There are $g\,d^3k/(2\pi)^3$ states per unit physical volume. Integrating the filled ball gives $n=gk_F^3/(6\pi^2)$. Weighting that integral by $\hbar^2k^2/(2m)$ gives $U/V=g\hbar^2k_F^5/(20\pi^2m)=(3/5)nE_F$. At fixed $N$, $E_F$ is proportional to $(N/V)^{2/3}$, so $U=(3/5)NE_F$ scales as $V^{-2/3}$. Hence $p=(2/3)U/V=(2/5)nE_F$. These continuum integrals do not give exact finite-box shell occupancies.
