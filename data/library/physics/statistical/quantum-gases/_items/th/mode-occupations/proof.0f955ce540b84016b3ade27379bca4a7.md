For a fermion the single-mode normalization is $1+x$, so the probability of its occupied state is $x/(1+x)$, also its mean. For a boson, $\sum_{n\ge0}x^n=(1-x)^{-1}$ and $\sum_{n\ge0}n x^n=x/(1-x)^2$, with absolute convergence for $|x|<1$. Their ratio is $x/(1-x)$. The factorization into modes follows from additive mode energies and unconstrained total particle number. In a fixed-$N$ ensemble the occupations are generally correlated.
