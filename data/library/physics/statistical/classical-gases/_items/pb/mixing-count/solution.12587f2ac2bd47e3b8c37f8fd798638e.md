Initially $Z_i=(V/\lambda_T^3)^{2N}/(N!)^2$. Finally $Z_f=(2V/\lambda_T^3)^{2N}/(2N)!$. Their ratio is $2^{2N}(N!)^2/(2N)!$, independent of $T$; both energies equal $3Nk_BT$. Thus $\Delta S=k_B\log[2^{2N}(N!)^2/(2N)!]$. Stirling's formula gives $\Delta S=(k_B/2)\log(\pi N)+o(1)$, which is subextensive. The initial exact left/right number constraint was released; macroscopic identical-gas mixing has no extensive entropy increase. For distinct species each independently gains a factor $2^N$, so $\Delta S=2Nk_B\log2$, an extensive change.
