The normalized density is $(m/(2\pi k_BT))^{3/2}e^{-m|\mathbf v|^2/(2k_BT)}$. Oddness gives each mean component zero. Each component variance is $k_BT/m$, so the mean squared speed is $3k_BT/m$. The radial speed density is $4\pi v^2(m/(2\pi k_BT))^{3/2}e^{-mv^2/(2k_BT)}$ for $v\ge0$. Its positive stationary maximum satisfies $2/v-mv/(k_BT)=0$, giving $v_{\rm mp}=\sqrt{2k_BT/m}$. This differs from the root mean square speed $\sqrt{3k_BT/m}$.
