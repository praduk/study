Each coordinate integral contributes $V$; the three momentum integrals for one particle give $(2\pi m/\beta)^{3/2}$. Division by $(2\pi\hbar)^3$ yields $\lambda_T^{-3}$. Independence factors the $N$-particle integral, proving $Z_N$. Now $\log Z_N=N\log V-(3N/2)\log\beta+\text{constant}$, with the constant independent of $V,\beta$. Its two indicated derivatives give $U$ and $p$. Gaussian decay justifies differentiation under each momentum integral.
