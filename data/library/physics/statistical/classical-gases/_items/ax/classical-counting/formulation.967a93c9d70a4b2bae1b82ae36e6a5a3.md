For $N$ identical, noninteracting, spinless, nonrelativistic particles of mass $m$ confined to volume $V$, the Maxwell-Boltzmann approximation uses


$$
Z_N=\frac1{N!(2\pi\hbar)^{3N}}\int_{V^N}d^{3N}q\int_{\mathbb R^{3N}}d^{3N}p\,\exp\left[-\beta\sum_{j=1}^N\frac{|\mathbf p_j|^2}{2m}\right].
$$


The phase-space cell and $1/N!$ encode state counting; classical equations of motion alone do not fix them. Negligible interactions and dilute quantum occupation are required. With internal degeneracy, the internal state sum must also be included.
