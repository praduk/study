For @physics:statistical:geometry:df:exponential-family,

$$
\partial_a\Psi=\langle A_a\rangle,\qquad
G_{ab}=\partial_a\partial_b\Psi
=\langle(A_a-\langle A_a\rangle)(A_b-\langle A_b\rangle)\rangle.
$$

The matrix is positive semidefinite, and @[positive definite]math:algebra:linear-foundations:df:bilinear-forms at every finite parameter exactly when the family is minimal. In a minimal family the mean coordinates $u_a=\partial_a\Psi$ are valid local coordinates. The local Legendre dual $\Psi^*(u)=\eta^au_a-\Psi(\eta)$ satisfies $D^2\Psi^*=G^{-1}$ in those coordinates.
