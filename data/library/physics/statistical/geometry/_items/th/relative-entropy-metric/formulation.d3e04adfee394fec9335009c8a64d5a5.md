For two nearby finite-parameter distributions in @physics:statistical:geometry:df:exponential-family, define $D(p\Vert q)=\sum_i p_i\log(p_i/q_i)$. Then

$$
D(p_\eta\Vert p_{\eta+h})
=\Psi(\eta+h)-\Psi(\eta)-h^a\partial_a\Psi(\eta)
=\tfrac12G_{ab}(\eta)h^ah^b+O(|h|^3).
$$

The remainder is locally bounded as $h\to0$ in a fixed parameter coordinate system. The quadratic term is intrinsic under smooth reparametrization, even though relative entropy itself is not a symmetric distance.
