Differentiate the finite sum to get $\partial_\mu\Xi=\beta\sum_iN_i e^{-\beta(E_i-\mu N_i)}$. Dividing by $\beta\Xi$ proves the first formula. The normalized derivative is $\partial_\mu p_i=\beta(N_i-\langle N\rangle)p_i$. Summing $N_i\partial_\mu p_i$ proves the second. Infinite occupation sums require convergence of the differentiated series; it is not automatic from formal notation.
