Put $a=e^{\beta\mu}V/\lambda_T^3>0$. Then $\Xi=\sum_{N\ge0}a^N/N!=e^a$ and $p(N)=e^{-a}a^N/N!$. Shifting the absolutely convergent series gives $\langle N\rangle=a$ and $\langle N(N-1)\rangle=a^2$, hence variance $a$. The relative standard deviation is $a^{-1/2}$. Thus concentration occurs when the mean is large within this model; it is not a theorem about fluctuations of every interacting gas.
