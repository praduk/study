For rotation around axis 1, retain terms first order in $(\omega_2,\omega_3)$ while $\omega_1=\Omega$. Euler equations give $I_2\dot\omega_2=(I_3-I_1)\Omega\omega_3$ and $I_3\dot\omega_3=(I_1-I_2)\Omega\omega_2$. Thus $\ddot\omega_2=\Omega^2(I_3-I_1)(I_1-I_2)\omega_2/(I_2I_3)$, with negative coefficient. Cyclic relabeling gives negative coefficient about axis 3 and positive coefficient about axis 2, because the chosen moment lies between the others. Negative coefficients yield sine/cosine solutions; positive coefficients yield growing and decaying exponentials. The longitudinal perturbation is constant to first order.
