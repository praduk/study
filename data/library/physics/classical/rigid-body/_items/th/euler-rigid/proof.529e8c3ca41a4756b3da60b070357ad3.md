Spatial angular momentum is $L=R I\omega$. Its derivative is $R[I\dot\omega+\omega\times(I\omega)]$, since $R^T\dot R$ represents cross product with $\omega$. Equating this to $R\tau_b$ proves the vector and component equations. With zero torque, dot the vector equation with $\omega$ to obtain $d(\omega^TI\omega/2)/dt=0$. Put $L_b=I\omega$; then $\dot L_b=-\omega\times L_b$, so $d|L_b|^2/dt=-2L_b\cdot(\omega\times L_b)=0$.
