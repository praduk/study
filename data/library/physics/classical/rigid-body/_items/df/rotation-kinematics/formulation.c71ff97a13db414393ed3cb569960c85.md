A rigid body preserves distances between its material points. Relative to its center of mass, write $x=R(t)a$ with fixed body coordinates $a$ and $R(t)\in\SO(3)$. Differentiating $R^TR=I$ shows $\dot R R^T$ is skew. Define spatial angular velocity $\Omega$ by $(\dot R R^T)x=\Omega\times x$, so $\dot x=\Omega\times x$. Body components are $\omega=R^T\Omega$.

An angular velocity has units $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. The instantaneous vector $\omega$ is not in general the time derivative of a single vector of finite rotation angles, because three-dimensional rotations do not commute.
