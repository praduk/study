With linear density $M/\ell$, $I_{11}=0$ and $I_{22}=I_{33}=\int_{-\ell/2}^{\ell/2}a_1^2(M/\ell)da_1=M\ell^2/12$. Off-diagonal entries vanish. About an endpoint perpendicular axis, integrate $\int_0^\ell s^2(M/\ell)ds=M\ell^2/3$. Equivalently shifting the axis adds $M(\ell/2)^2$ because the centered first moment is zero. The zero moment about the rod's own axis follows from treating its radius as zero; a rod with finite cross-section generally has a positive axial moment.
