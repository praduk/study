Circular balance gives $mv_c^2/r_0=GMm/r_0^2$, hence $v_c=\sqrt{GM/r_0}$. For an outward radial launch, energy is $E=mv_0^2/2-GMm/r_0$. At infinity the potential approaches zero, so $E\ge0$ is necessary. When $E=0$, energy gives $\dot r=\sqrt{2GM/r}>0$, whose separated solution reaches every finite radius and tends to infinity as $t\to\infty$; it is the threshold. Thus $v_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{2GM/r_0}=\sqrt2v_c$. Since $[G]=\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}$, both square roots have velocity units.
