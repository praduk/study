For $V(r)=-\kappa/r$ with $\kappa>0$ and $\ell>0$, every collision-free orbit segment has

$$
r(\phi)=\frac{p}{1+e\cos(\phi-\phi_0)},\qquad p=\frac{\ell^2}{\mu\kappa},\qquad e^2=1+\frac{2E\ell^2}{\mu\kappa^2}.
$$

Only portions with positive denominator describe finite positive radius. For $E<0$, the orbit is an ellipse with semimajor axis $a=-\kappa/(2E)$.
