Central torque is zero, so the vector angular momentum is constant and perpendicular to $x$ and $\dot x$. Its nonzero value confines motion to its orthogonal plane. The polar kinetic energy is $\mu(\dot r^2+r^2\dot\phi^2)/2$, and conservation of $p_\phi$ gives the displayed angular rate. Substitute that rate in energy. The radial Euler–Lagrange equation is $\mu\ddot r=-V'(r)+\ell^2/(\mu r^3)=-V'_{\rm eff}(r)$, so a constant radius is a critical point. With $r=r_0+\eta$, retain the linear term to obtain $\ddot\eta+[V''_{\rm eff}(r_0)/\mu]\eta=0$.
