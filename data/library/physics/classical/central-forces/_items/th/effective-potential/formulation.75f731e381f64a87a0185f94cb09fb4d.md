For a central potential $V(r)$ away from $r=0$, a trajectory with nonzero angular-momentum magnitude $\ell=|x\times\mu\dot x|$ lies in a fixed plane. Choose its orientation so $\mu r^2\dot\phi=\ell>0$. Then

$$
E=\frac\mu2\dot r^2+V_{\rm eff}(r),\qquad V_{\rm eff}(r)=V(r)+\frac{\ell^2}{2\mu r^2}.
$$

A circular orbit satisfies $V'_{\rm eff}(r_0)=0$. Its radial linearization has frequency squared $V''_{\rm eff}(r_0)/\mu$.
