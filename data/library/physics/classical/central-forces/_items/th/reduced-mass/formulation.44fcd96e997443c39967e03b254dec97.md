For two particles with masses $m_1,m_2>0$ and potential $V(|x_1-x_2|)$, set $M=m_1+m_2$, $\mu=m_1m_2/M$, $R=(m_1x_1+m_2x_2)/M$, and $r=x_1-x_2$. Their Lagrangian is

$$
L=\frac M2|\dot R|^2+\frac\mu2|\dot r|^2-V(|r|).
$$

Thus the relative motion is a one-body central-force problem of mass $\mu$, and $\ddot R=0$.
