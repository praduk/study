The positive definite matrix $M$ has a symmetric positive square root. Let $z=M^{1/2}\eta$. Then $\ddot z+Az=0$ with symmetric $A=M^{-1/2}KM^{-1/2}$. By the finite-dimensional real spectral theorem choose an orthonormal eigenbasis $w_j$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_j$. Put $v_j=M^{-1/2}w_j$. It satisfies $Kv_j=\lambda_jMv_j$ and the stated orthonormality. Expanding $z=\sum_j a_jw_j$ gives $\ddot a_j+\lambda_ja_j=0$. Positivity of $K$ implies $w^TAw>0$ for nonzero $w$, hence $\lambda_j>0$.
