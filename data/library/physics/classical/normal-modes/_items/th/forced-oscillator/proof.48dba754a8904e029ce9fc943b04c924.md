Substitute the complex ansatz to obtain $(k-m\omega^2-i b\omega)X=F_0$; the imaginary term makes the denominator nonzero. The difference from this particular solution solves $mr^2+br+k=0$. Both roots have negative real part: either they are a conjugate pair with real part $-b/(2m)$ or two negative reals. Repeated roots only add a factor $t$, still decaying. Multiplying the original equation by $\dot x$ gives $d(m\dot x^2/2+kx^2/2)/dt=F\dot x-b\dot x^2$. Average over one steady-state period; the derivative averages to zero and $\langle\dot x_p^2\rangle=\omega^2|X|^2/2$.
