For $m\ddot x+b\dot x+kx=F_0\cos\omega t$ with $m,b,k,\omega>0$, the unique sinusoidal particular solution is $x_p=\operatorname{Re}(Xe^{-i\omega t})$, where

$$
X=\frac{F_0}{k-m\omega^2-i b\omega}.
$$

Every solution approaches it as $t\to\infty$, and its mean absorbed power is $b\omega^2|X|^2/2$.
