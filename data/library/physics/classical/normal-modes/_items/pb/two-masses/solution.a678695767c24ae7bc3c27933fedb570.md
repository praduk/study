The stiffness matrix has diagonal $k+c$ and off-diagonal $-c$. Its eigenvectors $(1,1)$ and $(1,-1)$ have eigenvalues $k$ and $k+2c$. Thus $\omega_+=\sqrt{k/m}$ and $\omega_-=\sqrt{(k+2c)/m}$. The initial vector is $A[(1,1)+(1,-1)]/2$, so $x_1=A(\cos\omega_+t+\cos\omega_-t)/2$ and $x_2=A(\cos\omega_+t-\cos\omega_-t)/2$. Substitution verifies both initial conditions and equations. For weak coupling the nearby frequencies produce slow modulation, although total mechanical energy remains constant.
