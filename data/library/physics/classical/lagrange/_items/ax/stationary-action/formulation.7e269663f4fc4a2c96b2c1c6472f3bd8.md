For conservative particles subject to ideal holonomic constraints, choose $L(q,\dot q,t)=T-V$ and postulate that physical paths make

$$
S[q]=\int_{t_0}^{t_1}L(q,\dot q,t)\,dt
$$

stationary under smooth variations fixing both endpoint configurations and times. The action has units $\mathrm{J\,s}$. Stationary means zero first variation; it does not mean a global minimum. Additional dissipative forces require additional modeling and are not supplied by the ordinary $T-V$ action. This postulate selects a physical model; the variational equation derived next is mathematics.
