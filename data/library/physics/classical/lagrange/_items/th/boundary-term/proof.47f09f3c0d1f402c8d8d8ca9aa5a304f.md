Along any path, the added integral is $F(q(t_1),t_1)-F(q(t_0),t_0)$ by the chain rule and the fundamental theorem of calculus. It is constant across fixed-endpoint variations, so both actions have identical first variations. Since $dF/dt=\partial_iF\dot q^i+\partial_tF$, differentiation with respect to $\dot q^i$ gives the momentum shift. This proves equivalence even though the coordinate expression of canonical momentum changes.
