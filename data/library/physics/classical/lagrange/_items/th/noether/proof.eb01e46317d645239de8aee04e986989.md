The induced velocity variation is $\delta\dot q^i=d\xi^i/dt$. Thus $\delta L=L_{q^i}\xi^i+L_{\dot q^i}d\xi^i/dt$. On a solution, replace $L_{q^i}$ by $dp_i/dt$ and use the product rule to obtain $\delta L=d(p_i\xi^i)/dt$. Subtract the assumed $dB/dt$. Consequently $dJ/dt=0$. A translation of all particles gives linear momentum and a rotation gives its angular-momentum component when the potential has the corresponding symmetry.
