Let $q\mapsto\Phi_\epsilon(q)$ be a smooth one-parameter family with $\Phi_0=\id$, generator $\xi^i(q)$, and infinitesimal change $\delta L=dB(q,t)/dt$ for every path, where $B$ is smooth. Along Euler–Lagrange solutions,

$$
J=\sum_i L_{\dot q^i}\xi^i-B
$$

is constant. This statement covers fixed-time transformations, including invariance with $B=0$.
