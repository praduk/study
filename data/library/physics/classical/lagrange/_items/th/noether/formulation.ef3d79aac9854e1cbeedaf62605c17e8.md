Let $q\mapsto\Phi_\epsilon(q)$ be a smooth one-parameter family with $\Phi_0=\id$, generator $\xi^i(q)$, and infinitesimal change $\delta L=dB(q,t)/dt$ for every path, where $B$ is smooth. Along Euler–Lagrange solutions,

$$
J=\sum_i L_{\dot q^i}\xi^i-B
$$

is constant. This statement covers fixed-time transformations, including invariance with $B=0$.

For a time-independent configuration symmetry with $B=0$, a regular Legendre transform carries the symmetry to phase space. Its conserved expression $p_i\xi^i$ is the component $J_\xi$ of @physics:classical:geometry:df:moment-map; the intrinsic proof is @physics:classical:geometry:th:momentum-conservation. The lifted symmetry preserves the canonical two-form, so one construction simultaneously explains linear and angular momentum. For the more general quasi-invariance in this theorem, retain the correction $-B$; the bare cotangent momentum need not then be conserved.
