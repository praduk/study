Position is $(\ell\sin\theta,-\ell\cos\theta)$, so $T=m\ell^2\dot\theta^2/2$ and choose $V=mg\ell(1-\cos\theta)$. Euler–Lagrange gives $m\ell^2\ddot\theta+mg\ell\sin\theta=0$. Multiplication by $\dot\theta$ shows that $E=m\ell^2\dot\theta^2/2+mg\ell(1-\cos\theta)$ is constant. Linearization at $\theta=0$ gives $\ddot\theta+(g/\ell)\theta=0$, with angular frequency $\sqrt{g/\ell}$ in $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$. This frequency describes small oscillations, not arbitrary-amplitude pendulum motion.
