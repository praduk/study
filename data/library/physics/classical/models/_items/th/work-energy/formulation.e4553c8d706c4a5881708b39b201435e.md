Along a $C^2$ trajectory satisfying $m\ddot x=F$, the kinetic energy $T=m|\dot x|^2/2$ satisfies $\dot T=F\cdot\dot x$. If $F=-\nabla V(x,t)$ for a $C^1$ potential, then

$$
\frac{d}{dt}(T+V)=\partial_t V.
$$

Thus a time-independent conservative force preserves $T+V$. Work $\int F\cdot dx$ and potential energy have units $\mathrm J=\mathrm{N\,m}$.
