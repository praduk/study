For fixed masses, define $P=\sum_a m_av_a$ and $L=\sum_a x_a\times m_av_a$ about a fixed inertial origin. Opposite internal pair forces imply $\dot P=\sum_a F_a^{\rm ext}$. If each pair force is also central, then $\dot L=\sum_a x_a\times F_a^{\rm ext}$.

Momentum has units $\mathrm{kg\,m\,s^{-1}}$; angular momentum has units $\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}$.
