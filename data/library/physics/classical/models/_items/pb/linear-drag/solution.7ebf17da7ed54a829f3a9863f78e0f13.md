Solving the scalar equation gives $v=v_0e^{-bt/m}$, and integration gives $x=(mv_0/b)(1-e^{-bt/m})$. The limiting distance is $mv_0/b$; the speed tends to zero without reaching it at finite time. Since $bv$ is force, $[b]=\mathrm{kg\,s^{-1}}$. Kinetic energy is $mv_0^2e^{-2bt/m}/2$, so the total lost kinetic energy is $mv_0^2/2$. Directly, $\int_0^\infty bv(t)^2dt=mv_0^2/2$. The drag model transfers that energy to the environment; it does not destroy total energy of a larger closed system.
