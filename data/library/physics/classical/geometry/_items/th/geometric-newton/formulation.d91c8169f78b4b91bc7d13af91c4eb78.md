For the autonomous natural Lagrangian in @physics:classical:geometry:df:kinetic-metric, a $C^2$ path satisfies Euler–Lagrange exactly when

$$
\nabla_{\dot q}\dot q=-\operatorname{grad}_g V.
$$

Here $\nabla$ is the @math:diffgeo:connections:th:levi-civita connection and $g(\operatorname{grad}_gV,w)=dV(w)$. For constant $V$, the path is an affinely parametrized geodesic of the kinetic metric. No length-minimizing assertion is needed.
