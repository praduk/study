For a regular closed orbit of an autonomous one-degree-of-freedom Hamiltonian, orient the orbit by time and define

$$
J=\frac1{2\pi}\oint p\,dq.
$$

$J$ has units $\mathrm{J\,s}$. For an ordinary bound oscillator the integral measures oriented phase-space area divided by $2\pi$. Near a separatrix the period may diverge, so neither a smooth action coordinate nor a slow-variation approximation should be assumed without checking its hypotheses.
