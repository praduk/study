The energy curve has semiaxes $\sqrt{2E/(m\Omega^2)}$ and $\sqrt{2mE}$, so its area is $2\pi E/\Omega$; direct time-oriented integration of $p\,dq$ is positive with that value. This proves the action formula. In the parametrization, the coefficient of $d\theta\wedge dJ$ in $dq\wedge dp$ is $\cos^2\theta+\sin^2\theta=1$. The Hamiltonian becomes $H=\Omega J$. Hamilton equations in canonical coordinates $(\theta,J)$ yield the claimed rates. The angle is defined modulo $2\pi$ and is singular at the zero-energy point.
