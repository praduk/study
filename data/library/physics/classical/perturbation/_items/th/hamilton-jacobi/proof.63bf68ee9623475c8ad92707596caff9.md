The first Hamilton equation is the chosen ODE. Differentiating the Hamilton–Jacobi equation in $q^i$ gives $S_{tq^i}+H_{q^i}+\sum_jH_{p_j}S_{q^jq^i}=0$. Along the curve, $\dot p_i=S_{q^it}+\sum_jS_{q^iq^j}\dot q^j=-H_{q^i}$, using symmetry of the Hessian. Thus the second Hamilton equation holds. $S$ is an action, so $S_t$ and $H$ have matching energy units.
