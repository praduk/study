The pullback of $dQ\wedge dP$ is $ab\,dq\wedge dp$, so canonicity is equivalent to $ab=1$. Then $q=Q/a$ and $p=aP$, giving $K=a^2P^2/(2m)+m\Omega^2Q^2/(2a^2)$. Hamilton's equations yield $\dot Q=a^2P/m$, $\dot P=-m\Omega^2Q/a^2$, hence $\ddot Q=-\Omega^2Q$. The effective coordinate mass changes to $m/a^2$, but the product determining frequency does not. Setting $Q=aq,P=ap$ is canonical only for $a^2=1$.
