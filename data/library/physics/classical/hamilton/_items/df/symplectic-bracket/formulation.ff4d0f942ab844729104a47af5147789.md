On a cotangent coordinate chart use

$$
\omega=\sum_i dq^i\wedge dp_i,\qquad i_{X_H}\omega=dH,\qquad
\{f,g\}=\sum_i(f_{q^i}g_{p_i}-f_{p_i}g_{q^i}).
$$

Then $X_H=H_{p_i}\partial_{q^i}-H_{q^i}\partial_{p_i}$, so $X_Hf=\{f,H\}$. In particular $\{q^i,p_j\}=\delta^i_j$. A canonical transformation is a local @[diffeomorphism]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map preserving $\omega$; it may mix coordinates and momenta. These definitions fix signs and prevent identifying a canonical momentum with mass times velocity in models where that identification is false.

These coordinate formulas are the local expressions of @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:symplectic on the canonical cotangent bundle in @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:th:cotangent-symplectic. A metric is unnecessary. In particular canonical coordinate changes preserve the two-form, while arbitrary changes of a chosen phase-space metric need not preserve it.
