On a cotangent coordinate chart use

$$
\omega=\sum_i dq^i\wedge dp_i,\qquad i_{X_H}\omega=dH,\qquad
\{f,g\}=\sum_i(f_{q^i}g_{p_i}-f_{p_i}g_{q^i}).
$$

Then $X_H=H_{p_i}\partial_{q^i}-H_{q^i}\partial_{p_i}$, so $X_Hf=\{f,H\}$. In particular $\{q^i,p_j\}=\delta^i_j$. A canonical transformation is a local diffeomorphism preserving $\omega$; it may mix coordinates and momenta. These definitions fix signs and prevent identifying a canonical momentum with mass times velocity in models where that identification is false.
