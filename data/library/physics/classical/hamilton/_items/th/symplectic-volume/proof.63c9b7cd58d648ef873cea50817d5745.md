At each fixed time, Cartan's formula gives $\mathcal L_{X_H}\omega=d(i_{X_H}\omega)+i_{X_H}d\omega=d(dH)+0=0$, where $d$ differentiates phase-space variables. For the flow map $\Phi_{t,t_0}$, $d(\Phi_{t,t_0}^*\omega)/dt=\Phi_{t,t_0}^*(\mathcal L_{X_{H(t)}}\omega)=0$. Its initial pullback is $\omega$, proving preservation. Pullback commutes with wedge products, hence also preserves $\omega^n/n!$. This argument uses the Lie-derivative identity in @math:diffgeo:forms:df:interior-lie and its usual flow interpretation.
