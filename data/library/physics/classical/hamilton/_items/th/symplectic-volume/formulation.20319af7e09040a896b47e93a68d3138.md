A smooth possibly time-dependent Hamiltonian flow preserves $\omega$ and its volume form $\omega^n/n!$ wherever the flow exists. In canonical coordinates it therefore preserves phase-space volume, even when $H$ depends explicitly on time.

The coordinate-free preservation statement and its bracket consequences are proved in @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:th:hamiltonian-flow. For time-dependent $H_t$, @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:th:transport-form supplies the pullback derivative used here. Neither result needs a globally defined canonical chart or complete flow.
