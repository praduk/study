On a regular Legendre chart, Euler–Lagrange equations are equivalent to

$$
\dot q^i=H_{p_i},\qquad \dot p_i=-H_{q^i}.
$$

Along these paths, $dH/dt=\partial_tH=-\partial_tL$, evaluated at corresponding variables. In particular an autonomous Hamiltonian is conserved.

The same equations combine into $\iota_{\dot z}\omega=d_PH_t$ for $z(t)\in T^*Q$, with $d_P$ differentiating phase-space variables. This follows by contracting $\dot q^i\partial_{q^i}+\dot p_i\partial_{p_i}$ with $dq^i\w dp_i$.
