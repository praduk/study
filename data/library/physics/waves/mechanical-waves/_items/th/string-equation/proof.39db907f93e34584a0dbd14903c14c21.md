For a segment $[x,x+dx]$, net vertical force in the linear model is $T[y_x(x+dx)-y_x(x)]$. Divide Newton's equation by $dx$ and pass to the smooth limit to get $\mu y_{tt}=Ty_{xx}$. Tension divided by line density has dimensions $\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-2}}$. Differentiating either proposed traveling profile twice gives $y_{tt}=c^2y_{xx}$. The sign of its argument determines the motion of a fixed profile feature.
