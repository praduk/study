For $s=0.30\,\mathrm m$, $s'=fs/(s-f)=0.15\,\mathrm m$ and magnification is $-0.15/0.30=-0.5$: the real image is inverted and half the object height. For $s=0.050\,\mathrm m$, $s'=-0.10\,\mathrm m$ and magnification is $+2$: the virtual image is upright and enlarged. An ordinary screen behind the lens can receive the first focused image. In the second case the emerging rays diverge and only their backward extensions meet at the reported position; putting a screen at that virtual position does not capture that image from the emerging beam.
