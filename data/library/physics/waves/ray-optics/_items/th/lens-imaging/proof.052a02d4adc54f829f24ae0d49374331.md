The object-to-image transfer is $P(s')L(f)P(s)$. Its upper-right entry is $s+s'-ss'/f$. Rays from one object point have different input slopes, so their output heights coincide exactly when this entry is zero. Solving gives $s'=fs/(s-f)$, nonzero and finite under the hypotheses. The upper-left entry then equals $1-s'/f=-s'/s$, proving magnification. When $s=f$ the upper-right entry is the nonzero constant $f$ for every finite $s'$, corresponding to collimated output rather than a finite image.
