For an object plane a distance $s>0$ before an ideal thin lens with $f\ne0$, a finite image plane at signed distance $s'$ after it exists when $s\ne f$ and satisfies

$$
\frac1s+\frac1{s'}=\frac1f,\qquad m_{\rm image}=-\frac{s'}s.
$$

A positive $s'$ is a real image behind the lens; a negative $s'$ is a virtual image determined by backward continuation of emerging rays.
