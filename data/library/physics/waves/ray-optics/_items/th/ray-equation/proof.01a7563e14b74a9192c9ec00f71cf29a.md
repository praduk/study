For an arbitrary regular parameter $\tau$, the integrand is $L=n(x)|x'|$. Its velocity derivative is $n x'/|x'|$ and its position derivative is $|x'|\nabla n$. Fixed-endpoint variation and integration by parts give $d[nx'/|x'|]/d\tau=|x'|\nabla n$. Divide by $ds/d\tau=|x'|$ to obtain the equation. Write $t=dx/ds$ with $|t|=1$. Since $dn/ds=\nabla n\cdot t$, expansion gives $n\,dt/ds=\nabla n-(\nabla n\cdot t)t$. Constant $n$ therefore implies constant tangent, and the last expression is precisely the normal gradient component.
