A regular $C^2$ stationary optical path, parametrized by Euclidean arclength $s$, satisfies

$$
\frac{d}{ds}\left(n\frac{dx}{ds}\right)=\nabla n.
$$

Thus rays are straight in a uniform index, and only the component of $\nabla n$ normal to the ray bends its direction.

The invariant formulation is the geodesic equation of $g_{\mathrm{opt}}=n^2\delta$, or Hamilton's equations for $K=\tfrac12g_{\mathrm{opt}}^{-1}(p,p)$ on $T^*M$. The complete conversion, including the distinction between Euclidean and optical arclength, is @physics:waves:geometry:th:optical-metric.
