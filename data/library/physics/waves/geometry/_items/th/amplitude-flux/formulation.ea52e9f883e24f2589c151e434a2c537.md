Let $(M,g)$ be an oriented @[Riemannian manifold]math:diffgeo:connections:df:riemannian-metric, $n>0$ smooth, $k_0>0$ a fixed inverse-length scale, and $\varepsilon>0$ dimensionless. In the scalar model

$$
(\varepsilon^2\Delta_g+k_0^2n^2)u_\varepsilon=0,
\qquad u_\varepsilon=a\,e^{ik_0S/\varepsilon},
$$

take a real smooth phase $S$ and complex smooth amplitude $a$. The coefficients of orders $\varepsilon^0$ and $\varepsilon^1$ in the residual vanish when

$$
|dS|_g^2=n^2,\qquad 2\langle da,dS\rangle_g+a\Delta_gS=0.
$$

Under the transport equation, the flux vector $j=|a|^2\operatorname{grad}_gS$ has $\operatorname{div}_g j=0$. Equivalently its $(\dim M-1)$-form $\iota_j\operatorname{vol}_g$ is closed. Flux through the ends of a ray tube with tangent sides is therefore equal. This is a residual calculation; a uniform error estimate requires separate control, especially near @physics:waves:geometry:df:phase-space-rays caustics.
