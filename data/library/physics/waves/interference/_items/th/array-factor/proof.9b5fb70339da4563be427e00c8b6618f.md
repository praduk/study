The finite geometric sum gives $S_N=(1-e^{iN\delta})/(1-e^{i\delta})$. Factoring numerator and denominator as $-2ie^{i\theta/2}\sin(\theta/2)$ yields a unit-modulus phase times $\sin(N\delta/2)/\sin(\delta/2)$. Take squared modulus. At an integer multiple of $2\pi$, every summand is one, so the exact value is $N^2$ without division by zero. For equally spaced slits, the single-slit intensity envelope multiplies this array factor.
