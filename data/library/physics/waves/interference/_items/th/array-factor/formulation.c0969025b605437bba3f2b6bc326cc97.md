For $N\ge1$ identical coherent emitters whose successive phases at an observer differ by $\delta$, the total amplitude relative to one emitter is $S_N=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}e^{ij\delta}$. For $\delta\notin2\pi\Z$,

$$
|S_N|^2=\frac{\sin^2(N\delta/2)}{\sin^2(\delta/2)}.
$$

At $\delta\in2\pi\Z$ the value is $N^2$.
