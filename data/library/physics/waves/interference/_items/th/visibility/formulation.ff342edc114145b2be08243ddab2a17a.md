For two fields in the quadratic model, scan an additional relative phase $\delta$ while leaving $I_1,I_2$ and $\Gamma_{12}$ fixed. Then

$$
I(\delta)=I_1+I_2+2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\delta}\gamma_{12}).
$$

For $I_1+I_2>0$, visibility is $\mathcal V=(I_{\max}-I_{\min})/(I_{\max}+I_{\min})=2\sqrt{I_1I_2}|\gamma_{12}|/(I_1+I_2)$, interpreting it as zero if one beam is absent.
