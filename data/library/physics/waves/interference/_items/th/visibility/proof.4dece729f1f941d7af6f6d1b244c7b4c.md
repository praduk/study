Expand the squared modulus of $e^{i\delta}U_1+U_2$ and average. The cross term is $2C\operatorname{Re}(e^{i\delta}\Gamma_{12})$, giving the intensity formula. As phase varies, its real part ranges from minus to plus $|\Gamma_{12}|$. Therefore $I_{\max,\min}=I_1+I_2\pm2\sqrt{I_1I_2}|\gamma_{12}|$, and substitution yields visibility. @[Cauchy–Schwarz]math:analysis:hilbert-spaces:th:cauchy-schwarz and $2\sqrt{I_1I_2}\le I_1+I_2$ imply $0\le\mathcal V\le1$. An absent beam has no cross term.
