The output is $E_0(1,i)^T/\sqrt2$. With the convention $e^{-i\omega t}$, the field at $z=0$ is $E_0(\cos\omega t,\sin\omega t)^T/\sqrt2$. It traces a circle: at $t=0$ it points along $+x$ and initially moves toward $+y$. This explicit statement avoids a handedness convention. For axis $a=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta)$, the transmitted complex amplitude is $E_0(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)/\sqrt2$, with squared modulus $E_0^2/2$, independent of $\theta$. Since incident and post-plate squared norms are $E_0^2$, the final polarizer transmits one half of the intensity.
