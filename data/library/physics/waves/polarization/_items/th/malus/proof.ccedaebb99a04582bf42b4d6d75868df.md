Write the incident field as $E_0b$. Projection gives $E_0(a\cdot b)a$, whose squared norm is $|E_0|^2(a\cdot b)^2$. Multiplication by the common factor $C$ gives the fraction. For the unpolarized ensemble, average $I_{\rm out}=C|a^T\mathcal E|^2$ to get $C a^T\langle\mathcal E\mathcal E^*\rangle a=I_{\rm in}/2$, using $a^Ta=1$. The result follows from the specified ensemble, not from assigning an arbitrary single polarization to unpolarized light.
