Expand $|X-x|^2=|X|^2-2X\cdot x+|x|^2$ inside the Fresnel integral. Factoring the first exponential, deleting $e^{ik|x|^2/(2z)}$ leaves exactly the stated Fourier transform. The inequality $|e^{is}-1|\le|s|$ gives absolute error at most $(\lambda z)^{-1}\int|A(x)|k|x|^2/(2z)\,d^2x$, bounded by the displayed expression. Where the leading amplitude is zero this absolute estimate cannot be divided by that amplitude to obtain a relative error.
