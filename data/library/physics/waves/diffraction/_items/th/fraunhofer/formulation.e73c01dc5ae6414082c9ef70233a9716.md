Suppose $A\in L^1(\R^2)$ is supported in $|x|\le a$. Within the Fresnel model define

$$
U_F(X,z)=\frac{e^{ikz+ik|X|^2/(2z)}}{i\lambda z}\widehat A(kX/z),\qquad
\widehat A(\xi)=\int A(x)e^{-i\xi\cdot x}d^2x.
$$

Then $|U-U_F|\le[k a^2/(2\lambda z^2)]\|A\|_1$. The approximation is controlled relative to the overall amplitude scale when $ka^2/(2z)\ll1$, not uniformly relative to intensity at a diffraction zero.
