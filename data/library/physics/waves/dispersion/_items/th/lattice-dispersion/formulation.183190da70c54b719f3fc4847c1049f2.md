For the ideal infinite chain $m\ddot u_n=K(u_{n+1}-2u_n+u_{n-1})$, with $m,K,a>0$ and equilibrium spacing $a$, a normal wave $u_n=Ae^{i(kna-\omega t)}$ obeys

$$
\omega^2=\frac{4K}{m}\sin^2(ka/2).
$$

Wave numbers differing by $2\pi/a$ are identical on the lattice. The long-wavelength speed is $a\sqrt{K/m}$.
