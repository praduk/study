Substitute the ansatz and cancel its nonzero common factor: $-m\omega^2=K(e^{ika}-2+e^{-ika})=-4K\sin^2(ka/2)$. On lattice points, replacing $k$ by $k+2\pi/a$ multiplies the factor by $e^{2\pi in}=1$. For $ka\to0$, $\sin(ka/2)/(ka/2)\to1$, so the positive branch satisfies $\omega/|k|\to a\sqrt{K/m}$. Finite lattice spacing supplies dispersion even though each spring force is linear.
