Taylor's theorem gives $\omega(k_0+\kappa)=\omega(k_0)+\omega'(k_0)\kappa+R(\kappa)$ with $|R|\le M\kappa^2/2$. Extract the carrier exponential from the integral. Replacing $e^{-itR}$ by one produces exactly $\psi_0$. Since $|e^{i\theta}-1|\le|\theta|$ for real $\theta$, the absolute difference is at most $(2\pi)^{-1}\int|a||t|M\kappa^2/2\,d\kappa$, bounded as stated. Thus the shape-translation approximation has an explicit absolute error bound on time scales where $|t|M\delta^2$ is small.
