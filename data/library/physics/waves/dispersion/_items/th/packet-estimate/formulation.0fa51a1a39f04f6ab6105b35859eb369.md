Let $\delta>0$, $M\ge0$, and $a\in L^1(\R)$ be supported in $|\kappa|\le\delta$, and let $\omega$ be real $C^2$ on $[k_0-\delta,k_0+\delta]$ with $|\omega''|\le M$. The packet

$$
\psi(x,t)=\frac1{2\pi}\int a(\kappa)e^{i[(k_0+\kappa)x-\omega(k_0+\kappa)t]}d\kappa
$$

is approximated by $\psi_0=e^{i(k_0x-\omega(k_0)t)}A(x-\omega'(k_0)t)$, where $A(s)=(2\pi)^{-1}\int a(\kappa)e^{i\kappa s}d\kappa$. Uniformly in $x$,

$$
|\psi-\psi_0|\le\frac{|t|M\delta^2}{4\pi}\|a\|_{L^1}.
$$
