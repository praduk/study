On this interval $\omega=2\sqrt{K/m}\sin(ka/2)$, so $v_p=2\sqrt{K/m}\sin(ka/2)/k$ and $v_g=a\sqrt{K/m}\cos(ka/2)$. Both tend to $a\sqrt{K/m}$ at zero. At the upper edge $v_p\to2a\sqrt{K/m}/\pi$, while $v_g\to0$. Phase crests can therefore advance in the mathematical phase description while a packet narrowly concentrated near the band edge has little leading-order envelope drift. Curvature of the dispersion relation can still spread the packet; the group approximation has a finite validity time.
