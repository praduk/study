A packet calculation using real $\omega(k)$ describes an ideal lossless band. Absorption often requires complex frequency or wave number and changes both amplitudes and phases. Then a measured pulse maximum can move because the waveform is reshaped, rather than because a fixed portion of information moved at that speed.

A sharply switched signal is not narrow-band. Its front depends on spectral behavior outside a small Taylor expansion around a carrier. Therefore a group-velocity formula alone cannot establish causality, superluminal communication, or the behavior of a discontinuous front.
