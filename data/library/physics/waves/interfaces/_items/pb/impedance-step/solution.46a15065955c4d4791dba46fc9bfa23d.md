Since $c=\sqrt{T/\mu}$, $c_2/c_1=1/3$ and $Z_2/Z_1=3$. Therefore $r=(1-3)/(1+3)=-1/2$ and $t=2/(1+3)=1/2$. The transmitted power fraction is $3(1/2)^2=3/4$, while the reflected fraction is $1/4$. A negative real reflection coefficient means a phase reversal of $\pi$ relative to the incident displacement phasor at the junction. Squaring the transmitted displacement coefficient alone would incorrectly give one quarter rather than three quarters of the power.
