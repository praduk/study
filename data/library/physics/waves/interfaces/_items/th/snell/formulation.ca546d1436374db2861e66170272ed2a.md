Under the stationary plane optical-interface model, a reflected traveling wave has equal incidence and reflection angles, and a transmitted traveling wave satisfies

$$
n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2.
$$

Angles are measured from the interface normal in the incidence plane. This equation is conditional on a real transmitted traveling wave existing.

Tangential phase matching is equality of the pullbacks of the wave covectors to the interface: $\iota^*k_1=\iota^*k_2$, where $\iota$ is its inclusion. This coordinate-free restriction of one-forms uses @math:diffgeo:forms:df:forms-pullback; the Euclidean norm and constitutive relation $|k_j|=n_j\omega/c_0$ turn it into the familiar sine formula.
