In medium 2 the Helmholtz dispersion relation is $k_{\parallel}^2+k_{2z}^2=(n_2\omega/c_0)^2$. Tangential phase matching gives $k_{\parallel}=(n_1\omega/c_0)\sin\theta_1$. Subtract to obtain the formula. Above the critical angle the normal wave numbers are $\pm i\kappa$; the factor $e^{ik_{2z}z}$ is bounded as $z\to\infty$ only for $+i\kappa$, giving exponential decay. At equality the normal wave number is zero, the grazing threshold. The evanescent field is not a propagating transmitted ray; a second nearby interface can change the boundary problem.
