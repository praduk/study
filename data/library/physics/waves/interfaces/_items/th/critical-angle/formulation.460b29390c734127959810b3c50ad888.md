Suppose $n_1>n_2>0$, $0\le\theta_1<\pi/2$, and medium 2 occupies $z>0$. The transmitted scalar Helmholtz mode with the matched tangential wave number has

$$
k_{2z}^2=(\omega/c_0)^2(n_2^2-n_1^2\sin^2\theta_1).
$$

For $\sin\theta_1>n_2/n_1$ the bounded outgoing-side solution decays as $e^{-\kappa z}$ with $\kappa=(\omega/c_0)\sqrt{n_1^2\sin^2\theta_1-n_2^2}$.
