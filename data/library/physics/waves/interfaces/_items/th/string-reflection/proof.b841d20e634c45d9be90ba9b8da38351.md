Use $y_1=\operatorname{Re}[(e^{ik_1x}+re^{-ik_1x})e^{-i\omega t}]$ and $y_2=\operatorname{Re}[te^{ik_2x}e^{-i\omega t}]$, where $k_j=\omega/c_j$. Matching displacement gives $1+r=t$, and matching force gives $k_1(1-r)=k_2t$. Solving yields the amplitudes, since $Tk_j=\omega Z_j$. A traveling wave with amplitude $A$ carries mean power magnitude $\omega^2Z_j|A|^2/2$ by the string energy formula. This gives the fractions. Finally $\mathcal R+\mathcal T=[(Z_1-Z_2)^2+4Z_1Z_2]/(Z_1+Z_2)^2=1$.
