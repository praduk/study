For the massless equal-tension string junction, let a harmonic displacement wave of amplitude one arrive from medium 1. Define $c_j=\sqrt{T/\mu_j}$ and $Z_j=T/c_j>0$. Reflected and transmitted displacement amplitudes are

$$
r=\frac{Z_1-Z_2}{Z_1+Z_2},\qquad t=\frac{2Z_1}{Z_1+Z_2}.
$$

The reflected and transmitted power fractions are $\mathcal R=|r|^2$ and $\mathcal T=(Z_2/Z_1)|t|^2$, with $\mathcal R+\mathcal T=1$.
