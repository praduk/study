Fourier transformation gives $|\nabla\phi|^2$ a quadratic weight $k^2$ and $(\Delta\phi)^2$ weight $k^4$. Their ratio is $k^2/M^2$ for $k\ne0$. Thus the derivative expansion is controlled by $k/M\ll1$. This Euclidean estimate is explicit: it does not justify extending the truncated higher-derivative equation to arbitrarily high real-time frequencies, where additional spurious modes can appear.
