The symbol $\widehat\phi(x)$ in continuum QFT is interpreted through smeared fields $\widehat\phi(f)$ for test functions $f$, on a specified common dense domain. Correlation functions such as $\langle\Omega,\widehat\phi(f)\widehat\phi(g)\Omega\rangle$ are smeared distributions.

One cannot infer that $\widehat\phi(x)^2$ or an infinite vacuum-energy sum is an ordinary finite quantity. Composite fields require a prescription. Finite box size, a short-distance regulator, and renormalized observables solve different parts of the problem.
