The canonical commutators give $[a_j,a_k^\dagger]=\delta_{jk}$. Direct multiplication gives $a_j^\dagger a_j=(\omega_jq_j^2+p_j^2/\omega_j-1)/2$, proving the operator identity on the common domain. By definition of the oscillator Fock representation, $a_j^\dagger a_j$ acts by $n_j$ on its normalized occupation basis. This yields the eigenvalues; the basis spans that representation, so there are no missing basis states there.
