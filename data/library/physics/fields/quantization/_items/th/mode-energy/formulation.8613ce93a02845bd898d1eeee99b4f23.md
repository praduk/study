In the oscillator Fock representation of @physics:fields:quantization:ax:canonical-cutoff, define $a_j=(\sqrt{\omega_j}q_j+ip_j/\sqrt{\omega_j})/\sqrt2$. Then

$$
H=\sum_{j=1}^M\omega_j(a_j^\dagger a_j+\tfrac12),\qquad E_{n_1,\ldots,n_M}=\sum_{j=1}^M\omega_j(n_j+\tfrac12).
$$

In SI each term is $\hbar\omega_j(n_j+1/2)$.
