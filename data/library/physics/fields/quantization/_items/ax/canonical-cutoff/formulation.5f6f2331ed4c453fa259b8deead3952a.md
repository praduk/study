After putting a free field in a periodic finite box and retaining finitely many independent real normal coordinates, postulate one quantum oscillator per coordinate. For frequencies $\omega_j>0$, use $[q_j,p_k]=i\delta_{jk}$, commuting distinct coordinates, and

$$
H=\frac12\sum_{j=1}^M(p_j^2+\omega_j^2q_j^2).
$$

These relations hold on the common oscillator domain. They are quantization assumptions for the regulated model. A massless zero-frequency mode requires separate treatment and is excluded here. Removing the cutoff is a further limit, especially delicate with interactions.
