An occupation-number basis is $|2,0\rangle$, $|1,1\rangle$, and $|0,2\rangle$. Their energies are $\hbar\omega(2+1/2+1/2)=3\hbar\omega$. The two-mode vacuum energy is $\hbar\omega$, so subtracting that constant gives excitation energy $2\hbar\omega$. Their linear span is the whole two-quantum eigenspace, so normalized superpositions have the same energy. The degeneracy is three; counting labeled particles would overcount bosonic basis states. The finite constant shift leaves energy differences unchanged in this fixed nongravitational model.
