The derivatives are $\mathcal L_{\dot\phi}=\dot\phi$, $\mathcal L_{\partial_i\phi}=-\partial_i\phi$, and $\mathcal L_\phi=-m^2\phi-\lambda\phi^3/6$. Thus $\ddot\phi-\Delta\phi+m^2\phi+\lambda\phi^3/6=0$. The momentum is $\pi=\dot\phi$ and $\mathcal H=\pi^2/2+|\nabla\phi|^2/2+m^2\phi^2/2+\lambda\phi^4/24\ge0$. This algebra establishes positivity of the classical density; it is not a global existence theorem for arbitrary data or a quantum construction.
