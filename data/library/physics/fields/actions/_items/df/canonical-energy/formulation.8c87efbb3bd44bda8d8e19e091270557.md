Relative to a chosen time coordinate, define $\pi_a=\partial\mathcal L/\partial\dot\phi^a$. If the map from velocities to momenta is invertible, its Legendre transform is $\mathcal H=\pi_a\dot\phi^a-\mathcal L$, with velocities expressed in terms of fields and momenta; $H=\int\mathcal H\,d^3x$.

Degeneracy of this map signals constraints. Maxwell potentials provide such a case: one must impose the constraint and handle gauge freedom, not invert a singular matrix. Positivity of energy also depends on the chosen Lagrangian and boundary conditions.
