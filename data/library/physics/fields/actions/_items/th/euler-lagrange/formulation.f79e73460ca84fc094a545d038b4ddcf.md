For a $C^2$ density and a $C^2$ field with a defined first variation, stationarity under all compactly supported smooth variations is equivalent to

$$
\partial_\mu\left(\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi^a)}\right)-\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial\phi^a}=0
$$

for every component $a$.
