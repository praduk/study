For a variation $\phi+\epsilon h$, differentiate the integrand at $\epsilon=0$ to obtain $\delta S=\int(\mathcal L_{\phi^a}h^a+\mathcal L_{\partial_\mu\phi^a}\partial_\mu h^a)\,d^4x$. Integrate the second term by parts; compact support removes the boundary term. The coefficient of each arbitrary $h^a$ vanishes by the fundamental lemma of the calculus of variations. Conversely these equations make the integrated first variation zero.
