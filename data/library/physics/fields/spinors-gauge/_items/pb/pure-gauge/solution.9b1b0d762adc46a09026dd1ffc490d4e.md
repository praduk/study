Differentiation gives $dA=d(g^{-1})\wedge dg=-g^{-1}dg\wedge g^{-1}dg=-A\wedge A$, so $F=0$. The global converse can fail because of holonomy. For example on a circle with angular coordinate modulo $2\pi$, the globally defined $U(1)$ connection $A=ia\,d\theta$ is flat, but its parallel transport around the circle is $e^{-2\pi ia}$. For $a\notin\Z$ this differs from one, so no single-valued gauge transformation trivializes it. The local curvature and global transport encode different information.
