For constant Dirac matrices satisfying @physics:fields:spinors-gauge:df:dirac-hamiltonian, on smooth @[compactly supported]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity spinors,

$$
H_D^2=(-\hbar^2c^2\Delta+m^2c^4)I.
$$

A sufficiently smooth solution of $i\hbar\partial_t\psi=H_D\psi$ therefore satisfies the Klein-Gordon equation componentwise. The converse is not asserted.
