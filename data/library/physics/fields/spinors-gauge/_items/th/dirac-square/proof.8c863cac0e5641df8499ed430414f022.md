In the square of the derivative term, commuting spatial derivatives replace $\alpha_i\alpha_j$ by half its anticommutator, giving $-\hbar^2c^2\Delta I$. The derivative-mass cross terms vanish by $\{\alpha_i,\beta\}=0$, and the mass square is $m^2c^4I$. Applying $i\hbar\partial_t$ a second time yields $-\hbar^2\partial_t^2\psi=H_D^2\psi$. Rearrangement gives $(c^{-2}\partial_t^2-\Delta+m^2c^2/\hbar^2)\psi=0$.
