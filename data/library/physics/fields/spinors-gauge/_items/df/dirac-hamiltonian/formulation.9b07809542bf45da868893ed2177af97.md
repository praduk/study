Choose Hermitian matrices $\alpha_1,\alpha_2,\alpha_3,\beta$ with $\{\alpha_i,\alpha_j\}=2\delta_{ij}I$, $\{\alpha_i,\beta\}=0$, and $\beta^2=I$. Here $\{A,B\}=AB+BA$. The free Dirac Hamiltonian in SI is

$$
H_D=-i\hbar c\sum_{i=1}^3\alpha_i\partial_i+\beta mc^2.
$$

It acts on spinor-valued wavefunctions; a domain such as $H^1(\R^3,\C^4)$ specifies the free self-adjoint realization for the standard four-component matrices. Spinor fields in QFT are subsequently quantized with fermionic statistics. The Hamiltonian formula fixes conventions without requiring a competing spacetime gamma-matrix signature.
