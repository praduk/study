A geometric field on spacetime $M$ is a section $\phi$ of a specified bundle $E\to M$: at each $x$ it chooses an allowed value in the fiber $E_x$. Scalar fields are sections of a trivial line bundle; a vector field is a section of $TM$; electromagnetic field strength is a section of $\Lambda^2T^*M$. A connection is additional differentiation data, as in @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:bundle-connection, rather than a globally defined matrix-valued one-form in every bundle.

On an oriented $n$-manifold a Lagrangian is an $n$-form depending on the fields and the specified derivatives, so $S[\phi]=\int_M\mathcal L$. On a metric background it is often $\mathcal L=L(\phi,\nabla\phi,g)\operatorname{vol}_g$. A coordinate expression $L_{\rm density}\,d^nx$ must transform as this same top form. The first-derivative model in @physics:fields:actions:df:local-action is its coordinate description. @[Compact support]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity, boundary data, and the admissible space of sections remain part of the variational problem.
