On an oriented pseudo-Riemannian manifold of dimension $n\ge2$, let $A$ be a connection for a matrix Lie group $G$ with a fixed symmetric nondegenerate Lie-algebra pairing $B$ invariant under the whole group: $B(\operatorname{Ad}_g a,\operatorname{Ad}_g b)=B(a,b)$ for every $g\in G$, where $\operatorname{Ad}_g a=gag^{-1}$. Differentiation gives $B([a,b],c)+B(b,[a,c])=0$. This infinitesimal identity alone would not guarantee invariance under disconnected components of $G$. Extend $B$ to algebra-valued forms by wedging their form factors. Let $F=dA+A\w A$ locally and fix $g$. For a nonzero constant $\kappa$, the action

$$S[A]=-\frac1{2\kappa}\int_M B(F\w\star F)$$

has Euler-Lagrange equation $d_A\star F=0$ for arbitrary smooth @[compactly supported]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:partition-unity connection variations. Independently, every connection obeys $d_AF=0$. The statement assumes a smooth classical field and existing integrals, or a compact region with interior-supported variations.
