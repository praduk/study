For a local action, a vertical infinitesimal variation $\delta\phi^a=Q^a$ changes field values while keeping spacetime coordinates fixed. It is a variational symmetry when the induced variation of the density is a divergence, $\delta\mathcal L=\partial_\mu B^\mu$, identically before imposing the equations of motion.

The parameter is constant for a global internal symmetry. Allowing it to depend on spacetime generally changes the problem and can require a gauge field. A symmetry of the equations need not automatically be a symmetry of a chosen action.
