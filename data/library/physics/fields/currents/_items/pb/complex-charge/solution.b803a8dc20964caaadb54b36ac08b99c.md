The density is invariant, so $B^\mu=0$. Differentiating in the complex coordinates (equivalently combining the two real derivatives) gives

$$
j^\mu=i(\phi^*\partial^\mu\phi-\phi\partial^\mu\phi^*).
$$

The field equations are $(\Box-m^2)\phi=0$ and its conjugate. Consequently $\partial_\mu j^\mu=i(\phi^*\Box\phi-\phi\Box\phi^*)=0$. The overall charge sign follows the chosen phase generator; replacing the generator by its negative reverses it. This current becomes electric current only after specifying charge normalization and electromagnetic coupling.
