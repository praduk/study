For the smooth fields and density of @physics:fields:actions:th:euler-lagrange, suppose $\delta\phi^a=Q^a$ is a variation with $\delta\mathcal L=\partial_\mu B^\mu$. Then

$$
j^\mu=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi^a)}Q^a-B^\mu
$$

satisfies $\partial_\mu j^\mu=0$ on every solution.

The conserved vector density can be represented by a flux three-form. In a volume convention, @physics:foundations:geometric-models:th:conserved-current-form identifies zero divergence with closure, and Stokes gives charge conservation with its necessary boundary condition. This is the coordinate-independent content of the on-shell current equation.
