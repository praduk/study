If a $C^2$ first-derivative density has no explicit dependence on $x$, define

$$
T^\mu{}_{\nu}=\frac{\partial\mathcal L}{\partial(\partial_\mu\phi^a)}\partial_\nu\phi^a-\delta^\mu{}_{\nu}\mathcal L.
$$

Every smooth solution obeys $\partial_\mu T^\mu{}_{\nu}=0$. This canonical current need not be symmetric or gauge invariant without an improvement.
