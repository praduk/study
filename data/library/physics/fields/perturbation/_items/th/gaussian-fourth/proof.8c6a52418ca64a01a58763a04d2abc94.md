The moment generating function is $M(t)=\exp(t^TCt/2)$, including for singular $C$ by representing $X$ as a linear image of a standard Gaussian. Four derivatives at $t=0$ select only the quadratic term in the exponential series of the quadratic form, namely $(t^TCt)^2/8$. Assigning the four derivative labels to the two paired factors gives exactly the three pairings shown. Gaussian moments are finite, so differentiation under expectation is justified near zero.
