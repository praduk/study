In the interaction picture the formal evolution is $U_I(t,t_0)=\mathcal T\exp[-i\int_{t_0}^tH_I(s)\,ds]$ in natural units. Its first terms are $I-i\int H_I(s)ds-\int_{t_0}^t ds\int_{t_0}^sdr\,H_I(s)H_I(r)$. Later times multiply on the left.

For bounded continuous operators on a finite time interval, the iterated-integral series is norm-convergent by an exponential majorant. Interacting field Hamiltonians are generally unbounded and singular; the formal expression does not supply that convergence proof. Perturbative calculations must state the regulator and the meaning of their expansion.
