When suitable incoming and outgoing asymptotic states exist, the scattering operator $S$ maps incoming state data to outgoing data. A matrix element $\langle f|S|i\rangle$ is an amplitude; probabilities require the Born rule, state normalization, and any phase-space or flux factors relevant to the measured rate or cross section.

The existence of an $S$ matrix is an assumption needing analysis, especially with long-range forces and massless particles. A Feynman diagram organizes terms in a calculation; it is not an observed spacetime trajectory of a virtual particle.
