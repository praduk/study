For a linear differential operator $P$, a Green kernel $G(x,y)$ satisfies $P_xG(x,y)=\delta(x-y)$ distributionally with additional specified conditions. A retarded kernel has support only when $x$ lies in or on the causal future of $y$; it describes response to a source with no earlier response.

An advanced kernel uses the past instead. A Feynman two-point function implements a quantum time-ordering prescription and is generally not the retarded response kernel. An inverse symbol in Fourier space does not determine which of these objects is intended.
