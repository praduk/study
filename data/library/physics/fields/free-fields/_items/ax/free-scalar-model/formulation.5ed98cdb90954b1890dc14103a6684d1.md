In flat spacetime and natural units, the classical free scalar model has real field $\phi$, mass parameter $m\ge0$, and density $\mathcal L=-\tfrac12\partial_\mu\phi\partial^\mu\phi-\tfrac12m^2\phi^2$. Its equation is $(\Box-m^2)\phi=0$, or $\ddot\phi-\Delta\phi+m^2\phi=0$.

A quantum version describes free spin-zero quanta after quantization. Applying this model to a real excitation requires a stated physical identification; not every scalar wave in a material has this relativistic dispersion. In SI the mass term in the wave operator is $(mc/\hbar)^2$ when $x^0=ct$.
