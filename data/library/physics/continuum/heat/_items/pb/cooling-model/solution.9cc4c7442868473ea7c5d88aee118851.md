Energy balance gives $mc_h\dot T=-hA(T-T_\infty)$. Thus $T=T_\infty+(T_0-T_\infty)e^{-t/\tau}$ with $\tau=mc_h/(hA)$. For $T_0\ne T_\infty$ the deviation halves at $\tau\log2$; if the initial deviation is zero it remains zero. Here $[h]=\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}$. The uniform-temperature approximation requires internal conduction to be fast compared with external heat exchange. A small ratio $hL/k$ for a representative internal length $L$ motivates that approximation; it is not supplied by solving this ODE.
