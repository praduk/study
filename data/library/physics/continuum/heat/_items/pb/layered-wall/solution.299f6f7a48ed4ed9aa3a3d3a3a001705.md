Steady balance makes heat flux $q$ constant. In each layer $dT/dx=-q/k_i$, so the temperature drops are $qL_i/k_i$. Adding gives $q=(T_h-T_c)/(L_1/k_1+L_2/k_2)$ and heat rate $\dot Q=Aq$. The interface temperature is $T_h-qL_1/k_1=T_c+qL_2/k_2$, which lies between the outer temperatures. The total resistance is $R_{\rm th}=L_1/(k_1A)+L_2/(k_2A)$ in $\mathrm{K\,W^{-1}}$. Perfect contact imposes continuity of both temperature and normal heat flux; contact resistance would change the temperature matching.
