Let $\theta$ be a smooth solution of $\theta_t=\alpha\Delta\theta$ on a fixed bounded smooth region, with $\alpha>0$ and either $\theta=0$ or $\partial_n\theta=0$ on the boundary. Then

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\frac12\int_B\theta^2dx=-\alpha\int_B|\nabla\theta|^2dx\le0.
$$

For insulated boundaries, the mean temperature is conserved. The estimate also proves uniqueness among smooth solutions with the same initial and boundary data.
