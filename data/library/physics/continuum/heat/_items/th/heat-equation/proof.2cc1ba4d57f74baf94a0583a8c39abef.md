Insert $q=-k\nabla T$ into balance and use constant $k$ to get $-\operatorname{div}q=k\Delta T$. Divide by $\rho c_h$. For scaling, write $x=Lx'$, $t=\tau t'$, $T=T_*+\Delta T\,\theta$. The coefficients of $\theta_{t'}$ and $\Delta'\theta$ match when $1/\tau=\alpha/L^2$. This identifies a time scale, not a universal numerical decay time for every geometry. The dimensions of $\alpha$ are $\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$.
