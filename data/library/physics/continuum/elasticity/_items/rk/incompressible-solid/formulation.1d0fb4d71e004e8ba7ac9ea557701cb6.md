Sending the bulk modulus $K$ large penalizes $\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon=\operatorname{div}u$. The ideal incompressible linear solid instead imposes $\operatorname{div}u=0$ exactly and introduces an undetermined pressure in $\sigma=-p\mathbf1+2\mu\varepsilon$.

This pressure enforces the constraint and is found with the displacement from balance and boundary data. Substituting an infinite value of $\lambda$ into a finite matrix formula is not a meaningful calculation. Incompressibility in a solid does not eliminate resistance to shear: the shear modulus $\mu$ remains positive.
