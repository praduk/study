For the homogeneous linear elastic equation without body force, a plane wave $u=\operatorname{Re}(a e^{i(k\cdot x-\omega t)})$, $k\ne0$, has longitudinal and transverse branches

$$
c_L^2=\frac{\lambda+2\mu}{\rho_0},\qquad c_T^2=\frac\mu{\rho_0},\qquad \omega^2=c_{L,T}^2|k|^2.
$$

The longitudinal polarization has $a$ parallel to $k$; the two transverse polarizations have $a\cdot k=0$. Positive elastic energy makes both squared speeds positive.
