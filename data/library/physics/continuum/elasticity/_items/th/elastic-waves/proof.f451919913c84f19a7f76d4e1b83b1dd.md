Substitute the ansatz: $\rho_0\omega^2a=\mu|k|^2a+(\lambda+\mu)k(k\cdot a)$. Decompose $a$ orthogonally into a component parallel to $k$ and a perpendicular component. The first component is multiplied by $(\lambda+2\mu)|k|^2$, while the second is multiplied by $\mu|k|^2$. This gives the two branches. From $K=\lambda+2\mu/3>0$ one has $\lambda+2\mu=K+4\mu/3>0$. Dividing Pa by density gives speed-squared units.
