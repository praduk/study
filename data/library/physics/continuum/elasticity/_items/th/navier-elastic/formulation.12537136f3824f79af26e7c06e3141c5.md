In the linearized model with constant reference density $\rho_0>0$ and constant Lamé moduli, momentum balance becomes

$$
\rho_0u_{tt}=\mu\Delta u+(\lambda+\mu)\nabla(\operatorname{div}u)+\rho_0 b.
$$

Spatial and reference derivatives are identified only to the retained first order. This is an equation within small-displacement elasticity.
