In components, $\sigma_{ij}=\lambda\partial_ku_k\delta_{ij}+\mu(\partial_ju_i+\partial_iu_j)$. Taking $\partial_j$ gives $\mu\Delta u_i+(\lambda+\mu)\partial_i\partial_ku_k$. Linearized acceleration is $u_{tt}$ and mass density is the constant reference value to this order. Insert these in local momentum balance to obtain the equation. Every force-density term has units $\mathrm{N\,m^{-3}}$.
