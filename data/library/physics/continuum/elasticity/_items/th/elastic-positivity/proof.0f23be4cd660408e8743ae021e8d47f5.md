Decompose $\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0+(\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon)\mathbf1/3$ with trace-free $\varepsilon_0$. Orthogonality in the matrix inner product gives $\varepsilon:\varepsilon=\varepsilon_0:\varepsilon_0+(\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon)^2/3$. Hence $W=\mu\varepsilon_0:\varepsilon_0+(K/2)(\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon)^2$. If both coefficients are positive, only zero strain has zero energy. Conversely choose a nonzero trace-free strain to force $\mu>0$, and a nonzero multiple of the identity to force $K>0$.
