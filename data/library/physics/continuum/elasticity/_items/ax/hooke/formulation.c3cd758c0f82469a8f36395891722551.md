For small displacement gradients about an unstressed reference body, isotropic linear elasticity assumes

$$
\sigma=\lambda(\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon)\mathbf1+2\mu\varepsilon,\qquad \varepsilon=\tfrac12(\nabla u+(\nabla u)^T).
$$

The Lamé moduli $\lambda,\mu$ have units Pa. Here $\mu$ is a shear modulus, not a fluid viscosity. The associated stored energy per reference volume is $W=\lambda(\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon)^2/2+\mu\varepsilon:\varepsilon$. Linear strain, isotropy, and reversible response are distinct assumptions. Plastic flow, damage, and finite rotations require additional treatment.
