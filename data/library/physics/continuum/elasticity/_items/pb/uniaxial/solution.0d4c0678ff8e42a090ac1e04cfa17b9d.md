Taking the trace of Hooke's law gives $\operatorname{tr}\varepsilon=s/(3\lambda+2\mu)$. Therefore $\varepsilon_{11}=[s-\lambda s/(3\lambda+2\mu)]/(2\mu)=s(\lambda+\mu)/[\mu(3\lambda+2\mu)]$ and $\varepsilon_{22}=\varepsilon_{33}=-\lambda s/[2\mu(3\lambda+2\mu)]$. Hence $Y=\mu(3\lambda+2\mu)/(\lambda+\mu)$ and $\nu_P=\lambda/[2(\lambda+\mu)]$. Positivity implies $\lambda+\mu>0$, so these expressions are well-defined, with $Y>0$ and $-1<\nu_P<1/2$. Off-diagonal strains are zero because the corresponding stresses are $2\mu\varepsilon_{ij}$.
