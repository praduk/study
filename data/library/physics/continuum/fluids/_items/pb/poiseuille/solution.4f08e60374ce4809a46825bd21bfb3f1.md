The axial equation reduces to $0=G+\eta r^{-1}(ru')'$. Integrate to get $ru'=-Gr^2/(2\eta)+C$. A nonzero $C$ would produce a logarithmic singularity, so $C=0$. A second integration and $u(a)=0$ give $u=G(a^2-r^2)/(4\eta)$. Then $Q=2\pi\int_0^a u(r)rdr=\pi Ga^4/(8\eta)$ in $\mathrm{m^3\,s^{-1}}$. Substitution verifies the ODE and regularity. The pressure drop supplies power per pipe length $GQ$, balanced by viscous dissipation in this steady model. The derivation does not establish stability of the profile at every Reynolds number.
