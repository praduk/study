The momentum equation gives $\eta u''=0$. No slip imposes $u(0)=0$, $u(h)=U$, so $u=Uy/h$. The shear stress is $\sigma_{xy}=\eta\partial_yv_x=\eta U/h$. The force that the top plate exerts on the fluid is in the motion direction, so supplied power per area is $\eta U^2/h$. Directly, the dissipation per volume is $2\eta D:D=\eta(U/h)^2$ because the two shear components of $D$ each equal $U/(2h)$. Integrating over the gap gives the same power per area.
