For a smooth steady inviscid flow with constant $\rho>0$ and body force $b=-\nabla\Phi$, the quantity $B=|v|^2/2+p/\rho+\Phi$ is constant along each streamline. If additionally $\nabla\times v=0$ on a connected region, $B$ is constant throughout that region. The terms have units $\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-2}}$.

Let $u=v^\flat$ and $\Omega=du$. For the steady constant-density assumptions here, the one-form Euler equation is $\iota_v\Omega+dB=0$, using the identity proved in @physics:continuum:geometry:th:kelvin-circulation. Evaluation on $v$ gives $v(B)=0$ by antisymmetry; if $\Omega=0$, it gives $dB=0$. Thus streamline and global Bernoulli statements follow from two different contractions of the same intrinsic equation. Kelvin circulation also describes the time-dependent barotropic case.
