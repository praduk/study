Divide the steady Euler equation by $\rho$ and dot with $v$. Since $v\cdot(v\cdot\nabla)v=v\cdot\nabla(|v|^2/2)$, the result is $v\cdot\nabla B=0$. Along a streamline $dx/ds=v(x)$ this is $dB/ds=0$. The vector identity $(v\cdot\nabla)v=\nabla(|v|^2/2)-v\times(\nabla\times v)$ makes the full equation $\nabla B=v\times(\nabla\times v)$. If vorticity is zero, $\nabla B=0$, and connectedness implies a single constant. Without that extra condition different streamlines can have different constants.
