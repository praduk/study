Dot $\rho D_tv=\operatorname{div}\sigma$ with $v$ and integrate. The convective integral is $\int_B\operatorname{div}(\rho|v|^2v/2)dx=0$, using incompressibility and the boundary condition. Integration by parts gives $\int_Bv\cdot\operatorname{div}\sigma=-\int_B\sigma:\nabla v$, since boundary velocity is zero. Pressure contributes $p\operatorname{div}v=0$. Write $\nabla v=D+W$ with $W$ skew. Symmetry gives $D:W=0$, so the viscous term is $-2\eta\int_BD:D$. This proves both equality and sign.
