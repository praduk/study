Let a smooth incompressible solution occupy a fixed bounded region with $v=0$ on its boundary, no body force, and constants $\rho>0$, $\eta\ge0$. Then

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\int_B\frac\rho2|v|^2dx=-2\eta\int_B D:D\,dx\le0.
$$

This is mechanical kinetic-energy loss, not loss of total energy including heat.
