The pressure term has divergence $-\nabla p$. In components, $\partial_j(2\eta D_{ij})=\eta\partial_j(\partial_jv_i+\partial_iv_j)=\eta\Delta v_i+\eta\partial_i\operatorname{div}v$. Constancy of $\eta$ permits taking it outside derivatives, and incompressibility removes the second term and the second-viscosity stress. Insert this divergence into local momentum balance. Dividing units $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-1}\,s^{-1}}$ by $\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}$ gives $[\nu]=\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$.
