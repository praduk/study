For constant density $\rho>0$, constant shear viscosity $\eta\ge0$, incompressibility, and the Newtonian stress closure, momentum balance becomes

$$
\rho(\partial_tv+(v\cdot\nabla)v)=-\nabla p+\eta\Delta v+\rho b,\qquad \operatorname{div}v=0.
$$

This derivation does not assert existence or regularity for arbitrary initial data. The kinematic viscosity $\nu=\eta/\rho$ has units $\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}$.
