The divergence is $-a/2-a/2+a=0$, and the curl is $\zeta=(0,0,2b(t))$. Let $r_\perp^2=x^2+y^2$. Direct differentiation gives

$$
\partial_tv+(v\cdot\nabla)v
=\bigl((a^2/4-b^2)x,\ (a^2/4-b^2)y,\ a^2z\bigr).
$$

The rotational cross terms cancel because $\dot b=ab$. Thus $\rho_0D_tv=-\nabla p$ holds for

$$
p=-\frac{\rho_0}{2}\bigl((a^2/4-b^2)r_\perp^2+a^2z^2\bigr)+p_0(t),
$$

with arbitrary spatially constant $p_0(t)$. Each axial material line stretches by $e^{at}$, because $\dot z=az$, while transverse area shrinks by $e^{-at}$. Hence $D\Phi_t e_z=e^{at}e_z$ and $\zeta_t=2b_0e^{at}e_z=D\Phi_t\zeta_0$. This is the Cauchy formula at constant density. For $a>0$ the vorticity magnitude grows even though its two-form is advected: the shrinking transverse area preserves flux.
