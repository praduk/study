On $\R^3$, fix constants $a$ and $b_0$ with units $\mathrm{s^{-1}}$, and define

$$
v(x,y,z,t)=\left(-\tfrac a2x-b(t)y,\ b(t)x-\tfrac a2y,\ az\right),
\qquad b(t)=b_0e^{at}.
$$

Take constant density $\rho_0>0$ and no body force. Verify that this is a smooth incompressible Euler flow with a suitable pressure. Compute the vorticity and check @physics:continuum:geometry:th:cauchy-vorticity. This is a local model on bounded regions of space; no finite total energy on all of $\R^3$ is assumed.
