Contraction with a nowhere-zero volume form is an isomorphism from tangent vectors to two-covectors in three dimensions: this is immediate on a basis, so $b$ exists uniquely. For any @[diffeomorphism]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map $\Phi$ and vector field $b$, direct evaluation on tangent vectors gives

$$
\Phi^*(\iota_bm)=\iota_{\Phi^*b}(\Phi^*m),
\qquad (\Phi^*b)(X)=D\Phi(X)^{-1}b(\Phi(X)).
$$

Apply this to the two advected forms. It yields $\iota_{\Phi_t^*b_t}m_0=\Omega_0=\iota_{b_0}m_0$, hence $\Phi_t^*b_t=b_0$ by injectivity of contraction. This is the claimed formula. Differentiating $b_t(\Phi_t(X))=D\Phi_t(X)b_0(X)$ gives $\partial_tb+Db\,v=Dv\,b$, which is the stated bracket equation. The Euclidean specialization uses $\Omega=\iota_\zeta\mu$ and $m=\rho\mu$.
