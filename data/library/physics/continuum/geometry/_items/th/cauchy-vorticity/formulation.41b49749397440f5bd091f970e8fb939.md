In three dimensions, assume both the positive mass form $m=\rho\mu$ and the vorticity two-form $\Omega$ are advected by the same orientation-preserving flow as in @physics:continuum:geometry:df:material-forms. Define the vector field $b$ uniquely by $\iota_bm=\Omega$. Then

$$
b_t(\Phi_t(X))=D\Phi_t(X)b_0(X).
$$

In Euclidean coordinates, if $\zeta=\nabla\times v$, this is $\zeta_t/\rho_t=D\Phi_t(\zeta_0/\rho_0)$ along particles. The vector equation is $\partial_tb+[v,b]=0$, where $[v,b]=Db\,v-Dv\,b$.
