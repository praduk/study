Insert $F=\mathbf1+\nabla u$ into $F^TF$ and expand. The squared stretch along $n$ is $1+2n^T\varepsilon n+|\nabla u\,n|^2$. Taylor expansion of the square root at one gives the length claim, uniformly over unit $n$ in finite dimensions. Expanding the determinant polynomial around the identity, its linear term is $\operatorname{tr}(\nabla u)=\operatorname{div}u$ and every remaining term has degree at least two. Thus small strain is justified when the displacement gradient, including rotations in this approximation, is small.
