The chain rule along $X$ gives the first equation, and applying it componentwise to $v$ gives acceleration. Differentiate $\partial_t\chi=v(\chi,t)$ with respect to $X$ to get $\dot F=(\nabla v\circ\chi)F$. Jacobi's determinant formula gives $\dot J=J\operatorname{tr}(F^{-1}\dot F)=J\operatorname{tr}(\nabla v\circ\chi)$, using cyclic invariance of trace. This is $J\operatorname{div}v\circ\chi$.
