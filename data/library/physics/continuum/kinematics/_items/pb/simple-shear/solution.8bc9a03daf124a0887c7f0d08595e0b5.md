The deformation gradient is $F=\begin{pmatrix}1&\gamma&0\\0&1&0\\0&0&1\end{pmatrix}$, so $J=1$. Then $E=\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}0&\gamma&0\\\gamma&\gamma^2&0\\0&0&0\end{pmatrix}$, while $\varepsilon$ has the same off-diagonal entries and zero diagonal. The image of $e_2$ is $(\gamma,1,0)$, with stretch $\sqrt{1+\gamma^2}$. Its length change begins at order $\gamma^2/2$, which linear strain omits. Exact volume is preserved even though material lines change length and angle.
