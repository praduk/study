A smooth motion is a family of orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms $x=\chi(X,t)$ from a reference body $B_0$ to its current region $B_t$. The label $X$ follows a material particle. Define $F=\partial\chi/\partial X$, $J=\det F>0$, and the spatial velocity by $v(\chi(X,t),t)=\partial_t\chi(X,t)$.

$F$ and $J$ are dimensionless when both coordinate systems use metres. $J$ is the local ratio of current to reference volume. A material description fixes $X$; a spatial description fixes $x$. The same derivative symbol must not silently alternate between them.
