A smooth motion is a family of orientation-preserving @[diffeomorphisms]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map $x=\chi(X,t)$ from a reference body $B_0$ to its current region $B_t$. The label $X$ follows a material particle. Define $F=\partial\chi/\partial X$, $J=\det F>0$, and the spatial velocity by $v(\chi(X,t),t)=\partial_t\chi(X,t)$.

$F$ and $J$ are dimensionless when both coordinate systems use metres. $J$ is the local ratio of current to reference volume. A material description fixes $X$; a spatial description fixes $x$. The same derivative symbol must not silently alternate between them.

The coordinate-free operation taking a spatial field back to particle labels is pullback by $\chi_t$, as in @math:diffgeo:forms:df:forms-pullback. Mass and vorticity require different tensor degrees; @physics:continuum:geometry:df:material-forms distinguishes their transport laws. The Jacobian $J$ is the coordinate expression of $\chi_t^*\mu=J\mu_0$ when the reference and spatial volume forms are fixed.
