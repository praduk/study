Dot momentum with $v$ to obtain $\partial_t(\rho_0|v|^2/2)=-v\cdot\nabla p'$. Multiply pressure continuity by $p'/(\rho_0c^2)$ to obtain $\partial_t[p'^2/(2\rho_0c^2)]=-p'\operatorname{div}v$. Add and use the divergence product rule. If $p'=f(x-ct)$ with no stationary velocity component, momentum is satisfied by $v_x=f(x-ct)/(\rho_0c)$; direct substitution also verifies continuity. Then $j_x=p'^2/Z\ge0$. The flux has units $\mathrm{Pa\,m\,s^{-1}}=\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$.
