The pressure-density relation changes continuity into $\partial_tp'=-\rho_0c^2\operatorname{div}v$. Differentiate once in time, use $\partial_tv=-\nabla p'/\rho_0$, and commute smooth derivatives to get the wave equation. Taking curl of momentum gives $\partial_t(\nabla\times v)=0$. Thus a time-independent solenoidal velocity perturbation can coexist with the acoustic pressure solution; the pressure wave equation alone is not the entire vector initial-value problem.
