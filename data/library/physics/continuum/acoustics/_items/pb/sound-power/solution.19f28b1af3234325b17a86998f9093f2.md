The impedance is $Z=\rho_0c=408\,\mathrm{Pa\,s\,m^{-1}}$. The velocity amplitude is $P/Z=1/204\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\approx0.00490\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}$. Since $j_x=p'^2/Z$ and $\langle\cos^2\rangle=1/2$, the mean intensity is $P^2/(2Z)=1/204\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}\approx0.00490\,\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}}$. The coincident numerical values have different units. Doubling pressure amplitude multiplies intensity by four within the linear acoustic model.
