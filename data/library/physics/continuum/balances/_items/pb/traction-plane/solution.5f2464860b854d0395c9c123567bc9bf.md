Multiplication gives $t=\sigma n=(4,2,0)/\sqrt2\,\mathrm{MPa}$. Its normal component is $\sigma_n=n\cdot t=3\,\mathrm{MPa}$, tensile under the chosen convention. Subtract $\sigma_n n$ to get $t_{\parallel}=(1,-1,0)/\sqrt2\,\mathrm{MPa}$ with magnitude $1\,\mathrm{MPa}$. The check $|t|^2=10\,\mathrm{MPa}^2=\sigma_n^2+|t_{\parallel}|^2$ confirms orthogonal decomposition. The shear traction is a vector in the plane, not an additional normal force.
