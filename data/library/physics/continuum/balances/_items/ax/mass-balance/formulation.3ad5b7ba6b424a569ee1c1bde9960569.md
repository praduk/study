For a material with no mass sources or sinks, postulate that every material subregion $B_t=\chi(B_0,t)$ satisfies

$$
\frac{d}{dt}\int_{B_t}\rho(x,t)\,dx=0,
$$

where $\rho\ge0$ is mass density. This is a physical conservation premise. In reacting mixtures, an individual chemical species can have a source even when the total mixture mass obeys this balance. The changing integration region follows the material, rather than being a fixed laboratory control volume.
