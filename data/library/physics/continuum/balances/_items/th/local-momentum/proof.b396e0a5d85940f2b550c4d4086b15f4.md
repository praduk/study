The same change-of-variables calculation used for mass transport gives $d\int_{B_t}\rho v_i/dt=\int_{B_t}[\partial_t(\rho v_i)+\operatorname{div}(\rho v_i v)]dx$. Expand the integrand; the terms containing $v_i[\partial_t\rho+\operatorname{div}(\rho v)]$ vanish by continuity, leaving $\rho D_tv_i$. The divergence theorem converts the surface integral to $\int_{B_t}\partial_j\sigma_{ij}dx$. Equality for all subregions yields the pointwise equation by localization. Expanding continuity in reverse proves the conservative form.
