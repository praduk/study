For smooth fields and material @[diffeomorphisms]math:diffgeo:manifolds:df:smooth-map, mass conservation for every material subregion is equivalent to

$$
\partial_t\rho+\operatorname{div}(\rho v)=0.
$$

Equivalently $\rho(\chi(X,t),t)J(X,t)=\rho_0(X)$ is independent of time. For positive density constant along each particle, the condition becomes $\operatorname{div}v=0$.

Equivalently the mass form satisfies $(\partial_t+\mathcal L_v)(\rho\mu)=0$, or $\chi_t^*(\rho_t\mu)=\rho_0\mu_0$ when the reference mass form is chosen accordingly. Expanding $\mathcal L_v(\rho\mu)=(v(\rho)+\rho\operatorname{div}_gv)\mu$ recovers the continuity equation on any fixed oriented Riemannian spatial manifold. This follows from @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:th:transport-form and makes material conservation independent of coordinates.
