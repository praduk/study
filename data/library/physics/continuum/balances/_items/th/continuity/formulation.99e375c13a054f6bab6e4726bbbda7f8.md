For smooth fields and material diffeomorphisms, mass conservation for every material subregion is equivalent to

$$
\partial_t\rho+\operatorname{div}(\rho v)=0.
$$

Equivalently $\rho(\chi(X,t),t)J(X,t)=\rho_0(X)$ is independent of time. For positive density constant along each particle, the condition becomes $\operatorname{div}v=0$.
