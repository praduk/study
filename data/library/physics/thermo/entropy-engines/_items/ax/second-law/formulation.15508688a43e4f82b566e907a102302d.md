The macroscopic second-law framework assumes an entropy state function with


$$
\Delta S\ge\sum_j Q_j/T_j
$$


for a closed system exchanging heat with fixed positive-temperature reservoirs; use the corresponding integral for varying reservoir temperatures. Heat entering is positive. Equality is the reversible limit; the difference is entropy production. An isolated system therefore cannot decrease its thermodynamic entropy. This is not a claim about the sign of every microscopic fluctuation. Reference: [MIT entropy notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-020-thermodynamics-of-materials-spring-2021/mit3_020s21_l05.pdf).
