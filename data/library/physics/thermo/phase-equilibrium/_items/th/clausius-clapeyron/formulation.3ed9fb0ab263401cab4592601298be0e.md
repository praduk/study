In a vaporization model, assume throughout a temperature interval that $\Delta v=k_BT/p$, the vapor is ideal, and the latent heat per particle is a positive constant $\ell$. Then the model coexistence pressure satisfies


$$
\log\frac{p_2}{p_1}=-\frac{\ell}{k_B}\left(\frac1{T_2}-\frac1{T_1}\right).
$$
