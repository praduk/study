A bulk phase is a homogeneous equilibrium branch with specified intensive properties. At coexistence, distinct phases can share $T,p$ and the chemical potential of each exchangeable species. A first-order transition has a discontinuity in a first derivative of the equilibrium free energy. For a reversible conversion of one particle from phase 1 to 2 at coexistence, define the latent heat $\ell=T(s_2-s_1)$, where $s_i$ is entropy per particle. Interfacial energies and finite-size rounding are excluded from this bulk description.
