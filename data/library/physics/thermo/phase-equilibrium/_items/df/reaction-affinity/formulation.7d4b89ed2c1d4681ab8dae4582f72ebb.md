For a reaction with stoichiometric numbers $\nu_i$ (products positive), choose a reaction coordinate $\xi$ so that $dN_i=\nu_i\,d\xi$. At fixed $T,p$, $dG=(\sum_i\nu_i\mu_i)d\xi$. An interior reaction equilibrium requires $\sum_i\nu_i\mu_i=0$. With the activity convention $\mu_i=\mu_i^\circ+k_BT\log a_i$, where each $a_i>0$ is dimensionless, this condition becomes


$$
\prod_i a_i^{\nu_i}=\exp\left[-\frac{\sum_i\nu_i\mu_i^\circ}{k_BT}\right].
$$


For ideal gases one may choose $a_i=p_i/p^\circ$. Nonideal mixtures require measured or modeled activities. Writing a logarithm of a dimensional pressure would make the chemical-potential convention undefined.
