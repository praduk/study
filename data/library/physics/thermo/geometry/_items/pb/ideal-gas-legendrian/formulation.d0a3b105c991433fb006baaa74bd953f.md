Fix $N>0$, constant $C_V>0$, $T_0,V_0>0$, and reference entropy $S_0$. Here $C_V$ is the total constant-volume heat capacity in J/K. Define

$$
U(S,V)=C_VT_0\exp\bigl((S-S_0)/C_V\bigr)(V/V_0)^{-Nk_B/C_V},\qquad V>0.
$$

Compute $T$ and $p$ from this potential, check the contact condition, and express its Helmholtz potential in coordinates $(T,V)$. Verify the ideal-gas law and the stated heat capacity. This potential chooses a particular zero of internal energy.
