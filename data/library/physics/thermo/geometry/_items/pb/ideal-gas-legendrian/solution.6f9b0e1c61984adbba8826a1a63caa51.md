Differentiation gives $T=U_S=U/C_V>0$ and $p=-U_V=Nk_BU/(C_VV)=Nk_BT/V$. Therefore $dU-TdS+pdV=0$ on the graph, which is Legendrian in @physics:thermo:geometry:df:thermodynamic-contact.

Taking a logarithm yields $S=S_0+C_V\log(T/T_0)+Nk_B\log(V/V_0)$. Since $U=C_VT$, the Helmholtz potential is

$$
F(T,V)=C_VT-T\bigl[S_0+C_V\log(T/T_0)+Nk_B\log(V/V_0)\bigr].
$$

Directly, $-F_T=S$ and $-F_V=Nk_BT/V=p$, verifying @physics:thermo:geometry:th:legendre-contact. Finally $(\partial U/\partial T)_V=C_V$ and $pV=Nk_BT$. Every logarithm has a dimensionless argument. The model is valid only where its constant heat-capacity and ideal-gas assumptions are appropriate; it does not impose a low-temperature third-law limit.
