Define $\beta=T\,dS-p\,dV$. Direct application of @math:diffgeo:forms:df:exterior-derivative gives $d\beta=-(T_V+p_S)\,dS\w dV$. If $\beta=dU$, then $d\beta=0$ by @math:diffgeo:forms:th:d-squared, proving necessity. Conversely the specified derivative identity makes $\beta$ closed, so the @math:diffgeo:stokes-cohomology:th:poincare-lemma on a star-shaped domain produces $U$ with $dU=\beta$. Two such potentials differ by a function with zero differential; path connectedness makes that difference constant. On a domain with nontrivial first cohomology, closedness alone need not produce a global potential; periods around closed curves must also vanish.
