On a star-shaped open domain in fixed-$N$ coordinates $(S,V)$, let $T(S,V)$ and $p(S,V)$ be smooth. A smooth energy $U$ with

$$
dU=T\,dS-p\,dV
$$

exists exactly when $T_V=-p_S$. It is unique up to an additive constant on that domain. This is the integrability condition for the equilibrium graph to satisfy @physics:thermo:geometry:df:thermodynamic-contact. The statement concerns consistency of specified equations of state; it does not establish their empirical validity or stability.
