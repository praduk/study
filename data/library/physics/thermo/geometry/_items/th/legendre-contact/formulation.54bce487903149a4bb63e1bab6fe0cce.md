For the contact coordinates in @physics:thermo:geometry:df:thermodynamic-contact, replacing $U$ by $F=U-TS$ and the pair $(S,T)$ by $(T,-S)$ preserves the contact form:

$$
\alpha=dF+S\,dT+p\,dV-\mu\,dN.
$$

More generally, for $\alpha=dz-\sum_i y_i\,dx^i$ and any subset $I$ of indices, set

$$
\widetilde z=z-\sum_{i\in I}x^iy_i,
\qquad \widetilde x^i=y_i,\quad \widetilde y_i=-x^i\quad(i\in I),
$$

leaving other pairs unchanged. Then $d\widetilde z-\sum_i\widetilde y_i\,d\widetilde x^i=\alpha$. On an equilibrium graph, expressing the transformed potential as a smooth function of the new independent variables requires the corresponding partial Legendre map to be locally invertible.
