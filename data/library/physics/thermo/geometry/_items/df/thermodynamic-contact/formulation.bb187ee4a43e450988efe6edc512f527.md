For one material species with no work coordinates other than volume, consider coordinates $(U,S,V,N,T,p,\mu)$ on a seven-dimensional open domain. Use the contact form

$$
\alpha=dU-T\,dS+p\,dV-\mu\,dN.
$$

It has energy units. In the canonical notation of @math:diffgeo:geometric-structures:df:contact, take $z=U$, $q=(S,V,N)$, and conjugates $(T,-p,\mu)$; hence $\alpha\w(d\alpha)^3$ is nonzero. The ambient coordinates are independent; a material's equation of state imposes relations between them.

A smooth equilibrium fundamental relation $U=\mathcal U(S,V,N)$ defines the three-dimensional graph $T=\mathcal U_S$, $p=-\mathcal U_V$, $\mu=\mathcal U_N$. Its pullback of $\alpha$ vanishes, so it is Legendrian. This is the geometric form of @physics:thermo:potentials:ax:fundamental-relation. The graph is additional physical input, not something determined by the contact condition alone. At fixed $N$, the corresponding five-dimensional contact chart uses $\alpha=dU-TdS+pdV$.
