At fixed $V$, $dU=T\,dS$; at fixed $p$, $dH=T\,dS$. Thus $C_V=T S_T|_V$ and $C_p=T S_T|_p$. The chain rule yields $C_p-C_V=T S_V|_T V_T|_p$. The Maxwell relation gives $S_V|_T=p_T|_V$. Since $dV=V\alpha\,dT-V\kappa_T\,dp$, holding $V$ fixed gives $p_T|_V=\alpha/\kappa_T$. Also $V_T|_p=V\alpha$. Multiplying proves the formula. No division by a vanishing compressibility has been made.
