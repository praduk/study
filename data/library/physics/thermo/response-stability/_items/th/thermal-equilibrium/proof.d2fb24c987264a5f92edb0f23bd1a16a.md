Write $s(u)=S_1(u)+S_2(U-u)$. The fundamental relation implies $s'(u)=1/T_1-1/T_2$, so stationarity gives equal temperatures. Differentiating $S_i'=1/T_i$ yields $S_i''=-1/(T_i^2 C_{V,i})$. At the stationary point, $s''=S_1''+S_2''<0$ if both capacities are positive. The second derivative test proves strict local maximality. Endpoint maxima need not satisfy the interior derivative condition.
