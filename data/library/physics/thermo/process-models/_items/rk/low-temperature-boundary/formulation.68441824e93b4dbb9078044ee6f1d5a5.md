The formula $C_V=cNk_B>0$ cannot by itself specify a finite limiting entropy as $T\downarrow0$: at fixed $V,N$, its entropy difference is $cNk_B\log(T/T_0)$, which diverges to $-\infty$. Thus extending that classical constitutive model to arbitrarily low temperature is inconsistent with a finite zero-temperature reference entropy. A quantum spectrum, degeneracy, and the order of thermodynamic and zero-temperature limits must be specified before asserting a third-law limit. This is a model-boundary calculation, not a proof of an empirical third law.
