The classical dilute ideal-gas model has $pV=Nk_BT$. A calorically perfect version additionally assumes $U=cNk_BT$ with temperature-independent $c>0$ over the specified range. For nonrelativistic monatomic particles with no active internal degrees of freedom, the classical model uses $c=3/2$. Interactions, quantum degeneracy, and thermally activated internal states limit these assumptions. The equations are material models, not consequences of the first law alone.
