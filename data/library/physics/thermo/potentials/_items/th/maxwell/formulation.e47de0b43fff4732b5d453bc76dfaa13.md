On a fixed-$N$ single-phase chart where $F(T,V)$ is $C^2$,


$$
\left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_T=\left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial T}\right)_V.
$$

The same identity is $d(-S\,dT-p\,dV)=0$, an instance of $d^2F=0$ from @math:diffgeo:forms:th:d-squared. Expanding it gives $(S_V-p_T)\,dT\w dV=0$. Thus Maxwell relations are integrability conditions for the contact equilibrium graph, and @physics:thermo:geometry:th:equation-state-integrability supplies the converse potential construction on a star-shaped chart.
