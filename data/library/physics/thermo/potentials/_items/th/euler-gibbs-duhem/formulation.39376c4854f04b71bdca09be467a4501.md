If differentiable bulk energy is degree-one homogeneous in $(S,V,N)$, its fundamental relation implies


$$
U=TS-pV+\mu N,\qquad S\,dT-V\,dp+N\,d\mu=0.
$$

For a $C^2$ degree-one homogeneous energy, differentiating Euler's identity also gives $D^2U\,(S,V,N)^T=0$. The radial extensive direction is therefore a null direction of this @[Hessian]math:uganalysis:metric-multivariable:df:hessian. A thermodynamic Hessian is not automatically a @[positive definite]math:algebra:linear-foundations:df:bilinear-forms metric on the full extensive cone: independent state coordinates or a fixed scale must first be chosen. This matters when comparing contact equilibrium geometry with the explicitly normalized information metric in @physics:statistical:geometry:df:exponential-family.
