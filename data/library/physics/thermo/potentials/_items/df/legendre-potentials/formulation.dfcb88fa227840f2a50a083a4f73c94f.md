Define $H=U+pV$, $F=U-TS$, and $G=U-TS+pV$. Differentiating gives


$$
dH=T\,dS+V\,dp+\mu\,dN,\qquad dF=-S\,dT-p\,dV+\mu\,dN,
$$




$$
dG=-S\,dT+V\,dp+\mu\,dN.
$$


The natural coordinates replace extensive variables by their conjugates. Smooth local Legendre descriptions require suitable invertibility; phase coexistence can instead require convex thermodynamic constructions.

These substitutions are contact transformations in @physics:thermo:geometry:th:legendre-contact. For example $dU-TdS+pdV-\mu dN=dF+SdT+pdV-\mu dN$ on the full ambient space. A contact transformation always makes sense there, but using $(T,V,N)$ as coordinates on a particular equilibrium graph still needs the local invertibility already stated above.
