Define $H=U+pV$, $F=U-TS$, and $G=U-TS+pV$. Differentiating gives


$$
dH=T\,dS+V\,dp+\mu\,dN,\qquad dF=-S\,dT-p\,dV+\mu\,dN,
$$




$$
dG=-S\,dT+V\,dp+\mu\,dN.
$$


The natural coordinates replace extensive variables by their conjugates. Smooth local Legendre descriptions require suitable invertibility; phase coexistence can instead require convex thermodynamic constructions.
