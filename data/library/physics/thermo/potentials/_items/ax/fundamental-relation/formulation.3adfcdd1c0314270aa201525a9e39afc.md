Assume a smooth single-phase relation $U(S,V,N)$ with no further work coordinates and physical identifications


$$
dU=T\,dS-p\,dV+\mu\,dN.
$$


Thus $T=U_S$, $p=-U_V$, $\mu=U_N$. This describes adjacent equilibrium states, not irreversible heat. Only reversible closed processes have $\delta Q_{\rm rev}=T\,dS$. Reference: [MIT potential notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-020-thermodynamics-of-materials-spring-2021/mit3_020s21_l06.pdf).

Equivalently the graph of the equations of state is Legendrian for $\alpha=dU-TdS+pdV-\mu dN$ in @physics:thermo:geometry:df:thermodynamic-contact. Applying the exterior derivative to its pulled-back first law gives the integrability relations between response functions; @physics:thermo:geometry:th:equation-state-integrability proves the fixed-$N$ case. The geometric condition organizes the postulated equilibrium relation while leaving its physical origin explicit.
