For a piston moving against external resisting pressure $p_{\rm ext}$, with only pressure work, $\delta W=p_{\rm ext}\,dV$. Expansion gives positive work. Quasistatic means arbitrarily close to equilibrium; reversible additionally excludes friction, finite thermal gradients, and other entropy production. In the reversible limit $p_{\rm ext}$ approaches $p$, giving $\delta W_{\rm rev}=p\,dV$. Adiabatic means $Q=0$, not constant temperature.
