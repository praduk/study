For a closed stationary system, with bulk kinetic and external potential energies excluded from $U$,


$$
\Delta U=Q-W.
$$


Here $Q>0$ is heat entering and $W>0$ is work done by the system. Heat is energy transferred because of a thermal difference; work is nonthermal transfer through forces or controlled parameters. Neither is stored as a state variable. Open systems require transported energy to be included. This empirical balance uses the opposite work sign from the [MIT first-law notes](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/3-020-thermodynamics-of-materials-spring-2021/mit3_020s21_l03.pdf).
