Diagonalize $\rho$ with nonnegative eigenvalues $\lambda_i$ summing to one. At least one is positive, so $\sum\lambda_i^2>0$. Also $(\sum\lambda_i)^2=\sum\lambda_i^2+2\sum_{i<j}\lambda_i\lambda_j$, giving the upper bound. Equality means every pair product is zero, so exactly one eigenvalue is nonzero and it must be one. This is precisely a rank-one orthogonal projector. Conversely such a projector has trace of its square equal to one.
