For any matrix $A$ on subsystem $A$, cyclicity of the finite trace and commutation of operators on separate factors give


$$
\operatorname{tr}[\rho'_{AB}(A\otimes I)]=\operatorname{tr}\left[\rho_{AB}\left(A\otimes\sum_jK_j^*K_j\right)\right]=\operatorname{tr}[\rho_{AB}(A\otimes I)].
$$


By the defining partial-trace identity the two reduced matrices have the same trace pairing against every $A$. Choosing matrix units shows each of their entries agrees, proving equality. Conditioning on a particular outcome can alter the conditional remote state, but learning which conditional state applies requires the classical outcome information.
