For a density matrix $\rho_{AB}$, define $\rho_A=\operatorname{tr}_B\rho_{AB}$ by


$$
\langle i|\rho_A|j\rangle=\sum_b\langle i,b|\rho_{AB}|j,b\rangle
$$


in orthonormal product bases. It is the unique matrix satisfying $\operatorname{tr}(\rho_A A)=\operatorname{tr}(\rho_{AB}(A\otimes I))$ for every $A$. A bipartite pure state is entangled if it is not a product vector; a mixed state is separable if it is a convex combination of product density matrices, and entangled otherwise. Reduced-state mixedness signals entanglement for a globally pure state, but not for an arbitrary mixed global preparation.
