Spatial rotations act on quantum states by unitary operators, allowing a projective action on rays. The infinitesimal generators are angular momenta $J_i$, with convention $U_z(\theta)=e^{-i\theta J_z/\hbar}$ and


$$
[J_i,J_j]=i\hbar\sum_k\epsilon_{ijk}J_k.
$$


For a scalar wavefunction, orbital angular momentum is $\mathbf L=\mathbf X\times\mathbf P$. Intrinsic spin is an additional representation acting on internal components; it is not a classical rigid ball spinning in space. Differential commutators require a common invariant domain. In finite spin spaces all matrix products are defined.
