The positive projector is $(I+\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma)/2$. Its expectation in $|0\rangle$ is $(1+\cos\theta)/2=\cos^2(\theta/2)$, since the $\sigma_x,\sigma_y$ diagonal elements vanish and the $\sigma_z$ value is one. The negative probability is $\sin^2(\theta/2)$. Thus $\langle S_{\mathbf n}\rangle=(\hbar/2)\cos\theta$. Since $S_{\mathbf n}^2=\hbar^2I/4$, the variance is $\hbar^2\sin^2\theta/4$. The result is independent of $\phi$ because this preparation is rotationally symmetric about its quantization axis at the level of probabilities.
