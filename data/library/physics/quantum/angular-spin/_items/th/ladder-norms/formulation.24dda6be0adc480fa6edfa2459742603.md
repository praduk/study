Let Hermitian matrices $J_i$ obey the angular-momentum commutators. Put $J_\pm=J_x\pm iJ_y$ and $J^2=J_x^2+J_y^2+J_z^2$. If a normalized common eigenvector has $J^2\psi=\hbar^2j(j+1)\psi$ and $J_z\psi=\hbar m\psi$ for real $j,m$, then


$$
\|J_+\psi\|^2=\hbar^2[j(j+1)-m(m+1)],
$$




$$
\|J_-\psi\|^2=\hbar^2[j(j+1)-m(m-1)].
$$


A nonzero $J_\pm\psi$ has $J_z$ eigenvalue $\hbar(m\pm1)$.
