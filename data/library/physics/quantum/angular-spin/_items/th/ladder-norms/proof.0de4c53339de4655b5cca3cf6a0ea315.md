Since $J_+^*=J_-$, the first squared norm is $\langle\psi|J_-J_+\psi\rangle$. Expansion and $[J_x,J_y]=i\hbar J_z$ give $J_-J_+=J^2-J_z^2-\hbar J_z$. The other product is $J_+J_-=J^2-J_z^2+\hbar J_z$. Applying the eigenvalue equations proves both formulas. Also $[J_z,J_\pm]=\pm\hbar J_\pm$ directly from the original commutators, so $J_zJ_\pm\psi=\hbar(m\pm1)J_\pm\psi$. Positivity imposes the corresponding bounds on allowed ladder steps.
