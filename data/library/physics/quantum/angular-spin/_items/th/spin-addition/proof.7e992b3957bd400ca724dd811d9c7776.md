On a single factor $S_+|1\rangle=\hbar|0\rangle$, $S_+|0\rangle=0$, with the reversed actions for $S_-$. Summing the two-factor actions gives $J_\pm|s\rangle=0$ and $J_z|s\rangle=0$, hence $J^2|s\rangle=0$. For the symmetric basis denote vectors $t_+,t_0,t_-$. Directly, $J_-t_+=\sqrt2\hbar t_0$, $J_-t_0=\sqrt2\hbar t_-$, $J_-t_-=0$, and $J_+$ reverses this chain. Their $J_z$ values follow from the product labels. Apply $J^2=J_+J_-+J_z^2-\hbar J_z$ to each: the results are respectively $(2+1-1)\hbar^2$, $2\hbar^2$, and $(0+1+1)\hbar^2$. These four orthonormal vectors span the product space.
