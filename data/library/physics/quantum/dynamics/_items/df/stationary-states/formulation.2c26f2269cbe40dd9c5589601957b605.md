A normalized eigenvector $H\phi_n=E_n\phi_n$ evolves as $e^{-iE_nt/\hbar}\phi_n$. Its probabilities for every time-independent observable are constant. A superposition of distinct energies has cross terms with angular frequencies $(E_n-E_m)/\hbar$. Degenerate eigenvectors can be superposed without creating such relative time dependence. An energy eigenstate may carry a stationary probability current; time-independent density does not require zero current.
