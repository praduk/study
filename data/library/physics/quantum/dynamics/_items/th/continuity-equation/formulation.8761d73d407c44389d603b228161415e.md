Suppose $\psi(x,t)$ is continuously differentiable in $t$ and twice continuously differentiable in $x\in\mathbb R^d$, and solves


$$
i\hbar\partial_t\psi=-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\Delta\psi+V\psi
$$


with real $V$. Then $\rho=|\psi|^2$ and $\mathbf j=(\hbar/m)\operatorname{Im}(\overline\psi\nabla\psi)$ obey $\partial_t\rho+\nabla\cdot\mathbf j=0$.
