The equation and its complex conjugate give


$$
\partial_t|\psi|^2=\frac{i\hbar}{2m}(\overline\psi\Delta\psi-\psi\Delta\overline\psi).
$$


The real-potential terms cancel. Expanding the divergence of $\mathbf j=\hbar(\overline\psi\nabla\psi-\psi\nabla\overline\psi)/(2mi)$ cancels the gradient-product terms and leaves the negative of that expression. This proves the local identity. Integrating over a bounded smooth region and applying the divergence theorem gives loss of probability equal to outward flux; global normalization also requires control of flux at infinity.
