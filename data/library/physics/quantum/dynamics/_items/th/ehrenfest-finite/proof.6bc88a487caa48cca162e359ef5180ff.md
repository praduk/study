Differentiate all three factors. Since $\dot\psi=-iH\psi/\hbar$ and the first slot is conjugate-linear, the bra derivative contributes $i\langle\psi|HA\psi\rangle/\hbar$. The ket derivative contributes $-i\langle\psi|AH\psi\rangle/\hbar$, and the remaining term is $\langle\psi|\dot A\psi\rangle$. Combining gives the formula and the conservation statement. Finite dimension makes every product well-defined; extensions to differential operators require separate domain control.
