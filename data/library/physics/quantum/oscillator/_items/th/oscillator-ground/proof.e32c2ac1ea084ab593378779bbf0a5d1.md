Expand the nonnegative norm $\|\hbar\psi'+m\omega x\psi\|^2$. Its cross term is $2\hbar m\omega\,\operatorname{Re}\int x\overline{\psi'}\psi\,dx$. Integration by parts gives $\operatorname{Re}\int x\overline{\psi'}\psi\,dx=-\|\psi\|^2/2=-1/2$, with zero Schwartz boundary term. Therefore


$$
q[\psi]=\frac1{2m}\|\hbar\psi'+m\omega x\psi\|^2+\frac{\hbar\omega}{2}.
$$


Equality requires $\hbar\psi'+m\omega x\psi=0$, whose solutions are $C e^{-m\omega x^2/(2\hbar)}$. Gaussian integration fixes $|C|$ as stated; every such normalized function attains equality.
