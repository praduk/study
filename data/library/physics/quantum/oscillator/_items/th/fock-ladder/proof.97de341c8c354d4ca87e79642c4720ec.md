Directly, $aa^\dagger|n\rangle=(n+1)|n\rangle$ and $a^\dagger a|n\rangle=n|n\rangle$, also for $n=0$. Subtraction and the definition of $H$ prove both identities on their stated domain. If $\psi=\sum c_n|n\rangle$ is a nonzero eigenvector, each coordinate obeys $[\hbar\omega(n+1/2)-E]c_n=0$. At least one coefficient is nonzero, so $E$ is one listed value, and distinctness forces all other coefficients to vanish. No claim that arbitrary formal ladder algebras automatically yield this representation is used.
