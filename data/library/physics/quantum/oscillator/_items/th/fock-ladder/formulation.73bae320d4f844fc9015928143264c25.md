On $\ell^2(\mathbb N_0)$ with basis $|n\rangle$, define on finite sequences $a|0\rangle=0$, $a|n\rangle=\sqrt n|n-1\rangle$, and $a^\dagger|n\rangle=\sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle$. Set $H|n\rangle=\hbar\omega(n+1/2)|n\rangle$ with diagonal domain $\sum_n(n+1/2)^2|c_n|^2<\infty$. Then on finite sequences $[a,a^\dagger]=I$ and $H=\hbar\omega(a^\dagger a+I/2)$. Its normalized eigenvectors are phase multiples of $|n\rangle$ with the listed energies.
