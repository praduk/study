Both $a$ and $a^\dagger$ change the number label, so $\langle X\rangle=\langle P\rangle=0$. In the squares, the terms $a^2,(a^\dagger)^2$ also have zero diagonal expectation, while $\langle aa^\dagger+a^\dagger a\rangle=2n+1$. Thus


$$
\Delta X^2=\frac{\hbar(2n+1)}{2m\omega},\qquad \Delta P^2=\frac{m\hbar\omega(2n+1)}2.
$$


Their product of standard deviations is $\hbar(n+1/2)$, equal to $\hbar/2$ exactly for $n=0$. These calculations use finite basis states lying in every product domain involved.
