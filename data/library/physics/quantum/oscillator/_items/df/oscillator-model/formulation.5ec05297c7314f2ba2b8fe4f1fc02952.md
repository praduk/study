For $m,\omega>0$, the harmonic-oscillator model uses


$$
H=\frac{P^2}{2m}+\frac{m\omega^2X^2}{2},\qquad a_{\rm osc}=\sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{m\omega}}.
$$


Here $X\psi=x\psi$ and $P\psi=-i\hbar\psi'$ on a suitable common domain. The positive energy form is $q[\psi]=\hbar^2\|\psi'\|^2/(2m)+m\omega^2\|x\psi\|^2/2$ on functions with weak derivative and $x\psi$ in $L^2$. This specifies the standard lower-bounded realization through its closed form. The model approximates motion near a stable quadratic minimum only while omitted anharmonic terms remain small.
