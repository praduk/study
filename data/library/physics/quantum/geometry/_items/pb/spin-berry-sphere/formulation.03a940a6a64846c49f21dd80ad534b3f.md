Consider the normalized spinor

$$
\psi(\theta,\phi)=\begin{pmatrix}\cos(\theta/2)\\ e^{i\phi}\sin(\theta/2)\end{pmatrix},
\qquad 0<\theta<\pi.
$$

It is the $+1$ eigenvector of $\mathbf n\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$, where $\mathbf n=(\sin\theta\cos\phi,\sin\theta\sin\phi,\cos\theta)$. Compute its Fubini–Study metric, Berry connection and curvature. Find the holonomy around the latitude $\theta=\theta_0$ with $\phi$ increasing from $0$ to $2\pi$. State what extra physical condition is needed to identify this with the geometric phase of an evolving eigenstate.
