For @physics:quantum:geometry:df:berry-connection, a change of local unit frame $\psi'=e^{i\chi}\psi$ gives $\mathcal A'=\mathcal A-d\chi$ and $\mathcal B'=\mathcal B$. If a loop $C$ bounds an oriented surface $\Sigma$ on which a smooth unit frame exists, its holonomy is $\exp(i\int_\Sigma\mathcal B)$.

For a $C^2$ Hermitian matrix family $H(\lambda)$ with a simple eigenvalue $E_n$, choose a local normalized eigenvector $n$ and an orthonormal eigenbasis at the point considered. In real parameter coordinates,

$$
\mathcal B_{ab}
=-2\operatorname{Im}\sum_{m\ne n}
\frac{\langle n,\partial_aH\,m\rangle\langle m,\partial_bH\,n\rangle}{(E_n-E_m)^2}.
$$

The eigenvalue $E_n$ must remain separated from the other eigenvalues on the local parameter region. No smooth choice of every other eigenvector is required for this pointwise sum.
