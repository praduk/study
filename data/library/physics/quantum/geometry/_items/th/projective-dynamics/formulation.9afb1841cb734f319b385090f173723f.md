For @physics:quantum:geometry:df:projective-state-space and a Hermitian matrix $H$, define the ray function $h_H([\psi])=\langle\psi,H\psi\rangle$ with $\|\psi\|=1$. Then the two-form $\omega$ is symplectic and the Hamiltonian vector field of $h_H$ has horizontal lift

$$
X_{h_H}=-\frac{i}{\hbar}(H-h_HI)\psi.
$$

It is the projection of $i\hbar\dot\psi=H\psi$. For Hermitian $A,B$, the ray Poisson bracket satisfies

$$
\{h_A,h_B\}=\frac1{i\hbar}\langle\psi,[A,B]\psi\rangle.
$$

For a continuous time-dependent Hermitian matrix the same identification applies at each time to its vector field; time independence is needed only for conservation of the Hamiltonian itself.
