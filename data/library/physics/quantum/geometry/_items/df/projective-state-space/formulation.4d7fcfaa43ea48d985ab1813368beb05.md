For the finite-dimensional Hilbert space $\mathcal H=\C^n$, $n\ge2$, with inner product conjugate-linear in its first slot, the pure-state manifold is

$$
\mathbb P(\mathcal H)=(\mathcal H\setminus\{0\})/\C^*=\CP^{n-1}.
$$

Local charts are ratios $z^j/z^k$ on the set $z^k\ne0$; their smooth holomorphic transition maps give the quotient its manifold structure. A ray can be represented by a unit vector $\psi$, and tangent vectors to the ray space have unique horizontal representatives $\xi\in\psi^\perp$. Replacing $\psi$ by $e^{i\chi}\psi$ replaces the horizontal representatives by the same phase.

Our normalization of the Fubini–Study metric and the physical symplectic form is

$$
g_{\rm FS}(\xi,\eta)=\operatorname{Re}\langle\xi,\eta\rangle,
\qquad \omega(\xi,\eta)=2\hbar\operatorname{Im}\langle\xi,\eta\rangle.
$$

They are independent of the unit representative. For an arbitrary infinitesimal change of a unit lift, $\xi=(I-|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)d\psi$, so

$$
ds_{\rm FS}^2=\langle d\psi,d\psi\rangle-|\langle\psi,d\psi\rangle|^2.
$$

The metric is dimensionless and positive on nonzero ray tangents. The two-form has action units; @physics:quantum:geometry:th:projective-dynamics proves its closedness and its Hamiltonian relation to Schrödinger evolution. This is a finite-dimensional geometric realization of @physics:quantum:states-measurement:ax:pure-states, not a replacement for unbounded-operator domain conditions in infinite dimension.
