Write $\psi=e^{ikx}+re^{-ikx}$ on the left and $\psi=te^{iqx}$ on the right. Continuity of $\psi,\psi'$ gives $1+r=t$ and $k(1-r)=qt$. Solving yields $r,t$. The current of $Ae^{ikx}$ is $\hbar k|A|^2/m$, while a left-going exponential has its negative. Thus reflected and transmitted flux fractions are $R=|r|^2$ and $T=(q/k)|t|^2$. Algebra gives $(k-q)^2+4kq=(k+q)^2$, proving flux conservation. These scattering states encode fluxes and are not normalized bound-state vectors.
