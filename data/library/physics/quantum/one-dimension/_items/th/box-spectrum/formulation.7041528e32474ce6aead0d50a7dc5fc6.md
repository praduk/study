For $H=-(\hbar^2/2m)d^2/dx^2$ on $L^2(0,L)$ with domain $H^2(0,L)\cap H_0^1(0,L)$, every normalized eigenfunction is, up to phase,


$$
\phi_n(x)=\sqrt{\frac2L}\sin\frac{n\pi x}{L},\quad E_n=\frac{\hbar^2\pi^2n^2}{2mL^2},\quad n=1,2,\ldots.
$$


This statement classifies eigenfunctions; it does not assert completeness from the ODE argument alone.
