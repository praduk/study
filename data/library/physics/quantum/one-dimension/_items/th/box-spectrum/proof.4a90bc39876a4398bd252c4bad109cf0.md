For an eigenfunction, integration by parts with zero endpoint values gives $E\|\phi\|^2=(\hbar^2/2m)\|\phi'\|^2\ge0$. If $E=0$, $\phi'=0$ and both boundary conditions force $\phi=0$, impossible. Thus $k=\sqrt{2mE}/\hbar>0$. Solving $\phi''+k^2\phi=0$ gives $A\sin(kx)+B\cos(kx)$. The condition at zero gives $B=0$, and the condition at $L$ gives $kL=n\pi$ with positive integer $n$. The integral of $\sin^2(n\pi x/L)$ is $L/2$, proving normalization. Each listed function belongs to the stated domain and verifies the eigenvalue equation.
