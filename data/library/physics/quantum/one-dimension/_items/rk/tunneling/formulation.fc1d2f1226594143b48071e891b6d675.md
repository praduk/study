In a constant region where $V_0>E$, the stationary equation is $\psi''=\kappa^2\psi$ with $\kappa=\sqrt{2m(V_0-E)}/\hbar$. Its basis is $e^{\pm\kappa x}$. A single decaying exponential carries zero current, but a matched complex superposition through a finite forbidden region can carry nonzero current. Therefore one cannot infer zero transmission merely from the absence of local classical kinetic energy. Matching across both boundaries determines transmission. Energy remains the stationary eigenvalue $E$ throughout; tunneling does not temporarily violate energy conservation.
