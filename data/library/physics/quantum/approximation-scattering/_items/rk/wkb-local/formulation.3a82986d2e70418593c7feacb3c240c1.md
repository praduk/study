Away from turning points in a smooth one-dimensional potential, put $p(x)=\sqrt{2m(E-V(x))}>0$. Substituting $\psi=Ae^{iS/\hbar}$ into the stationary equation and matching successive formal powers of $\hbar$ gives $S'^2=p^2$ and $2A'S'+AS''=0$. Thus the local leading waves are


$$
\psi_\pm(x)\approx \frac{C_\pm}{\sqrt{p(x)}}\exp\left[\pm\frac i\hbar\int^x p(s)\,ds\right].
$$


The ignored term involves $\hbar^2A''$; slow variation relative to the local wavelength is required, often tested by $\hbar|p'|/p^2\ll1$ together with control of higher derivatives. The formula becomes singular at $p=0$, so turning-point matching is separate. No general uniform error bound or global quantization condition is claimed by this local formal calculation.
