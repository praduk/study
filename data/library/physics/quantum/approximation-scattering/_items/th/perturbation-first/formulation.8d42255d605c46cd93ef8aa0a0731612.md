Let $H(\lambda)=H_0+\lambda V$ be Hermitian matrices for real $\lambda$. Suppose a normalized differentiable eigenpair satisfies $H(\lambda)u(\lambda)=E(\lambda)u(\lambda)$ near zero, with $E_0=E(0)$ simple. Then


$$
E'(0)=\langle u_0|Vu_0\rangle.
$$


Choosing phase so $\langle u_0|u'(0)\rangle=0$, the coefficient along another normalized eigenvector $u_m$ of $H_0$ is $\langle u_m|u'(0)\rangle=\langle u_m|Vu_0\rangle/(E_0-E_m)$.
