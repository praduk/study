Choose an orthonormal eigenbasis and write $v=\sum c_j u_j$. Then $\langle v|Hv\rangle=\sum E_j|c_j|^2$ and $\sum|c_j|^2=1$. Subtracting $E_0$ gives $\sum(E_j-E_0)|c_j|^2\ge0$, with equality precisely when all coefficients at larger eigenvalues vanish. A restricted minimum is taken over fewer normalized vectors, so it cannot lie below $E_0$. Inclusion of trial subspaces makes the admissible set larger and therefore cannot increase the minimum. Convergence to an infinite-dimensional physical spectrum needs additional approximation and domain results.
