Let $H(\lambda)=H_0+\lambda V$ be Hermitian matrices, and suppose a normalized differentiable eigenpair tends to $u_0$ in the eigenspace of $H_0$ with eigenvalue $E_0$. If $P_0$ is the projector onto that eigenspace, then


$$
P_0VP_0u_0=E'(0)u_0.
$$


Thus first-order shifts of such branches are eigenvalues of the perturbation restricted to the degenerate eigenspace.
