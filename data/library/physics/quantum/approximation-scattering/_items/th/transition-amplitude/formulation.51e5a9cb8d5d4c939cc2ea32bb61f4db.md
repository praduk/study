Let $H_\lambda(t)=H_0+\lambda V(t)$ be finite-dimensional, with continuous Hermitian $V$ on $[0,t]$. Starting in an eigenvector $|n\rangle$ of $H_0$, the derivative at $\lambda=0$ of the interaction-picture coefficient for $m\ne n$ is


$$
c_m^{(1)}(t)=-\frac i\hbar\int_0^t e^{i(E_m-E_n)s/\hbar}\langle m|V(s)|n\rangle\,ds.
$$
