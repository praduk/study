Let real $V\in L^\infty(\mathbb R^3)$ be supported in a ball $B\subset\mathbb R^3$, and fix $k>0$. Consider the outgoing integral-equation model


$$
\psi_\lambda(x)=e^{ikz}-\lambda C\int_B\frac{e^{ik|x-y|}}{|x-y|}V(y)\psi_\lambda(y)\,dy,\qquad C=\frac{m}{2\pi\hbar^2}.
$$


For sufficiently small real $\lambda$, it has a unique bounded solution on $B$, extended by this equation outside $B$, whose amplitude satisfies uniformly in unit $\mathbf n$


$$
f_\lambda(\mathbf n)=-\lambda C\int_B e^{-ik\mathbf n\cdot y}V(y)e^{iky_z}\,dy+O(\lambda^2).
$$
