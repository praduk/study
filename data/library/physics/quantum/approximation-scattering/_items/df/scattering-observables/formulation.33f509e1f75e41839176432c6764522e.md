For a three-dimensional elastic scattering model with equal incoming and outgoing wavenumber $k>0$, choose asymptotic normalization, with the corresponding differentiated asymptotics used for the current,


$$
\psi(\mathbf x)=e^{ikz}+f(\widehat{\mathbf x})\frac{e^{ikr}}r+o(r^{-1}).
$$


The differential cross section is outgoing scattered flux per solid angle divided by incident flux density. For a scalar Schrödinger particle this gives $d\sigma/d\Omega=|f|^2$, with units $\mathrm{m^2/sr}$, since a spherical outgoing wave has radial current $(\hbar k/m)|f|^2/r^2$ and the incident density is $\hbar k/m$. The amplitude has units of length. Long-range Coulomb asymptotics, inelastic channels, and normalizable wave packets need modified or limiting formulations.
