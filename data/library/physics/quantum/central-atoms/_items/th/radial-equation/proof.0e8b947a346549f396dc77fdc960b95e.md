Use $\Delta=\partial_r^2+(2/r)\partial_r+r^{-2}\Delta_{S^2}$. For $R=u/r$, differentiation gives $R''+2R'/r=u''/r$. The angular eigenvalue contributes $-\ell(\ell+1)u/r^3$. Substitute both terms into $-(\hbar^2/2m)\Delta\psi+V\psi=E\psi$ and multiply the radial coefficient equation by $r$. Normalization follows from the volume element $r^2dr\,d\Omega$ and the angular normalization. The calculation is local for $r>0$, so it cannot choose a self-adjoint boundary condition at a singular endpoint.
