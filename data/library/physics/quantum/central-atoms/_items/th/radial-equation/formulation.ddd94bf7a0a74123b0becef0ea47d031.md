Assume $\psi(r,\theta,\phi)=u(r)Y_{\ell m}(\theta,\phi)/r$ for $r>0$, where $-\Delta_{S^2}Y_{\ell m}=\ell(\ell+1)Y_{\ell m}$ and $\int_{S^2}|Y_{\ell m}|^2d\Omega=1$. Then the equation with a radial potential is equivalent on $r>0$ to


$$
-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}u''+\left[V(r)+\frac{\hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)}{2mr^2}\right]u=Eu.
$$


Furthermore $\|\psi\|^2=\int_0^\infty|u(r)|^2dr$. Boundary conditions at zero and infinity remain part of the operator model.
