For one electron bound to a nucleus of charge $Ze$, the nonrelativistic relative-motion model uses reduced mass $m_r$ and $V(r)=-Ze^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0r)$. Its standard Coulomb realization has bound energies


$$
E_n=-\frac{m_r Z^2e^4}{2(4\pi\epsilon_0)^2\hbar^2n^2},\qquad n=1,2,\ldots.
$$


Allowed orbital labels are $\ell=0,\ldots,n-1$ and $m_\ell=-\ell,\ldots,\ell$, giving $n^2$ orbital states at each energy, or $2n^2$ including electron spin when spin interactions are omitted. There is also an ionization continuum above zero. This spectral classification is source-backed; completeness and the full radial quantization proof are not included here. The model omits fine structure, hyperfine effects, finite nuclear size, and radiation corrections.
