The $\sigma_x$ eigenvectors are $(|0\rangle\pm|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, with overlaps $(1\pm i)/2$, both of squared modulus $1/2$. Direct multiplication gives $\sigma_y\psi=\psi$, so its $+1$ outcome has probability one and its $-1$ outcome zero. The vector $(|0\rangle+|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$ has the same computational-basis probabilities as $\psi$ but different $\sigma_x$ probabilities. Relative phase therefore contains operationally measurable information.
