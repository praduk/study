Set $u=(A-\langle A\rangle)\psi$ and $v=(B-\langle B\rangle)\psi$. Cauchy-Schwarz gives $\Delta A\Delta B=\|u\|\|v\|\ge|\langle u|v\rangle|\ge|\operatorname{Im}\langle u|v\rangle|$. Self-adjointness and the product-domain assumptions imply


$$
\langle u|v\rangle-\langle v|u\rangle=\langle\psi|(AB-BA)\psi\rangle.
$$


The left side is $2i\operatorname{Im}\langle u|v\rangle$, proving the inequality. For position and momentum on a common Schwartz domain, $[X,P]\psi=i\hbar\psi$, hence $\Delta X\Delta P\ge\hbar/2$. That last commutator is not an equality of operators on every vector of $L^2$.
