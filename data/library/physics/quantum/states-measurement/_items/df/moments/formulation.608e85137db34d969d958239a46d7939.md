For normalized $\psi\in D(A)$ and self-adjoint $A$, define $\langle A\rangle_\psi=\langle\psi|A\psi\rangle$ and


$$
\Delta_\psi A=\|(A-\langle A\rangle_\psi I)\psi\|.
$$


Its square is the variance of the spectral measurement distribution. Writing it as $\langle A^2\rangle-\langle A\rangle^2$ through an operator product requires $\psi\in D(A^2)$; the norm definition needs only $D(A)$. Dispersion across repeated identically prepared measurements is distinct from experimental calibration error or a disturbance caused by another measurement.
