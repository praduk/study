# Geometry, prerequisite definitions, and linked items

The Mathematics and Physics library now develops physical models with intrinsic geometric structures and explicit prerequisite references. This change adds 72 entries: 33 Mathematics prerequisites and 39 Physics entries, including 40 definitions, 26 theorems with complete proofs, and six problems with complete solutions. It expands 78 existing entries and adds 534 labeled prerequisite links across 365 entries. Overlap between these groups gives 461 new or changed entries. All new entries start outside review.

## Content and conventions

Mathematics additions cover vector-space and module distinctions, duality, determinants, bilinear forms, eigenvectors, convexity, Hessians, probability, operator domains, distributions, Sobolev spaces, partitions of unity, pseudo-Riemannian metrics, the Hodge star, symplectic and contact structures, Lie groups, connections, curvature, holonomy, Killing fields, and Clifford algebras. Existing module language is made explicit without treating an arbitrary coefficient ring as a field.

Physics additions span every existing course: state manifolds and flux forms; kinetic metrics and momentum maps; Hamiltonian rays and optical metrics; relativistic Maxwell equations, potentials, cohomology and the action; observer field splitting; contact thermodynamics; finite-state Fisher geometry; projective quantum dynamics and Berry curvature; advected mass and vorticity; Killing currents; and bundle-valued field actions and Yang–Mills variation. Existing component calculations are retained.

The electromagnetic convention is signature $(-+++)$, orientation $dx^0\wedge dx\wedge dy\wedge dz$ with $x^0=ct$, and $F_{0i}=-E_i/c$. With current three-form $\mathcal J=c^{-1}\iota_J\operatorname{vol}_g$, the equations are $dF=0$ and $d\star F=\mu_0c\mathcal J=\mu_0\star J^\flat$. The action, observer contractions, source normalization and Lorentz force have been checked against that convention. Hamiltonian geometry uses $\iota_{X_H}\omega=dH$. Quantum inner products remain conjugate-linear in the first slot, while the Mathematics Hilbert-space convention remains linear in the first slot.

The conditional statements specify smoothness, domains, orientation, boundary flux, positivity, finite-dimensional or finite-state scope, and spectral gaps as appropriate. Yang–Mills requires the bilinear pairing to be invariant under the entire gauge group, including disconnected components. Neither geometric notation nor a variational calculation establishes an empirical postulate, a global analytic existence theorem, or a controlled approximation by itself. The glossary audit fills consequential gaps; it is not a claim that every term in the entire curriculum has been exhaustively formalized.

Inspected background sources include [Conrad on differential-form Maxwell equations](https://virtualmath1.stanford.edu/~conrad/diffgeomPage/handouts/maxwell.pdf), [Tong on general-relativity geometry](https://davidtong.org/teaching/general-relativity/grhtml/S3), and [Tong on Hamiltonian mechanics](https://davidtong.org/teaching/classical-dynamics/dynhtml/S4). Further inspected sources support the [contact-geometric thermodynamic setting](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.7340), [fluid one-form formulation, Appendix A](https://www.math.toronto.edu/khesin/papers/newton_diffeos.pdf), and [Berry phase convention, original paper §2](https://michaelberryphysics.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/berry120.pdf). The stored derivations and worked examples are independently authored; the course guides identify the applicable sources and limits.

## Reading and performance

References are visibly highlighted in both themes and retain keyboard, hover and touch previews. Every reading page has a Linked items section covering incoming tags from headers, formulations, proofs and solutions to any variant of the selected entry. Source entries appear once in authored order and navigate to the exact referencing variant. The list initially loads 40 entries and supports continuation, changed-library resets, empty results and retry. Review prompts preserve answer-reveal gating.

Incoming references are indexed lazily once per immutable library snapshot. Markdown parsing is cached by exact source text with a bounded cache; ordinary writes and direct-file checks invalidate the appropriate snapshot. The reader batches simultaneous references and deduplicates requests in flight without retaining stale completed results across page visits. The API contract and invalidation details are in ../SEARCH.md.

A like-for-like check on the unchanged 12-reference Physics course map reduced reference HTTP requests from 12 to one and total page API requests from 15 to five, including the new backlinks request. One before/after run reached heading plus resolved references in 1,197.8 ms / 859.8 ms; these timings are individual local samples, not a general latency guarantee. On the original 1,371-entry corpus, direct-store measurements gave about 92.6 ms to construct the initial incoming map and a 0.023 ms median for a warm linked page. A batch of 11 references took 0.042 ms versus 0.258 ms for 11 individual store calls. Index construction itself was about 1.90 s after versus 1.80 s before, so no index-build speedup is claimed.
